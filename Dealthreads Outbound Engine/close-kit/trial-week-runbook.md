# 14-Day Pipeline Proof Runbook

Internal delivery standard for one client. The system is installed in five business days and operated for 14 calendar days.

## Before day 1

- [ ] Payment confirmed and SOW countersigned.
- [ ] Kickoff questionnaire completed.
- [ ] Kickoff, live dial block, and day-14 verdict booked.
- [ ] Client workspace and folder created.
- [ ] HubSpot/client CRM, calendar, notification channel, suppression file, and sender access requested.
- [ ] Calling owner confirmed: named client operator or managed caller.

## Days 1–2 — lock the economics and target

- [ ] Record specialty, geography, company criteria, buyer roles, exclusions, and suppression.
- [ ] Record gross profit per placement, expected placements per good client, realistic close rate, and conversation capacity.
- [ ] Write the one-paragraph ICP and get written approval.
- [ ] Order client-owned domains and inboxes; configure SPF, DKIM, and DMARC.
- [ ] Start warmup.
- **Client-visible output:** approved ICP, structural ROI model, infrastructure receipts, and dated launch plan.

## Days 2–4 — build the machine

- [ ] Build and verify 500 target client accounts.
- [ ] Attach the best available hiring, expansion, leadership, regulatory, facility, or niche-specific signal.
- [ ] Rank the queue by fit, reason now, reachability, and expected client value.
- [ ] Draft email, call, LinkedIn, positive-reply, referral, OOO, and no-interest paths.
- [ ] Run final suppression and quality checks.
- [ ] Obtain written messaging approval.
- **Client-visible output:** first 50 rows, scoring explanation, channel playbooks, and approval record.

## By business day 5 — launch

- [ ] Configure live email from the Dealthreads warmed Proof fleet while client domains continue warming.
- [ ] Configure the client instance: provider IDs, CRM, calendar, notification channel, brief schedule, reply SLA, and owners.
- [ ] Confirm stop-on-reply, tracking, caps, timezone, suppression, and escalation rules.
- [ ] Send signed receiver checks and record the result.
- [ ] Confirm morning brief, hot-reply alert, Call-Ready Queue, scorecard, CRM sync, and calendar logging.
- **Client-visible output:** launch receipt showing the system, controls, active audience, and first queue.

## Days 6–13 — operate the proof

- [ ] Triage every reply within the agreed SLA.
- [ ] Generate the dossier, response draft, and next action for every valid positive.
- [ ] Offer two meeting times before using the calendar-link fallback.
- [ ] Create immediate call tasks from positive replies and meaningful package engagement.
- [ ] Run the live 60-to-90-minute dial block and record dispositions and objections.
- [ ] Coordinate LinkedIn actions around the same account thread.
- [ ] Review daily positive reply → conversation → booked → held movement.
- [ ] Reallocate the remaining audience only when evidence supports the change.
- **Client-visible output:** daily brief and transparent activity/revenue-chain view.

## Day 14 — Pipeline Verdict

- [ ] Report agreed activity completed.
- [ ] Report positive replies, samples/packages, live conversations, meetings booked, meetings held, qualified opportunities, and sourced pipeline separately.
- [ ] Show the top-performing segments, signals, messages, objections, and unresolved gaps.
- [ ] Show fulfillment hours, tool costs, and the client's structural payback math.
- [ ] Make one recommendation: continue at $4,500, scope managed calling from the measured workload, refine with a specific change, or stop.
- [ ] Ask directly: "Do you want the first 90 days to start on [date]?"
- **Client-visible output:** written Pipeline Verdict and signed decision.

## Handoff

Client receives:

- target and suppression files;
- domains and inboxes;
- approved playbooks;
- CRM export;
- recordings, transcripts, and dispositions;
- Proof scorecard and Pipeline Verdict.

If Client continues on the $4,500 plan, collect the $2,500 month-one balance. Do not add a completion fee.

## Hard rules

- Never guarantee meetings, placements, opportunities, or revenue.
- Never count booked and held meetings as the same metric.
- Never hide weak results behind activity totals.
- Never launch without approval and suppression.
- Never allow a positive reply to exist without a human owner and next action.
- Client tools remain on the client's card at cost.
- Exclude The Calendar Group from every target universe.
