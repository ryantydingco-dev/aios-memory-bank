# Dealthreads VSL Script (3 minutes, ~460 words at a natural 150 wpm)

Talking head, direct to camera. One take energy, like you're explaining it to one staffing owner across a desk. Optional cutaways: SmartLead dashboard, Salesfinity mid-dial, the site's machine diagram, a call recording waveform. Don't script the cutaways, just grab them after.

Delivery notes: the parenthetical beats are pauses, not words. Read it out loud twice before recording; anywhere your mouth stumbles, change the words to yours. The script promises infrastructure, never meetings. Keep it that way if you improvise.

---

**[HOOK]**

Every meeting on my calendar this month came from a cold email or a cold call. That's been true my entire career. And in the next three minutes I'll show you how to find out, in five days, whether outbound can do the same thing for your business. Without hiring anyone.

**[WHO'S TALKING]**

Quick background so you know who you're listening to. My first sales job was selling Kirby vacuums door to door. From there I went into commercial roofing, where I built my first real outbound systems. LinkedIn, cold email, and always, always the phones. Then I went into tech as an SDR, and inside one year at the same company I was promoted from SDR to senior SDR, and then into a growth account manager role. Every one of those steps came from the same skill: starting conversations with strangers who ended up buying. I still prospect every single day. Now I install that entire system for other companies.

**[THE PROBLEM]**

Here's the problem with how most companies test outbound. (beat) Option one, you hire an SDR. That's 85 grand all in, three to four months of ramp, and if pipeline never shows up, you still don't know if it was the rep, the list, or your market. (beat) Option two, you hire an agency. They rent you results from infrastructure they own. Lists you never see, domains they keep, reporting you can't audit. The day you stop paying, all of it disappears.

**[THE TRIAL]**

So here's what I do instead. In five days, I install your complete outbound system and run it live while you watch. (beat) Day one, we lock in your ICP on one call. You bring two things: who you sell to, and what you want them to hear. I build a scored list of four to five hundred of your exact buyers, verified emails, direct numbers. And I register email domains and inboxes in your name. Not mine. Yours. (beat) Days two through five, the machine runs. I'm on the phones personally every day. Your offer goes out by email from my already warmed sending fleet, so there's no two week wait. And you get a daily report with every call recording and every reply. (beat) Day six is the verdict. Your real numbers, and an honest read: go, refine, or no.

**[THE GUARANTEE]**

Now the part that matters. I don't guarantee meetings. Nobody honest can, and anyone who does is guessing with your money. What I guarantee is the machine. Everything I just listed, delivered and live in five business days, with receipts, or you don't pay. And whatever the verdict says, you keep all of it. The list. The domains. The scripts. Every recording. You walk away owning a working outbound system either way.

**[CTA]**

The trial is two thousand dollars, and it credits fully into month one if we keep working together. I take two of these a month, because I'm the one dialing. (beat) There's a calendar right below this video. Pick a time. Thirty minutes, direct. And if it's not a fit, I'll tell you straight.
