# The Napkin Game Plan (2026-07-16)

## 1. The only numbers that matter

**Daily (the 5-line scoreboard):**
- 600 new leads emailed (machine does it)
- 75 dials → 5 real conversations
- Every reply answered inside 1 hour
- 1 meeting booked every 2 days
- 0 same-day fulfillment misses

**The funnel, rough (weekly at full pace):**
3,000 new leads → ~30 replies → ~10 positive → 4 meetings booked → 3 held → 1 Pipeline Proof sold

**Money per unit:**
- 14-Day Pipeline Proof: $2,000 (credits to month 1)
- Staffing Client Pipeline OS: $4,500/mo; client team works the prepared call queue
- Managed calling: scoped after the Proof from measured volume, coverage, and contractor cost
- Cost per client: ~$0 (tools on their card). Your fixed cost: ~$1,000/mo tools.

## 2. One customer to $10K/month (customer-count ladder)

| Milestone | MRR | What it takes | Rough timing |
|---|---|---|---|
| Customer 1 (Proof → Pipeline OS) | $4,500 | ~10 held meetings, one strong 14-day Proof | Weeks 1-3 |
| Customer 2 | $9,000 | Same again; over-deliver #1 for the case study | Weeks 4-6 |
| Customer 3 (Pipeline OS) | **$13,500** | Pipeline is compounding by now; recordings + referrals from #1 and #2 do half the selling | Weeks 6-10 |

Each customer ≈ 10-12 held meetings ≈ 3-4 weeks of hitting the daily scoreboard. That's the whole business: hit the 5 lines daily, and the ladder climbs itself.

## 3. Sustainability rules (what makes it a business, not a sprint)

1. **Retention is the real product.** 90-day terms, weekly reporting, honest verdicts. A core client kept 6 months = $27K; churn resets the ladder. Over-deliver Pipeline Proofs ruthlessly.
2. **Known ceiling, planned break.** At 4 clients or 15+ delivery hours/week, hire the first delivery contractor out of margin. That's the blueprint's trigger, not a judgment call.
3. **The machine feeds itself.** Bulk pull monthly, inject 4K/week, new dealthreads-branded inboxes warming at all times. Pipeline never depends on your energy level.
4. **One vertical until it's boring.** Staffing through early September (~24K TAM). Then the same playbook onto MSPs (68K TAM) = the second $10K, at near-zero marginal build cost.
5. **Every Proof makes marketing.** Recordings, real numbers, before/afters → LinkedIn posts, call-recording proof, the live-calls section. Month 3's pipeline should be part inbound.
6. **Weekly scoreboard, 5 lines, no more:** sends / conversations / meetings held / Proofs in flight / MRR. If all five trend right, ignore everything else.
