## ICP definition (one profile, four verticals)

Owner-led US B2B service or software company, 2 to 50 employees, whose revenue depends on a steady flow of new client conversations and who has no dedicated SDR. Decision maker: owner / founder / managing partner. Trigger signals that promote a lead to Tier 1: hiring internal BD/sales (they want growth and have budget), recent LinkedIn activity about pipeline or hiring, agency size 5 to 25 (big enough to pay, small enough that the owner IS the buyer).
