# Sprint scorecard — how the day gets scored (2026-07-24 → 2026-08-24)

No ranks, no cosplay. The business is the game; the score is real. Three rules:

1. **Reps earn points.** Text the bot (same chat as the brief):
   `ASK Angel` +100 · `DIALS 75` +2 each · `WARM 20` +10 each (cap 20/day) ·
   `REC` +150 · `CLOSED 1500` when money lands.
   The machine adds verified outcomes on its own: confirmed positive +50,
   same-day package +50, booked meeting +250. Machine sends earn nothing —
   only what you did counts.
2. **150 points = a W day.** One ask, or 75 dials, or a recording. Consecutive
   Ws build a 🔥 streak. Every day of the sprint is a W or an L — the record
   (e.g. 9-3) prints on the morning scoreboard next to dollars closed.
3. **The weights are the strategy.** An ask outscores 50 dials because asks are
   where money lives. A meeting is the biggest automatic award. Warm touches
   cap daily so the goldmine hour gets worked every day, not binged once.
   `SCORE` any time for today's count, record, and streak.

The point of the whole thing (Ryan, 2026-07-26): hours that used to go to
Call of Duty go to the business, and the business gives back the same loop —
instant feedback per action, a visible score, a reason to queue one more rep.
Integrity rule: a logged ASK means a number actually left your mouth.
