## THE PROCESS — Trial. Verdict. Engine.

**1 · The Trial (5 days, $2,000).** Day 1 we install: 400 to 500 scored, verified buyers, domains and inboxes registered to you, sequences and call scripts approved by you. Days 2 to 5 we run it: daily dials on a parallel dialer, your offer emailed to the full list from our already-warmed fleet (that's how email fits in a 5-day window; nobody starting fresh can do this), video follow-ups on warm replies. Daily report, every call recorded.

**2 · The Verdict (day 6).** A written Math of Sales review and an honest call: go, refine, or no. If outbound doesn't fit your market, we'll say so.

**3 · The Engine (monthly).** If the math works, the system runs every week: email, phones, buying-signal targeting, weekly reporting in your own portal. Your $2,000 credits fully into month one.
