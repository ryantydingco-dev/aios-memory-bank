## Pre-Send Checklist

- Correct recipient and company.
- Human reply is genuinely positive; not OOO, unsubscribe, spam block, or departed employee.
- Suppression checked across SmartLead, Sendr, Salesfinity, HubSpot, and QuickBooks.
- Customer relationship owner identified.
- Current 2026 order, quote, and A/R checked where relevant.
- Product shown maps to a real SKU with realistic inventory and decoration.
- Logo is letter-perfect and approved as a visual concept, not production art.
- Pricing includes product, decoration, freight, setup, art, kitting, spoilage, and margin.
- Delivery claim is feasible and approved.
- Links and attachments open correctly.
- Source and next action will be logged immediately after the human sends.

## Pre-Publish Checklist

- Written permission for customer name, logo, reply, and order details.
- No personal emails, phone numbers, invoice numbers, private footers, or accessible private links.
- "Inquiry," "quote," "booked order," "invoiced revenue," and "cash collected" used accurately.
- Current company facts use approved language.
- CTA maps to one offer and one audience.
- Final publication remains a deliberate human action.
