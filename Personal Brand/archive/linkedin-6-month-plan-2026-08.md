Built 2026-08-20 from a live scrape of the profile (`linkedin-scrape-2026-08-20.md`), the Caleb Ralston plan, the content production system, and the Aug profile rewrite doc.

Goal: a network and community that generates organic leads for Creative Alternatives and for AI automation work, with Ryan becoming the known operator for AI-run revenue systems in real SMBs. One brand serves both. The story is already decided: the guy actually transforming a real 27-year, $2.65M family print shop with AI, receipts on screen.

Hard guardrails this plan lives under:
1. LinkedIn is a daily 2-hour block, 7 days a week (upgraded from 1 hour on 2026-08-20 at Ryan's call). The block counts as ask-generation, not just content: comments and warm DMs are asks.
2. No public monetization asks for 6 to 12 months. The brand warms inbound; it doesn't pitch. DMs open conversations, they don't pitch either.
3. No fabricated numbers. Observed rates only, and say when you don't know.
4. 90-day metric is things published + conversations started, not views.

## Part 2: The engine (what runs every day for 26 weeks)

Upgraded 2026-08-20: daily 2-hour block, 7 posts a week, prospect commenting, warm DMs. Full run-of-show, calendar, and scripts live in `linkedin-daily-block-playbook.md`. Summary:

### Daily cadence

1 original post every day Mon through Sun (content factory drafts, Ryan rewrites 10 to 20% and posts), 15 to 20 substantive comments a day weighted toward prospects and their watering holes, and 10 warm DM touches a day (new connection requests with a note + follow-ups with engaged people). Weekends carry the personal narrative formats since B2B reach drops 30 to 50% on Sat/Sun and story posts are the format least hurt by it.

Comments and DMs are the growth lever at 1,561 followers, not posts. Comment mix: roughly 50% prospects and prospect watering holes (event planners, ops and office managers, firm admins, camp directors), 30% peer builders (AI-for-SMB operators, outbound people, promo/print industry folks), 20% aspirational large accounts (Jason Bay tier and up). A thoughtful comment on a 50k-follower post gets seen by more of your ICP than your own post does right now.

### DM rules (the short version)

Warm only: people who engaged with your content, accepted a connection, commented anywhere you commented, or fit ICP and just posted something real. First message never pitches. Connection format: opening line alone, blank line, hook, blank line, question. Stay under ~15 connection requests a day so the account never trips LinkedIn's ~100 to 150/week throttle.

### Pillar mix

| Pillar | Share | Formats (already defined, never invent a fourth) |
|---|---|---|
| Authority | ~40% | Teardown: open a real system on screen, show what it did this week, real numbers |
| Narrative | ~30% | Sprint Log: scoreboard, what happened, what it taught. Post-Mortem whenever something breaks (the format nobody else in this niche can make) |
| Community | ~20% | Comments, Maclaine crossovers, spotlighting customers or peers, poll posts |
| Offer | max 10%, max 1/week | Mockup wedge shown as story ("someone replied to a cold email, here's what they got back in 40 minutes"), never a pitch |

### Standing content rules

1. Every post gets one contextual credibility line with an observed number ("1 positive per ~900 sends across 77 inboxes" beats "15 years in sales").
2. Use the named IP out loud until people repeat it: Honest Math, the Signal Engine, the Reactivation Engine, the Mockup Wedge. Kenny is the recurring character; the shop floor is the recurring set.
3. Repurposing chain stays law: film once, cut five ways. LinkedIn text posts are rewritten native, never pasted transcript.
4. Humanizer audit pass before anything ships.
5. 70/20/10: 70% proven formats, 20% single-variable iterations, 10% weird swings.

### Measurement (honest math native)

- Multiplier Sheet: 90-day median engagement per format, every post logged as a multiple, judged at day 11, refreshed monthly. Kill any format under 1x three times running.
- Inbound log: every DM, connection note, or reply that mentions your content goes in a file with date, who, what they said, and which post pulled them. This is the north star, not followers.
- Ask every warm lead: "what made you confident to reach out?" Log the answer.

Realistic 6-month targets at daily cadence: 5,000 to 8,000 followers, 180+ originals published, ~2,500 comments, ~1,800 DM touches, and by month 3 to 4 the first genuinely organic inbound (the DM layer should produce conversations well before that). Don't judge the posting system before month 4; judge the DM system weekly on conversations started.

## Part 4: Tripwires

1. A day passes with zero posts: the block moved or friction crept in. Fix the physical thing (factory draft not ready, posting not scheduled), not the motivation.
2. The post eats the whole block and comments/DMs get skipped: wrong order. DMs and comments come first inside the block; they're the asks. Post ships with less polish that day.
3. Any format under 1x median three times: killed at the monthly review. If daily volume drags the 90-day median down at the week-4 review, Sunday goes first.
4. You catch yourself drafting a "5 AI tools that will change your business" post with no CA receipt in it: delete it. That's the common enemy's content, not yours.
5. A DM contains a pitch, a link to buy anything, or a discount: it doesn't send. Conversations only for 6 months.
6. Connection requests exceed ~15/day or acceptance rate drops under 30%: pull volume back before LinkedIn does it for you.
7. Follower count becomes the number you check daily: reread this doc. The conversation and inbound logs are the score.
