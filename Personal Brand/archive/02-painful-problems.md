# Painful Problems List — the standing ideation engine
*Caleb's Gift Formula: (Painful problem + my unique solution) × contextual credibility × proven wrapper. Each row below is a video/post seed. Add to this list forever — more problems = more content ideas forever.*

ICP: small-business owners ($1–10M) and the sales/ops people inside them, skeptical of AI hype.

| # | Painful problem (theirs) | My unique solution | Contextual credibility line |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "We tried cold email and got nothing" | Honest math first: funnel-backwards from observed rates; tripwires kill dead campaigns before they burn weeks | "We observed ~1 positive per 700–1,100 sends across a 100-inbox infrastructure — and planned around it" |
| 2 | Leads go cold because nobody follows up | Reactivation engine: your existing customer base converts ~10x cold | "Mined a 2,700-customer QuickBooks base into 981 reactivation targets for a $3.2M business" |
| 3 | "AI tools demo great, then die in our business" | You are the source, AI is the multiplier — install AI on top of a working manual process, never instead of one | "I run 15+ AI pipelines inside one real company; the ones that survived all replaced a process I'd done by hand first" |
| 4 | Don't know WHO to sell to this month | Sell to moments, not industries — trigger events (new openings, trade shows, mergers, hiring) | "Our signal engines found 85 trade-show exhibitors and 89 brand-new gyms with buy-now windows, from free public data" |
| 5 | Lead data costs a fortune (Apollo/ZoomInfo bills) | Self-sourced enrichment at ~$0.02/contact from owned scrapers + free registries | "Cut our per-contact cost to about two cents; paid tools became the gap-filler" |
| 6 | Owner IS the sales team and has no time | One repeatable outbound motion, draft-only automation, human approves every send | "I run CA's entire outbound solo alongside everything else — because the machine drafts and I decide" |
| 7 | Deliverability: emails land in spam | Infrastructure discipline: warmup, reputation monitoring, signature hygiene, inbox-level audits | "96 of our 100 inboxes sit at 100% reputation; one account-level signature once branded every send wrong — audited and fixed" |
| 8 | Generic AI copy sounds like everyone else | Worker/verifier split: fresh-context AI graders + a human rewrite pass; never self-grade | "Every CA email passes independent verifier agents before I ever see it" |
| 9 | "We can't compete with the big guys" (4imprint problem) | Wedge on the #1 buyer pain the giants can't personalize (our free mockup wedge) | "A top-5% distributor competes with a billion-dollar catalog — we win on the thing they can't do at scale" |
| 10 | Referrals dried up, no repeatable acquisition | Multi-channel warm board: email + LinkedIn + calls stacked on the same small list | "Warm board beats raw volume in our observed data" |
| 11 | Hiring an agency burned them | Share the knowledge, sell the execution — judge any vendor by whether they show their playbook openly | "Here's our entire playbook, free; hire us only if you don't want to run it yourself" |
| 12 | Can't tell if marketing is working | Track conversions and inbound quality, not views/opens; ask every buyer "what made you reach out?" | "We grade every campaign as a multiple of its own 90-day baseline" |
| 13 | Legacy business, tribal knowledge in the owner's head | Document the owner (interviews, call recordings) → turn instincts into systems AI can run | "Kenny has 25 years in his head; my job is turning it into engines" |
| 14 | Fear that AI content will embarrass the brand | Hard copy rules enforced in the pipeline (no discounts, no hype, no fabricated claims) | "Our factory has anti-fabrication rules baked in; I still edit the last 10% by hand" |
| 15 | "Where do I even start with AI?" (overwhelm) | Start with the revenue process that already works and is most repetitive; one engine at a time | "We built one engine at a time for a year — each one shipped and verified live before the next" |

## Usage
- LinkedIn factory: pull one row per post; problem = hook, solution = body, credibility line = proof sentence.
- YouTube: each row supports multiple videos over time (write 2–3 credibility variants per row as they accumulate).
- Review monthly: add new problems from sales calls, DMs, and Kenny's customer conversations (Caleb: DMs/customer questions beat polls — 5 people asking the same thing in 3 hours = make it now).
