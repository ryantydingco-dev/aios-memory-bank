# Multiplier Sheet Rules (Caleb's data system)
Data lives in `04-multiplier-sheet.csv` (move to Google Sheets when convenient).

1. **Benchmark = MEDIAN (not mean) of the last 75–90 days**, per platform AND per format. Refresh monthly — the bar rises with you.
2. **Judge at day 11** post-publish (content often pops ~day 7). Log the multiplier, not raw views: "1.5x", not "1,200 views."
3. **Track only what changes behavior:** posts/month (the #1 input metric), watch time / avg view duration, multiplier, and **inbound quality** (who DM'd/replied — this is the north star). Views are a weighted metric: "in favor of conversion, never at its expense."
4. **Attribution question on every warm reply/discovery call:** "What made you confident to reach out?" Log the answer in inbound_quality_notes. Make more of whatever they name.
5. **Kill rule:** an experiment scoring <1x three times in a row dies (unless it purely fuels your enjoyment — then keep at low frequency).
6. **Monthly top-10% review:** look at the best 10% of the last 30–90 days, find the 1–2 elements present in ALL of them, replicate those in everything. Never study the bottom 10%.
7. **70/20/10 the output:** 70% proven formats, 20% single-variable iterations of proven, 10% weird swings. "We love a good flop."
8. Log which contrarian lever each post tests (honest-math / receipts-over-demos / moments-not-industries / etc.) — after 90 days, the lever with the most quote-backs and DMs becomes THE brand belief.
