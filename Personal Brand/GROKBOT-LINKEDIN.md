## DM scripts

Format: opening line, blank line, hook, blank line, question. Cap 10 connection requests a day. Pull volume if accept rate drops under 30% or replies become shallow.

**They posted an event:**
> Saw your post about [the event].
>
> I spend half my week around event and merch projects, so I always notice the details.
>
> What part ended up being the most fun to put together?

**After they accept:**
> Thanks for connecting.
>
> Your [post/comment] on [thing] stuck with me. I am in the middle of modernizing a 27-year business and post the build as it happens.
>
> What are you working on this quarter?

**They engaged with a post (WARM, after 24h):**
> Noticed you [commented on / reacted to] the [topic] post.
>
> Curious what landed. I'm trying to see which parts of this build are useful versus just interesting to me.

**Mockup offer (only after a two-way conversation reveals a need):**
> You mentioned [the event] is coming up in [month].
>
> Standing offer: tell me the event and I'll send mockups with your logo already on the gear, free, usually same day. No call, no deck.
>
> Want me to put a couple together?

If they are on a CA list, the first line names that moment (opening, show, last order year), not "saw your post."

## Hard rules

- No em dashes. No LinkedIn-influencer voice.
