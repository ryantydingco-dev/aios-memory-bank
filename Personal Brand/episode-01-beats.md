# EPISODE 1 — "I got laid off today. Here's the plan."
Record: 2026-08-04. Length: 5-7 min. One take. No script.

**Why today and not tomorrow:** this is the only day you can film the day it
happened. The whole channel is a documentary; this is frame one.

## Setup (2 minutes)
- Camera on the desk, you in your chair, normal clothes, no lighting rig
- Second screen or share: the morning brief / scoreboard
- Do not clean the desk. Do not change shirts. That IS the format.

## The beats (talk, don't read)

**1. The fact. (30 sec)**
What happened this morning. Plainly. No drama, no spin, no "excited to announce."
You got laid off. You got 8 weeks. That's it.

**2. What I've actually been building. (90 sec — screen share)**
Show the brief. Show the scoreboard. Say the real numbers out loud:
- 5,668 emails last week
- 18 replies
- 7 people who raised their hand
- **0 dollars so far**
Say the zero. The zero is the entire reason anyone will believe you at week 12.

**3. The honest diagnosis. (60 sec)**
Seven people said yes and I never asked a single one of them for money. I was
building the machine instead of selling with it. That's not a tooling problem,
it's me.

**4. The plan. (2 min)**
Eight weeks. Close the seven. Make the $497 offer the front door. Call the people
who already replied. Twenty personal notes a day to people who know me. And
publish this, whether it works or not.

**5. The commitment. (30 sec)**
What this channel is: every week, the real numbers, including the weeks where the
number is zero. No hooks, no "I made $10k in 30 days." If it fails, you'll watch
it fail here.
