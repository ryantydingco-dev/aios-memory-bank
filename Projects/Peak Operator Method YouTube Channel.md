## Core Story

Ryan's girlfriend and her dad run a $3M revenue promotional products business. The business has customers and revenue, but no strong go-to-market system, no modern operating system, and QuickBooks is the main source of truth.

Ryan will use the business as the proof environment for practical AI implementation: lead generation, customer reactivation, sales follow-up, quoting, dashboards, SOPs, reporting, and business efficiency.

## Audience

- Traditional SMB owners and family businesses with revenue but messy systems.
- AI-curious operators who want practical implementation, not tool hype.
- 9-to-5 tech, sales, and account management people who want to use AI to become operators or business builders.
