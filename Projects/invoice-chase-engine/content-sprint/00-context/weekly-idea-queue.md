# Weekly Ideabrowser Episode Queue

## Week focus

Turn Ideabrowser ideas into boring business experiments Ryan can build in public.

Every candidate below must become:

```text
one buyer
one painful workflow
one visible artifact
one paid test
one YouTube episode
```

## Selection rule

Pick the episode with the clearest artifact Ryan can show today.

The best idea is not always the highest score. The best daily episode is the one that can become visible proof on screen.
