# Today's YouTube Episode Pack

## Episode

**Title:** I'm Turning a $650K Invoice Recovery Into an AI Service

## Episode premise

Ryan helped Kenny at Creative Alternatives, a 25+ year business, help recover $650K in open invoices over 90 days.

The interesting part is that Kenny already had QuickBooks. The invoices existed. The accounting software was not the broken piece.

The leak was after the invoice got sent:

- Who follows up?
- When do they follow up?
- Did the client receive it?
- Did anyone track promises to pay?
- Did small overdue invoices quietly become old overdue invoices?

Ryan connected QuickBooks to a Claude-powered follow-up system and created email sequences so invoice reminders went out more consistently and fewer invoices slipped overdue.

Now the YouTube/business experiment is simple:

**Can this one-off workflow become a repeatable AI service called the AR Leak Audit / Invoice Chase Engine?**

## Why this is interesting

This is not interesting because "AI is coming for accounting."

It is interesting because a real business already had the core system, QuickBooks, and still had a manual follow-up gap that cost attention, time, and cash flow.

Most boring businesses do not need a moonshot AI app. They need the tiny company living inside their inbox to stop forgetting things.

This episode is Ryan showing the first repeatable piece of the service:

**AR Leak Audit: 15 places invoices leak after they're sent.**

## Cold open

"A 25-year business had QuickBooks, sent invoices, and still had $650K sitting in open invoices.

QuickBooks was not the problem.

The problem was what happened after the invoice got sent.

Kenny was manually emailing clients one by one, trying to keep track of who had paid, who promised to pay, who had a dispute, and who just went quiet.

So I connected QuickBooks to a Claude-powered follow-up workflow and helped create reminder sequences that made the chase more consistent.

Over 90 days, that helped recover $650K in open invoices.

Now I'm trying to answer a very boring, very useful question:

Can I turn that workflow into a repeatable AI service business?"
