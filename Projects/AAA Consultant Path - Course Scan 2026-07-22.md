# AAA Accelerator — AI Consultant Launchpad (Course Scan)

Scanned: 2026-07-22 · Source: hub.aaaaccelerator.com/c/the-consultant-path · 6 sections, 25 lessons

## What the course is
A beginner roadmap to landing a first AI-consulting client by selling an **"AI Tools Audit"** — a diagnose-and-prescribe service (the "doctor" frame: you don't build the medicine, you diagnose and prescribe off-the-shelf AI tools). No coding required. Origin story: Mert & Ali (Istanbul) went from 12 months of hype-chasing to $300K in 6 months by selling the audit.

## Core model
- **Offer:** AI Tools Audit — identify a business's bottlenecks, quantify their cost, recommend specific off-the-shelf AI tools with ROI math.
- **What clients buy:** measurable ROI ("trade $1,000 to stop losing $24,000/yr"), not "AI." You're paid for the Knowledge Gap.
- **Pricing ladder:** Client #1 free (for testimonial) → #2–4 at $250 (anchored against "$1,000 standard fee, portfolio-building discount") → raise with every win → $1,000 audits → $5k retainers (Scaling Stage, unlocked after 3 paid clients).
- **Acquisition:** warm outreach. Claimed stat: 91% of members who landed a first client in under 9 days used warm outreach. Ask for help, not a sale.

## The pipeline (Phase by phase)
**Phase 1 — Get the meeting**
- Magic Message (4 elements: Authority, Reason, Scarcity, Soft Ask): "I've been learning AI tools that save business owners hours on admin work. I'm building my portfolio, so I'm doing 3 free AI audits this month. Do you know anyone?"
- 3 reply branches: referral → ask for intro; "can't think of anyone" → keep-me-in-mind; "I need this" → 15-min call + Calendly link.
- Minimum stack: dedicated Gmail + free Calendly (15-min "Discovery Call") + Google Meet. No website/LLC/logo.
- Volume: mine 30+ warm contacts (phone/LinkedIn/email/FB), 10–30 msgs/day, daily 60–90-min outreach block, tracker spreadsheet.

**Phase 2 — Discovery → Audit**
- 15-min discovery call: explain process, collect basics, book the 60-min audit live on the call (doctor's intake frame). No pitching/diagnosing.
- Pre-audit intake form (Google Form) sent within 15–60 min of discovery call: pain points, tools, team structure.
- **AI Senior Partner Strategy:** feed form responses + website text to Claude → outputs a Business Snapshot + Investigation Zones (a call-plan cheat sheet).
- 60-min audit call: ask questions, keep them talking, attach numbers (time, people, cost) to every pain. **No Solution Rule** — never recommend tools live; "I'll test options against your workflow and come back with the right one." Record with Fireflies.

**Phase 3 — The deliverable**
- **Investigator prompt:** feed all 4 data sources (form, website, pre-call report, transcript) to Claude → prioritized bottleneck zones with quotes + cost math.
- Research tools (TheresAnAIForThat.com; vet: credible reviews, free trial, sane pricing). Pick Top 3 winners: "[Tool] — $X/mo — solves [bottleneck], saves [X hrs/wk]."
- **AI Opportunities Report:** 3-part slide deck (Report Builder prompt → Gamma/Canva → PDF, client logo on slide 1). Real cost math, named tools + pricing.
- Present live on a 30-min call — NEVER just email the deck (≤20 min presenting). Ask for a 30-second video testimonial ON the call, recorded live.

**Phase 4 — Get paid**
- Value = Skills × Experience. Reps over Revenue: low-price audits are paid practice.
- **Proof Line:** "I recently helped [client type] identify [X tools] that will save them [$ / hours] per month." Leads every message and call.
- Client #2 plays: (1) Hinge Referral from client #1, (2) proof-based warm outreach. Simple invoice + 1-page agreement. Build list to 100–200 contacts, daily outreach block.
- Loop unchanged until 3 paid clients → unlocks Scaling Roadmap ($10k–$50k/mo stage; also gateway to AAA agency model and the $5k retainer upsell where the $1,000 audit is the entry point).
