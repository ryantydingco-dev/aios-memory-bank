# Dealthreads Outbound

## What it is

Dealthreads is Ryan’s active **fractional SDR / managed outbound service**. It installs a client-owned outbound system, runs it live for five days, and lets the client judge the working machine before committing monthly. It does not guarantee meetings.

This project is distinct from the older Dealthread brokerage-AIOS kit and the DealThreads website-intake widget.

## Operating model

Four channels feed one CRM:

1. SmartLead — cold email and reply activity.
2. Salesfinity — calls, prioritizing engaged email leads.
3. LinkedIn — connection and conversation continuity.
4. Sendr — personalized video for warm replies, meeting confirmation, and recap.
5. HubSpot — intended source of truth for contacts, deal stages, notes, and outcomes.
6. cal.com — booking path.

Daily priority order:

1. Triage replies and fulfill valid sample requests the same day.
2. Call engaged leads and sample recipients.
3. Work fresh Tier-A phone queues.
4. Continue LinkedIn touches.
5. Deliver live trials before lower-priority activity.
