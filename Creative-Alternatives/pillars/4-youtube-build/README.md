# Pillar 4 — YouTube build-in-public

Film and publish the transformation of Creative Alternatives. The build is the show. Runs in parallel with every other pillar from day one.

## The premise

Ryan takes a 25-year, word-of-mouth, $3.2M promo-products business and modernizes it with AI — on camera. Real business, real owner, real stakes. That's a stronger hook than a generic "AI tips" channel: there's a protagonist (Kenny), a transformation arc, and verifiable results.

## How it connects

- **Substance comes from the other three pillars.** Every mapped workflow, built automation, campaign result, and hard decision is an episode.
- **Craft comes from the CGE skills** (Ryan's YouTube Growth Engine training is already installed as skills).

## Workflow per episode

1. **Pick the idea** — validate with `cge-video-idea-finder` (Icahn Method). Pull from `/episode-capture` notes.
2. **Package it** — `cge-holy-trifecta` for title + thumbnail + intro.
3. **Script it** — `youtube-script-writer`.
4. **Produce** — record + edit (Remotion/media tools available if needed).
5. **Review the upload** — `youtube-launch-loop` to read the analytics and log the lesson.

## Files

- `series-plan.md` — the series arc and episode backlog.
- `episodes/` — one folder/file per episode (outline → script → post-mortem).

## The rule

Don't manufacture drama or fake results. The real transformation is enough, and fabricated proof is expensive on camera. Respect Kenny's boundaries on what's filmable (`[CONFIRM]` early).
