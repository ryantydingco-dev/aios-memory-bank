## His arc (why it worked)
1. **Urgency hook** — AI is a tidal wave "becoming surfable," the time is now, most people aren't using it yet (internet / Bezos-Musk analogy).
2. **Lesson 1: just start** — "ask Claude." Have a conversation with the tool; stop hunting tutorials.
3. **Proof story** — the time-tracking app he built: 8 hours → 30 min → 3 min across 2023→2025. A non-coder builds a real thing.
4. **Core mechanic: "it's all about data"** — pipe business data (Gmail, CRM, project mgmt, Slack, Zoom transcripts) into one database, give Claude read access → "my eye of Sauron" that sees the whole company.
5. **How to start (non-digital business)** — first *capture/track* your workflow so AI can see it.
6. **Small wins** — auto inbox cleanup by rules, flag missed tasks, auto-draft post-call notes + follow-up emails from Zoom transcripts.
7. **Caveat** — data privacy/siloing: decide what's OK to put in a cloud AI (NDAs, HIPAA, bank info).
8. **80/20 rule** — AI gets you 80%, the human finishes the last 20%. Don't expect full hand-off — this is where most people quit too early.
9. **Vision** — dashboards where one human approves/denies the AI's proposed actions = the work of 100 people.
10. **CTA** — go use Claude, use voice-to-text, comment, report back.
