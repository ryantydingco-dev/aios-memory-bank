## Why he's your template

## The anatomy of his video (what makes it work)
1. **Result-first hook** — opens with "I made 3x more money in 30 days" + an income graph. He leads with *proof*, not preamble. Then names the problem, promises the payoff ("4 upgrades"), says "let's get into it."
2. **Numbered framework** — the body is **4 "upgrades."** Clean, followable, listicle structure. (Yours: the 5 workflows / 5 rounds — same thing.)
3. **Explain → SHOW it live** — every point is *demonstrated* on screen (the terminal actually doing the work). The screen-record IS the proof. He doesn't just talk about it.
4. **Constant on-screen graphics** — a text card for each point, a simple diagram (Team Agent vs Single Agent), the results graph. Light motion graphics throughout.
5. **Builds a REAL thing, start to finish** — an actual $9/mo tool, not theory. (Yours: a real CA automation.)
6. **Funnel close** — recap graphic → pitch the free Skool community (his lead magnet) → like + join. (Yours: the free AI Agent Playbook.)
