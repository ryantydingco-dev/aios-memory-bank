### 7 AI agents you can build to run your business — with the exact workflow for each

## First, the 10-second version
A **chatbot** answers when you talk to it. An **AI agent** has a goal and works on its own — it watches for a trigger, makes a plan, uses your tools (email, accounting, calendar, CRM), takes action, and checks its own work. **Chatbots talk. Agents do.**

**The one rule:** don't automate your whole business at once. Pick **ONE** agent below — the most boring, repetitive task you have — and build just that. Get one win, then add the next.

---

## How to read each agent
- **Trigger** — what kicks it off
- **What it does** — the steps it runs on its own
- **Keep human** — the part you don't hand over
- **Time saved** — the realistic payoff
- **Build with** — no/low-code tools to wire it up

> **Build-with toolbox (pick one to start):** n8n · Zapier · Make · Gumloop · CrewAI · MindStudio. All let you connect a trigger → an AI step → your apps without heavy coding.

## 1. The Invoice-Chase Agent  💰 *(best first build — pure money)*
- **Trigger:** an invoice goes past due in your accounting software.
- **What it does:** drafts a polite reminder in your voice → sends it → waits → follows up firmer if still unpaid → logs replies → flags disputes.
- **Keep human:** the big-client relationship calls.
- **Time saved:** 70–90% less time on invoicing chase-ups.
- **Build with:** n8n or Zapier + your accounting tool (QuickBooks, etc.) + email.

## 2. The Customer Reactivation Agent  🔁
- **Trigger:** a weekly scan of your customer list.
- **What it does:** flags customers who haven't ordered in 9+ months → ranks them by past spend → drafts a personal win-back message for each → hands you a sorted list.
- **Keep human:** the phone call to your highest-value lapsed customers.
- **Time saved:** turns "we should really reach out to old customers" (never happens) into a ready list every Monday.
- **Build with:** Gumloop or n8n + your customer/order data + email.

## 3. The AI Receptionist (Voice Agent)  ☎️
- **Trigger:** an inbound phone call.
- **What it does:** answers naturally → captures who they are + what they need → answers FAQs (hours, pricing ballpark, turnaround) → books a callback or texts you a clean lead summary.
- **Keep human:** the actual quote and the close.
- **Time saved:** stops leads from hitting voicemail and vanishing when you're slammed.
- **Build with:** a voice-agent platform (Vapi, Bland, Synthflow, or similar) + your calendar.

## 4. The Quote-Drafting Agent  📝
- **Trigger:** a new request comes in (email or form).
- **What it does:** reads the ask → pulls the right products + your pricing → drafts a full quote ready for one-click approval.
- **Keep human:** approve / tweak / send.
- **Time saved:** 20-minute quote → 30-second check.
- **Build with:** n8n or Make + your product/price list + email.

## 5. The Inbox-Triage Agent  📥
- **Trigger:** a new email lands.
- **What it does:** sorts it (order / quote / supplier / junk) → summarizes long threads → drafts replies for routine ones.
- **Keep human:** approval on what actually sends.
- **Time saved:** hours a week back if your inbox is chaos.
- **Build with:** Zapier or n8n + Gmail/Outlook.

## 6. The Lead-Gen / Outbound Agent  🎯  *(bonus)*
- **Trigger:** you pick a target type (e.g. a customer segment or area).
- **What it does:** finds matching prospects → enriches them → drafts a personalized first touch (bonus: a custom visual/mockup) → queues for review.
- **Keep human:** approve before anything sends — and keep it compliant.
- **Time saved:** replaces hours of manual list-building + writing.
- **Build with:** Clay or n8n + an email/LinkedIn tool.

## 7. The Order & Supplier Follow-Up Agent  📦  *(bonus)*
- **Trigger:** an order is placed.
- **What it does:** tracks proof approvals + ship dates → chases the supplier when something's late → alerts you on delays → updates the customer.
- **Keep human:** exceptions and any money decisions.
- **Time saved:** kills the "where's my order?" back-and-forth.
- **Build with:** n8n or Make + your order data + email.

## Why most agent projects fail (so yours won't)
Around 40% of agent projects get abandoned — but it's almost never the AI's fault:
- **41% had unclear success criteria** → *Fix:* define exactly what "done right" looks like before you build.
- **33% didn't give the agent access** to the tools/data it needed → *Fix:* connect the real systems.
- **The rest:** no human checking the edges → *Fix:* always keep a human on exceptions.

**Scope it tight. Give it access. Watch the exceptions.** That's the whole game.
