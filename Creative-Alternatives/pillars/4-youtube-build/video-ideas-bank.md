## ⏱️ Recency rule — AI niche (READ FIRST)
In AI a 6-month-old *topic* can be dead. But we tested filtering muses to "this month" and it mostly surfaces **off-niche hype** (AI song covers, AI dramas, agent courses, stock plays) — noisy. So the lever isn't muse-age, it's **topic currency**:
- **Ride what's surging NOW**, confirmed by **trend data + AI news**, not just muse age. Tools: `vidiq_outliers publishedWithin:thisWeek/thisMonth` + `vph`, `vidiq_trending_videos`, plus a WebSearch on current AI releases.
- **Current wave (as of now):** AI **agents** ("not chatbots — the software layer that does real work"), surging tools **n8n (+125%)** & **Claude Code (+938%)**, **AI voice agents / receptionists**, and concrete releases like **Meta's WhatsApp Business AI agent**.
- **Two streams:** *Timely* = newsjack a release (big reach, short shelf-life — film within days). *Evergreen* = "how I use AI for [business task]" (compounds). Run both.
- **Re-validate every ~2 weeks** — AI moves fast. Always CTR-score before producing.
