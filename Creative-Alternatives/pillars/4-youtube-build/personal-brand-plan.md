# Personal Brand Plan — Ryan / Creative-Alternatives-AIOS

> Created 2026-08-05. Runway reality: 8 wks severance + family-loan backstops = ~6 months to make it work.
> The revenue sprint (plans/60-day-revenue-sprint.md) is untouched by this — content never displaces dials.
> Voice + guardrails: ca-brand-voice skill. Raw/uncut rule: AI never writes Ryan's words.

## Positioning (the one-liner)
**"I got laid off, so I went all-in: taking my girlfriend's dad's 27-year-old, $3M word-of-mouth business
from 1999 into the AI age — and documenting every step so I never need a corporate job again."**

- The BRAND is Ryan (the operator on a journey). CA is Season 1 — the current proof.
- Brand around the journey, not a niche: audience follows the person; the business/AI/systems are the vehicle.
- Long-term: the brand is what makes Dealthreads inbound-only viable and gives CA its online presence for free.

## The content engine (one input, every platform)
**Input:** OBS records every workday (already running) + phone clips for real-life moments (Kenny, deliveries, walk-ins).

| Layer | What | Where | Cadence |
|-------|------|-------|---------|
| Anchor | Weekly episode of the series (Laid Off → All In arc, see episode list from session 2026-08-05) | YouTube | 1/wk |
| Clips | Daily scoreboard short + 1 real-moment short (from CLIP THAT markers) | TikTok + IG Reels + YT Shorts (same clip, all 3) | 1–2/day |
| Text | Daily-ish build note: what happened, real numbers, what worked/failed | LinkedIn (primary — where B2B swag buyers + Dealthreads clients live), X optional mirror | 5/wk |
| Capture | Free lead magnet per value video (playbook/prompt/template) → email list from day 1 | Beehiiv/ConvertKit-tier [pick one] | grows passively |

**Division of labor:** Ryan talks (raw, unscripted). Claude packages: titles/thumbnails (ICAHN + vidiq scoring),
clipping, captions, scoreboard data before recording, publishing cadence, idea bank.

## Rules
1. Stakes on screen: every piece names the clock + a real number. Never round up; bad weeks air.
2. Anonymize customer/financial data before anything hits a screen (brand bible guardrail).
3. Work leads, camera follows. Content edits live in evenings/weekends; 10–6 belongs to acquisition.
4. Same story everywhere — one narrative, repurposed, not three content strategies.
5. Kenny/Maclaine approve anything they're in or that touches customers.

## 6-month arc
| Month | Brand milestone (alongside revenue sprint) |
|-------|--------------------------------------------|
| 1 | Launch: LinkedIn layoff post, video #1 (the layoff + the plan), daily shorts habit, handles secured, email capture live |

## Metrics (measure the channel in reach/brand, CA in pipeline — never confuse them)
Weekly: posts shipped vs planned (the only controllable), views, followers, email subs, inbound DMs.
Vanity numbers don't gate decisions until month 4; consistency does.
