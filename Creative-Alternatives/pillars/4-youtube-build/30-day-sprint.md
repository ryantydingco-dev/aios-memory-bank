# 30-Day Video Sprint — Creative Alternatives AI Build

## The engine (non-negotiables)
- **Value-first standard:** every video *shows a real AI thing working on CA* and hands the viewer a copyable how-to (the prompt/workflow in the description). The work IS the content — one real demo per video.
- **ICAHN every idea:** each video models a proven muse/format (real demand), never a clone.
- **Cadence:** **2 long-form / week** (backbone) + **near-daily Shorts** (reach). Floor if a build slips = 1 long-form that week. Don't break the Shorts streak.
- **One pipeline per video** (the skills, in order): ICAHN muse → Holy Trifecta (title/thumbnail/intro, CTR-scored) → script (youtube-script-writer) → **do the real CA work + capture results** → record (talk + screen-record) → edit + cut Shorts → publish → launch-loop (log the lesson).

## Shorts — the reach layer (1/day, Mon–Fri minimum)
Sources, in priority:
1. **Cut from each long-form** — the result reveal, the on-screen prompt, the "watch this" moment (3–5 Shorts per long-form).
2. **Standalone one-prompt tips** — "the exact AI prompt I use to [X]" (one prompt, 30–45s).
3. **Story hits** — the AOL-email-from-1999 bit, "an $3M business with no systems," etc.
Post to YouTube Shorts + TikTok + IG Reels + a LinkedIn version.

## Per-video pipeline (run this every time)
1. **ICAHN** — confirm the muse/demand (vidiq_outliers) before producing.
2. **Do the CA work** — build the real thing, time it, capture what AI caught.
3. **Holy Trifecta** — title + thumbnail + intro, CTR-scored (vidiq_score_title).
4. **Script** — youtube-script-writer (value-first demo, the `[FILL IN]`s = your real numbers).
5. **Record** — talk to camera + screen-record the demo (anonymize sensitive data).
6. **Edit + cut Shorts.**
7. **Publish → launch-loop** — read the first-24h data, log the lesson, feed the next idea.

## Reality check / the floor
8 long-forms in 30 days is ambitious for a solo creator who's *also* doing the real CA work. If a build slips: that week drops to **1 long-form**, you lean on #4 or #8 (no-build videos), and you **never miss the daily Short.** Consistency of at-bats > perfection. Machine Mastery rule: batch — record 2–3 talking-head segments in one sitting, cut a week of Shorts at once.
