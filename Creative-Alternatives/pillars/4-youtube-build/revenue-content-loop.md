# Revenue-to-Content Loop

Content documents the Creative Alternatives transformation; it is not a separate business during the $1M window.

## Publishable inputs

A content idea must begin with a real CA event:

- Revenue won or lost.
- A campaign result or customer objection.
- A workflow before-and-after.
- A store, mockup, quote, or fulfillment lesson.
- Kenny's operating judgment.
- A measurable failure and the decision it caused.

If there is no receipt, artifact, or verified event, there is no content assignment.

## Weekly loop

1. Friday's revenue review identifies one story with the best business lesson.
2. Confirm privacy, customer naming, financial, and claim boundaries.
3. Record one simple build-in-public episode or talking-head explanation.
4. Reuse the same recording for one LinkedIn post and up to two short clips.
5. Track whether it creates qualified conversations, store-preview requests, or trust—not generic views alone.

## Priority rule

The daily revenue power list comes first. Content uses surplus time or records work already being done. A late reply, mockup, quote, or store preview cancels content work for that block.

## Keep/kill rule

After every ten published pieces:

- Keep topics that create qualified attention or sales conversations.
- Repackage the strongest proof with a new angle.
- Kill formats that attract generic AI viewers but no CA buyers, partners, or useful operator audience.
