# Backend Discovery Questions — Creative Alternatives

> Ask these in order. The goal is to understand the systems well enough to build the first backend operating layer.

## Short version

If time is limited, answer these first:

1. Where do new orders come in?
2. Where do printer/vendor confirmations come in?
3. Is there one open-order list today?
4. Are purchase orders created in QuickBooks?
5. Which spreadsheets run the business?
6. Where do approved proofs live?
7. Who checks vendor costs against customer invoices?
8. Who follows up on unpaid invoices?
9. Which vendor/printer relationships are most important?
10. What must stay Kenny-only?

## Maclaine questions

### QuickBooks

1. Which QuickBooks product/version do you use?
2. Do you use estimates, invoices, sales receipts, purchase orders, bills, or projects?
3. When a customer says yes, what gets created in QuickBooks first?
4. Do you create purchase orders in QuickBooks?
5. Do vendor bills get tied to customer jobs?
6. How are customer payments matched to open invoices?
7. What makes reconciliation hard?
8. Which reports do you check weekly?
9. What do you wish QuickBooks showed you every morning?
10. Are item costs tracked anywhere?

### Spreadsheets

1. What spreadsheets do you open every week?
2. Which one would break the business if it disappeared?
3. What does each spreadsheet track?
4. Which spreadsheet duplicates QuickBooks?
5. Which spreadsheet has pricing, product costs, or margins?
6. Which spreadsheet only you know how to use?

### AR and payments

1. Who owns AR follow-up now?
2. How often do you review AR?
3. Do you send statements?
4. Do some customers get special treatment?
5. Are any 90+ invoices actually bad debt?
6. When do you require deposits?
7. What is the most annoying payment/reconciliation task?

## Kenny questions

### Order flow

1. Walk me through one recent order from first request to payment.
2. Which parts happen in your head?
3. When do you personally need to be involved?
4. What makes a job risky?
5. What slips when things get busy?
6. What do customers ask you for status on?

### Quoting and supplier choice

1. How do you decide price?
2. How do you decide vendor/printer/decorator?
3. What should never be automated in quoting?
4. Which suppliers are trusted for which kinds of jobs?
5. What supplier mistakes are expensive?
6. What customer promises do we need to protect?

### Do-not-disrupt

1. Which customers require personal handling?
2. Which vendors require personal handling?
3. What should Ryan not change without asking you?
4. What would make an automation annoying instead of helpful?

## Printer/vendor questions

Ask these for the top 5 vendors/printers/decorators:

1. What do they produce or decorate?
2. How do we send them orders?
3. Do they require POs?
4. Who owns the relationship?
5. How do they confirm receipt?
6. How do they confirm pricing?
7. How do they confirm production/ship dates?
8. How do they send invoices?
9. What usually goes wrong with them?
10. Are they safe to systematize, or should Kenny handle them personally?

## Email questions

1. Which inboxes receive customer orders?
2. Which inboxes receive proofs/approvals?
3. Which inboxes receive vendor confirmations?
4. Does Kenny still use AOL for live business?
5. Can Maclaine see Kenny's order threads?
6. Is there a shared inbox?
7. Are labels/folders used consistently?
8. What email search do you do constantly?
9. What attachments get lost?
10. Is there a naming convention in subject lines?

## Proof/artwork questions

1. Where do original art files live?
2. Where do proof files live?
3. Who creates proofs?
4. What is the naming convention?
5. How do we know which proof is approved?
6. How do we make sure the printer gets the approved version?
7. What happens when the customer changes artwork after approval?

## Shipping questions

1. Do most orders ship to CA or direct to customer?
2. Who receives tracking?
3. Where is tracking saved?
4. Who tells the customer?
5. What happens if a shipment is short or late?
6. Does delivery confirmation trigger invoicing?

## Revenue protection questions

1. How do we know when a customer should reorder?
2. Is reorder timing seasonal, event-based, or random?
3. Can we quickly pull "same as last year" order details?
4. Who follows up on reorders?
5. Which accounts should get proactive calls?
6. Which accounts have gone quiet and worry you?

## Evidence to collect

For each answer, try to capture at least one artifact:

- screenshot
- CSV export
- email thread
- spreadsheet link
- vendor portal screenshot
- sample PO
- sample invoice
- sample proof
- tracking email

If there is no artifact and the answer is "someone knows," mark it as a founder/team-memory dependency.
