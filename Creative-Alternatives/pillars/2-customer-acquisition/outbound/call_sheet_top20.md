## SCRIPT A — one-year lapse (Tier 2, Tier 3)
Warm, short, one question, then stop talking.

> "Hi [name], this is Ryan with Creative Alternatives — we're Kenny's shop, we did your
> [WHAT THEY BOUGHT] last year.
>
> I'm calling because it's about the time of year you'd be thinking about [fall staff gear
> / back-to-school spirit wear / the fall season], and I wanted to catch you before you
> got busy rather than after.
>
> Are you working on anything for the fall yet?"

**Then be quiet.** Let them answer.

If yes → "Great. Want me to put your logo on a few options and send it over? Costs you
nothing and you'll have it today." (This is the mockup wedge. It is the whole engine.)

If no / not yet → "No problem. Want me to send a couple of ideas anyway so it's sitting
there when it comes up?" Almost nobody says no to that.

If price came up last time → do not defend it on the phone. "Let me have Kenny look at
that directly." Hand off.

## SCRIPT B — the habit breakers (Tier 1)
Different call. You are not selling, you are finding out what happened. Genuine curiosity
outperforms a pitch here, and if something went wrong you need to know it anyway.

> "Hi [name], Ryan with Creative Alternatives, Kenny's shop. You worked with us in 2024
> and again in 2025 and then we didn't hear from you this year, and honestly I wanted to
> call and find out whether that was just how the year went or whether we dropped the
> ball on something.
>
> Which was it?"

If we dropped the ball → **let them talk, do not defend, write it down verbatim.** Then:
"That's on us. Let me get Kenny on this." A recovered relationship is worth more than
the order, and Kenny needs to hear it.

If just how the year went → "Makes sense. What are you working on for the fall?" and you
are back in Script A.

## RULES
- **Business hours only.** This list has been dead for a year because nobody could call
  it during the workday. That constraint is gone. Use it.
- **Log every call the day you make it** in the pipeline tracker. At 15% of originated,
  an unlogged call is unpaid work.
- **Any real interest gets a mockup the same day** — that is the SOP and it is what
  converts (see the DRRT and TONE lookbooks for the standard).
- **Do not batch these into email.** Email is why this list has been quiet. The whole
  point is that you can now do the thing email cannot.
- 10 calls a day clears the top 20 in two days and the top 40 in a week.
