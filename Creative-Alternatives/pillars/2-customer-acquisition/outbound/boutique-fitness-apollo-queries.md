# Creative Alternatives — Boutique Fitness Apollo Search Queries

> **Created:** 2026-05-05
> **Purpose:** Mirror the Summer Camps campaign (10.1% reply, 2 clients landed) for the boutique fitness vertical.
> **Strategy:** Independent, owner-operated gyms only. Skip franchise chains where corporate brand control kills the offer.

---

## Why Boutique Fitness Mirrors Summer Camps

| Summer Camps Win Factor | Boutique Fitness Equivalent |
|-------------------------|------------------------------|
| Captive identity-driven audience (kids, parents) | Members buy box/studio-branded apparel as belonging |
| Single decision-maker (Camp Director) | Owner-operator (Box Owner, Studio Owner, Head Coach) |
| Zero ecom infrastructure | None of these have a real merch store |
| Seasonal merch demand spikes | Open season, anniversary WODs, retreats, recital season |
| Operator is busy, non-technical | Coaches/instructors, not store-builders |

---

## Universal Apollo Filters (apply to all queries below)

- **Industry:** `Health, Wellness & Fitness` + `Sports`
- **Employee count:** `1-25` (critical — strips out franchisor HQs and corporate chains)
- **Country:** United States
- **Titles:** Owner, Founder, Co-Founder, CEO, President, Head Coach, Studio Owner, Box Owner, Studio Manager, General Manager
- **Excluded titles:** Intern, Coach Assistant, Front Desk
- **Has email:** Yes (verified preferred)

## Phase 1 — Highest-Fit Niches (launch first)

### 1. CrossFit Boxes

**Apollo Keywords (any of):**
```
CrossFit, CrossFit affiliate, CrossFit gym, CrossFit box, functional fitness gym
```

**Domain heuristics:** `crossfit*.com`, `*crossfit.com`

**Estimated Apollo TAM:** 3,000-4,000 contactable owners (~5,000 affiliates total in US)

**Excluded keywords:** `Orangetheory`, `F45`, `Anytime Fitness`, `Planet Fitness`

**Why it's #1:** Affiliates own their brand and merch — CrossFit HQ does not control apparel. Members already buy box-branded shirts/hoodies as identity. Affiliate fee pressure ($3k+/yr to HQ) means owners are actively hunting incremental revenue.

---

### 2. BJJ / Jiu-Jitsu Academies

**Apollo Keywords (any of):**
```
Brazilian jiu-jitsu, BJJ, jiu jitsu academy, jiu-jitsu, gracie, MMA gym, mixed martial arts academy
```

**Domain heuristics:** `*bjj*.com`, `*jiujitsu*.com`, `*gracie*.com`, `*mma*.com`

**Estimated Apollo TAM:** 7,000-10,000 contactable owners (~10-15k academies total in US)

**Excluded keywords:** `karate kids`, `taekwondo for kids` (different buyer profile, kid-focused MA tends to be franchised)

**Why it's a strong fit:** Gi, rashguard, patch, and academy-shirt culture is more obsessive than CrossFit. Almost universally independent. Owner is the head instructor — single decision-maker.

## Phase 2 — Strong Alternates (expand after Phase 1 hits 5%+ reply)

```
yoga studio, hot yoga, vinyasa, ashtanga, kundalini, yoga teacher training
```

### 4. Climbing Gyms

```
climbing gym, bouldering gym, indoor climbing, rock climbing gym
```

## Skip List — Do NOT Target

These are franchise chains where corporate brand control prevents franchisees from running their own merch:

- Orangetheory Fitness
- F45 Training
- Pure Barre
- Club Pilates
- CycleBar / SoulCycle
- Anytime Fitness / Planet Fitness / 24 Hour Fitness
- Title Boxing Club
- 9Round / iLoveKickboxing
- YogaSix / CorePower Yoga / YogaWorks

If a franchisee surfaces in Apollo, exclude them at the keyword/domain level.

## How to Run the Apollo Pulls

1. Open Apollo → Search → People
2. Apply universal filters (industry, employee count, US, has email, titles)
3. Add niche-specific keyword filter (any of the keywords listed above)
4. Add exclusion keywords for the niche
5. Sort by `Email Verified Status: Verified` first
6. Export in batches of 1,000-2,000 to CSV
7. Drop into the lead engine campaign YAML for ICP verification + email writing

For automated pulls via Apollo MCP, see `module-installs/lead-engine/modules/signal_apollo.py` — use the same filter pattern.
