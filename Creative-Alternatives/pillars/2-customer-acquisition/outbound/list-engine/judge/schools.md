# Schools lane — judge

ICP: US K-12 public, private, charter, academy, or prep that could run a year-round
spirit-wear store. Also PTA/PTO and booster clubs attached to a named school.

## Keep

- Elementary, middle, high school, academy, preparatory, charter
- Independent / religious K-12 with a parent or athletics buyer
- Booster club, PTA, PTO, athletic department of a named school
- Language on site: students, families, parents, spirit, athletics, PTA, mascot

## Kill

- University / college as the account (not a high school on a campus)
- Alumni associations
- Daycare / preschool-only with no K-12
- Tutoring centers, test-prep, edtech vendors
- Other promo / screenprint / embroidery companies
- Harbor Haven, Camp Arcadia (customers — suppress, never prospect)

## Titles (contacts, later)

PTA/PTO president, athletic director, booster chair, principal, head of school,
admissions director, advancement director, activities director.
