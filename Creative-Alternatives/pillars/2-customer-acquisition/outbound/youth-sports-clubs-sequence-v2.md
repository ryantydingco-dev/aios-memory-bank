## Tone Notes

- **Keep it casual and friendly.** Club owners and directors, not corporate executives.
- **Email 1 is ONLY a question.** No explaining the offer. Let curiosity earn the reply.
- **The offer sells itself.** Free store, no inventory, earn on every order. Don't over-explain.
- **Farm & Forge as social proof.** Email 2 is where you show this is real, not theoretical.
- **Fundraising language matters.** "Revenue stream" and "fundraiser" resonate more than "promotional products."
- **Seasonal urgency.** Email 4 ties to natural sports season rhythms.
