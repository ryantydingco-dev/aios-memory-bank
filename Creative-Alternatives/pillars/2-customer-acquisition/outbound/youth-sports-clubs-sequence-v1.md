## Tone Notes

- **Keep it casual and friendly.** These are club owners and directors, not corporate executives. They respond to warmth, not polish.
- **The summer camps campaign worked because it was short, direct, and zero-pressure.** Don't overcomplicate it.
- **The offer sells itself.** Free store, no inventory, earn on every order. Don't over-explain — just make sure they understand there's no risk.
- **Farm & Forge as social proof.** Reference it in Email 2 to show this isn't theoretical — you just did this for a real club.
- **Fundraising language matters.** Youth sports clubs are always looking for revenue. Framing merch as a fundraiser is more compelling than framing it as "promotional products."
- **Seasonal urgency.** Email 4 ties to the natural rhythm of sports seasons — this creates real timing pressure without being salesy.
