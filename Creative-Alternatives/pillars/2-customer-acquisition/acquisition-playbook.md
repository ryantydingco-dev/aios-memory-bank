# Customer Acquisition Playbook — The Only Job

> One focus: acquire customers. Every play here is an assignment with a cadence, not an idea.
> Daily targets live in [plans/daily-power-list.md](../../plans/daily-power-list.md). Sprint context: [plans/60-day-revenue-sprint.md](../../plans/60-day-revenue-sprint.md).

## The machine (three motions, one list)
Every prospect gets hit on up to 3 channels off the SAME list: SmartLead email → Salesfinity call → manual LinkedIn touch (Claude-drafted). Multi-touch is the system; single-channel is the fallback.

---

## DAILY (the relentless five + the mockup spear)

| Play | Concrete assignment | Cadence |
|------|--------------------|---------|
| **Dials** | 100+ on Salesfinity: repliers → openers → cold. Ask = free mockup. | Daily 10:30–12:30, 3:00–6:00 |
| **Unsolicited mockup drop** | Pick 10 dream targets, generate mockup w/ THEIR logo, send via email + LinkedIn: "Made this for you — want the real thing before the holiday rush?" | 10/day, inside maker block |
| **Reply SLA** | Every reply answered <1 hr; every "not now" gets a follow-up date. | All day |
| **Same-day mockup/quote** | Every warm signal → mockup or quote before 6 PM. | Daily 1:00–3:00 |
| **Follow-up sweep** | All open quotes + scheduled circle-backs. Orders close on touch 3–5. | Daily 1:00–3:00 |
| **LinkedIn touches** | 10–15 manual sends, Claude-drafted, same list as dials. | Daily, 20 min |

## WEEKLY RHYTHMS

| Play | Concrete assignment | Cadence |
|------|--------------------|---------|
| **Lumpy mail** | 5–10 packages: near-cost sample (Kenny's vendors) + printed mockup card w/ their logo. Track sends; call 3 days after delivery. | Batch Mondays, ship same day |

## RULES OF THE ATTACK
1. Same list, three channels. A prospect who got the email gets the call gets the LinkedIn touch.
2. The mockup is always the ask. Never "hop on a call."
3. Volume compounds only if follow-up is 100%. New leads never outrank warm ones.
4. Track everything in the daily log; Friday decides what lives and dies.
5. Partial days count. Do the five in miniature (20 dials, 3 mockups) rather than zero.
