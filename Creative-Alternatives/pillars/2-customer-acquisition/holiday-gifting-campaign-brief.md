# Campaign 1: Corporate Holiday Gifting — Brief

> Sprint campaign C1 (see [60-day sprint](../../plans/60-day-revenue-sprint.md)). Created day 1, 2026-08-05.
> Window: budgets open Sept, orders placed Sept–Nov for Nov–Dec delivery. Outreach starts NOW.

## Offer
Year-end employee gifts / client appreciation swag, handled end-to-end: curated kit or branded gear,
free mockup with THEIR logo up front, CA handles sourcing → decoration → kitting → delivery before the holidays.
Hook: "27 years doing this; here's a free mockup of what your team's gift could look like."

## ICP
- **Who:** HR / People Ops / Office Manager / EA / Marketing at 25–500-employee companies; owners at <25.
  Titles: Head of People, HR Manager, Office Manager, Executive Assistant, Marketing Manager, Founder/Owner.
- **Where:** NY/NJ/CT core (CA's home turf + "local vendor" trust), expandable nationally.
- **Sweet-spot verticals:** law firms (existing CA motion), financial services, healthcare/medical offices,
  construction/trades HQs, agencies, nonprofits (donor + staff gifts).
- **Signals that upgrade a lead:** actively hiring (growing team = bigger order), new office, recent award/anniversary,
  new People leader (wants an early win). Enrich via /ca-signals.
- **Exclusions:** <10 employees w/o owner contact, gift-card-culture tech remote-first cos, existing CA customers
  (those go to the cross-sell track, NOT cold email).

## Angle / message spine
1. Timing truth: teams that order gifts in Sept get first pick + on-time delivery; Nov scramblers get leftovers.
2. Proof: 27 years, $2.5M+/yr, name-drop adjacent verticals (camps→schools→offices story).
3. CTA: "Want a free mockup with your logo? Reply with a logo or just say 'sure'." (mockup-tease, per ca-outbound style)
4. Sender: Ryan (professional-consultative voice per existing campaign conventions).

## Build steps (MacBook)
1. /ca-outbound with this ICP → AI Ark pull → site crawl → ca-personalization-subagent (situation_line + cta_line = mockup tease).
2. /list-quality-scorecard gate before upload. /spam-word-checker on copy.
3. DRAFT campaign in SmartLead (house rule: never auto-send). Fleet = CA + Dealthreads domains.
4. Same list → Salesfinity (call day 2+) and LinkedIn manual-touch queue (Claude drafts).

## Success metrics
Reply % (target ≥5% w/ mockup tease), positive replies, mockups sent, quotes, orders. Read weekly, kill/scale per /experiment-design.
