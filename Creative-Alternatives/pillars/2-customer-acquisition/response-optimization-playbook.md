## The response arsenal (beyond the mockup)

**Channel layering (biggest untapped — the tools already exist):**
- **Opened-twice-no-reply → LinkedIn touch** via Sendr: view + connect w/ note ("sent you something about {{company}} retreat gear — no rush"). Feed the list weekly from SmartLead.
- **Opened-twice-no-reply → call block**: dialer during the 10:30 outbound block; script = "I made your firm a couple of mockups, want me to email them?" (The pain corpus: proactive humans are the least-claimed differentiator in the industry.)
- Cadence: email D0 → email D3 → LinkedIn D5 → call D7-10 → breakup email.

**Reply-friction reducers (free, in-copy):**
- Multiple-choice CTA test: "Worth a look? (a) send them (b) not our thing" — lowers the reply bar to one keystroke.
- Referral redirect in breakups (now live in Retreat step 3): "if this isn't your desk, who should I ask?" — turns dead ends into forwards.

**Warm re-touches (highest hit-rate lists in the house):**
- **The 76+ historical repliers** (camps + squash replier CSVs in data/): humans who already replied once. Camps re-touch in December ("stores for summer 2026 open now"); squash at season start. Near-free replies.
- The 88 all-time replies across campaigns: quarterly friendly check-in from Maclaine, never salesy.

**Signal-based openers ({{signal}} field, upgraded):**
- Award congrats (financial firms publicize awards — Invst had 4 in their signature), new office, new partner class, event announcements. A signal-mined opener doubles as proof we actually looked.

**The physical escalation (dream-25 only):**
- For the top 25 accounts by value: print ONE real sample of their logo item and mail it with a handwritten note. The mockup magnet made physical. Expensive per unit, absurd reply rates on lumpy mail, and it's content (film the making).

**Social proof compounding:**
- The review vacuum play: 20 Google/Trustpilot reviews from Kenny's happy customers → "the most-reviewed promo shop you've heard of" line in every sequence. (Reactivation side quest.)

## Standing rules

- One niche live at a time; next in queue flips only when the active one dries (~200 not-started threshold).
- Never change copy mid-test-window; judge at 250+ sends/variant.
- Every reply gets the mockup motion inside the hour, business hours. That's the brand.
- Weekly: numbers → Sunday review; one lever change per week maximum.
