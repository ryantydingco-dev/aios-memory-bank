# Swag Handled — Salesfinity cold-call script, Creative Alternatives

> The phone leg of the corporate machine. Calls land **Day 6 and Day 16** of the cadence — AFTER the email + LinkedIn touches, so the prospect has (ideally) already seen the mockup. Voice = **Maclaine** (warm, founder's-daughter, not a telemarketer). Goal of the call is NOT to sell — it's to confirm the mockup landed and book 15 minutes. Keep it short, human, and curious.

## Before you dial
- Pull up their **mockup + the {{signal_line}}** (the research finding) so the open is specific.
- Tokens: `{{first_name}}` · `{{company}}` · `{{signal_line}}` · `{{swag_angle}}` · `{{calendar_link}}`.
- One goal per call: **a booked 15-minute slot**, or permission to send the mockup to the right person.

---

## Call 1 — Day 6 (warm, references the mockup)

**Open (pattern interrupt + permission):**
> "Hi, is this {{first_name}}? Hey {{first_name}}, it's Maclaine over at Creative Alternatives — did I catch you at an okay time for thirty seconds?"

*(If "who?" / "what's this about?")* → "Totally fair — I actually emailed you earlier this week. {{signal_line}} I sent over a quick mockup of what a branded welcome kit for {{company}} could look like."

**The reason (one breath, no pitch-dump):**
> "The short version — we handle branded swag for companies start to finish. Onboarding kits, event gear, client gifts — designed, warehoused, and shipped on demand, so it's never a last-minute scramble for whoever's closest to it. We've done it 27 years, everyone from local firms to teams at Thermo Fisher and Trinity Health."

**The qualifying question (then stop talking):**
> "Quick question — is branded swag something that lands on your plate over there, or is it someone else's headache?"

### Branches
- **"Yeah, that's me / we deal with that"** →
  > "Perfect — that's exactly why I called. Did the {{company}} mockup come through okay? … Let me grab 15 minutes to walk you through how it'd actually run for you. What's better, later this week or early next?" → book → send {{calendar_link}}.
- **"We already have a vendor"** →
  > "Most folks we work with did too — usually two or three they're juggling. We just make it one, faster, and you stop managing it. Worth seeing the mockup either way? I can have it in your inbox in two minutes." → if yes, confirm email, soft-book.
- **"What's it cost?"** →
  > "Design, the mockups, and the account management are free — you only pay for the products, at competitive pricing. The real savings is the time and the headaches you stop paying in. Happy to show you exactly on a quick call."
- **"Not right now / no budget"** →
  > "No problem at all. When's your next new-hire wave or event? That's when this gets painful — I'll have the mockup ready for then. Mind if I keep it on file for {{company}}?"
- **"Just email me"** →
  > "Will do — I'll send the {{company}} mockup and a couple times to grab 15 minutes. If it's not a fit, just tell me to close the file. Thanks {{first_name}}."
- **Gatekeeper / wrong person** →
  > "No worries — who'd be the best person for branded gear and onboarding swag over there? Happy to send them the {{company}} mockup so it's not on your plate." → get name/email.

**Voicemail (Call 1):**
> "Hi {{first_name}}, it's Maclaine Scher at Creative Alternatives. {{signal_line}} I sent over a mockup of a branded onboarding kit for {{company}} and wanted to put a voice to it — we handle branded gear for companies start to finish so it's never a scramble. If it's worth a look I'm at [callback#], or just reply to my email and I'll send the mockup right over. Thanks {{first_name}}."

## Call 2 — Day 16 (final + value-add, then close the loop)

**Open:**
> "Hey {{first_name}}, Maclaine at Creative Alternatives again — I'll keep this to twenty seconds. I sent over that {{company}} welcome-kit mockup a couple weeks back and wanted to close the loop before I file it away."

**The ask:**
> "Is branded swag — onboarding, events, client gifts — something worth taking off your plate this year, or should I check back when your next wave comes up?"

### Branches
- **Interested** → book 15 minutes, send {{calendar_link}}.
- **Maybe later** → "Got it — I'll set a reminder for [their timeframe] and keep the {{company}} mockup on file. If it gets painful before then, just reply to my email."
- **Not a fit** → "No problem, appreciate you telling me straight. I'll close the file — and if branded gear ever becomes a headache, you know where we are."

**Voicemail (Call 2 — breakup):**
> "Hi {{first_name}}, Maclaine at Creative Alternatives — last one from me. That {{company}} mockup is on file whenever branded gear becomes a headache: onboarding, an event, client gifts. Just reply to my email and it's yours. Either way, thanks for the time and good luck with everything at {{company}}."

## Rules
- **Any live answer that's even slightly warm → stop the automation, route to a real conversation with Maclaine same day.** (HubSpot: Engaged.)
- **Don't read the script.** It's a map, not a teleprompter — match their energy, keep it conversational.
- **One ask per call.** Booked 15 minutes, or the name of the right person. Nothing else.
- **Reference the real signal** ({{signal_line}}) in the first 10 seconds — that's what makes it land as a 1-to-1, not a dial.
- **Mobiles** come from the AI Ark reveal (top-of-queue Tier-1 first); calls are a smaller motion than email, so dial the strongest signals.
