# Event Kit Partner — cold copy for event planners (email · LinkedIn)

> The planner channel: one planner runs 5–20 events a year and EVERY one needs what CA sells.
> This is a **relationship/channel play, not a one-off sale** — win the planner, win their whole calendar.
> ICP config: `config/ca_outbound.yaml → icps.event_planners` (AI Ark verified 2026-07-10: ~50k US planners
> across core titles; 753 in SC alone). Maclaine's voice. Built on what the SmartLead data proved wins:
> named-peer proof, specific, confident tone, full credible signature. **No discount gimmicks — ever.**

## The offer (what we're actually selling them)

**"Your invisible swag department."** Planners' documented pains with promo (BizBash/industry research):
vendor sprawl (hundreds of suppliers, wild MOQs, quote-only pricing), quality roulette, deadline terror,
and kitting/logistics nobody wants to own. CA solves all four, and adds a weapon nobody else offers:

1. **Free client-logo mockups for THEIR pitches.** A planner pitching a client can say "and here's your
   branded event kit" with a photoreal mockup CA made — before any order exists. CA becomes the planner's
   sales tool, not a vendor. This is the wedge; lead with it.
2. **One contact, deadline-guaranteed.** 24–48h proofs, ~2wk production, 27 years of hitting event dates.
3. **Kitting + venue drop-ship.** Attendee kits, speaker gifts, staff apparel — warehoused, kitted,
   shipped to the venue. The part planners hate most, gone.
4. **White-label + margin (agencies).** CA works invisibly under the agency's brand; the planner marks up
   or takes the rev-share (the existing 10–12% store offer, pointed at their recurring org clients).
   Margin on every event, zero inventory risk.

**Approved facts only:** since 1999 (27 years) · 2,700+ organizations · 75,000+ orders · 24–48h proofs ·
~2wk production · teams at Thermo Fisher and Trinity Health · "we print anything on everything."
Never mention AI. Never fabricate a client, stat, or price.

## Deliverability rule (house standard)
**Email teases the mockup; LinkedIn + the reply SHOW it.** Email #1 is plain text, no images, no links.

## Two sub-segments, same offer, different first line
- **`in-house`** — corporate / association / nonprofit planner (they ARE the buyer): lead with "your
  events' swag, handled."
- **`agency`** — event agency / independent producer (channel partner): lead with the free-pitch-mockup
  weapon and margin.

## LINKEDIN lane (runs parallel — where the mockup SHOWS)
- **Connect note (no pitch):** "Hi {{first_name}}, fellow events-world person here (we're the branded-gear side). {{signal_line}} Would be glad to connect." *(signal_line is a standalone sentence per the rules above; if empty it drops cleanly. The old "Loved {{signal_line}}" rendered broken grammar on every send.)*
- **After accept, DM:** the agency/in-house Email-1 pitch compressed to 2 sentences + attach the mockup
  image directly (LinkedIn is the show-don't-tell channel).
- Target the same list; planners live on LinkedIn more than almost any B2B persona.

## Association / chapter play (the warm layer — run as /ca-openings-style motion, not cold email)
- **MPI (~17k engaged, ~90 chapters) · PCMA (~8.4k) · ILEA (~2k, 51 chapters).** Local chapters
  (MPI Carolinas, ILEA etc.) hold monthly events that THEMSELVES need swag — sponsor or supply one
  chapter event and you're in the room with 50 planners at once. Membership directories = warm lists.
- This overlaps `ca_sponsors.py` — a chapter sponsorship is the single highest-leverage $500 CA can spend
  in this channel.

## Targeting rules
- **Exclude** wedding/bridal/party/social planners (favors ≠ branded merch) — filter is in the ICP config.
- **Prefer** agencies 2–50 staff, in-house planners at 25–1000-staff orgs; skip enterprise (agency-locked).
- **Prioritize** planners whose profile shows corporate/conference/nonprofit-gala work, CMP certification,
  or an in-house event team (the AI Ark sample's first SC hit ran a $5M event budget — that's the profile).
- Personalization (arm A) = their real event niche from site/LinkedIn. Rules per `/ca-personalization-subagent`;
  A/B per `/experiment-design` (min 300/arm before judging).
