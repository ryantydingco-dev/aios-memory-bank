# Swag Handled — cold copy (email · LinkedIn · phone)

> Ready-to-load copy for the corporate "Swag Handled" machine (`../swag-handled-build-plan.md`). Maclaine's voice. Built on what the SmartLead data proved wins: **named-peer proof, specific, confident tone, full credible signature** — and what loses: generic "free X" subjects, hedgy "just curious," bare sig. Launch: Mon (first cohort = firms **hiring now**, NY-metro, 25–500).

## Deliverability rule (important)
**Email teases the mockup; LinkedIn + the personalized page SHOW it.** No embedded images or links in email #1 — keep it plain text so it lands. The visual wedge does its job on LinkedIn (video DM + page) and on the reply.

## SALESFINITY — phone

### Call opener — Day 6 (warm, references the mockup)
"Hi, is this {{first_name}}? Hey {{first_name}}, it's Maclaine over at Creative Alternatives. Did I catch you for thirty seconds?

I emailed you earlier this week about a welcome-kit mockup I put together for {{company}}. The short version: we handle branded gear for companies (onboarding kits, event stuff, client gifts) so it's not a scramble for whoever's closest to it. I mocked up what {{company}}'s could look like.

Quick question: is branded swag something that lands on your plate over there?"

→ *If yes / curious:* "Perfect. Let me send the mockup and grab 15 minutes to show you how it'd run. What's a good time this week?"
→ *If "we have a vendor":* "Most folks do, usually two or three. We make it one and you stop managing it. Worth seeing the mockup either way?"

### Voicemail
"Hi {{first_name}}, it's Maclaine Scher at Creative Alternatives. I emailed you about an onboarding-kit mockup I put together for {{company}} and wanted to put a voice to it. We handle branded gear for companies start to finish so it's never a last-minute scramble. If it's worth a look I'm at {{phone}}, or reply to my email and I'll send the mockup right over. Thanks {{first_name}}."

### Objection quick-hits (phone)
- **"We already have a vendor."** → "Most do, and they're juggling a couple. We make it one, faster, and you stop managing it."
- **"We don't order much."** → "That's exactly who this is for. There's no swag person, so it falls through the cracks. We become that, with no overhead on you."
- **"What's it cost?"** → "Design and the mockups and account management are free. You pay for the products at competitive pricing and stop paying in time and headaches."
- **"Not right now."** → "No problem. When's your next new-hire wave or event? I'll have the mockup ready for then."
