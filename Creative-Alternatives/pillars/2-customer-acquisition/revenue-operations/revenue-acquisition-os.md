# Revenue Acquisition OS

> Purpose: make the next revenue action obvious every working day and connect daily behavior to the $1M goal.
> Window: September 1, 2026 through February 28, 2027.
> Governing plan: `../../../plans/1m-6-month-game-plan.md`.

## The operating rule

Revenue is judged weekly because orders are lumpy. Controllable behavior is judged daily.

Every working day must do four things in order:

1. Protect existing demand: clear replies and prevent interested buyers from aging.
2. Advance open money: follow up on quotes, mockups, proofs, and store opportunities.
3. Create new demand: work warm accounts first, then the three approved cold channels.
4. Prepare tomorrow: every open opportunity has one owner and one dated next action.

If a higher-priority block is red, lower-priority building, content, and internal projects stop until it is recovered.

### Funnel diagnostics

Track these by channel, not blended:

- **Calls:** dials → live connects → qualified next steps → mockup/quote opportunities → wins.
- **LinkedIn:** DMs → replies → qualified conversations → mockup/store yeses → quotes → wins.
- **SmartLead:** delivered emails → positive replies → qualified conversations → quotes → wins.
- **Warm:** first-touches → responses/conversations → quotes → wins.

Formulas:

- Connect rate = connects / dials.
- Qualified-next-step rate = qualified next steps / live connects.
- Positive reply rate = positive replies / delivered messages.
- Conversation rate = qualified conversations / first-touches.
- Quote rate = quotes sent / qualified conversations.
- Win rate = wins / quotes sent.
- Average order value = attributable revenue / wins.
- Revenue per qualified conversation = attributable revenue / qualified conversations.
- Revenue per selling hour = attributable revenue / human selling hours.

Do not use opens, impressions, raw sends, likes, content volume, or AI outputs as success KPIs.

## Daily runbook

### 0. Prepare the queue before the day starts

Codex maintains the system, scores data, and prepares reusable tools. Grokbot may research and draft. Neither sends customer-facing messages.

Tomorrow's queue must already contain:

- 15 warm accounts with a real reason to contact them.
- 25 open follow-ups with a dated next action.
- The highest-value mockup, store, proof, or quote-stage opportunity.
- At least 400 Salesfinity-ready records due for active calling, drawn from a 1,000-record reservoir and validated for direct/mobile phone, buyer role, timezone, DNC status, and a sourced reason-to-call.
- 25 LinkedIn revenue touches, with no more than 15 new connection requests.

If the queue is missing, the first task is building it. Do not browse randomly for prospects during selling time.

### 1. Clear replies

- Triage SmartLead and active inbox replies.
- Answer interested replies first.
- Route quantities and pricing requests to Maclaine the same day.
- Suppress negatives and unsubscribes.

Exit condition: zero untriaged replies and no interested reply older than 24 hours.

### 2. Advance open money

- Complete 25 useful follow-ups.
- Work highest expected value first.
- Every follow-up adds a mockup, product idea, verified deadline, answer, proof, or decision request.
- Never send "just checking in."

Exit condition: 25 complete or every legitimate open follow-up has been handled.

### 3. Work warm demand

- Complete 15 first-touches to reorder, dormant, gifting, camp, or store accounts.
- Calls count as touches but are recorded separately.
- Reference verified order history or a real buying moment.

Exit condition: 15 complete and every result has a disposition and next action.

### 4. Deliver one conversion asset

Deliver the highest-value item the pipeline needs: a mockup, store preview, proof package, product shortlist, or complete quote handoff to Maclaine.

Exit condition: the buyer or Maclaine has what is needed to take the next step.

### 5. Create cold demand

Only three cold channels are allowed:

1. Two Salesfinity blocks totaling 3 hours using the script and objection system in `../outbound/cold-call-playbook.md`. The day is green at 300 dials or 25 live conversations, whichever happens first, provided at least 3 qualified next steps are created.
2. One human LinkedIn block targeting 25 buyer touches using Grokbot-prepared research/drafts. No more than 15 may be new connection requests.
3. SmartLead cold email, monitored for replies, bounce health, and quote-stage conversations.

The Salesfinity target ramps: days 1–3 use 2–3 parallel lines and a 200-dial floor; days 4–10 target 300 dials or 25 live conversations. Do not move to 4–5 lines until dropped-call behavior, call quality, caller-ID health, and follow-up capacity have been reviewed. SmartLead is judged by positive replies, qualified conversations, quotes, and revenue, not send volume alone.

## The 10 AM–5 PM selling day

Lists and drafts are prepared before 10 AM by Grokbot/Codex workflows. Ryan's selling window is not used for list building.

| Time | Block | Output |
|---|---|---|
| 10:00–10:20 | Reply control | Interested replies answered; queue triaged |
| 10:20–11:50 | Salesfinity block 1 | 150+ dials or strong live-conversation volume |
| 11:50–1:10 | Warm + follow-up block 1 | 15 warm touches and first 10 follow-ups |
| 1:10–1:40 | Break; agents prepare second half | No prospecting research by Ryan |
| 1:40–2:40 | LinkedIn revenue block | 25 buyer touches; max 15 new requests |
| 2:40–4:10 | Salesfinity block 2 | Reach 300 dials or 25 live conversations |
| 4:10–4:45 | Follow-up + conversion move | Reach 25 follow-ups; deliver/route one asset |
| 4:45–5:00 | Closeout | Scorecard and tomorrow's queue confirmed |

Live positive replies interrupt any block. A buyer response is more valuable than another outbound touch.

### 6. Close the day

- Log activity and outcomes in `templates/daily-revenue-scorecard.csv`.
- Update each open opportunity's next action and due date.
- Mark every driver green, yellow, or red.
- Build tomorrow's queue.

The day is not closed while an interested reply is aging or an open opportunity lacks a next action.

## Automatic recovery rules

- **Triage red:** all new prospecting pauses until replies are clear.
- **Warm touches red:** work the highest-value warm accounts until 15 are complete.
- **Follow-ups red:** work the quote/mockup/store list until it is exhausted.
- **Fulfillment red:** choose the highest-value promised deliverable and finish it before content.
- **Salesfinity red:** finish the second block or document the concrete constraint. If dials are high but connects are weak, fix data/caller health instead of increasing volume.
- **LinkedIn red:** complete buyer follow-ups first; never exceed 15 new connection requests to rescue the number.
- **Scoreboard red:** the next day cannot begin until yesterday is closed.
- **Two revenue-pace red weeks:** pause the lowest conversation-yield cold lane, diagnose quote-stage leakage, and move time to warm reactivation, gifting, camps, or stores.
- **Three red days in one week:** Friday review must identify and fix one structural constraint. Do not respond by adding more goals.

## Friday revenue review

Review in this order:

1. QuickBooks-reconciled attributable revenue versus the monthly cumulative target.
2. Quotes and quote dollars created, advanced, won, and lost.
3. Qualified conversations and conversion rates by warm, call, LinkedIn, and SmartLead.
4. SLA misses: aged replies, slipped proofs, and opportunities without next actions.
5. Time allocation: selling hours versus internal/build/content hours.
6. One decision only for the next week: keep, change, or kill one channel, segment, offer, or step.

Never change multiple funnel variables in the same week. Preserve what worked long enough to learn from it.

## Source and measurement contract

- QuickBooks controls invoiced revenue and customer/order truth.
- The local revenue tracker controls opportunity stage, owner, next action, attribution, and quote status.
- SmartLead controls email sends, delivery, replies, and bounce evidence.
- The call and LinkedIn logs control their respective human activities and outcomes.
- A close is counted once. Multi-touch detail stays in notes; one primary attribution is used for reporting.
- The July metrics snapshot is stale for live decision-making. Refresh source data before treating any current total as live.
