# New-customer offer and outreach system

> Draft only. This system does not authorize list building with personal data, cold sends,
> calls, LinkedIn actions, account changes, pricing, guarantees, or public claims.

## Positioning

**Creative Alternatives helps organizations turn important people-and-event moments into
useful branded merchandise that arrives ready — without making one employee manage a
catalog, three vendors, art files, packaging, and shipping.**

The merchandise direction favors retail-quality, useful everyday items, sustainable
choices where substantiated, personalization/packaging where it improves the recipient
experience, and drinkware when it fits the context. The lead is always the buyer outcome.

This is an expansion of CA's core promo-distribution offer. For camps, clubs, schools, and
member organizations, the existing branded Store Engine remains a distinct option.

## Offer packages

Pricing, minimums, margins, rush policy, inventory, storage, shipping, and guarantees are
all `[CONFIRM with Kenny]`.

### 1. Onboarding Ready

**Outcome:** A consistent first-day experience without HR packing and shipping boxes.

**Core:** planning brief; 2–3 curated product directions; branded proof; size/address
workflow; personalization/insert option; kitting/fulfillment plan; reorder memory.

**Likely products after discovery:** retail-quality tee or layer, durable drinkware,
notebook or practical desk item, useful bag, welcome card/packaging.

### 2. Event Ready

**Outcome:** Staff and attendees get the right branded items, delivered against the event
plan, without the marketer chasing vendors.

**Core:** date-backward plan; staff vs attendee needs; 2–3 item set; proof/approval path;
packaging and ship-to-event/office plan; post-event leftovers/reorder review.

### 3. Recognition Moments

**Outcome:** Employee, volunteer, or milestone recognition feels personal rather than
generic.

**Core:** recipient tiers; useful gift direction; names or message personalization;
considered packaging; address/privacy handling plan; repeatable recognition calendar.

### 4. Client Gifting, Considered

**Outcome:** A brand-appropriate gift people will use, presented with care and delivered
without an internal fulfillment project.

**Core:** recipient and policy discovery; 2–3 curated directions; note/packaging; address
collection method; approval sample; shipment tracking plan.

### 5. Managed Merchandise Program

**Outcome:** One governed program replaces ad-hoc requests and scattered vendors.

**Core:** annual moment map; approved product architecture; brand/spec memory; intake and
approval rules; bulk/store decision; inventory/reorder review; quarterly performance
conversation.

### Optional: Recruiting Kit / Exhibit Ready

These are Event Ready variants with buyer-specific planning:

- **Recruiting:** candidate audience, transportability, scan/follow-up bridge, and
  continuity into onboarding.
- **Exhibit:** booth/staff counts, ship destination, install window, useful attendee item,
  and post-show follow-up package.

## Discovery before products

Ask in this order:

1. What moment is this for, and what date cannot move?
2. Who receives it, where are they, and what should they feel/do?
3. Quantity, sizes, destinations, and approval stakeholders?
4. What worked or went unused last time?
5. Budget range or decision constraints?
6. Sustainability, packaging, personalization, or gift-policy requirements?
7. Bulk shipment, individual fulfillment, or managed store?

Only then curate a maximum of three directions: **safe**, **distinctive**, and
**lower-impact/practical alternative** where evidence and availability support it.

## Qualification

An opportunity becomes qualified when the buyer/use case, audience, timing, approximate
quantity, decision path, and operational feasibility are known. Budget can be a range or
constraint, but a quote does not leave CA without Kenny's review.

Disqualify or nurture:

- consumer/single-item requests outside the operating model;
- event date incompatible with approved production capability;
- price-only RFP with no strategic fit;
- unsupported sustainability or fulfillment requirement;
- no identifiable owner or use case;
- procurement/platform requirements CA has not approved.

Prioritize observable business triggers, not broad titles alone:

| Signal | Buyer | Offer angle |
|---|---|---|
| Multiple open roles / intern class | HR, recruiting | Onboarding Ready |
| Conference/exhibitor announcement | Marketing, events | Exhibit Ready |
| Office opening/relocation | Office, People | Office Merchandise Calendar |
| Retreat/annual meeting | Marketing, People | Event Ready |
| Anniversary/recognition program | People, office | Recognition Moments |
| Client event or year-end planning | Marketing, leadership | Client Gifting |
| Existing weak/inconsistent merchandise | Marketing, office | Managed Program |

Each prospect row records the public signal, observed date, source URL, buyer hypothesis,
and why the moment matters. Verify before outreach.

## Handoff to the tracker

Every researched or active opportunity uses one ID and records:

- account, buyer role, offer package, signal/source, and relationship;
- lead source and campaign/motion ID;
- stage, expected value, probability, owner, next action, and due date;
- quote/order dates and values;
- QuickBooks customer/invoice IDs once reconciled;
- campaign attribution and loss reason.

No performance claim is assumed. The weekly review decides which signals, buyers, offers,
and messages earn more effort based on qualified conversations, quotes, orders, and
reconciled revenue.
