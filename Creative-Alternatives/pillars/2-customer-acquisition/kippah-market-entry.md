## CA's wedge (vs incumbents)
1. **Reliability, not domestic production**: CA's kippot also come from overseas (~19 days
   door to door, confirmed by Kenny 7/25). Do NOT claim 'made here' or a speed win over
   competitors. What IS true and sellable: we hold the date, work backwards from it, and one
   family shop owns the whole thing end to end.
2. **Instant proof**: mockup engine puts the kid's name on a kippah in the pitch itself.
3. **The package**: kippot at ~$10/unit is the door ($500-1,200/order). The money is the
   party bundle: hoodies, socks, caps as dance-floor swag = $2-5K/family. Sell the bundle.
4. **Referral loop**: synagogue admins + mitzvah DJs/planners/venues, not one-off parents.
