# "Swag, Handled" — the Grand Slam offer for corporate & professional firms

> The corporate sibling to `home-run-offer.md`. A Hormozi-grade Grand Slam offer for firms that buy promo products *for themselves* (not to resell to members) — built so they take the call. Includes the offer, the multi-channel cadence, and the targeting. This is the camp-off-season expansion bet.

## The offer in one sentence
**Your company's branded swag — onboarding kits, event gear, client gifts, the company store — completely handled by one partner. We design it, source it, warehouse it, kit it, and ship it on demand. One contact, 24–48hr proofs, nothing for you to manage.**

Internal name: **The Swag Concierge.** Buyer-facing: **"Swag on Autopilot"** / "Your branded merch, handled."

## Why corporate buys (the jobs) — and why it hurts today
Promo for a company isn't about members repping a brand — it's about **getting recurring stuff done without the hassle:**
| The job | When it hits | The pain today |
|---------|--------------|----------------|
| **Onboarding kits** | Every new hire | Scrambling before a start date; runs out; falls on HR |
| **Events / conferences** | Throughout the year | Tight deadlines, last-minute, vendor can't turn it around |
| **Client / closing gifts** | Q4 + deal-driven | Generic junk that reflects poorly |
| **Company store** | Ongoing | Nobody wants to manage internal orders |
| **Uniforms / staff apparel** | Periodic | Multiple vendors, inconsistent |

The through-line pain: **juggling 2–3 vendors, slow turnarounds, managing art/proofs/reorders/storage themselves, and it always lands on whoever has the least time.** They don't have a "swag person." That's the wedge.

## The Grand Slam (Hormozi value equation)
**(Dream Outcome × Perceived Likelihood) ÷ (Time Delay × Effort & Sacrifice)** — max the top, crush the bottom.

| Lever | How this offer maxes it |
|-------|--------------------------|
| **Dream outcome** | "Swag that makes you look sharp, handled by someone else — you never scramble before an event or a new-hire start date again." |
| **Perceived likelihood** | They **see a mockup of their own branded kit before they commit a dollar** + 27 yrs / 75,000 orders + named corporate peers (Thermo Fisher, Trinity Health, Farm Credit) + 24–48hr proofs |
| **Time delay** | Mockup in the first touch · proofs in 24–48hr · warehoused + shipped on demand (no waiting on each order) |
| **Effort & sacrifice** | **They do nothing.** We design, source, store, kit, and ship. One point of contact who picks up the phone. |

**Note on the money (be honest):** unlike the Store Engine (rev-share), corporate buys directly — CA earns its **production margin**. So the *service layer* is free (design, mockups, account management, warehousing setup) and competitively priced on goods. The Grand Slam isn't "free products" — it's **free done-for-you + speed + one vendor + zero hassle.** That's what they actually want.

## The value stack (everything they get — lead with the whole thing)
1. **A dedicated swag concierge** — one human who knows their brand, picks up the phone (no account-manager runaround).
2. **Free design + unlimited mockups** — see it before you buy.

- **Industries:** law firms, accounting/CPA, real estate brokerages, insurance agencies, marketing/creative agencies, dental/medical/vet groups, financial advisors, architecture/engineering, consulting.
- **Size:** **25–500 employees** — big enough to buy swag regularly, small enough that the office manager / marketing / HR person *is* the reachable decision-maker (no procurement dept).
- **Geography:** **NY-metro + Northeast first** (CA's footprint + local proof), then expand.
- **Titles:** Office Manager, Operations Manager, Marketing Manager/Coordinator, HR / People Ops, Executive Assistant, Partner / Owner (small firms).
- **Buy-now triggers (rank these first):** actively hiring (job posts = onboarding need) · recent funding/growth · upcoming event/conference · new office opening · work anniversary season · Q4 (gifts).
