# Monk Mode Schedule v2 — Max Acquisition + Personal Brand

Rewritten 2026-08-09 after Ryan's call: v1 was too light on acquisition action. v2
doubles daily outbound volume and turns the lead engine into a background machine that
runs WHILE Ryan sells. Rule unchanged: acquisition owns business hours, brand owns the
edges.

## The lead engine runs in the background (the "system that gets more leads")

Ryan's hands are not the lead engine — the agent is. Every morning (5 min) Ryan kicks
off the day's **agent build queue**; Claude runs enrichment, list-builds, and campaign
prep in the background during phone hours; Ryan reviews output in the evening block.

**Standing weekly output quota for the engine: +500 fresh enriched leads/week minimum,
one new campaign segment shipped per week** (gated by the 3%-by-300 tripwire before
activation, but BUILT regardless).

**🔥 DEADLINE: 99,333 AI ARK credits expire Aug 16.** Week 1's engine work is
non-negotiable: enrich EVERY remaining segment while credits exist —
fall sports/schools/homecoming, galas/nonprofits, corporate retreats/holiday kickoffs,
plus a second wave of Oct-Nov trade shows (High Point Market, Greenbuild, ISSA, etc.).
Load the whole magazine this week even though launches stay staggered.

## Multichannel rule (why LinkedIn is in the day)
Same lead, three surfaces: email (campaign) → LinkedIn connect (face + name) →
phone/DM when warm. Priority order for the LinkedIn block: (1) people who REPLIED to
any campaign, (2) multi-opens/clickers from Smartlead stats, (3) cold exhibitor
marketers from this week's show. Never pitch in the connect note; the Loom/DM does the
talking after acceptance (HeyReach handles request automation where wired).

## Brand pillar (unchanged from v1)
"I'm using AI agents to grow my family's 27-year print shop." Mon system / Tue number /
Wed craft / Thu paste-able how-to / Fri founder log. Real artifacts verbatim, no
fabricated proof, educate-don't-gate, deal beats post 100% of the time.

## Hard guardrails
1. Phone blocks are immovable. Brand and engine work NEVER borrow from them.
2. Engine work is the AGENT'S job during the day — if Ryan is hand-building lists at
   10am, something is wrong (that's what the morning kick + evening review are for).
3. No new content systems during monk mode. Capture → post → back to work.
4. Sept 6 checkpoint reviews everything: lanes, brand ROI, engine reply rates. What
   converted gets more hours; what didn't gets cut without sentiment.
