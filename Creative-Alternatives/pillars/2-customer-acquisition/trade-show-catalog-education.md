## How to talk about pricing
Catalog prices in the YAML are the supplier's PUBLISHED list (C/retail) pricing. CA
buys at net (lower). Never quote from this sheet — Maclaine quotes. What this sheet
gives you: honest floor/ceiling instincts and minimums, so you never promise an
impossible number or get anchored by a prospect's guess.

## Ten things that make us sound like we know the business (because now we do)
1. **Minimums vary wildly**: tumbler = 36 pieces, power bank/speaker/backpack/blanket
   = 24-25, most handouts = 100-300. Small VIP orders are possible on premium items.
2. **The tumbler beats retail brands on math**: Hit's Himalayan does hot/cold 6 hours
   at roughly half a retail-brand price, and we CAN'T sell Yeti anyway (no account).
3. **UL-listed matters on tech**: the Exo power bank's UL cert is the answer when a
   compliance-minded buyer hesitates on batteries. Lithium items SHIP GROUND ONLY —
   build that into show timelines.
4. **Setup charges are real**: e.g. $45 on lip balm, $20 on pens. On small orders they
   move the per-unit price. Maclaine folds these in.
5. **Full-color isn't universal**: Digibrite/dye-sub on Hit items, digital inkjet on
   Leeds tech, but some items are 1-color pad/screen only. The mockup must match the
   decoration the SKU actually offers (the Yeti lesson, now a rule).
6. **The tote trick**: the show bag carries every competitor's literature with OUR
   client's logo facing out. Best cost-per-impression item on the floor.
7. **Lanyards are worn advertising**: chest height, all day, every aisle.
8. **Socks: sublimation, not knit-in** — knit-in logos are a custom mill program we
   don't stock. The SUB100 takes full-calf vivid print and is USA-made.
9. **Carhartt via SanMar** gives real retail-brand credibility at beanie money —
   confirm program access before promising it.
10. **What we deliberately excluded**: guessed SKUs (none), pre-release Gemline packs,
    gag concepts. Fun = high keep-rate, not jokes (Ryan's rule, 2026-08-10).
