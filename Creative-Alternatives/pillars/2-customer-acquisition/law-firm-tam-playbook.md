# Law Firm TAM & Targeting Playbook

> Triggered by the first real reply: Wil Antonides (Miller Johnson, ~230-attorney Michigan firm)
> buying swag for a PRACTICE GROUP retreat in September. This doc sizes the segment, extracts
> what that reply teaches, and turns it into targeting + messaging. Sources at bottom; day-1
> campaign data from Smartlead (Law campaign 3562940).

---

## 1. TAM: the numbers

- **~463,600 US law firms** (2025). But 40% are solo and 75% have ≤6 attorneys — not our buyers.
- **~1.37M lawyers.** NY + CA alone hold ~27% of them (NY: ~190K — CA's home market).
- **US promo products market: $27.1B/yr** (2025). Corporate spend benchmark: **$20–100 per recipient** per occasion.
- **The real segment tiers:**
  | Tier | Firms (est.) | Profile | Play |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | BigLaw (AmLaw 200 / NLJ 350) | ~350 | Multi-office, many practice groups, premium budgets (Latham gives Patagonia + Yeti to *recruits*) | Land ONE practice group / office / recruiting team, expand. Long cycles. |
  | **Midsize 20–200 attorneys — THE SWEET SPOT** | thousands nationally | Real events + gifting budgets, NO procurement dept — an Office/Ops Manager buys (exactly who replied) | Core outbound target. Fast decisions, multi-occasion LTV. Miller Johnson is this tier's upper end. |
  | Boutique 7–19 attorneys | ~100K | Holiday + client gifts more than retreats | Volume tier for gifting-season sends. |
  | Solo/≤6 | ~350K | — | Skip for outbound. |

**Order math:** a 50-person practice-group retreat at $50–100/head = **$2.5–5K order**. A firmwide
holiday gifting program = multiples of that. And each firm has MULTIPLE occasions per year —
this is a land-and-expand segment, not one-and-done.

## 2. What Wil's reply teaches (the strategic extractions)

1. **The buyer unit is the practice group, not the firm.** Miller Johnson has many practice
   groups; Wil is ONE of them. Every midsize+ firm is really 5–15 potential buyers (practice
   groups, offices, recruiting, marketing, HR). Never mark a firm "contacted" because one
   person got an email.
2. **Occasions drive purchases, not general need.** Wil isn't "interested in branded gear" —
   he has a RETREAT IN SEPTEMBER. The occasion creates the budget and the deadline. Messaging
   that names the occasion beats capability pitches.
3. **Ops/Office Manager titles convert.** Our list targeting was right. Add: legal
   administrators (ALA members), legal marketing (LMA), recruiting coordinators.
4. **Hiring signals work as personalization** ({{signal}} = "saw you're hiring X") but
   occasion signals should work even better — see §4.

## 3. The law-firm occasions calendar (the messaging engine)

Rotate the email-1 hook by season; the offer never changes, the OCCASION does:

| When to SEND | Occasion (buy window) | Hook angle |
|---|---|---|
| Jan–Feb | New partner class announcements | congratulations gifts for the new partners |
| Mar–Apr | Spring partner retreats; summer program prep | retreat kits; summer associate welcome kits (ordered Apr–May) |
| May–Jul | Summer associate season (BigLaw standard: multiple premium swag touchpoints per recruit) | "your summer class deserves better than a pen" |
| **Jul–Sep** | **Fall practice-group/partner retreats + OCI recruiting season** | **retreat kits (Wil's exact use case) + recruiting-table swag** |
| Oct–Dec | Client holiday gifting (the biggest season) + staff appreciation | tasteful client gifts, ordered before Thanksgiving |
| Always-on | New office openings, firm anniversaries, lateral onboarding, deal/verdict celebrations | congratulations/milestone angle |

**Right now (July) we're entering the strongest law window of the year: retreat season + OCI + holiday planning all stack Jul–Nov.**

## 4. Signal upgrades (feed to AI ARK / the {{signal}} line)

Current signal = hiring posts (works — it's what Wil got). Add these scrapes:
- Careers/OCI pages mentioning on-campus interview schedules or summer programs
- LinkedIn posts mentioning retreat / offsite / all-hands / firm outing
- Press releases: partner promotions, new offices, anniversaries ("celebrating 50 years")
- NALP directory (firms with summer programs = confirmed multi-occasion buyers)
- Firm size 20–200 attorneys as the primary list filter; NY-metro weighted first (home market + LSNYC/Mirman proof both NYC)

## 5. Messaging implications (apply to sequences + mockups)

1. **Premium is the bar.** BigLaw hands recruits Patagonia and Yeti. Lead mockups with the
   quarter-zip/premium tier, never the cheap-pen tier. (Our Miller Johnson trio was right.)
2. **Occasion-specific mockups win.** "Here's your retreat kit" (Sept date implied) beats
   "here's branded gear." The mockup SOP should theme the trio to the occasion when known.
3. **Season-rotate email 1.** Aug send: retreat angle. Oct send: holiday client-gifting angle.
   Same list can be re-approached ~6 months later with a different occasion (AAA re-approach rule).
4. **Practice-group expansion motion:** after ANY law-firm order ships, a 30-day follow-up:
   "want us to put together the same for [other practice groups / your recruiting team /
   holiday client gifts]?" Multi-buyer firms = built-in referral surface.
5. **Geography:** NY-metro first (Kenny's home turf, 190K lawyers, both proof clients are NYC).

## 6. Day-1 campaign baseline (for measuring improvements)

180 sent · 84 opens (47%) · 1 bounce (0.55% — excellent) · 2 replies · **1 Interested (Miller
Johnson — practice-group retreat)**. Lists on hand: ~1,700 law contacts across the swag_*_law
CSVs; campaign loaded with 1,241. Expansion target: 5–10K midsize-firm contacts, NY-metro
weighted, with occasion signals.

Directional revenue math (small sample, revisit at 1,000 sends): if ~1 interested lead per
~180 sends holds, 2,000 sends/mo ≈ 11 interested/mo; closing even a quarter at $2.5–5K first
orders ≈ **$7–14K/mo new law revenue before reorders and expansion** — on ONE vertical of 13.
