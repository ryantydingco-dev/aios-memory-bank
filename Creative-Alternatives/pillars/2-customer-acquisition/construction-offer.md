# Creative Alternatives — Construction (GC/Builder) Offer & Cold Copy

**Niche:** General contractors & builders, Northeast (NY/NJ/CT/MA/PA/RI/NH/VT/ME/MD/DE), 25–1,000 employees.
**TAM:** ~5,698 addressable contacts (loose) → ~3,500–4,000 clean GCs after precision filter.
**Buyers:** office manager, operations manager, marketing manager, HR manager, director of people, EA.

## The Grand Slam Offer — "The Job-Site Gear Program"
One vendor for everything that carries the logo: **Carhartt crew apparel, Yeti client gifts, hi-vis/safety, trade-show kits** — designed, warehoused, shipped on demand, 24–48hr proofs, one rep.

**Value stack (only costs us when they buy):**
- Free personalized **Crew Kit mockup** (digital, $0 — the hook)
- Free **company store** build + free **logo digitizing** (labor, not COGS)
- **Founding-account pricing** locked if set up this quarter (urgency + lock-in)
- Order-funded bonus (e.g., branded beanie per crew member *on the first apparel order*)
- Guarantee: 48-hr proofs + remake-free if the crew doesn't love the first kit
- Physical sample reserved for the close (pre-production sign-off on a live order)

**Why it's sticky:** company store + auto-reorder + one rep = effortless reorders, painful to leave → recurring bulk revenue. Crew turnover + wear-out = it recurs on its own.
