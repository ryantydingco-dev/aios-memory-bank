# Pillar 3 — Online presence

Turn CA's credible existing website and 27 years of proof into a measurable inbound acquisition system. The site is live and in good shape visually; the opportunity is niche authority, search visibility, conversion, attribution, and a repeatable buyer-content engine.

> Starts in parallel around week 3 (see `context/strategy.md`). Pairs with the YouTube push.
>
> **Current governing LinkedIn workflow:** `linkedin-content-engine.md`. It assigns distinct purposes and sustainable cadences to Ryan, Kenny, and Maclaine and only drafts from real business events.

## Starting state

- The Squarespace website is live with useful proof, service pages, reviews, and direct calls to action.
- The root domain redirects correctly to `www`.
- Public page titles are generic and meta descriptions are blank.
- The sitemap includes `/home`, which canonicalizes to `/`.
- No dedicated commercial pages currently target camps, racquet clubs, law firms, schools, clubs, corporate teams, or events.
- Google Business Profile ownership, analytics, Search Console, Ads, CRM tracking, current location/service area, and public-asset permissions remain `[CONFIRM]`.

## The work (in compounding order)

1. **Three-person LinkedIn engine:** event capture, differentiated voices, human approvals, and proof-safe drafts.
2. **Shared foundation:** technical SEO, analytics, HubSpot attribution, forms, qualification, reporting, Google Business Profile, and a reusable commercial-page template.
3. **Evidence-led authority:** the outbound segment decision and real proof determine which niche receives focused investment first.
4. **Buyer-content engine:** planning guides, product judgment, process proof, customer outcomes, and case studies distributed through search, social, email, and partners.
5. **Compounding loop:** closed customers become reviews, case studies, referrals, reorders, and stronger acquisition evidence.

The full operating system lives in `inbound/`. The governing rollout plan is `../../plans/inbound-master-implementation-2026-07-10.md`.

## Tools

- Marketing skills (`marketing:*`), `linkedin-*` skills for LinkedIn.
- Blotato for publishing, Notion for planning, GA4/Google Places for measurement.
- Media: Pexels, ElevenLabs, HeyGen (MCP + media tools) for assets.

## Definition of done (first cycle)

- Shared website, analytics, CRM, qualification, and reporting behavior is tested.
- Camps, racquet clubs, and law-firm pages are live with approved proof and working conversion paths.
- Each Wave 1 niche has an original resource and a controlled distribution/search program.
- Qualified inbound leads, quotes, customers, and revenue are attributable by source, niche, offer, and campaign.
- A weekly operating review makes explicit scale, revise, pause, or consolidate decisions.
