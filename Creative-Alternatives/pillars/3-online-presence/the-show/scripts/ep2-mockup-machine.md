## Beat 2 — The handoff (2:30-6:30) [AI co-host + SCREEN]
You: "Okay. Walk them through it. And don't skip the part where you mess up."
[AI SEGMENT — Intern presents, cloned voice, screen captures under its narration]:
1. **Find the logo** — "I pull it straight off their website. Every firm has an SVG in the
   theme folder. They never hide it well."
2. **The reference card** — logo composited clean. "This is the part humans skip. The model
   needs a perfect reference or it improvises. You do not want me improvising a law firm's
   name."
3. **The generation** — mockups materializing (real ones: quarter-zip, tumbler, notebook).
   SHOW THE REAL PROMPT on screen 3-5s (the design-lock line: "reproduce the logo EXACTLY —
   do not warp, rewrite, re-letter" + negative block).
4. **The QC rule** — "Every letter gets checked. One garbled letter and the whole thing dies.
   The logo is their NAME." Show a real rejected generation if we have one (honesty beat).
5. **The lookbook** — Gamma pages turning. "Seven pages. Their brand colors. Takes me about
   fifteen minutes. It used to take an agency two weeks."
[Intern hands back]: "Then a human sends it. They don't let me talk to customers." Beat.
You: "We don't let it talk to customers."

## Beat 3 — The payoff (6:30-8:00) [REAL + SCREEN]
- The real numbers, plainly: "First law campaign: about half of them opened it. One wrote
  back interested. That one reply is now a conversation about a hundred and twenty pieces
  for their firm retreat."
- The honest framing: "One reply doesn't sound like much until you know what a 120-piece
  corporate order is worth to a family shop. This is why you build machines: it does this
  every single morning while I'm at my day job."
