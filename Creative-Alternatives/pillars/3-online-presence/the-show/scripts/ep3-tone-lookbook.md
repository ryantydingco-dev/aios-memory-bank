# Episode 3 — "I Built My Friend's Yoga Studio a Merch Line She Didn't Ask For"

> The TONE lookbook story — warm episode, shows range + craft + the honest "it looked too
> AI" fix. Target 7-9 min. Needs Gabby's blessing before publish (she comes off great —
> the episode is essentially a gift — but ask first).

## Cold open (0:00-0:20) [REAL]
"My friend Gabby quit corporate America and opened a yoga studio. So I did what any normal
friend does. I built her an entire merch line overnight. She didn't ask."

## Intro + interrupt (0:20-1:00) [REAL + AI]
- Premise line. [AI INTERRUPT]: Intern appears in a (rendered) yoga pose or holding the
  rolled TONE mat. **"I made her a yoga mat. I don't have a body."** Glitch out.

## Beat 1 — The why (1:00-2:30) [REAL + SCREEN]
- Gabby's LinkedIn post on screen (with permission): "personality re-entering my body after
  exiting corporate America." To-camera: "That line IS a t-shirt. She just didn't know it yet."
- The gift logic = the same machine from Episode 2 pointed at a friend. "New studios bleed
  cash on everything. Merch is how members become billboards. She shouldn't have to think
  about it yet."

## Beat 2 — The build + the failure (2:30-6:00) [AI co-host + SCREEN]
You: "Show them. Including the embarrassing part."
[AI SEGMENT — Intern presents]:
1. Site scrape → logo → the studio kit renders (tank, crewneck, mat, bottle, tote).
2. The statement pieces: F*CK CORPORATE and BET ON YOURSELF (her own words, from the post).
3. **THE FAILURE (the beat that builds trust):** show the v1 mockups. Intern, flatly:
   **"These are bad. I drew the letters myself. You can tell."** You: "You can really tell."
   The fake brush script, the mushy kerning — say the quiet part: "This is the AI slop
   everyone hates. We hate it too."
4. **THE FIX:** "So we stopped letting me draw. We found her actual brand font" — show the
   font-detective bit: site said Syne, logo was actually Poppins Black, the side-by-side
   comparison sheet — "sampled the exact teal from her logo, built the print like a real
   print designer would, and told me to apply it EXACTLY. I render fabric and light. The
   typography is human."
5. v3 reveal: the folded crewneck, the hanging tank. Before/after side-by-side.

## Beat 3 — The payoff (6:00-7:30) [REAL]
- The lookbook pages turning. The delivery: texting it to Gabby / her reaction (capture it
  for real if possible — even just the text-message screenshot with permission).
- To-camera: "Total cost: about an hour and some GPU time. For her: a merch line and proof
  somebody bets on her too. That's the whole point of having this machine."

## Close (7:30-8:30) [REAL]
"If you know somebody who just opened something — a studio, a shop, a bar — comment their
logo and what they do. We'll make one. The Intern doesn't sleep anyway."
Intern (off-frame or quick materialize): **"I genuinely don't. It's fine."**
Final frame REAL. "Real people since 1999."
