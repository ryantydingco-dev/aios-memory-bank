# Episode 1 — "I Gave a 27-Year-Old Print Shop an AI Employee"

> The pilot. Mostly documentary footage from install day (Friday) + two staged pieces from
> the studio block. Target 8-10 min. The Intern's ARRIVAL episode — it "moves into" the Mac
> Studio, so its first appearance lands late in this episode, not the intro (origin story
> beats the formula once).

## Cold open (0:00-0:20) [REAL — install-day footage]
Unboxing shot. VO or to-camera:
"My girlfriend's family has run a print shop for 27 years. Same building, same family. Kenny
still answers the phone. And today I'm giving them their first AI employee. It came in a box."

## Beat 1 — The stakes (0:20-2:00) [REAL]
- To-camera (morning beat): who you are in one line, what CA is, what your deal is. "I'm not
  a filmmaker. I'm the guy who married into a print shop. Almost."
- Shop b-roll under: presses, thread, boxes.
- Maclaine soundbite: what her week actually looks like (the manual grind, in her words).
- Kenny establishing shot. One line about him: "Kenny has done this for 27 years and trusts
  exactly none of what I'm about to do."

## Beat 2 — The install (2:00-5:00) [REAL — documentary]
- First boot, setup montage, honest narration of what's actually going on it (QuickBooks
  first, layers not leaps — say it plainly: "We're not feeding it 27 years of files on day
  one. We're starting with the books.")
- You giving it the first command out loud (Wispr Flow moment). Let the audience see the
  real workflow: you just... talk to it.
- SHOW the failed/awkward moment if one happens. Do not cut around it.

## Beat 3 — Kenny meets it (5:00-6:30) [REAL — the money shot]
Whatever actually happened Friday. His skepticism is the scene. Do not script Kenny.

## Beat 4 — The Intern arrives (6:30-8:00) [AI + REAL, shot in studio block]
- To-camera: "Here's the part I haven't told them yet. I didn't just install software.
  I trained it on me."
- [AI SHOT 1]: The Intern materializes (soft glitch) beside you, wearing the same navy zip
  you're wearing. Looks around. Looks at you. First line (cloned voice, slightly too clean):
  **"So. This is the shop. It's smaller than the photos."**
- You, deadpan: "It's got my face. It's got my voice. It works nights. Kenny doesn't know yet."
- [AI SHOT 2 — optional]: Intern picks up/receives a real object you toss (the handoff
  signature — a blank tee or the Diet-Coke-style prop). Studio block: film your toss + hold.

## Close (8:00-9:00) [REAL]
End-of-day beat, tired, honest: "Day one. It's got the books. Next week it starts earning its
desk — I'm going to show you the machine that got a law firm to ask us for 120 pieces of
merch. That one's already built. See you then."
Final frame: the shop, real, lights off. Title card: "Real people since 1999."
