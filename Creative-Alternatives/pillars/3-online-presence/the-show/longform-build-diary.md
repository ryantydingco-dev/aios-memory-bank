## Episode skeleton (8-12 min)
1. **Cold open (0:00-0:20)** — the stakes, mid-action. "A law firm just asked us for 120
   pieces. Here's the machine that made that happen."
2. **Intro + interrupt (0:20-1:00)** — premise reminder in one line, Intern barges in.
3. **The problem (1-3 min)** — real business context. What CA needs this week and why it's
   hard. Kenny/Maclaine footage lives here.
4. **The build (3-8 min)** — how the system got built. Intern co-hosts the technical part.
   Screen capture + real desk footage. Show the actual prompts/configs (give-give-give).
5. **The payoff (8-10 min)** — did it work? Real results, real numbers, or honest failure.
   The final frame is always REAL (world-bible rule).
6. **Close (10-12 min)** — community beat (Joey's warmth: thank, tease next episode's build,
   one soft CTA). Free artifact in description every episode (skill, prompt, template).
