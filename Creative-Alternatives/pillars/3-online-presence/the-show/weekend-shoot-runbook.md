      side is where the Intern lives.
- [ ] Light the ROOM, not just your face (AI shots inherit the plate's lighting).
- [ ] Tape your feet position.
- [ ] IP sweep: remove every branded/licensed object from frame except CA items (one plush in
      frame kills generations — Joey's Miffy lesson).

2. Ep 3 real shots ("The TONE Lookbook") — to-camera beats + the phone-look shot

**Rules while shooting:** 2 takes minimum per beat. Flubbed lines are keepers (honesty
texture). Final frame of every episode is REAL. Don't delete anything.
