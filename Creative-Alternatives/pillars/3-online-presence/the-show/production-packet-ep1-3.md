# Production Packet — Episodes 1-3 (Ryan + The Intern)

> Shot-by-shot for the first three videos. All three shot in ONE session, one location, one
> tripod setup (batch = GenHQ rule). Total filming: ~45-60 min. AI shots generated after via
> `avatar-pipeline.md`. Every episode follows the spine: Setup (real) → Escalation (AI) →
> Grounding (real wins, warmly). 9:16 vertical, 15-30s finals.

## Shared setup (do once)
- Location: cleanest wall/desk corner available (shop version = v2 after Friday). REMOVE all
  branded/IP objects from frame (posters, plushies, logos) except CA items — eligibility.
- Camera: ZV-E10 on tripod, LOCKED. Frame yourself slightly off-center, leaving clear space
  on the opposite side — that space is where The Intern lives. Mark your feet with tape.
- Light the room evenly (not just your face) — the AI shots inherit the plate's lighting.
- Props: 1 blank navy tee + 1 PRINTED CA tee (same style — print one at the shop this week),
  phone, a cardboard box (weight gag).
- For every scene: film your real take, then 3 seconds of EMPTY locked frame (clean plate for
  the AI generation), then hold position variants for the return-catch shots.

## EPISODE 1 — "The Handoff" (the thesis video — post first)
**Hook text on screen:** "our AI can't do the one thing that matters"
1. **REAL (0-4s):** You to camera, holding blank navy tee: "This is our AI intern. Watch this."
   Toss the tee toward the empty side of frame, off-screen.
2. **AI (4-11s):** [plate + character sheet] The Intern materializes with a soft glitch,
   catches the tee, gives it one spin — the CA print MATERIALIZES onto the fabric — tosses it
   back toward camera-left. (PHYSICS: real fabric weight on catch and toss; print appears as
   a sweep of light across the chest, not a jump-cut.)
3. **REAL (11-17s):** You catch the REAL printed tee (the physically printed one). Beat. Look
   at it, look at camera: "It does the preview. We print the real thing." Small shrug:
   "27 years. Real people." 
**End card (17-19s):** CA logo on navy · "AI previews. Real people deliver."
Caption: "day 1 of employing an AI intern at a 27 year old print shop" + vertical hashtags.

## EPISODE 2 — "The Intern" (the character introduction — post second)
**Hook text:** "we gave the AI intern its first job"
1. **REAL (0-5s):** You, working: "Intern — client needs 500 hoodies by Friday. Can you
   handle it?"
2. **AI (5-12s):** The Intern materializes, salutes, and instantly conjures a floating wall of
   perfect hoodie mockups (front/back/detail). Proud. Nods confidently.
3. **REAL (12-20s):** You, unmoved: "No — physically. MAKE them. Physically." Cut back to:
4. **AI (20-25s):** The Intern looks at its hands. Looks at the box on the floor. Tries to
   nudge it. Nothing. Looks up: on-screen text bubble — **"...that sounds like a Kenny problem."**
5. **REAL (25-28s):** You, deadpan to camera: "Yeah. It does." *(Kenny is now seeded.)*
**End card:** "Creative Alternatives — the humans make the stuff."

## EPISODE 3 — "Two Ryans, One Deadline" (the banter rhythm — post third)
**Hook text:** "arguing with myself is now part of the job"
1. **REAL (0-4s):** You, stressed, at desk: "Okay — the law firm retreat order. We need
   mockups for the quarter-zip, the tumbler—"
2. **AI (4-8s):** The Intern materializes already holding all three mockups fanned out like
   playing cards. Smug.
3. **REAL (8-13s):** "...I was going to say we also need—" 
4. **AI (13-17s):** The Intern snaps: a notebook mockup appears mid-air. Even smugger.
5. **REAL (17-24s):** Beat. "Fine. Who's driving them to the client?" Cut to:
6. **AI (24-27s):** The Intern glitches, flickers... vanishes. 
7. **REAL (27-30s):** You grab your keys off the desk. "Every time." Walk out of frame.
**End card:** "Real people since 1999."

## Post-production checklist (per episode)
- [ ] 2 takes per AI shot → pick cleaner (hands, likeness, outfit) → upscale winner
- [ ] Assemble: cut on action, real frame first and LAST always
- [ ] Hook text overlay first 3s, end card 2s (engine templates)
- [ ] Captions written per platform (draft with skills, human-edit for voice)
- [ ] Export clean (no music) → trending sound added in-app per platform
- [ ] QC vs world-bible tone rules: deadpan? one joke? under 30s? glitch-tell present?
- [ ] File into the 2-week buffer — these three are launch week, days 1-3
