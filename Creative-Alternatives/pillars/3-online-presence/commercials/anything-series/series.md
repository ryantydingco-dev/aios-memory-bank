## Production rules (inherited from the v1 run, all proven)
1. Keyframe still first: nano banana, 9:16, 2k, logo UUID d550bcf6-3972-4e56-9446-0078a55c981c,
   hardened lock ("logo appears EXACTLY ONCE... nothing else printed anywhere").
2. QC letter-perfect at full size. One garbled letter = regenerate. Watch for the two known
   failure modes: dark letters on dark surfaces (put logo on a white panel/band) and duplicate
   hallucinated logos in the background.
3. Animate with seedance_2_0, --start-image = approved still, 5s, 11-block prompt, SMALL
   motion only (push-in, settle wobble, light drift). Deadpan = stillness is the joke.
4. Frame-check first/mid/last. Then post as-is (no overlay) or with the one-word overlay
   style from the spot ("anything.") if it needs the context.
5. Buffer rule: episodes queue in the 2-week buffer, never posted hand-to-mouth.
