## POSITION / TITLE

You are not an owner yet, so anything implying equity is a lie a buyer could catch. But
"Business Development" reads junior for someone who is the entire commercial function.
What a buyer actually needs to believe: *this person can make decisions and get my order
done.*

**Recommended: `Head of Growth`**
Signals seniority, claims no equity, and is literally accurate — you own finding
customers and building the systems that do it. Also grows into ownership cleanly.

| Option | When to use it | Risk |
|---|---|---|
| **Head of Growth** ← pick this | Default. Modern, senior, honest | Slightly startup-flavored for a traditional buyer |
| **Director of Business Development** | If your main buyers are law firms, schools, municipalities | Safe but forgettable |
| **Head of New Business** | Warmest, plainest English | Least searchable |
| Partner / Co-Owner | — | **Don't.** Not true today, and it's the one thing a customer could discover |

Whatever you pick, use it identically on LinkedIn, in your email signature, and when you
introduce yourself on calls. Inconsistency is what makes a small shop look shaky.
