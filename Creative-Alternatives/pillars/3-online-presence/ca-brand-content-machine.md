## The core creative insight: the mockup wedge IS the content

The single best thing CA can post is the thing it already does to win deals: **putting a real
organization's logo on real products, beautifully, fast.** Every mockup post is simultaneously
brand awareness, a service demo, and a lead-gen mechanism ("comment your logo"). GenHQ's
highest-engagement community template (the Instant Spec Ad Maker) is literally this format.
Nobody in promo products is doing it well. That's the lane.

## The weekly production system (~3 hours of human time, machine does the rest)

1. **Engine batch (no humans):** the mockup line generates 8-10 branded kits/week (real
   prospects from the outbound lists = doubles as Stage-1 wedge assets, or "comment your logo"
   requests once those start). assemble.py cuts each into a reveal video with hook text +
   end-card. That alone covers 4-5 posts/week.
2. **Shop session (60-90 min, once a week, Maclaine's phone):** batch-film 10-15 ASMR/b-roll
   clips + 1-2 kit reveals. Covers 3-4 posts/week for weeks.
3. **Kenny capture (opportunistic):** one story per week, even just audio — the engine overlays
   b-roll. Filming boundaries per the ops-audit kit Section 10.
4. **Assembly + queue:** everything cut, captioned, and queued into a **2-week buffer minimum
   before launch** (AAA rule — never post hand-to-mouth). Approval: Ryan or Maclaine eyeballs
   every post before it queues (house rule; also catches logo/claim errors).

## AAA rules in force (the ones that decide success)

- **Consistency > perfection.** The first 30-50 posts will be mid. Post anyway — reps build
  the skill and the algorithmic trust. Nobody's watching early; that's the safe window.
- **Hook in 1-3 seconds** with proof or motion (the press peeling, the logo landing on the kit).
- **Value metrics, not vanity:** track DMs, "comment your logo" replies, quote requests, and
  site clicks — NOT likes/views. 200 views with 3 quote requests beats 100K views with none.
- **Own the audience:** add a simple email capture ("retreat season checklist" or lookbook
  download) once traffic starts; social followers are rented.
- **6-12 month horizon** for compounding inbound; the flywheel payoff (outbound reply-rate lift
  from prospects seeing a live brand) starts much sooner. Measure both.
- **No fabricated claims/testimonials ever** — real orders, real numbers (27 years, 75K orders),
  real stories only.

## Revenue mechanics (how organic actually converts here)

1. **"Comment your logo" → DM → mockup → quote** — the content IS the wedge; replies feed the
   same reply→mockup machine built for email.
2. **Occasion posts → deadline urgency → quote requests** (retreat/holiday calendars).
3. **Outbound warming** — law-firm admins who got Maclaine's email check the socials; an active,
   premium feed converts fence-sitters. Track reply-rate on warmed vs cold.
4. **Local/vertical hashtag + geo presence** → camps, clubs, and firms finding CA when they
   search. Google Business Profile (pillar 3 foundations) catches the spillover.

## Brand voice: the anti-big-machine (the positioning that wins)

CONFIRMED 2026-07-01: CA has ZERO social presence — greenfield. The positioning against
4imprint/Custom Ink/Vistaprint-style megamachines is the whole brand:

> **They're a vending machine. We're a family.** 27 years, same family, same shop. A real
> person mocks up your gear, a real person answers when something's wrong, and Kenny still
> picks up the phone.

Voice rules for every post and bio:
- **Faces and first names, always.** Kenny, Maclaine, the shop floor. Never stock photos,
  never corporate-speak. The megamachines physically cannot copy this.
- Warm, a little funny, zero polish-for-polish's-sake. A real shop posting, not a brand
  "doing content."
- Show the hands: real presses, real thread, real boxes. (The AI engine makes the *mockups*
  and the *editing* — the humans and the shop stay unmistakably real on camera. Never present
  AI-generated scenes as real production footage; mockups are shown AS mockups/previews,
  which is honest — that's literally the product.)
- Bio template (adapt per platform): "Family print shop since 1999. We put your logo on
  anything — and a real human answers. 75,000+ orders. Just call Kenny. 📞"
