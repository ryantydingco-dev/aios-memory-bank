# Niche Page And Offer Briefs

## How to use this file

Each brief defines the niche-specific layer that sits on top of the shared site and funnel system. Copy is draft until approved. Claims marked `[CONFIRM]` must not be published.

## Summer camps and youth programs

### Status

Wave 1 - Build first. Detailed implementation lives in `plans/camp-inbound-experiment-brief-2026-07-10.md`.

### Buyer

Camp owner, director, operations director, camp-store lead, or administrator responsible for staff and camper gear.

### Core job

Get the right camp merchandise delivered on time without catalog overload, sizing chaos, leftover inventory, or parent-ordering headaches.

### Draft hero

**H1:** Summer camp merchandise without the scramble.

**Support:** Creative Alternatives designs, sources, prints, and delivers the apparel and camp gear your staff, campers, and families actually want. Choose a bulk order, a managed store, or a mix of both.

### Offer and CTA

- Entry offer: personalized 2027 camp merchandise concept.
- Primary CTA: **Plan My 2027 Camp Merch**
- Urgent CTA: **I Need A Rush Restock**

### Product/use-case groups

- Staff and camper apparel
- Opening-day and counselor kits
- Camp-store assortment
- Parent and alumni ordering
- Session, reunion, and anniversary merchandise
- Rush and mid-season restocks

### Proof required

- Approved camp names or anonymized case studies
- Real tees, hoodies, hats, bottles, bags, and unusual products
- Seasonal timeline knowledge
- Approved public facts: since 1999, 2,700+ customers, 75,000+ orders, 24-48 hour digital proofs

### Objections to answer

- We already have a supplier.
- We do not want leftover inventory.
- Parents complain about ordering and sizing.
- We are already late.
- We need both staff bulk orders and a parent store.
- We do not have time to choose from thousands of products.

### Search themes

Summer camp merchandise, custom camp apparel, camp store merchandise, camp staff shirts, online camp store, camp promotional products.

### Content anchors

- 2027 camp merchandise planning calendar
- Camp store vs bulk order
- 90/60/30-day ordering timeline
- Staff/camper/parent assortment guide
- Mid-season restock checklist

## Squash, racquet, and padel clubs

### Status

Wave 1 - Build second.

### Buyer

Club GM, head professional, tournament director, membership lead, pro-shop buyer, or academy director.

### Core job

Keep club, team, tournament, and recognition merchandise consistent, desirable, and on schedule without managing multiple vendors.

### Draft hero

**H1:** Club merchandise built by people who understand racquet communities.

**Support:** From performance apparel and member stores to tournament gifts, championship boards, locker nameplates, and the unusual pieces clubs struggle to source, Creative Alternatives handles the full program.

### Offer and CTA

- Entry offer: personalized club collection or tournament plan.
- Primary CTA: **See My Club Collection**
- Tournament CTA: **Plan My Tournament Merch**

### Product/use-case groups

- Club and team performance apparel
- Member lifestyle apparel
- Online club and pro-shop stores
- Tournament participant and sponsor merchandise
- Awards, trophies, boards, and recognition
- Towels, bottles, bags, grips, and accessories
- Staff and professional apparel

### Proof required

- Public testimonials already on the CA site from squash professionals and club leaders
- Kenny's category history, stated accurately
- Real club apparel, boards, locker plates, towels, and tournament products
- Approved timelines and reorder examples

### Objections to answer

- We already buy from a racquet brand.
- Our members want premium products, not logo slaps.
- The pro shop is too small to hold inventory.
- Sponsors need correct logos and placements.
- Tournament dates cannot move.
- We need both performance and off-court apparel.

### Search themes

Custom squash club apparel, squash tournament merchandise, racquet club merchandise, club pro-shop apparel, padel club custom apparel, racquet club online store.

### Content anchors

- Tournament merch checklist
- Club pro-shop starter assortment
- Performance vs lifestyle club apparel
- Tournament ordering timeline
- Recognition products beyond trophies

### Ad angle

Lead with category fluency and full-program capability, not free store setup. Search competitors already promise 48-hour mockups and apparel-only production; CA's differentiation is the relationship plus the breadth of what a club needs.
