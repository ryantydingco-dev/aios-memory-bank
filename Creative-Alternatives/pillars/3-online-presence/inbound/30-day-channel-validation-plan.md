# 30-Day Inbound Channel Validation Plan

**Window:** August 10–September 8, 2026  
**Pilot niche:** Summer camps and youth programs  
**Offer:** Personalized 2027 camp merchandise plan  
**Primary CTA:** Plan My 2027 Camp Merch  
**Channels:** LinkedIn, the CA website/Google organic layer, and one American Camp Association community path

## Why this pilot

Summer camps are the safest first inbound niche because CA already has verified customer history, strong lifetime value, relevant operating knowledge, and the best observed outbound response among tested segments. This test uses one niche and one offer across all channels so channel performance can be compared without changing the message at the same time.

The offer and public wording remain subject to Kenny and Maclaine's approval. This plan does not authorize publishing, association contact, ad spend, automated replies, or customer-facing claims.

## Test hypothesis

If CA gives camp directors useful 2027 planning guidance, proves that it understands camp ordering, and offers a specific merchandise-planning next step, then LinkedIn and an ACA community path should create early qualified conversations while the camp page begins building organic search visibility.

### What each channel is expected to prove

| Channel | Thirty-day question | Do not over-interpret |
|---|---|---|
| LinkedIn | Can real camp buyer education produce qualified visits, replies, or introductions? | Impressions without buyer action |
| Website and Google organic | Can the page convert targeted visitors and become indexable for relevant queries? | New-page search volume in only 30 days |
| ACA community path | Does concentrated industry distribution create higher-quality visits or conversations? | One unanswered partnership request |

Organic search is a 60–90 day learning lane. The first 30 days validate technical readiness, indexing, early impressions, and landing-page conversion. They do not determine the final SEO verdict.

## Buyer and qualification rule

### Primary buyers

- Camp owner or executive director
- Camp director
- Operations director
- Camp store or merchandise lead
- Youth-program leader with recurring apparel or merchandise responsibility

### Qualified inquiry

Count an inquiry as qualified only when all of the following are true:

1. The organization operates a real camp or youth program.
2. The contact owns, influences, or can refer the merchandise decision.
3. There is a real program, season, store, event, staff-kit, or merchandise need.
4. The timing is within the next 12 months.
5. The request is commercially plausible for CA and does not conflict with an active service issue or suppression rule.

Spam, job inquiries, vendor solicitations, students, and requests outside CA's delivery model do not count.

## KPI framework

### Primary outcomes

| KPI | Definition | Source | Thirty-day decision use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Qualified inquiries | Unique inquiries passing the five qualification rules | HubSpot plus human review | Determines whether the motion creates real buyer demand |
| Quote opportunities | Qualified inquiries that advance to a scoped quote request | HubSpot opportunity or reviewed tracker | Determines whether interest is commercially actionable |
| Attributed gross profit | Collected gross profit from customers first or materially assisted by this pilot | QuickBooks reconciliation | Lagging proof used after the 30-day window |

### Diagnostic drivers

- Targeted sessions by channel
- CTA clicks
- Form starts and submissions
- LinkedIn content-assisted replies or DMs
- Association referral sessions or introductions
- Search Console impressions and relevant queries
- Median first-response time

### Guardrails

- No unapproved customer name, logo, image, testimonial, number, or fulfillment promise is published.
- Every inquiry reaches a named human owner.
- First response occurs within one business day until a different SLA is approved.
- Buyer-facing content stays within the existing two-hour weekly human-time cap.
- No paid media launches without separate budget and conversion approval.
- No automated message, quote, price, vendor commitment, or customer promise.

## Provisional decision thresholds

These are operating thresholds, not industry benchmarks. Replace them after the first verified baseline.

| Result | Threshold | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| Strong signal | At least 5 qualified inquiries and 2 quote opportunities | Continue the niche and expand the best channel |
| Valid early signal | At least 3 qualified inquiries and 1 quote opportunity | Continue for another 30 days and improve the main constraint |
| Distribution failure | Fewer than 100 targeted sessions | Do not judge the offer; improve reach and placement |
| Conversion failure | At least 150 targeted sessions with zero qualified inquiries | Revise the offer, proof, page, or qualification path |
| Operational failure | Missed routing, broken attribution, unapproved claim, or response beyond the agreed SLA | Pause until repaired |

## Week 1: Approve and baseline

**August 10–16**

- Confirm the 2027 camp offer, public claims, response owner, response SLA, service area, and fulfillment boundaries with Kenny and Maclaine.
- Approve or reject each customer proof candidate and image.
- Verify Squarespace, GA4, Search Console, HubSpot, and Google Business Profile access without storing credentials in the repository.
- Record the pre-launch baseline: current camp-page sessions, camp inquiries, quote requests, and attributed customers.
- Select one ACA path for the pilot: business-affiliate directory, educational contribution, regional chapter, or newsletter placement.
- Create the four-week asset queue in the existing content editorial board.

**Exit gate:** business approvals and a named lead owner exist. If they do not, the pilot remains in preparation.

## Week 2: Assemble and test the funnel

**August 17–23**

- Stage the camp commercial page and planning-calendar resource from the existing copy.
- Add approved proof and buyer-useful visuals.
- Connect the shared form with camp, offer, and source values prepopulated.
- Install or validate attribution only in the approved environment.
- Run desktop and mobile QA.
- Submit controlled tests from LinkedIn, direct, and ACA-style links.
- Verify that each test reaches the correct human with first-touch source intact.

**Exit gate:** one controlled submission completes the full path from source link to human owner and is correctly classified.

## Week 3: Controlled activation

**August 24–30**

After explicit publication and contact approval:

- Publish the camp page and planning resource.
- Publish one buyer-education post through the approved CA, Ryan, or Maclaine voice.
- Publish one operator/proof post that points to the same camp destination.
- Submit or send the approved ACA directory, education, chapter, or newsletter approach manually.
- Request indexing and verify sitemap/canonical state.
- Review every inquiry within one business day and record its qualification outcome.

**Exit gate:** the three channels are live or have a documented external blocker, and every response has an owner.

## Week 4: Learn and decide

**August 31–September 8**

- Publish the second buyer-education asset only if it comes from real CA knowledge or proof.
- Review landing-page behavior, form completion, inquiry quality, response time, and channel attribution.
- Separate reach problems from conversion problems.
- Record one `keep`, `change`, `expand`, or `pause` decision for each channel.
- Carry qualified opportunities into the normal sales and quote process.
- Capture customer language, questions, and objections for the next page and content revision.

**Final decision:** continue camps for another 30 days, revise the offer or funnel, pause the niche, or request a controlled paid-search test.

## Weekly operating cadence

### Monday, 30 minutes

1. Review the prior week's scorecard.
2. Confirm the one conversion constraint.
3. Select no more than two public assets.
4. Assign owners and approvals.

### Daily, 10 minutes

1. Check for new inquiries and routing failures.
2. Qualify each inquiry.
3. Assign the next sales action.
4. Capture one useful buyer question or objection.

### Friday, 30 minutes

1. Update the scorecard.
2. Review channel quality, not just traffic.
3. Record one decision per channel.
4. Add the strongest learning to the content and page backlog.
