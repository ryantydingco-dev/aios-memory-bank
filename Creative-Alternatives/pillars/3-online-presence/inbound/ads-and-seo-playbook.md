# Ads And SEO Playbook

## Objective

Capture high-intent demand, learn how buyers describe their needs, and turn winning search evidence into durable organic authority.

Paid search and SEO use the same page and keyword map. Ads produce faster query evidence; SEO compounds the validated language and buyer problems.

## Search account structure

### Campaign naming

`CA | Search | [Niche/Service] | [Geo] | [Objective]`

Examples:

- `CA | Search | Camps | Northeast | Qualified Lead`
- `CA | Search | Racquet Clubs | US | Qualified Lead`
- `CA | Search | Law Firms | US | Qualified Lead`
- `CA | Search | Brand | US | Navigation`

### Ad-group naming

One intent family per ad group:

- Supplier/bulk
- Managed store
- Specific event or program
- Rush/deadline
- Gifting

Do not mix broad generic merchandise, gifting, store, and niche terms in one ad group.

## Match-type progression

### Research

- Exact and phrase match.
- Manual search-term review multiple times during the first week.
- Build negative keywords immediately.
- Optimize to qualified lead events only after event accuracy is verified.

### Evidence

- Expand phrase themes that produce qualified conversations.
- Test broad match only with reliable conversion values, negative discipline, and enough data.
- Keep brand traffic separate from non-brand acquisition.

### Scale

- Use value-based bidding only when quote, pipeline, customer, and revenue data are accurately returned.
- Split geography only when evidence supports different economics or messaging.
- Do not fragment low-volume niches into too many campaigns.

## Ad-message framework

### Headline roles

- Buyer/niche: `Summer Camp Merchandise`
- Problem: `Camp Apparel Without The Scramble`
- Outcome: `A Better Club Collection`
- Proof: `Family-Operated Since 1999`
- Process: `Digital Proofs In 24-48 Hours`
- CTA: `Plan Your 2027 Collection`

### Description formula

`[Specific products/program] for [specific buyer]. [CA service advantage]. [Concrete next action].`

Example:

> Custom tees, hoodies, staff gear and camp stores, curated by a team that understands seasonal deadlines. Send your logo and plan the next collection.

Do not claim lowest price, guaranteed delivery, free fulfillment, no inventory, revenue share, or exact launch timing until each claim is approved and accurate for that offer.

## Landing-page alignment

- Keyword appears naturally in title, H1, supporting copy, and relevant section headings.
- Page immediately confirms the niche and intent.
- CTA matches the ad promise.
- Proof supports the specific buyer concern.
- Form fields match the requested service.
- Mobile page is fast, readable, and easy to call from.
- No ad routes to the generic homepage when a matching commercial page exists.

## Technical SEO checklist

- One canonical URL per page.
- Resolve `/home` sitemap/canonical mismatch.
- Unique titles and descriptions.
- One descriptive H1.
- Crawlable internal links.
- Descriptive image filenames and alt text.
- Optimized real images without unacceptable visual loss.
- Organization/LocalBusiness details consistent with Business Profile.
- XML sitemap submitted in Search Console.
- No accidental `noindex` or blocked assets.
- HTTPS and preferred-domain redirects remain correct.
- Core Web Vitals monitored; fix user-impacting issues rather than chasing a score alone.
- Structured data validates and matches visible content.

## Organic content rules

Every indexable resource must contain at least two of the following:

- Original CA process knowledge
- Approved customer evidence
- Real product or proof images
- A decision framework, checklist, timeline, or calculator
- Kenny or Maclaine's expert recommendation
- Aggregated CA data approved for publication

Generic rewritten advice is not enough. Thin pages are consolidated or kept non-indexable.

## SEO measurement

Track by page and niche:

- Indexed status
- Impressions
- Clicks
- Query groups
- Organic landing-page sessions
- CTA clicks
- Forms and calls
- Qualified conversations
- Quotes
- Pipeline
- Customers and revenue

Search visibility without qualified pipeline is supporting evidence, not the final outcome.

## Paid-search review cadence

### New test

- Search terms: at least twice in week 1
- Lead quality: every submission
- Budget pacing: weekly
- Landing-page behavior: weekly
- Decision: after enough evidence to diagnose, not after an arbitrary number of days

### Established campaign

- Search terms and negatives: weekly
- Ads and landing page: monthly or after material evidence
- Pipeline and revenue: monthly
- Niche investment: quarterly

## Kill and revise logic

- Clicks but no form activity: page/message/intent review.
- Forms but no qualified leads: keyword and qualification review.
- Qualified leads but no quotes: offer, response, or operational-fit review.
- Quotes but no customers: sales, pricing, proof, and competitive review.
- Customers but poor economics or high operational burden: stop scaling until delivery improves.
- No impressions: verify indexing, volume, geography, and keyword specificity before adding content.
