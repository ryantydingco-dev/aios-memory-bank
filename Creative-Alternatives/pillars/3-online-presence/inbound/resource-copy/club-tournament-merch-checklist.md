## Hero

# A tournament merchandise checklist built around players and the date.

Use this before product selection. It helps a tournament director separate required operations from optional retail and identify the approvals that can delay everything else.

## Event facts

- Tournament name and dates
- Venue and receiving rules
- Player count and divisions
- Staff, volunteers, officials, and coaches
- Sponsors and required marks
- Registration, draw, and awards schedule
- In-hand date and backup destination
- Final merchandise approver

## Audience map

List each group separately:

- Players
- Staff and volunteers
- Officials or coaches
- Sponsors and VIPs
- Spectators and members
- Winners or award recipients

One product does not need to serve every group.

## Merchandise jobs

### Required event operations

- Staff identification
- Volunteer apparel
- Awards and recognition
- Registration or player packages
- Sponsor obligations

### Player experience

- Useful welcome item
- Apparel or accessory tied to the event
- Travel, hydration, or recovery product
- Division or date-specific keepsake

### Optional retail

- Event apparel
- Club-branded pieces
- Limited collection or preorder
- Member and spectator products

Keep optional demand separate from products the tournament must have on site.

## Artwork and sponsor approval

- Collect current club and event marks
- Request usable sponsor files
- Confirm logo hierarchy and placement
- Identify marks that cannot be altered
- Name the authorized approver for each stakeholder
- Set a final sponsor-art cutoff
- Decide what happens if a file arrives late

## Product review

For each item confirm:

- Audience and purpose
- Quantity and size range
- Product color
- Decoration method and location
- Inventory status or substitute rule
- Unit packaging
- Personalization, names, or divisions
- Proof owner
- Delivery carton or kit destination

## Timeline checkpoints

### Early planning

Confirm event facts, audiences, sponsors, merchandise jobs, budget guidance, and venue rules.

### Product direction

Narrow the assortment, confirm inventory assumptions, gather artwork, and identify long-lead risks.

### Proof and quantity lock

Approve artwork, sponsor placement, sizes, quantities, packaging, and delivery labels.

### Production and delivery

Follow the confirmed schedule. Do not treat this checklist as a guaranteed turnaround.

### Post-event review

Record unused products, fast-selling sizes, player feedback, staff needs, sponsor issues, and changes for the next tournament.

## Questions for the venue

- Who may receive shipments?
- What hours are accepted?
- Are advance warehouse or handling fees involved?
- How must cartons be labeled?
- Is there secure storage?
- Who confirms receipt?
- What is the backup destination?

## Common misses

- Sponsor artwork collected too late
- Staff apparel mixed into player cartons
- Awards treated like an ordinary product
- No owner for venue receiving
- Optional retail quantity guessed as required demand
- No substitute rule when inventory changes
