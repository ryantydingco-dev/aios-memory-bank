# Put the club calendar before the product catalog.

Member collections, staff apparel, tournaments, recognition, gifting, and seasonal events should not compete for attention at the last minute. Use this calendar to assign audiences, owners, and ordering models before choosing products.

## Map the club year

List the dates that apply:

- Season opening and closing
- Membership welcome or renewal
- Staff onboarding and uniform refresh
- Tournaments and championships
- Junior or academy programs
- Member-guest and signature events
- Committees, volunteers, and recognition
- Holiday or milestone gifting
- Pro-shop or store collection changes
- Reunions, alumni, or community events

## Separate the audiences

- Members
- Guests
- Staff
- Juniors and families
- Tournament players
- Volunteers and committees
- Sponsors and VIPs
- Pros, coaches, or instructors

An event gift, staff garment, and pro-shop piece may share a brand without sharing the same product or order path.

## Seasonal review cycle

### Before the operating season

- Review last year's best and worst products
- Confirm current marks and colors
- Identify staff and member needs
- Map tournament and event dates
- Choose the initial bulk, store, and event paths

### During the season

- Track member questions and sizes
- Record fast-moving products
- Review staff comfort and durability
- Capture approved event photographs
- Flag reorders before inventory becomes urgent

### Before signature events

- Confirm players, guests, staff, sponsors, and awards
- Lock venue and receiving details
- Gather sponsor artwork
- Separate required merchandise from optional retail

### After the season

- Review sell-through or distribution
- Record unused products
- Confirm recurring staff needs
- Document support burden
- Decide what should return, change, or stop

## Choose an operating model per moment

| Moment | Likely path | Main decision |
|---|---|---|
| Staff apparel | Bulk/reorder | Fit, durability, size and role |
| Member collection | Store or retail | Demand, assortment and support |
| Tournament | Event/bulk | Date, audience, sponsor and packing |
| Welcome gift | Bulk/gifting | Recipient, presentation and cadence |
| Recognition | Bulk/event | Names, awards and final approvals |

The table is a starting point. The supported store and fulfillment model must be confirmed.

## Assortment review

For every proposed product ask:

- Which audience and moment does it serve?
- Would a member choose it without the logo?
- Does quality fit the club?
- Is the brand treatment appropriate?
- Can sizes and colors be supported?
- Is it evergreen, seasonal, or event-specific?
- Who owns questions and reorders?

## Proof and learning loop

After each program capture:

- Products and audience
- Quantity or buyer demand
- Timing and approval issues
- Member or staff feedback
- Support and delivery burden
- Approved photos or quotes
- Reorder date
