## Hero

# Plan event merchandise backward from the in-hand date.

An event date cannot absorb an approval delay. This timeline identifies the work that must happen before production and the details that often surface too late.

Replace each planning window with a schedule confirmed for the actual product, decoration, quantity, destination, and event.

## Start with the event facts

- Event name and date
- Required in-hand date
- Venue and receiving contact
- Attendee count
- Staff, volunteers, speakers, sponsors, or VIPs
- Distribution plan
- Budget guidance
- Final decision owner
- Backup delivery destination

## Early planning window

### Define jobs and audiences

- Attendee welcome
- Staff identification
- Speaker or sponsor gifts
- Booth or activation
- Retreat or activity use
- Awards and recognition
- Optional retail

### Confirm the operating requirements

- One product or several groups?
- Bulk cartons, kits, room drops, or individual shipments?
- Sponsor or client marks?
- Personalization or names?
- Venue handling rules?
- Recipient data or privacy concerns?

## Product-direction window

For every item review:

- Audience and purpose
- Quantity and size range
- Inventory status
- Decoration method
- Product color and brand fit
- Samples if required
- Substitute rule
- Packaging and labeling
- Shipping path

Do not wait for final artwork to surface a product that cannot support the date.

## Artwork and approval window

- Collect current logos
- Confirm sponsor hierarchy
- Identify protected or restricted marks
- Assign legal, brand, client, and event approvals
- Set the date when new sponsors or changes stop
- Confirm final proof owner

Artwork received is not the same as artwork approved.

## Quantity and logistics lock

- Final attendee or recipient count
- Staff and volunteer sizes
- Kit components and inserts
- Carton labels
- Venue receiving hours
- Advance warehouse or handling instructions
- Contact who confirms receipt
- Backup destination and escalation owner

## Production and delivery window

The approved schedule now governs. Monitor:

- Product or inventory changes
- Proof status
- Production status
- Tracking and receiving
- Missing or damaged shipment escalation

This resource does not promise a universal turnaround or delivery result.

## Event-week checklist

- Count and inspect received cartons
- Separate products by audience or activity
- Confirm awards and named pieces
- Keep a small issue log
- Photograph approved finished work
- Record fast-moving or unused products
- Identify reorders only after feasibility review

## Post-event review

Within a practical review window, capture:

- What attendees used or valued
- What remained
- Staff and distribution issues
- Venue or shipping exceptions
- Sponsor feedback
- Support burden
- Products worth repeating
- Approved photos, quote, or case-study permission
