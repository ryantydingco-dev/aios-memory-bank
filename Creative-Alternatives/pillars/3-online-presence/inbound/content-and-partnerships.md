# Content And Partnerships System

## Purpose

Create buyer demand and trust using the work CA already performs. Content is not a separate publishing hobby; it is the reusable evidence layer for search, sales, social, referrals, directories, and partnerships.

## Two content systems

### CA buyer content

Audience: camp directors, club operators, legal marketers, administrators, People Ops, event planners, and merchandise buyers.

Job: demonstrate judgment, process, proof, responsiveness, and the next useful action.

### Ryan build-in-public content

Audience: operators, family-business owners, and AI-curious builders.

Job: document the transformation and create authority. It may assist CA demand but must not be counted as the direct buyer engine without attribution.

## Buyer-content pillars

### 1. Planning and timelines

- Seasonal merchandise calendars
- 90/60/30-day order plans
- Event deadline planning
- Reorder and restock guidance

Conversion role: captures informational search and future demand.

### 2. Product judgment

- What Kenny would choose for a particular buyer and moment
- Good/better/best assortments without publishing unapproved prices
- Product tradeoffs: durability, fit, decoration, shipping, audience
- Useful unusual items beyond generic apparel

Conversion role: proves curation and reduces catalog overload.

### 3. Process proof

- Logo to concept
- Concept to digital proof
- Proof to finished product
- Order rescue and deadline management
- Quality-control checks

Conversion role: demonstrates competence and lowers perceived risk.

### 4. Customer outcomes

- Approved case studies
- Testimonials
- Repeat-order stories
- Store or event program lessons
- Before/after operating improvement

Conversion role: supports page and sales claims.

### 5. Buyer education

- Store vs bulk order
- Print-on-demand vs timed group order
- One-location vs individual shipping
- Self-serve platform vs human-managed service
- How to choose a merchandise partner

Conversion role: frames CA's fit and filters poor-fit leads.

### 6. Brand personality

- `Just Call Kenny`
- Anything on everything
- Tasteful `swag crimes` and industry humor
- Family-business archive
- Kenny and Maclaine product debates

Conversion role: memorability and social distribution. Humor must still connect to proof or a buyer problem.

## One source asset, many uses

For each useful customer-approved project:

1. Capture objective, buyer, audience, products, date, constraints, decision, and result.
2. Capture approved photographs or proofs.
3. Produce one primary article, case study, or video.
4. Produce one LinkedIn post.
5. Produce one visual carousel or short video.
6. Add the proof to the relevant commercial page.
7. Add a sales snippet for email and calls.
8. Add a FAQ or objection answer if the project exposed one.
9. Ask for a review, referral, or case-study approval at the appropriate moment.

## Partnership strategy

The current source-of-truth registry is `config/ca_inbound_partners.yaml`. It contains 20 current official channels, each tied to one resource, a unique UTM source, cost evidence where published, and a decision gate. Generated working files live in `outputs/inbound/implementation/partnership-tracker.csv` and `partnership-scorecard.md`.

No registry entry authorizes outreach or spend. Every entry starts in `research`; Ryan approves any move to contacted, active, or paid.

## Partnership qualification

Prioritize a partner when:

- It reaches a concentrated CA buyer group.
- It has trust with that group.
- CA can provide useful education or proof.
- The relationship can produce referrals, content, directory visibility, or events.
- Terms, lead ownership, and customer experience are acceptable.

Avoid paid placements that provide only impressions without buyer identity, referral evidence, or attribution.

## Distribution rules

- Every asset has one primary buyer and one primary page.
- Repurpose after the source asset is credible; do not create ten shallow versions first.
- Ryan and Maclaine may reshare company content with their own perspective.
- Do not post customer work without permission.
- Do not expose private pricing, margin, order volume, or operational details.
- Update old resources when timelines, services, or operating terms change.
- Use one unique UTM source per approved partner; never combine several organizations under a generic partner link.
- Request current terms and audience evidence again before any paid placement, even when a public price is recorded.

## Content measurement

Track:

- Organic impressions and queries
- Clicks to commercial pages
- CTA clicks and form starts
- Assisted qualified conversations
- Direct qualified conversations
- Sales usage in active deals
- Referrals and partner introductions
- Approved backlinks and directory visits
- Quotes, pipeline, customers, and revenue assisted

Follower growth and views are supporting signals. They do not determine whether a buyer-content program continues.

## Monthly content review

1. Which asset generated qualified activity?
2. Which asset helped a sales conversation or objection?
3. Which niche lacks proof?
4. Which search queries deserve a resource or FAQ?
5. Which customer project should become the next case study?
6. Which low-value content should be updated, consolidated, or stopped?
