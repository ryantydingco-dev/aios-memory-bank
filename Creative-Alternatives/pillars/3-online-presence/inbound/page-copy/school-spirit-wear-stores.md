## Store or bulk order?

### Use a bulk order when

- The school knows the quantity and sizes
- The order is distributed at one location
- A team or event needs coordinated delivery
- The school wants tighter control of the final assortment

### Consider a managed store when

- Families or approved buyers should order directly
- A defined order window fits the school calendar
- The expected demand supports the production model
- Ownership of approvals, questions, and promotion is clear

### Use both when

Staff, event, or team needs are predictable but family purchasing works better through a direct-ordering path.

## Human-managed versus self-serve

Many school-store offers lead with free setup, no inventory, and fast launch. Those can be useful, but they do not answer every school question.

CA's validation angle is human help with product judgment, artwork, deadlines, and unusual needs. The page should not claim that every program is free, inventory-free, or launched on a fixed schedule.

The fit question is simple: does the school want a self-serve print-on-demand tool, or a person helping manage a merchandise program?

## FAQ

### Is the school store free?

Do not promise that universally. Store setup, products, production, payment, shipping, support, and financial terms are confirmed for the specific program.

### Does the school need to hold inventory?

That depends on the approved bulk or store model. CA will explain the production and fulfillment approach before launch.

### Can families order directly?

Potentially through a managed store. Confirm audience access, order window, payment, production, shipping, support, and promotion responsibilities.

### Can CA support staff and athletics too?

Yes, where the garment, decoration, quantity, approval, and delivery requirements fit. These needs may use separate assortments or ordering paths.

### Who approves the school logo and artwork?

The school identifies the authorized approver. CA does not treat a submitted logo as permission to produce it.

### Can CA handle a fast event deadline?

Share the date immediately. A person will review product availability, artwork, approval, production, and shipping before confirming feasibility.

### Is this a fundraiser?

It can be structured as an approved school merchandise program, but pricing, revenue, accounting, and payout terms must be confirmed before any fundraising claim is published.
