## FAQ

### Can CA handle both staff gear and camper merchandise?

Yes. The product and ordering model can separate staff, camper, parent, alumni, or event needs as appropriate.

### Can parents order directly?

CA currently operates online-store programs that allow approved audiences to order directly. Store windows, products, payment, shipping, customer service, and revenue terms are confirmed for each program. `[CONFIRM final wording]`

### Do we need to hold inventory?

That depends on the approved bulk or store model. The goal is to avoid unnecessary inventory while choosing a production method that supports quality and economics.

### How quickly will we receive a proof?

The current CA standard is 24-48 hours after the needed project information and usable artwork are available.

### How long does production take?

The current general guidance is about two weeks after proof approval, but the actual timeline depends on product availability, decoration, quantity, shipping, and season. The confirmed project timeline governs.

### Can CA help with a mid-season restock?

Yes, subject to product availability and a realistic production and shipping review. Call Kenny for urgent feasibility rather than relying on an automated promise.

### Can CA source more than apparel?

Yes. CA's approved public examples include bags, lanyards, trophies, boards, locker nameplates, towels, mats, magnets, glassware, stationery, and more.
