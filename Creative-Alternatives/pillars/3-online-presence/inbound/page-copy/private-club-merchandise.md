## Choose the operating model before the product list

### Bulk order

Best when the club knows the audience, quantity, and distribution plan and wants the order delivered together.

### Managed store

Best when an approved audience needs a defined collection and direct ordering. Store fit depends on expected demand and the supported production and fulfillment model.

### Event program

Best when the date cannot move and products, artwork, approval, packing, and delivery need one coordinated plan.

### Seasonal calendar

Best when membership, tournaments, staff, holidays, and recurring events create several merchandise moments during the year.

## A club collection is not a logo on everything

A useful assortment should answer:

- Who will buy, receive, or wear it?
- Where and when will they use it?
- Which pieces should feel understated or more visible?
- What price and quality range belongs at this club?
- What can be reordered reliably?
- Which items are event-specific rather than evergreen?
- Who owns approval and member questions?

Those decisions create a coherent collection and reduce unnecessary choices.

## FAQ

### Can CA support both a pro shop and club events?

Potentially. Share the current retail model, event calendar, audience, and ownership so CA can recommend one program or separate paths.

### Can members order directly?

CA operates managed online-store programs. The approved audience, products, order window, production, shipping, customer service, and financial terms are confirmed for each store.

### Can CA help with tournament gifts and sponsor items?

Yes. Start with the date, player or guest count, sponsors, presentation, budget guidance, and delivery location. Final feasibility depends on product and approval timing.

### Can we keep our current supplier?

Yes. CA can begin with a specific event, product category, store, or program where another approach would be useful.

### Does CA handle staff apparel?

Yes, subject to the garment, decoration, size, reorder, and operating requirements of the team.

### Can CA ship to individual members?

Potentially through an approved store or fulfillment model. Confirm the recipient count, data handling, production method, support, and shipping economics first.
