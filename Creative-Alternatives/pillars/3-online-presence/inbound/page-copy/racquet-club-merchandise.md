## Club store or bulk order?

### Bulk works well when

- The club needs coordinated team/staff pieces.
- The tournament or event has one deadline.
- The pro shop wants inventory it controls.
- Products require decoration economics that favor a group run.

### A managed store may work when

- Members order individually.
- The club wants an approved assortment without holding all sizes.
- The program uses timed order windows.
- The operating and fulfillment terms fit CA's current store model.

CA can recommend one model or a combination after understanding the club.
