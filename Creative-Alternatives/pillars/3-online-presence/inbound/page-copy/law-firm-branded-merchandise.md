## FAQ

### Can CA work from firm brand guidelines?

Yes. Upload or share the current guidelines and approved logo files. Artwork and placement remain subject to firm approval before production.

### Can CA ship to multiple offices or recipients?

Potentially. Share the destination count, addresses, presentation, timing, and recipient-data requirements so CA can confirm the supported shipping model before quoting.

### Does CA handle premium products and gifts?

Yes, within the available supplier, budget, branding, shipping, and timeline constraints. The goal is a curated recommendation appropriate to the relationship.

### Can CA support a firm retreat or event deadline?

Yes when the product, artwork, approval, production, and delivery path can support the date. Start with the event date and agenda.

### How quickly will the firm receive a proof?

CA's current standard is 24-48 hours after receiving the information and usable artwork required to prepare it. Complex sourcing or approval needs may change the sequence.
