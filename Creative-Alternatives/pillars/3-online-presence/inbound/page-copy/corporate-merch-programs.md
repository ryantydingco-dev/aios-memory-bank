## Right-sized is a capability, not a compromise

Enterprise merchandise platforms may include global inventory, complex procurement, warehouse networks, deep integrations, and software administration. CA should not imply those capabilities when they are not part of the approved program.

The fit is strongest when a team values:

- Direct access to a person
- Curated product direction
- Artwork and production judgment
- A manageable store or recurring calendar
- Help with unusual projects and deadlines
- One partner across bulk, gifting, and events

If the requirement is a global enterprise platform, say so early and avoid forcing the fit.

## FAQ

### Can CA build a company store?

CA operates managed-store programs. Fit depends on the audience, assortment, expected demand, payment, production, shipping, customer service, and ongoing ownership.

### Can CA ship onboarding or gifts to individuals?

Potentially. Confirm recipient count, address handling, packing, inserts, product availability, support, and delivery timing before quoting.

### Can different departments use the same program?

Yes when ownership and approval are clear. Some companies are better served by one shared assortment; others need separate event, gifting, and employee paths.

### Does CA integrate with procurement or HR systems?

No integration should be assumed. Share the required approval, purchasing, security, and data workflow so CA can confirm what is supported.

### Can CA handle a one-time project first?

Yes. A focused event, team order, gifting program, or onboarding batch is a useful way to test fit before building a recurring program.

### Can CA work with strict brand guidelines?

Yes. Provide current files and the authorized approver. Artwork and products remain subject to approval before production.
