### 1. Event and audience

Date, location, attendee count, staff, speakers, sponsors, activities, distribution, and the purpose of the merchandise.

### 2. Product and inventory check

Confirm availability, decoration, samples if needed, quantity, substitutions, and the risk of waiting.

Identify logo files, sponsor marks, names, legal or brand review, and the final decision owner.

### 4. Packing and delivery

Confirm bulk cartons, labeled groups, kits, inserts, venue rules, receiving hours, advance warehouse, hotel, office, or individual shipment.

### 5. Contingency

Document the last responsible approval date, substitute path, delivery owner, and what happens if one input changes.

## What "date-safe" should mean

Date-safe is a planning discipline, not a guarantee.

It means:

- The required in-hand date is captured first
- Product inventory is checked before commitment
- Artwork and approval ownership are explicit
- Production and shipping assumptions are documented
- Venue or recipient constraints are known
- Risks and alternatives are surfaced early

Do not publish a guaranteed-delivery, no-rush-fee, or fixed-turnaround claim without approval for the exact event offer.

## FAQ

### How late is too late?

It depends on the product, inventory, decoration, quantity, artwork, approval, shipping method, and destination. Share the date for a human feasibility review.

### Can CA deliver to a hotel or venue?

Potentially. Provide the receiving contact, address, hours, labels, fees, handling rules, event date, and backup destination before quoting.

### Can CA build attendee kits?

Potentially. Confirm products, assembly, packaging, inserts, labeling, addresses, timing, and data handling first.

### Can CA handle sponsor logos?

Yes when usable files and authorized approvals are available. The event owner remains responsible for confirming permission to use each mark.

### Can products ship to individual attendees?

Potentially through an approved fulfillment plan. Recipient count, address data, privacy, packing, support, shipping economics, and delivery windows must be confirmed.

### Can CA help choose products?

Yes. Share the audience, venue, agenda, budget guidance, distribution, and what the merchandise should accomplish.

### Can an agency use CA behind the scenes?

Potentially. The communication, pricing, concept, approval, support, and client-ownership model must be documented first.
