# Inbound Launch Checklist

## Gate 1 - Business approvals

- [ ] Current location and service area confirmed
- [ ] Store economics and fulfillment responsibilities confirmed
- [ ] Supported order, shipping, and kitting models documented
- [ ] Free-concept policy and capacity approved
- [ ] Response owners and practical SLA approved
- [ ] Public customer names, logos, photos, and testimonials approved
- [ ] Rush language and production claims approved
- [ ] Privacy policy and logo-upload handling approved

Evidence: dated decision note with owner.

## Gate 2 - Access

- [ ] Squarespace editor access
- [ ] Domain/DNS access or owner identified
- [ ] Google Analytics access
- [ ] Search Console access
- [ ] Google Ads access
- [ ] HubSpot admin/property access
- [ ] Call-tracking access or decision not to use it
- [ ] Google Business Profile access

Evidence: owner can open and manage each property; do not record credentials in the repo.

## Gate 3 - Shared data layer

- [ ] HubSpot fields created
- [ ] Company/contact/inquiry/deal model confirmed
- [ ] Lifecycle and qualification statuses created
- [ ] First-touch fields protected from overwrite
- [ ] Latest-touch update logic tested
- [ ] UTM and click-ID persistence tested
- [ ] QuickBooks reconciliation key chosen
- [ ] Spam and no-fit handling tested

Evidence: test record with every required property.

## Gate 4 - Website foundation

- [ ] Page titles and meta descriptions updated
- [ ] `/home` sitemap/canonical issue resolved
- [ ] Organization/LocalBusiness data validated after details confirmed
- [ ] Navigation includes Services, Industries, Work, and Resources
- [ ] Shared commercial-page template approved
- [ ] Shared form approved
- [ ] Phone, email, error, success, and mobile states tested
- [ ] Privacy and upload copy live

Evidence: crawl/export plus mobile and desktop review.

## Gate 5 - Wave 1 page

Repeat for camps, racquet, and law:

- [ ] Unique buyer and job defined
- [ ] Unique H1, offer, CTA, products, timeline, and FAQ
- [ ] Approved proof included
- [ ] Real or approved concept imagery included
- [ ] Matching service links included
- [ ] Matching resource or case study included
- [ ] Form pre-populates niche and offer
- [ ] GA4 events tested
- [ ] HubSpot routing tested
- [ ] Page owner and response owner named
- [ ] Index/canonical state correct

Evidence: completed end-to-end QA scenario.

## Gate 6 - Paid search

- [ ] Matching landing page is live and tested
- [ ] Campaign and ad group naming follows taxonomy
- [ ] Exact/phrase starting terms validated
- [ ] Shared and niche negatives loaded
- [ ] Location settings reviewed
- [ ] Brand campaign separated
- [ ] Raw submission and qualified-lead conversions separated
- [ ] Budget owner and research objective documented
- [ ] Search-term review scheduled
- [ ] Human lead-quality review assigned

Evidence: campaign preview and one test conversion; public launch requires Ryan approval.

## Gate 7 - Organic search

- [ ] Page appears in sitemap
- [ ] Search Console inspection passes
- [ ] Internal links are live
- [ ] Page is not accidentally noindexed
- [ ] Resource and proof pages link back
- [ ] Original content threshold met
- [ ] Index request submitted

Evidence: Search Console URL inspection and sitemap state.

## Gate 8 - Content and partnerships

- [ ] 12-week calendar has owners and publish dates
- [ ] First two original assets approved
- [ ] Proof-capture workflow active
- [ ] Customer permissions logged
- [ ] Partner list prioritized
- [ ] Partner value proposition drafted
- [ ] Every asset has one commercial destination and CTA
- [ ] UTMs created for distribution

Evidence: content briefs and partner tracker.

## Gate 9 - Reporting and operating cadence

- [ ] Executive dashboard
- [ ] Acquisition dashboard
- [ ] Sales queue/dashboard
- [ ] Operations/capacity view
- [ ] Weekly review owner and time
- [ ] Monthly QuickBooks revenue reconciliation
- [ ] Quarterly niche-registry review
- [ ] Decision log for scale, revise, pause, or consolidate

Evidence: first completed weekly review with a documented decision.

## Launch sequence

1. Shared data and website foundation
2. Camps page and funnel
3. Camp research search campaign and first resource
4. Racquet page, proof, and partner distribution
5. Law page, proof, and search test
6. First Wave 2 page chosen from evidence

## Stop conditions

Stop or pause launch when:

- Attribution is missing or incorrect.
- Customer-facing claims are unapproved.
- Form submissions are not routed to a human.
- Capacity cannot support the promised response or delivery.
- Search traffic is materially irrelevant.
- Lead quality is not being reviewed.
- Public customer assets lack permission.
- Privacy or upload handling is unclear.
