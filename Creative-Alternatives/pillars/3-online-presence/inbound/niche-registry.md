## Status definitions

- **Build:** full commercial page, content cluster, paid test, proof program, partnership lane, and nurture.
- **Validate:** one useful page or resource plus a controlled demand test.
- **Shared only:** serve through service pages, local visibility, referrals, and opportunistic outbound; no dedicated investment yet.

## Niche page requirements

Every dedicated niche page needs:

1. A named buyer and job to be done.
2. A niche-specific H1 and supporting promise.
3. The correct service path: bulk, store, gifting, event, or a combination.
4. Products organized around the buyer's use case rather than catalog categories.
5. At least one approved proof element.
6. A useful timeline, checklist, FAQ, or decision framework.
7. A niche-specific CTA and form value.
8. A title, meta description, canonical, internal links, and relevant schema.
9. Tested GA4 and HubSpot attribution.
10. A named human owner and response SLA.

If proof is missing, the page may be drafted but remains non-indexable until it offers enough original expertise to deserve search visibility.

## Quarterly review questions

- Which niches produced qualified conversations?
- Which produced quotes and customers?
- Which produced attractive revenue and acceptable delivery burden?
- Which search terms reveal new problems or adjacent services?
- Which closed customers can become approved case studies or referrals?
- Which pages have impressions but weak conversion?
- Which pages have no demand and should be consolidated?
- Which niche should move up, down, or out of the registry?
