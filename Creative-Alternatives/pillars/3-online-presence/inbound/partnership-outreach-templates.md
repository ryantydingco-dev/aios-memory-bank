# Partnership Outreach Templates

These are internal drafts. Ryan reviews every message before sending. Do not imply membership, endorsement, sponsorship, customer results, category exclusivity, or committed spend.

## Diligence questions before approval

- Is the audience concentrated and relevant?
- Does the program provide education, a useful listing, introductions, or only logo exposure?
- What current evidence supports traffic, attendance, or member use?
- Are direct competitors present or protected?
- Who owns contact consent and follow-up?
- Can every link use a unique UTM?
- What is the complete cost and term?
- What result would cause CA to renew, revise, or stop?

## Attribution rule

Every approved partner receives a unique source and campaign:

```text
utm_source=[registry utm_source]
utm_medium=partner|directory|referral
utm_campaign=2027-[niche]-[offer]-partner-validation
utm_content=[newsletter|directory|session|booth|referral]
```

Do not use one generic partner URL across several organizations.
