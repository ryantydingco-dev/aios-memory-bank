# Content Idea Engine — never face a blank page again

> 2026-07-05. Companion to `content-positioning.md`. "Unlimited" isn't a list — lists run out.
> It's a FORMULA that regenerates weekly, plus a seeded bank of ~80 ideas, plus a rotating calendar.
> The content factory (1:45 drops) and Friday record kit pull from this automatically.

## 1. The formula: FUEL × PILLAR × FORMAT

Every piece of content = one fuel source, shaped by the day's pillar, cut for the platform.

**Six fuel sources (these refill themselves):**
| Fuel | Where it comes from | Example |
|---|---|---|
| F1 · Today's real work | W/L notes, content drops, reply-watcher | "A law firm replied at 9:14. Mockups by 10:05." |
| F2 · A mined pain quote | `data/pain-points-corpus.md` (auto-injected into daily drops) | Buyer: "our vendor takes 5 days to send a proof" → react to it |
| F3 · A landscape stat | `plans/market-landscape-2026.md` | "The #1 promo company keeps only 45% of customers. Here's why." |
| F4 · A Kenny/Maclaine moment | 27 years of stories + reactions to the AI | Kenny sees the proof page for the first time (film it) |
| F5 · A customer artifact | Mockups, proof pages, store dashboards (permission/redacted) | Screen-record the proof page, blur the logo |
| F6 · A contrarian take | Where real complaints contradict industry marketing | "Swag isn't dead. Boring swag is." |

6 fuels × 6 pillars × 3 platforms = 108 combinations, and F1/F2/F4/F5 generate NEW instances every week. That's the unlimited part.

## 3. The rotating 4-week calendar

Weekly skeleton (matches blocks + factory):
- **Mon–Sat 1:45 PM**: factory drop executes the day's pillar (above), fueled by F1/F2 rotation
- **Wed**: buyer-facing post #2 of the week (manual pick from bank if factory's is personal-brand)
- **Fri 10:30**: record episode (record kit auto-arrives 10:00) + clip 5 shorts
- **Sun**: week plan approves next week's angles (factory's 7:05 plan references this bank)

Monthly layer: W1 scoreboard/transparency emphasis · W2 buyer-proof emphasis · W3 Kenny/story emphasis · W4 lesson/thesis emphasis. Quarterly recurring posts (8, 35, 54) refresh with new numbers.

## 4. Wiring (already live)

- Factory (`ca_content_factory.py`) injects 3 rotating pain quotes daily + saves every drop to `outputs/content-drops/`
- Record kit (`record_kit.py`, Fri 10:00 launchd) compiles the week's drops + W/L log + pain quotes into a read-then-record kit
- This bank is the Sunday-planning reference: pick next week's 6 angles in 5 minutes, or let the factory freestyle within pillars
