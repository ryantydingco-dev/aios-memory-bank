# 60-Day Revenue Sprint — All-In Plan

> Created 2026-08-04. Owner: Ryan. Goal: maximum new revenue through Creative Alternatives in 60 days (through ~2026-10-03), full-time capacity, no channel limits.
> **Comp (settled):** Ryan earns half the markup (~16% of revenue) on **any NEW customer he brings in**, once the invoice is paid. Existing-customer revenue = Kenny's money / Ryan's proof-of-value, not commission.
> **Payment-terms rule:** all new customers on deposit-up-front or card-on-file for first orders — protects Kenny AND pulls Ryan's commission inside the sprint window.

## The stack — commission channels first, proof channels second

### 1. Cold email engine — THE COMMISSION ENGINE
Three campaigns via /ca-outbound on the MacBook. Dealthreads domains join the fleet for capacity. DRAFT-approve-send, personalization subagents, reply-watcher triages.
- **C1: Corporate holiday gifting** (launch wk 1) — biggest market, perfect timing.
- **C2: Trade-show exhibitors** (launch wk 2, /ca-tradeshows) — hard deadlines, fast closes.
- **C3: Trigger events** (launch wk 2–3, /ca-openings + /ca-new-gyms + /ca-signals) — new gyms, openings, sponsors, rebrands.
- Weekly: /list-quality-scorecard before upload, /spam-word-checker on copy, kill/scale by reply data (/experiment-design).

### 2. Cold calling — SPEED LAYER
Unlimited hours makes this the differentiator; almost no promo competitor calls.
- Priority order: (a) email repliers same-hour, (b) email openers/clickers, (c) top-of-list cold (trade-show exhibitors + gifting ICP).
- 2–3 hour block daily, 40–60 dials. Script: trigger/occasion → "we do this for [similar org] → can I send you a free mockup?"
- The ask is always the **free mockup**, not a meeting. Mockup = the close accelerant.

### 3. Mockup machine — THE CONVERTER
Every warm conversation gets a mockup within 24h (logo on product, their colors). Runs on MacBook (image-gen keys live there).
- Target: 5–10 mockups/day at peak. Mockup → quote within 24h of any positive signal.

### 4. Content — THE COMPOUNDER (not a cash channel this window)
Document everything; OBS rig is ready. YouTube longform 1x/wk (the build), Shorts/LinkedIn daily clips. Voice per ca-brand-voice.
- Zero extra pipeline: film the work you're already doing.
- Payoff: audience + Dealthreads inbound later. Do NOT let it displace calling/quoting hours; content gets evenings/renders.

### 5. PROOF CHANNELS (Kenny's revenue, Ryan's credibility — timeboxed, never displace commission work)

**5a. Existing-customer holiday cross-sell.** Active 2025–26 corporates/schools pitched on year-end gifting (from `data.db`, Kenny approves list). Kenny/Maclaine voice, art on file = instant mockups. No commission — pure proof-of-value + trains the exact motion used on cold gifting leads. Cap: ~half a day/week to set up as a system Maclaine can run.

**5b. Camp reactivation — BUILD NOW, FIRES IN FALL
.** 803 lapsed customers / $3.58M recent revenue (dedupe + cross-check vs QBO first). Camps buy late fall–spring for summer 2027. Build the automated seasonal sequence in weeks 3–4; arm for Oct–Nov send. Spring proof, not sprint cash. Note: NEW camps landed via cold outbound in the fall window DO pay commission — fold a camps-2027 campaign into the cold engine in Sept.

**5c. A/R chase.** $378K overdue (Camp Becket $59K, Sloane $32K, Shibley $23K…). Draft chase emails for Kenny's approval (/invoice-chase pattern). Kenny's cash, strongest trust-builder — and the cautionary tale behind the new-customer deposit rule.

## Daily rhythm (gym coaching 5:00–9:30 AM; workday starts 10:00)
- **10:00–10:30** Triage: reply-watcher, overnight replies, scoreboard. Flag warm leads for calls.
- **10:30–12:30** Call block #1 (prime answer window). Repliers/openers first. 40–60 dials. Ask = free mockup.
- **12:30–1:00** Lunch (up since 4 AM — take it).
- **1:00–3:00** Maker block: mockups + quotes (24h SLA), then campaign ops (lists, copy, approve sends).
- **3:00–4:30** Call block #2: follow-ups + fresh dials.
- **4:30–6:00** Systems block: build automations/sequences; queue tomorrow's call list. Fridays: /weekly-review instead.
- **6:00** Hard stop. Content editing only on surplus energy — the one block allowed to slip.

**Content capture:** OBS records everything (safe scene — customer-data windows excluded); NO all-day streaming (focus tax + customer PII on screen). Ship: 1 edited weekly episode + daily shorts. Optional: one scheduled weekly "build with me" stream on safe (non-customer-data) work.

## Scoreboard (review every Friday, /weekly-review)
- Sends/wk, reply %, positive replies, calls/dials, mockups sent, quotes out, **orders closed, revenue, Ryan commission earned**, pipeline value.
- Track **new-customer revenue (commission) separately from existing-customer revenue (proof)** — different scoreboards, different purposes.
- Day-30 healthy: 3 campaigns live, 30+ positive replies, 10+ quotes, 2–5 NEW customers closed.
- Day-60 goal: $75–150K new-customer revenue closed (≈$12–24K commission, paid fast via deposit policy) + camp machine armed + 8 episodes shipped.

## Rules
1. Commission work (new-customer outbound, replies, calls, quotes) owns the prime hours; proof work is timeboxed.
2. Every positive signal gets a mockup within 24h.
3. Nothing sends without human approval (drafts only — house rule).
4. Content never displaces revenue hours; it documents them.
5. Dealthreads = inbound only. No agency prospecting during the sprint.
