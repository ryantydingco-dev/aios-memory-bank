**Exit criteria:** interviews done + context files filled · attribution rules written down · warmup running · DataOS answering "who's gone quiet?" with real names.

### PHASE 2 — Cold engine live, mockup-first (Weeks 5-8)

- **Launch cold on PROVEN segments, best weapon first:** Camps (10.1% reply — achieved WITHOUT the mockup) → Squash (5.2%) → then the tiered swag verticals (law/accounting/medical from the ranked lists). Lead every sequence with the mockup image — show their store/gear before pitching anything.
- **Run the AAA cold system verbatim:** 3-email sequence structure (problem + free resource + soft CTA → case study → three pains-as-assumptions + referral ask), internal-camo subject lines ("missed calls", "appointment enquiry" style — never value-prop subjects), 4 T's for personalized one-offs. Volume discipline: 30 cold/account/day, ramp 10-15 → 30, A/B at ≥250 sends/variant, verify every list (bounce <3%).
- **Benchmarks to manage by (AAA):** reply ≥2%, positive-reply ≥30% of replies, meeting-booked ≥30% of positive. Troubleshoot top-down: opens (deliverability/subject) → replies (relevance/offer) → positive (offer/list quality). Ignore open rate as a KPI.
- **Sales motion on calls:** Otto's 5-question discovery (biggest problem → cost → duration → 6-12-month do-nothing future → magic wand) → proposal as **Reality 1 vs Reality 2** built from their own numbers. For store deals, the close is easy (free, zero-risk, they earn); for corporate bulk, price with Kenny in the loop — always.
- **Content compounding:** weekly episode (the cold-engine build IS the content) + daily LinkedIn; track reply-rate lift on prospects who engaged with content vs. cold-cold — the flywheel proof.
- **AIOS layer 3:** IntelOS + CommandOS (Telegram) + **Daily Brief for Kenny** — the morning "here's your business" message. This is the trust moment that makes everything else easier, and it's Episode material.

**Exit criteria:** 2+ cold campaigns at benchmark · 10+ conversations/week across engines · first cold-sourced store or bulk order signed.

### PHASE 3 — Scale what pays, systematize the rest (Weeks 9-13)

- **Double down by the scoreboard:** whichever engine is producing Ryan-attributed revenue gets the tokens/hours. Re-test fitness ONLY as a wedge experiment (mockup + camps-grade copy — the 0.2% flop was an invalid test of weak copy, per the GTM plan).

- **Lock the economics:** with a quarter of DataOS history, compute real LTV per segment → set CAC ceilings → decide scale-up per vertical from the swag lists.
- **Systematize:** winning sequence → template; reactivation → quarterly cadence automation; referral ask baked into every delivered order (AAA: ask after ANY positive interaction). Task-audit round 2 — target 20-30% task automation by day 90.
- **Write the case study** (AAA consulting playbook): "27-year word-of-mouth business + AIOS = $X originated in 90 days" — this document is simultaneously CA marketing, Episode content, and the proof asset for everything Ryan does after.

**Exit criteria:** repeatable playbook per engine · seasonal store calendar live · case study drafted · day-90 numbers on the scoreboard.

## 3. The math (Ryan's 15% of originated revenue; CA margins 30-40% support it)

90-day originated-revenue scenarios — deliberately conservative, custom orders land faster than store GMV:

| Scenario | Originated in 90 days | Ryan @ 15% | What it takes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Base | $50K | $7.5K | Reactivation alone: ~15-20 dormant accounts back at ~$2.5-3K avg |
| Expected | $100-150K | $15-22.5K | Reactivation + 2-4 cold-sourced bulk orders + 2-3 stores signed |
| Strong | $250K+ | $37.5K+ | Cold engine hits benchmark on 2+ verticals + a whale account |

The 90-day number matters less than the **run-rate built by day 90**: three engines live means months 4-12 compound (stores reorder seasonally, reactivated accounts re-enter cycles, content inbound starts landing). $500K-1M originated in year one — $75-150K to Ryan — is the realistic aim IF the machine gets built and fed consistently. The #1 failure mode (per AAA, verbatim): inconsistent outreach. The machine doesn't sell itself.

## 5. Standing risks / honest caveats

- **Warmup tax gates Phase 2.** If the 71 inboxes are cold, cold email can't safely send until ~week 3-4. Start the clock in Week 1 or lose a month.
- **Kenny's trust is the critical path**, not the tech. Every phase front-loads a visible win for him (reactivated old customers → hours saved → the Daily Brief). Nothing ships outward without approval.
- **Single-machine risk:** InfraOS (Git/GitHub) on day one; the Mac Studio is the engine, not the only copy.
- **Ryan's capacity** (Drata + this): the AIOS exists to make one person run three engines. If something slips, drop content cadence first, cold second — never reactivation follow-through.
- **Numbers marked here are targets, not fabrications** — no invented results go in any customer-facing or public material (house rule).
