# Camp Inbound Experiment Brief - 2027 Demand Capture

> Created 2026-07-10. Owner: Ryan. First niche: summer camps. Second niche after the first validation cycle: squash/racquet clubs.
>
> Goal: build the inbound asset now, while the 2026 camp season is active, so it can earn authority, collect search data, secure industry placements, and capture buyers planning for 2027.

## Executive decision

Build one reusable niche inbound system, beginning with camps. Do not launch full ad, SEO, and content programs for many niches at once.

The reusable system has two layers:

1. **Shared infrastructure:** analytics, conversion events, HubSpot routing, form logic, mockup workflow, testimonial blocks, retargeting audiences, reporting, and page template.
2. **Niche layer:** buyer language, offer, products, proof, keywords, content, seasonality, and landing-page copy.

The launch order is:

1. **Summer camps:** highest verified revenue concentration and LTV; best historical outbound response.
2. **Squash/racquet clubs:** second-largest verified revenue concentration; strong proof and founder credibility.
3. **One earned expansion niche:** selected from actual performance and customer economics, not from a desire to cover the market.

## Why build camps now

July is late for most planned 2026 camp orders, but early for building 2027 demand.

- SEO pages need time to be crawled, indexed, linked, and improved.
- The active season produces the best raw material: product photos, rush stories, customer feedback, reorder patterns, and merchandising lessons.
- Camp directors can be interviewed while this year's problems are fresh.
- Industry directories, partnerships, and 2027 conference placements can be secured before the next buying window.
- A small search campaign can collect real query and conversion data before budgets scale.

The camp page will support two paths:

- **Primary:** Plan 2027 camp merchandise.
- **Secondary:** Need a rush reorder or in-season restock now?

## Verified strategic basis

- Camps represent approximately 41% of recorded revenue and about $20.8k average customer LTV.
- Squash/racquet organizations represent approximately 19% of recorded revenue.
- Camps and squash together represent approximately 60% of recorded revenue.

- Tees, sweatshirts, and other apparel tops are the center of CA's current product mix.
- CA has approved public proof: family-operated since 1999, 2,700+ customers, 75,000+ orders, and 24-48 hour digital proofs.

### Budget rule

Use July and August in research mode. The objective is to learn search terms, click quality, page conversion behavior, and lead quality.

Do not set a CAC target until the following are confirmed:

- Gross margin or contribution margin by order type
- Qualified-lead-to-quote rate
- Quote-to-customer close rate
- First-year camp revenue or contribution
- Online-store economics

When those are known:

`allowable cost per qualified lead = allowable camp CAC x lead-to-customer close rate`

Scale budget only after qualified conversations and pipeline are attributed correctly.

### Week 3 - Lead delivery system

### Week 4 - Search research launch

- Validate keywords in Keyword Planner.
- Launch exact and phrase campaigns at a controlled research budget.
- Review search terms and negatives at least twice during the first week.
- Confirm every lead is visible in HubSpot with source and niche.
- Do not optimize to form volume before lead quality is reviewed.

### Week 5 - Content and partnerships

- Publish the 2027 Camp Merchandise Planning Calendar.
- Create the first case study or proof story.
- Apply to the highest-fit directory or association opportunity.
- Repurpose both assets into buyer-facing social content.

### Week 6 - Review and decide

- Review search terms, page behavior, inquiries, qualified conversations, quotes, pipeline, and content engagement.
- Fix the largest conversion or qualification failure.
- Decide whether to continue research mode, scale, or pause paid search until the stronger seasonal window.
- Lock the camp-page template and clone its infrastructure for squash.

## Niche expansion rule

Build many niche pages only after the shared system works. A niche earns a full inbound engine when it has:

1. Verified revenue, LTV, or strategic value.
2. At least three credible customer examples or one strong case study.
3. A distinct recurring buyer problem and offer.
4. Search intent that can be validated in Keyword Planner or Search Console.
5. A repeatable delivery model CA can fulfill without creating operational pain.
6. A named owner for lead response and follow-up.

### Expansion tiers

**Tier 1 - Build full engine**

- Summer camps
- Squash/racquet clubs

**Tier 2 - Validate after camps and squash**

- Other member-based athletic or private clubs
- Schools and academies
- Law firms, if current customer economics and repeat-order behavior support a distinct offer

**Tier 3 - Do not build yet**

- Generic corporate swag
- Fitness/CrossFit as a broad category
- Real estate, accounting, consulting, agencies, and other outbound lists without verified inbound economics

Tier 2 starts with one page and one controlled demand test. It does not receive a full content cluster or large ad budget until it produces qualified conversations.

## Definition of done

The first experiment is complete when:

- The camp landing page is live, indexed, and conversion-tested.
- Every inquiry is attributed and routed into HubSpot.
- The personalized concept workflow has an owner, SLA, and human approval step.
- Paid search has produced a usable search-term and lead-quality dataset.
- At least two original camp content assets are live.
- At least one relevant association or directory placement has been pursued.
- The six-week review produces a documented scale, revise, or pause decision.
- The shared template is ready for the squash version without rebuilding tracking or CRM logic.
