## Offer ladder (every prospect walks up it)

1. **Free personalized mockup** (lead magnet — proven wedge, hits the industry's #1 buyer pain: slow quotes/mockups)
2. **First order, date-guaranteed** (risk-reversal: on-time or 10% off — 4imprint's instinct at family scale)
3. **Seasonal reorder program** (we call YOU in January/September — proactivity is the industry's #5 pain and nobody's system does it)
4. **Managed store / merch program** (the annuity; the mid-market gap SwagUp priced out and big distributors ignore)
