## Day-zero verdict: REALISTIC-STRETCH

The stack closes on paper at **$800K to $1.05M**, but only if the two warm levers perform. Pure new-logo cold outbound alone would be UNREALISTIC: at observed rates it caps around $75K to $150K over the whole window. The warm base carries this plan, and that's fine, because the warm base is real and mostly untouched.

Reference points that keep this honest: CA did $2.65M income in 2025 (QBO, verified). The 2024 new-customer cohort (307 accounts) produced $1.22M in year-one revenue through the old word-of-mouth motion. We're asking a deliberate engine to produce $1M in half that time. Hard, not fantasy.

## Blockers that invalidate the math (fix in Week 1, Sept 1–5)

Listed first because none of the funnel table matters if these stand.

1. **Kenny is the single pricing gate.** In July, 6 campaigns and 18,728 sends produced 161 replies and the pipeline jammed at the quote stage. Maclaine must own quoting end-to-end, with a price book or Kenny-approved margin bands, by Sept 5. This is the one conversation that unlocks every line in the table.
2. **Reply triage has no owner.** 71 replies once sat untouched. The daily power list fixes this with a hard SLA (triaged by 10am, zero carryover). Ryan owns it.
3. **Gmail migration is stalled** on Kenny's mailbox password (35GB import paused since 8/20). Finish it, then stop letting ops projects eat selling hours.
4. **Miller Johnson order value unconfirmed** ($2,600 vs "2,600 water bottles"). Confirm with Maclaine so the observed close economics are right.

Not a blocker but tracked in parallel: **$378K overdue A/R** is cash recovery, not revenue. Invoice-chase runs as a background system (already in `gtd/next-actions.md`), never counted toward the $1M.

2. Salesfinity-assisted human cold calling.

Track each channel separately. Do not add paid ads, automated LinkedIn sending, HubSpot, or another cold channel without Ryan explicitly changing the plan.

## Tripwires (numbers and dates attached)

- **Warm-rejection tripwire (Oct 15):** if 25+ reorder-rescue accounts have passed at honest pricing by Oct 15, STOP adding volume to that lane. Run the no-sales as interviews ("what would have made this a yes?") and fix the offer. Volume doesn't fix an offer your warmest people passed on.
- **Store tripwire (Oct 31):** fewer than 4 stores live by Oct 31 means the store line comes out of the math and the plan re-forecasts to ~$850K, with the shortfall named to Kenny rather than hidden.
- **Midpoint channel kill (Nov 30):** rank all five channels by cost-per-conversation (hours + spend per quote-stage conversation). Kill the worst outright. Reallocate the hours to camps and stores, which peak after this date anyway.
- **Speed SLAs, every day:** positive reply gets a human follow-up within 24 hours. Mockup or store preview goes out same day it's promised. A plan without SLAs leaks its own pipeline, and July proved it.
- **Re-forecast checkpoint (Feb 1):** whatever the cumulative number is on Feb 1, publish it, compare to the $775K line, and set the final-month push accordingly. No quiet goal-moving.

## What this plan deliberately does NOT do

- No fake urgency. Every deadline in the messaging is real: gifting ship-by dates, camp order windows, the store's school-year calendar.
- No new channels until the midpoint kill. LinkedIn content and YouTube keep running as compounders on surplus hours, never displacing the power list.
- No counting A/R recovery, and no counting Kenny's word-of-mouth reorders that would have happened anyway. If attribution is fuzzy on an account, flag it `[CONFIRM]` and settle it with Kenny that week, per the attribution rule the 90-day plan called for.
