## Scripts and templates

### Reorder rescue call script

Use for current/past customers who bought in 2025 but not yet in 2026.

Script:

"Hi [First Name], it's Maclaine from Creative Alternatives. Did I catch you for a quick second?

I was looking through our 2025 orders and saw we helped with [account/order if known] last year. I wanted to check before the season gets away from everyone: are you already set for this year's gear, or should we help you pull together the reorder?"

If they say they are not set:

"Perfect. I can make this easy. Tell me what you need this year: same items as last time, or do you want a few fresh ideas?"

If they say maybe later:

"Totally. When does it usually become urgent on your side? I can put a reminder in and send a few options before you're in a scramble."

If they say they have another vendor:

"No problem. If anything gets tight or you want a second option, we can still turn proofs quickly. Want me to send over a few ideas just in case?"
