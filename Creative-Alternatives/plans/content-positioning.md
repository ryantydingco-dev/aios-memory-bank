## The split

- **Ryan (personal)** = the character. Build-in-public operator modernizing a 27-year family business. Never "AI guru."
- **CA (buyer brand)** = the proof. Product-forward, mockup-forward, for law/financial admins + camp/club directors.
- **Kenny** = the heart. 25 years of relationship selling meets AI — the most watchable asset we have. Maclaine = the trusted operator voice.

## LinkedIn — the revenue platform

- **Why first:** buyers live here AND it warms the exact names SmartLead/Sendr are hitting (flywheel: feed-familiar prospects open cold email warm). Track reply-rate lift on engaged-vs-cold prospects.
- **Ryan's feed (5x/wk, factory-drafted):** build-in-public specifics — what broke, what it cost, the number that moved. Anti-fabrication rule stands.
- **Buyer-facing (1–2x/wk):** pure proof. Mockup reveals, "replied 9:14 → saw their logo on product 10:05," store screenshots (with permission). Free-mockup CTA lives ONLY on these.
- **Engagement:** comments on ICP posts > cold DMs. DM only after engagement.

## YouTube — the trust engine

- **Position: a documentary SERIES, not a tips channel.** "Using AI to transform my girlfriend's dad's $3.2M promo business." Episodes 2–6 already scripted (pillar 4).
- **Job #1: B2B due diligence.** Prospects google CA before replying — they must find real faces, real shop, real stakes. Kills the "risky small vendor" objection.
- **Cadence:** 1 episode/wk from the Friday block. Structure episodes around real tension: hot lead caught, Kenny reacts to the proof page, first originated order, the artist-loop fix.
- **Later, not now:** searchable how-to/SMB-AI-playbook content — harvest only after the series builds a library.

## TikTok / Reels / Shorts — the awareness feeder

- **Position: visual payoffs.** Logo → AI → product reveal in 15s (demos the literal offer). Day-slices ("5AM coach gym class / 10:30AM run a $3M business with AI"). Kenny reaction clips.
- **Hard rule: zero dedicated production.** 5/wk, all clipped from Friday long-form + phone captures. ICP skews LinkedIn/YouTube; short-form is top-of-funnel equity + future optionality.

## The one-story engine (solo-operator sustainable)

Real work → phone captures + W/L notes → Friday episode → 5 clips → daily LinkedIn drafts (factory) → buyer proof assets.
**Capture, don't create.** One habit, four outputs. Honest nightly W/L replies = better next-day drafts.

## Rules

1. Ryan is the character, CA is the proof, Kenny is the heart.
2. Show the artifact first (mockup, reply screenshot w/ permission, dashboard); the lesson emerges from it.
3. Never fabricate; customer names only with approval (house rules).
4. Platform-native: LinkedIn text+image · YouTube narrative · shorts = payoff in 3 seconds.
5. Scoreboard: content is judged by pipeline — replies mentioning content, inbound DMs, engaged-prospect reply lift. Views are not a KPI.
