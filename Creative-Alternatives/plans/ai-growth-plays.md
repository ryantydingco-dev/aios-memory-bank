# AI Growth Plays — Creative Alternatives

> Ideas to (1) bring in leads/revenue, (2) make ops efficient, (3) document it all as content.
> Ryan's mandate inside CA: become the in-house **"AI guy"** *and* **drive revenue**.
> Design rule: every play does double duty — it moves revenue AND becomes a video. The work and the marketing are the same motion.

## Revenue / lead-gen plays — the priority

1. **AI store-mockup first touch.** For every prospect, auto-generate a branded store mockup (their logo on real products) and *lead* the outreach with it. Show, don't pitch — the single biggest reply-rate lever for the web-store offer. → Content: *"I send prospects a mockup of their store before they even reply."*
2. **AI outbound engine at scale.** Apollo + enrichment + AI per-lead personalization on the winning segments (camps, clubs, schools, breweries, gyms, youth sports, franchises). The stack already exists; AI writes the personalization. → Content: *"Building an AI outbound machine for a 25-year-old business."*
3. **QuickBooks reactivation engine.** Maclaine's books hold every past customer + order. AI finds dormant and reorder-due accounts and drafts personalized win-back outreach. Fastest revenue — existing relationships, zero cold start. → Content: *"I found $X in dormant revenue hiding in their QuickBooks."*
4. **Niche inbound pages + local SEO.** CA has almost no web presence. AI spins up segment landing pages ("branded stores for summer camps," "for breweries," …) + a Google Business Profile. Inbound that compounds while you sleep. → Content: *"Giving a $3M word-of-mouth business its first website that actually sells."*
5. **Referral system.** The business already runs on word-of-mouth — systematize it. AI + QuickBooks flags happy/repeat customers → templated referral asks at the right moment.

## Efficiency plays — trust + capacity + content (confirm in the audit)

6. **The "daily brief" (steal the muse's mechanic).** Pipe QuickBooks + email + orders into one place; Claude answers *"what's overdue, who hasn't reordered, what shipped today."* Mirrors Chuck the Contractor's "eye of Sauron" — and films beautifully.
7. **AI quote/order drafting.** Likely the biggest time sink. AI drafts quotes from a request + supplier catalogs (Maclaine confirms the real flow).
8. **Auto follow-ups.** Proofs, approvals, invoices — auto-drafted from email/QuickBooks, human-approved.

## Content engine — post for a year → "the AI guy"

- **Flagship:** the YouTube build-in-public series (Episode 1 ready). One long video/week = the build.
- **Repurpose:** cut each episode into shorts (LinkedIn, IG, TikTok, X) — the `linkedin-*` + content skills already do this. One shoot → 10+ posts.
- **Two audiences, on purpose:** (a) CA buyers — camps/clubs/orgs, *"look what we make"* → leads; (b) the AI-for-business crowd → Ryan's operator brand (its own monetization later).
- **Every play above = a case study = content + a sales asset.**
