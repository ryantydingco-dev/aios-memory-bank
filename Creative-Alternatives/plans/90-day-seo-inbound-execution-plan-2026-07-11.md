# Creative Alternatives 90-Day SEO + Inbound Execution Plan

> **For Hermes:** Use subagent-driven-development skill to implement this plan task-by-task.

**Goal:** Build and launch a measurable organic acquisition system that turns nonbrand search, local discovery, referrals, and earned links into qualified promotional-products opportunities.

**Architecture:** One strong broad-market website foundation supports solution hubs, evidence-backed industry spokes, original decision resources, case studies, and local/partner distribution. Every page routes into one shared human-managed project brief and every opportunity is attributed through HubSpot to qualified lead, quote, customer, and QuickBooks-reconciled revenue.

**Tech Stack:** Squarespace, Google Search Console, GA4, Google Tag Manager, Google Business Profile, HubSpot, QuickBooks, Google Ads Keyword Planner, PageSpeed Insights, Rich Results Test, Screaming Frog (or equivalent crawler), Looker Studio or the existing CA metrics scripts, Canva, Google Drive, Hermes/Claude Code/Codex, Smartlead for outbound-assisted distribution.

---

## Set-in-stone strategic rules

1. **North Star:** qualified organic/inbound opportunities, quotes, customers, and gross profit—not traffic or form fills.
2. **Primary position:** **Corporate-ready branded merchandise, managed by a real person.**
3. **Primary conversion:** **Get My Curated Shortlist.**
4. **Primary market structure:** broad solution hubs plus selective evidence-backed vertical pages.
5. **No thin-page scale:** no product × industry × city permutations, raw supplier descriptions, or doorway pages.
6. **No page without proof:** every indexable spoke needs one real proof artifact and must answer at least three questions better than its parent hub.
7. **No unsupported promises:** deadlines, response times, fulfillment, errors, quality, reviews, customer counts, and pricing claims require evidence.
8. **One accountable owner:** every inbound lead has a named human owner and response expectation.
9. **Outbound supports inbound:** cold email distributes useful resources, creates branded search, and identifies page opportunities.
10. **Weekly publish/distribute/measure loop:** every week ships one meaningful asset, distributes it, and reviews qualified outcomes.

## Earned-link machine

The goal is relevant authority and referral traffic, not raw backlink count.

### Linkable assets

- Original planning timelines
- Budget/quantity frameworks
- Company-store decision guide
- Decoration/artwork guide
- Deadline checklist
- Original case studies
- Approved product/process photography
- Annual/seasonal buyer guides based on real CA knowledge

### Outreach target groups

1. Existing customers with resource/vendor pages
2. Suppliers and decorators with distributor/customer showcases
3. Event planners and venues
4. HR/People consultants and agencies
5. Camp, racquet, school, club, and association directories
6. Chambers and legitimate local organizations
7. Industry newsletters and podcasts
8. Adjacent vendors: printers, sign companies, booth designers, photographers, brand agencies
9. Educational resources with outdated or broken links
10. Journalists/bloggers covering events, HR, gifting, schools, sports, or small business

### Outreach rules

- Offer a genuinely useful resource or expert contribution.
- Personalize around the target’s audience and existing content.
- Never buy bulk links, use private blog networks, exchange at scale, or publish AI guest-post spam.
- Track target, contact, pitch, asset, response, placement URL, referral traffic, and resulting inquiries.
- A nofollow association/newsletter link with qualified referral traffic can be more valuable than an irrelevant high-metric link.
