# First 30 Days — Unified Operating Plan

**Window:** July 29–August 27, 2026
**Scope:** account-based outbound setup, three-person LinkedIn/content workflow, and backend modernization planning/pilots.
**Boundary:** no outreach, publishing, campaign activation, production integration change, QuickBooks write, deployment, commit, or push is authorized by this plan.

## Day-30 outcome

By day 30, Creative Alternatives has:

- One evidence-selected outbound segment and a reviewable 20-account cohort.
- A dry-run three-channel account cadence with working stop/suppression logic.
- A sustainable LinkedIn workflow with distinct Ryan, Kenny, and Maclaine voices and a measured weekly time cost.
- A validated open-order exception view and an offline quote-intake pilot.
- A human-approved decision on the first controlled commercial and operational pilots.

The detailed task tracker is `operating-system/trackers/30-day-execution.csv`.

## Week 1 — Decide and baseline

### Outbound

- Complete the segment scorecard with current timing, reachable-account, proof, and fulfillment evidence.
- Ryan marks exactly one segment `selected` or records why the decision remains blocked.
- Define the chosen segment's buyer-role hypothesis, account fit tiers, proof set, stop threshold, and decision date.
- Do not source or enroll a campaign cohort before selection.

### Content

- Confirm whether Kenny and Maclaine have/want personal LinkedIn profiles; define the CA-page fallback.
- Confirm public boundaries for customer names, logos, numbers, screenshots, and operational stories.
- Hold one 30-minute capture session: one real event or lesson for each person.
- Approve the three voice cards in `linkedin-content-engine.md`.

### Operations

- Inventory which current collectors/reports run here and which depend on credentials, machine permissions, or live accounts.
- Check freshness of the QuickBooks, open-order, and campaign data used by current reports.
- Select ten representative orders and three historical quote/order threads.
- Establish the four baselines: response time, order quality, margin evidence, and capacity.

### Week-1 decision review

- One selected segment, or a named evidence blocker.
- Content permission matrix understood.
- Sources marked current, stale, unavailable, or `[CONFIRM]`.
- No external action taken.

## Week 2 — Build the review surfaces

### Outbound

- Research 20 accounts in the selected segment.
- Complete account plans with why-now, buyer roles, proof relevance, and all suppression checks.
- Human-review each account; no contact data is exported to a sending tool.
- Draft the segment-specific email, LinkedIn, and call components from existing supporting copy.

### Content

- Draft one Ryan build post, one Ryan decision/lesson post, one Kenny founder note, and one Maclaine buyer/ops post.
- Each draft points to a source event and a proof/permission state.
- Review the set in 30 minutes; remove anything generic or duplicative.
- Store as drafts only unless separately approved for publication.

### Operations

- Run or reconstruct the existing read-only open-order reporting path.
- Compare ten normalized records against source evidence.
- Record duplicates, stale states, missing keys, and false exceptions.
- Produce an operator view with the top five exceptions, owner, next action, source, and freshness.

### Week-2 decision review

- At least 10 of 20 accounts are ready for a dry run.
- Four content drafts feel distinct and credible.
- Open-order view passes or has a bounded repair list.

## Week 3 — Dry-run and offline pilot

### Outbound

- Build ten account-level cadence plans without sending.
- Simulate: reply on email, opt-out on LinkedIn, referral on a call, active-customer discovery, and duplicate ownership.
- Verify the system stops or reroutes other touches correctly.
- QA facts, tone, account relevance, mockup promises, and human ownership.

### Content

- Capture new events during real work.
- Build the next one-week queue using only events that occurred.
- Measure total human time from capture through approval.
- Keep the output under the two-hour weekly cap.

### Operations

- Run the quote-intake assistant offline on three historical orders.
- Compare extracted packets to original source threads and final order evidence.
- Have Kenny/Maclaine label missing fields, wrong assumptions, and useful outputs.
- Measure draft time and correction rate.

### Week-3 decision review

- Dry run has no unresolved suppression or ownership defect.
- Content workflow fits the time cap.
- Quote-intake pilot has an evidence-backed expand/revise/stop recommendation.

## Week 4 — Readiness decisions

### Outbound controlled-pilot proposal

Write a one-page decision note:

- Selected segment and why now.
- Cohort size and account approval count.
- Channel cadence and owner.
- Reply SLA and quote handoff.
- Deliverability and suppression checks.
- Stop thresholds and review date.

Choose `go`, `revise`, or `stop`. `Go` means “request explicit authorization”; it does not start outreach.

### Content operating lock

- Keep only the voice/format combinations that are authentic and sustainable.
- Build a four-week event-driven editorial shell with no invented topics.
- Record which profile/page each voice would use and the approval owner.
- Choose a minimum viable cadence; no daily quota.

### Operations production-pilot proposal

Choose one validated candidate and write:

- User and pain.
- Inputs and source-of-truth hierarchy.
- Baseline and success threshold.
- Builder/verifier behavior.
- Human approval points.
- Failure, rollback, and data-retention behavior.

Request explicit approval before connecting or modifying any production integration.
