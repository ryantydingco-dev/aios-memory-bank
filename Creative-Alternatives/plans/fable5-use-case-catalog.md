## Pillar 1 — Operations (Kenny's hours back)

1. ★ **QuickBooks reconciliation assistant** — scripted matching for the mechanical 90%, AI flags only judgment calls. Attacks the 8-hr days directly. (Plan exists: `pillars/1-operations/quickbooks-reconciliation-ai-workflow.md`.)
2. ★ **Kenny's morning brief** — one daily artifact: orders in production, quotes outstanding, proofs unapproved, invoices unpaid, hot replies. The "executive quick win" every AAA coach says converts founders.
3. **Kenny inbox triage** — twice-daily sweep of the AOL-era inbox: folderize vendor confirmations/receipts (feeds reconciliation), pre-draft replies to inquiries, surface only action items. (Erik's "$12k email system.")
4. ★ **Quote drafter** — inquiry email → draft quote by matching the 75k-order history ("this school ordered these hoodies in 2023 at $X"). Human-approved, in Kenny's voice.
5. **Quote upgrade** — rebuild CA's quote from invoice-style to branded proposal: mockup rendered in, similar-org photos, testimonial, reorder terms, toggleable add-ons (rush, kitting, extra logo). Direct multiplier on close rate.
6. ★ **Margin mining on 75k orders** — quote-vs-actual analysis: where does CA systematically underprice (shipping, rush, setups, vendor drift)? 1 recovered point ≈ $32k/yr. Plus the hamburger/fries product-line analysis.
7. **Order-status board agent** — reply-watcher pattern pointed at the order pipeline: quote → proof → production → delivered → invoiced, with a `last-check` cursor. The antidote to "the business lives in Kenny's head."
8. **Proof-chaser** — auto-drafted nudges for unapproved proofs and unanswered quotes (day-3/day-7, never auto-sent).
9. **AR collections runner** — execute the existing `ar-collections-plan.md` as drafts + a weekly aging report.
10. **Deposit-sets-the-date policy** — instrument "no production slot until proof approval + deposit" into the order board.
11. **Vendor price-drift checker** — compare supplier catalogs/portals to CA's standing quotes each month.
12. **SOP extraction / succession insurance** — record Kenny walkthroughs, transcripts → SOP library. 27 years out of his head into files. (Also Episode material.)
13. ★ **The ops audit itself** — run the Morningside 2-phase sprint on CA: Otto's 5 questions with Kenny (recorded), Maclaine first, U-shape, Two Realities doc, power-grid deliverable. Fills `ops-discovery.md` with real numbers.
14. **4-number ROI scoreboard** — every build scored (cash saved, FTE×2, trouble avoided, opportunity enabled); the running total is the internal trust artifact AND the YouTube scoreboard.
15. **Month-end close skill** — "run month-end": reconcile, flag gaps, P&L narrative deck in CA's format.
16. **Per-customer folder brains** — top-50 accounts get folders (emails, orders, art history); an agent watches tone changes = churn-risk detector.
