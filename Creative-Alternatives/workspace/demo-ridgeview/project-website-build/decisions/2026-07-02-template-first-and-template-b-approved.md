# Decision: Template-first approach confirmed; template option B selected

**Date:** 2026-07-02

## Decision
Rachel confirmed the website build will use a template-first approach (not
fully custom). Of the three shortlisted templates, she chose **option B**
— the one with the large case-study section.

## Context
We'd sent Rachel a shortlist of 3 templates for approval and were waiting on
her to confirm whether she wanted template-first or fully custom (this had
been an open "[waiting]" item since kickoff). She reviewed the options on the
evening of 2026-07-02 and replied same night.

## Reasoning
- Rachel wants the big case-study section from option B — likely because
  case studies are a key trust/credibility element for her marketing
  consultancy audience.
- Going template-first (per the engagement's default approach, see
  `../CLAUDE.md`) keeps cost and timeline down; Rachel didn't push back on
  this, so no need to scope custom work beyond template limitations.

## Follow-on
Her same email added a new request — a "book a call" button in the header,
based on a bad experience with her last site burying that CTA. Tracked as a
new Active task, not part of this decision but worth noting as the reasoning
behind it (lead generation, learned from a past mistake).
