# Strategy — Creative Alternatives transformation

## The goal

Turn a 27-year, word-of-mouth, $3.2M custom-branding business into an **AI-run, marketed, modern operation** — and document the transformation publicly on YouTube. Make CA more efficient, win more customers, give it a real online presence, and turn the whole journey into a content engine.

## Channel north star (resolved 2026-06-23)

**The channel is the build-in-public story of turning CA into a modern, AI-run business. CA is the hero; Ryan is the operator showing how.** Decision: Ryan is **building CA** (equity + upside in one real business), NOT building an AI agency for other companies. The audience the channel builds is **optionality** — monetize it later, his way (product / course / sponsorship / selectively helping a few), decided in 6–12 months from strength. No agency grind.

**Two content buckets — keep them straight (this caused the confusion):**
- **Ryan's channel** — build-in-public; audience = business owners / AI-curious. Builds **brand + audience + optionality.** Does NOT directly get CA print customers (wrong audience).
- **CA's own marketing** — its site/social aimed at *its* buyers (squash clubs, schools, camps). Part of getting CA found.

**Where CA customers actually come from:** outbound (the AI store-mockup wedge) + CA's niche/social presence + referrals + word of mouth. The personal channel is the brand/optionality play, never the CA customer engine.

**Why there's no conflict:** the AI work Ryan does for each video *is* real value created in CA (more profit, more efficient, more valuable company). The content just documents value he's creating anyway — so the channel and CA never compete for his time.

## Current focus

**Pillar 1 — Operations.** Map how CA actually runs, then automate the biggest time-sinks. Everything else waits on this because you can't transform what you haven't mapped, and you can't safely scale customer volume onto a manual back office.

## Sequencing (why this order)

1. **Operations first.** Map → quantify time leaks → automate the top 2–3. Earns Kenny's trust, frees his time, and produces the first hard "AI saved X hours" proof. → Episode 1.
2. **Customer acquisition second.** Once the back office can absorb volume, scale the camp/club outbound that already works (10.1% on summer camps). Add a reactivation motion for past accounts.
3. **Online presence, in parallel from ~week 3.** Stand up the site, Google Business Profile, and a basic social cadence. Pairs with the YouTube push.
4. **YouTube, from day one.** Every pillar above is filmed. The build *is* the show.

## 90-day shape (draft — refine after the ops audit)

- **Weeks 1–2:** Ops audit with Kenny/Maclaine. Map workflows, quantify time, pick the first automation. Film Episode 1.
- **Weeks 3–5:** Build the first 1–2 ops automations. Stand up online presence basics. Channel goes live.
- **Weeks 6–9:** Scale Pillar 2 outbound on the winning segments. Reactivate dormant accounts.
- **Weeks 10–13:** Compound — second wave of automations, content cadence locked, first inbound from presence/YouTube.

## Goals confirmed with Kenny (interview 2026-07-12)

1. **Efficiency first** — invoice chasing (~$167K open AR) and "getting a handle on the entire system" are the named pains. Whatever can be automated, automate.
2. **Succession** — Kenny is starting to step away; Maclaine + Ryan take over. Systems must capture what's in his head.
3. **$1M of new revenue brought in by Maclaine + Ryan** over the next 6–12 months, beyond the existing camps/squash base, via the client-acquisition machine.

## What success looks like

- **Ops:** a named number of hours/week given back to Kenny, with the workflow documented.
- **Acquisition:** a repeatable engine landing new web-store logos, not one-off campaigns.
- **Presence:** CA is findable, credible, and posting; first inbound leads attributed to it.
- **YouTube:** a published series with a real audience, feeding attention back to CA and to Ryan's operator brand.

## Guardrails

- Nothing ships that makes Kenny's day harder.
- Customer/vendor/money actions are human-approved.
- Public content respects what Kenny approves showing (`[CONFIRM]` the boundaries early).

## Relationship to Ryan's other ventures

The consulting work (Dealthreads, Oloxa, the GTM engine) is **paused** in favor of this focus. The engine built there (outbound, enrichment, content systems) is reused here — that's why this workspace was seeded with it.
