CA has two offers. Keep them straight — they serve different goals.

What Kenny has sold for 25 years: **branded merchandise and promotional products, sourced and fulfilled for organizations.** Customer wants logo'd gear; CA handles design, supplier selection, production, and delivery. Won on relationships and reliability.

- **Buyer:** orgs Kenny knows or gets referred to.
- **Motion:** word-of-mouth, repeat, referral.
- **Margin model:** `[CONFIRM]` — markup on sourced goods.
- **Growth lever here:** retention, reorder, referral systems (Pillar 1 ops + Pillar 2 reactivation), not cold outbound.

## Offer 2 — Branded online stores (the growth wedge)

The productized offer Ryan + Maclaine run **outbound** for:

- **Buyer:** camps, clubs, schools, teams, member-based orgs (see `audience.md`).

- **Proof:** Farm & Forge Club (live), Summer Camps campaign at 10.1% reply.
- **Motion:** cold outbound (Origami → SmartLead → Sendr.io → Salesfinity) + warm referral.
- **This is the offer to scale in Pillar 2.**

## Why this structure matters

- **Pillar 1 (ops)** protects and scales Offer 1 — make the core machine efficient so it can carry more volume.
- **Pillar 2 (acquisition)** scales Offer 2 — the repeatable, outbound-able wedge for new logos.
- The two compound: web-store customers become long-term promo-distribution accounts.
