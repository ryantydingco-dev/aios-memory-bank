# Audience — who Creative Alternatives sells to

CA sells branded merchandise and promotional products to **organizations that need their logo on things** for their members, staff, events, or stores. The branded-web-store offer fits any org with a recurring base of people who buy branded gear.

## What makes a good-fit org

- Has a **recurring audience** that wants branded gear (members, fans, parents, staff).
- Runs events, seasons, or programs that drive merch demand.
- Lacks the time/skill to run a store themselves → CA's "we handle everything, you earn a share" lands.

## Read on the best wedge

Camps and clubs (seasonal, member-based, parent-funded) outperform corporate/SaaS in the data. The branded-store offer's "zero inventory risk, you earn 10–12%" is most compelling to orgs that want the upside but have no ops capacity. Lead Pillar 2 with the camp/club playbook that already works.
