# Daily Power List

> The six countable actions that move the $1M plan (`../plans/1m-6-month-game-plan.md`), every working day, Sept 2026 through Feb 2027.
> Full KPI definitions, channel calibration, and recovery rules: `../pillars/2-customer-acquisition/revenue-operations/revenue-acquisition-os.md`.
> Rule: the power list gets done before any unrelated build project, ops work, or content.

---

## The six

| # | Action | Green | Yellow | Red |
|---|--------|-------|--------|-----|
| 1 | **Replies triaged by 10am.** Every SmartLead + Maclaine-inbox reply sorted (hot / warm / not-now / dead), hot ones answered. Zero carryover to tomorrow. | 100% triaged by 10am | Done by noon | Any reply older than 24h untouched |
| 2 | **15 warm first-touches.** Reorder rescue, dormant ≥$10K, gifting, camp, or store accounts. Personal and grounded in their real history or buying moment. | 15+ | 10–14 | ≤9 |
| 3 | **25 follow-ups** on open quotes, mockups, store previews, and active opportunities. Every follow-up adds something; never "just bumping this." | 25+ | 15–24 | ≤14 |
| 4 | **1 store pitch OR 1 same-day mockup delivered.** Whichever the pipeline needs. A promised mockup that slips to tomorrow is an automatic red. | 1+ delivered | Pitched but not delivered | Nothing out, or a promise slipped |
| 5 | **High-volume cold acquisition.** Two Salesfinity blocks totaling 3 hours; 300 dials or 25 live conversations; 3 qualified next steps; 25 LinkedIn buyer touches with max 15 new requests; SmartLead controlled. | Call stop condition + 3 next steps; 25+ LI touches | 200–299 dials or 15–24 live conversations; 15–24 LI touches | Under 200 dials and under 15 live conversations; under 15 LI touches; or reply aged |
| 6 | **Scoreboard updated + tomorrow queue.** Log outcomes and prepare 15 warm names, 25 follow-ups, one conversion asset, 400 Salesfinity-ready records, and 25 LinkedIn touches. | Complete same day | Complete next morning | Missing or open opportunities lack next actions |

Three reds in a week means something structural is broken. Name it at Friday's review and fix the system, not the effort.

## SLAs (from the game plan, non-negotiable)

- Positive reply → human follow-up within 24 hours.
- Mockup or store preview → same day it's promised.

## Cold-outreach block

The only cold channels are SmartLead email, Salesfinity-assisted human cold calls, and human LinkedIn DMs. Record each separately. Salesfinity starts at 2–3 parallel lines; raise concurrency only after the first 10 working days are reviewed. Grokbot prepares research and drafts; Ryan sends and handles live calls.

## Daily shape

- **By 10am:** #1 done. Hot replies answered first, everything else sorted.
- **10:20–11:50:** first Salesfinity block.
- **11:50–1:10:** warm touches and first follow-ups.
- **1:40–2:40:** LinkedIn revenue block.
- **2:40–4:10:** second Salesfinity block.
- **4:10–4:45:** remaining follow-ups and the conversion move in #4.
- **Shutdown:** #6. Log the colors honestly and build tomorrow's queue.
- **Friday:** power list runs light (triage + follow-ups only), then the weekly scoreboard row in the game plan + `/review`.
