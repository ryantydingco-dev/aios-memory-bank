# Clip Harvest Workflow

The recording is raw material. Do not publish a full unreviewed workday by default.

## During Recording

Create clean transcript anchors by saying:

- `CLIP MARKER: The lesson is...`
- `DECISION: I am choosing...`
- `FAILURE: This did not work because...`
- `EVIDENCE: The number actually shows...`
- `RESULT: What we built is...`

Pause for one second before and after the clean explanation.

## After Recording

1. Save the full local recording.
2. Save or screenshot the artifact.
3. Generate or export the transcript.
4. Search for the five marker phrases.
5. Review thirty seconds before and after each marker.
6. Select one long-form story and three to five short clips.
7. Remove private data, dead time, repeated troubleshooting, and unsupported claims.

## Long-Form Story

Use this structure:

1. The problem and stakes.
2. The initial plan.
3. The key build decisions.
4. The obstacle or failed assumption.
5. The artifact or result.
6. The honest verdict.
7. The next mission.

Target: 8-15 minutes.

## Short-Clip Types

Harvest a mix:

- **Contrarian belief:** "The scripts were the reason I was not recording."
- **Decision:** "Here is why I chose the boring workflow."
- **Failure:** "We built the wrong thing for three weeks."
- **Reveal:** "This is the artifact that came out of the session."
- **Framework:** "Every useful agent needs an owner, trigger, exception, and approval gate."
- **Personal stake:** "I am building this after a 9-to-5 because..."

Target: 20-90 seconds. Give every clip a complete thought; do not rely on viewers knowing the stream context.

## File Naming

```text
S001-full-build-session-YYYY-MM-DD.mp4
S001-transcript.txt
S001-artifact-name.png
S001-longform-v1.mp4
S001-clip-01-title.mp4
```

## Review Gate

Before anything publishes:

- No customer or prospect personal information.
- No credentials, financial records, or private communications.
- Numerical claims match the evidence.
- The clip has enough context to be honest.
- The title describes what happened without exaggeration.
- Ryan explicitly approves the final export.
