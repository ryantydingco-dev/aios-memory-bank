# Daily Recording System

Created: 2026-06-28

Goal:

> Record at least one video every day around the $3M promotional products AI transformation.

This system prevents the daily question: "What should I record today?"

---

## The Rule

Every day has one anchor video.

Minimum viable video:

- 45-90 second short if time is tight.
- 6-10 minute YouTube/video podcast style if there is a real artifact.
- No day ends without one published or recorded asset.

The content engine can be messy. The streak matters.

## The Daily Menu

Each day, choose one of three:

1. **Proof Video** - show a system, artifact, dashboard, prompt, workflow, or before/after.
2. **Story Video** - explain what is happening inside the $3M business and what you are learning.
3. **Pattern Remix** - take a proven Meedro/1of10 hook and translate it into the promo-products AI transformation.

Default priority:

1. Proof
2. Story
3. Pattern remix

## Weekly Programming Grid

### Monday - Case Study Update

Theme: where the business is now and what gets built this week.

Examples:

- Week 1 Rebuilding a $3M Business With AI
- The AI System I Am Installing This Week
- What I Found Inside a $3M Business With No GTM System

### Tuesday - Revenue System

Theme: lead generation, buyer research, customer reactivation, quote follow-up.

Examples:

- The Follow-Up System I Would Build Before Spending $1 on Ads
- Turning Old Customers Into New Revenue With AI
- How I Would Find 500 Corporate Buyers With AI

### Wednesday - Operations System

Theme: dashboards, SOPs, order tracking, internal workflows.

Examples:

- QuickBooks Is Not an Operating System
- The Dashboard I Would Build Before Hiring Anyone
- Turning Owner Memory Into SOPs With AI

### Thursday - Tool Test

Theme: one AI tool applied to one real business workflow.

Examples:

- I Used Codex to Build a Business Workflow Instead of an App
- Claude Code vs Codex for Small Business Ops
- Firecrawl for Promo Products Buyer Research

### Friday - Operator Journey

Theme: 9-to-5 transition, account manager skill transfer, lessons from building after work.

Examples:

- I Work a 9-to-5. After Work I Build AI Systems for a Real Business
- Why Account Managers Might Be Perfect AI Operators
- The Difference Between Playing With AI and Becoming Useful With AI

### Saturday - 1of10 / Meedro Remix

Theme: take a proven outlier pattern and translate it into the case study.

Examples:

- I Copied a Viral AI Hook and Used It on a Real $3M Business
- What This Viral AI Video Gets Wrong About Real Businesses
- The Pattern Behind Viral AI Business Videos

### Sunday - Scorecard / Reflection

Theme: weekly recap, wins, stuck points, next build.

Examples:

- Week 1 Scorecard: Rebuilding a $3M Business With AI
- What Worked, What Failed, What I Am Building Next
- The AI Operator Lessons From This Week

## 20-Minute Prep Workflow

Use this before recording:

1. Pick today’s title from the queue.
2. Identify one visible artifact.
3. Write the cold open in one sentence.
4. Write the business verdict.
5. Check privacy.
6. Record.

## Recording Card Format

```text
Title:

Hook:

One idea:

Artifact to show:

Cold open:

Beat 1 - situation:
Beat 2 - problem:
Beat 3 - AI bet:
Beat 4 - artifact:
Beat 5 - verdict:
Beat 6 - next step:

CTA:

Privacy check:
- no customer names
- no vendor names
- no private financial data
- no live QuickBooks/customer records
```

## Meedro / 1of10 Role

Meedro and 1of10 do not decide the channel. They provide packaging intelligence.

Use them to answer:

- What hook structure is working?
- What title pattern is working?
- What emotional angle is working?
- What thumbnail contrast is working?
- What pacing/script structure is working?

Then translate into the fixed niche:

> Ryan is rebuilding a real $3M promotional products business with AI after work.

## Daily Non-Negotiables

- One video recorded every day.
- One artifact or story point per video.
- One clear business verdict.
- One CTA: follow along.
- Save the lesson into the memory bank.

## Emergency Low-Time Videos

Use these when there is no time:

1. "What I am building tonight inside the $3M business."
2. "The biggest system gap I noticed today."
3. "One AI workflow I would install before hiring anyone."
4. "One thing QuickBooks does not solve for this business."
5. "Why I would not start with a chatbot."

If you only have 10 minutes, record one of those as a short.
