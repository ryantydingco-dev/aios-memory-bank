## VIDEO 1 - THE OPENER (record this first)

**Title options:**

- I Am Using AI to Rebuild a $3M Promotional Products Business
- This $3M Business Has Revenue But No Systems. I Am Fixing It With AI
- I Work a 9-to-5. After Work I Am Building an AI Operating System for a Real Business

**Belief shift:**

Wrong: "AI is mostly prompts, chatbots, and content hacks."

New: "AI becomes valuable when you install it into real revenue and operations workflows."

**Format:** talking head + simple screen-share.

### Demo Plan

Have ready to screen-share:

1. A privacy-safe business map.
2. A current-state workflow diagram: QuickBooks, customers, quotes, orders, follow-up.
3. A first 5 systems roadmap.
4. A simple scorecard: revenue impact vs ease of implementation.

Do not show private customer data, financial details, vendor info, employee info, or live QuickBooks screens unless fully sanitized.

### Script

**Cold open**

This is a real $3M promotional products business. It has customers, revenue, and years of relationship equity. But the systems are basically not there. No real go-to-market engine. No modern ops dashboard. QuickBooks is doing most of the heavy lifting. I work a 9-to-5 in tech, and after work I am going to use AI to rebuild this business from the inside.

**Who I am**

I am Ryan. I am an account manager in tech, and I am trying to transition from employee to operator. My girlfriend and her dad run this promotional products business, and instead of making generic AI content, I am going to document what happens when you install AI into a real business that already makes money.

**The bet**

The bet is simple: AI should help this business find more leads, follow up better, reactivate past customers, organize quotes and orders, create SOPs, and make the team faster. Not someday. Not in theory. In actual workflows.

**Screen-share proof tour**

Here is the current-state map. QuickBooks is here. Customer follow-up is here. Lead generation is basically missing. Ops tracking is too manual. These are the first five systems I think matter: customer reactivation, quote follow-up, buyer research, ops dashboard, and SOP capture.

**What this channel is**

Every video, I am going to build one piece of this AI operating system and show the artifact on screen. If it works, you will see it. If it fails, you will see that too. The goal is not to look like an AI guru. The goal is to become useful inside a real business.

**CTA**

Follow along if you want to watch me rebuild a real $3M traditional business with AI after work.

### Packaging
