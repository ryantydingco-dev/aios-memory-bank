# Cron Jobs — 90-Day YouTube Sprint

Created in Hermes. Delivery: origin/current chat.

## Daily Jobs

1. **7:00 AM — Morning Content Mission**
   - Chooses today's video idea, hook, artifact, outline, and sprint plan.

2. **12:00 PM — Midday Build Artifact Sprint**
   - Defines proof artifact and gives Claude/Codex/Hermes prompts.

3. **5:00 PM — Recording Push**
   - Gives a direct recording script and rough 10-minute structure.

4. **8:00 PM — Publish and Repurpose Pack**
   - Gives title bank, thumbnail ideas, description, Shorts, LinkedIn posts, X thread, comments.

5. **10:30 PM — Daily Review and Tomorrow Seed**
   - Captures scorecard, lesson, and next follow-up.

## Weekly Jobs

6. **Monday 8:00 AM — Weekly Ideabrowser Mining**
   - Gives 7 daily video ideas and weekly theme.

7. **Sunday 6:00 PM — Sunday Analytics Review**
   - Reviews performance and extracts lessons/signals.

## Principle

The cron jobs should remove blank-page syndrome. Ryan still needs to do the only human parts:

- choose
- record
- inject real opinion
- publish
- sell
