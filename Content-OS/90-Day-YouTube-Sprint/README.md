## Creator Wedge

Ryan is documenting the transition from 9-to-5 tech account manager to AI operator by rebuilding a real $3M promotional products business with AI.

> I am installing AI into a traditional family business that already makes money but has almost no go-to-market or operating systems. QuickBooks is the main system. I am building the missing revenue and ops machine in public.

**Audience:** AI-curious operators, SMB owners, family businesses, tech/sales/account managers who want to use AI to become business builders.

**Money game:** Trust and proof first. Future monetization can become templates, community, implementation services, consulting, partnerships, or a broader AI operator offer.

## Sprint Goal

Publish build-in-public videos for 90 days around one clear story:

> Can I use AI to help a real $3M promotional products business generate more revenue and operate more efficiently?

The win is not just views. The win is becoming associated with practical AI implementation inside real traditional businesses.

Each video should ladder to the story:

- one real business problem
- one AI-assisted workflow or system
- one visible artifact on screen
- one honest result, lesson, or next step
- CTA: follow along while the business is rebuilt in public

## Daily Loop

1. **Find the leverage point** - lead gen, follow-up, quoting, customer reactivation, ops, reporting, SOPs, or tool setup.
2. **Build one artifact** - dashboard, prompt, lead list, SOP, automation, script, template, or before/after workflow.
3. **Record** - rough but publishable 8-15 minute YouTube video or short-form anchor.
4. **Repurpose** - cut shorts, draft LinkedIn/X, save the best hook for Meedro/VidIQ feedback.
5. **Review** - capture one metric, one lesson, and one follow-up idea.

## Default Video Structure

Follow `Teaching-Style.md` for delivery, but the story spine is now the $3M business transformation.

Hybrid structure:

1. **Cold open** - show the business problem or artifact first.
2. **The situation** - "$3M revenue, real customers, but no modern GTM/ops system."
3. **The AI bet** - what workflow you think AI can improve.
4. **Screen-share proof** - show the workflow, dashboard, prompt, list, or SOP being built.
5. **Business verdict** - what this could change, what is still missing, what might fail.
6. **CTA** - follow along as this gets installed after work.

Keep it raw and specific. Do not make generic AI tool reviews.

- one specific business problem

Do not wait for cinematic editing. Ship the proof.
