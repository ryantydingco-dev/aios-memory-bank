# Distribution & Trust Engine

Updated: 2026-06-28

How content across YouTube, LinkedIn, TikTok, Instagram, and Shorts builds trust around the $3M promotional products AI transformation.

---

## The Trust Mechanism

> Ryan is not teaching AI from theory. He is documenting AI implementation inside a real business with real revenue, real constraints, and real workflows.

That is the trust edge. Most AI creators show tools. Ryan shows what happens when AI gets installed into a traditional business that already makes money but lacks go-to-market and operating systems.

## The Core Story

Ryan works a 9-to-5 in tech as an account manager. After work, he is helping rebuild a $3M promotional products business run by his girlfriend and her dad.

The business has:

- revenue
- customers
- relationship equity
- QuickBooks

The business lacks:

- a modern go-to-market engine
- consistent lead generation
- systematic customer reactivation
- quote and follow-up discipline
- ops dashboards
- documented SOPs
- AI-assisted workflows

Every platform should point back to this story.

## Platform Jobs

### YouTube - Trust And Depth

Long-form build-in-public episodes. Show the business problem, the artifact, the system logic, and the result.

Best formats:

- case study episodes
- screen-share workflow builds
- weekly transformation logs
- "what I installed this week" recaps

CTA:

> Follow along while I rebuild this business with AI.

### LinkedIn - Operator Credibility

Text posts and carousels for business-minded people. Use LinkedIn to explain the business logic: revenue leaks, follow-up gaps, workflow maps, customer reactivation, and operator lessons.

Best formats:

- trench logs
- before/after workflow breakdowns
- "what most SMBs miss" posts
- lessons from applying account manager skills to AI implementation

CTA:

> Follow the build.

### TikTok / Reels / Shorts - Discovery

Short videos with one sharp hook and one practical observation.

Best formats:

- "This business does $3M but..."
- "I would not start with a chatbot..."
- "QuickBooks is not an operating system..."
- "The easiest revenue leak might be old customers..."

CTA:

> Follow for the rebuild.

### Instagram Stories - BTS And Personality

Raw daily check-ins:

- after-work build sessions
- sanitized screenshots
- whiteboard maps
- wins and stuck points
- "what I am building tonight"

CTA:

> Follow along.

## Repurposing Engine

The atomic unit is one real business action.

```
ONE BUSINESS ACTION
   -> one YouTube or anchor video
   -> three to five short clips
   -> one LinkedIn/X post
   -> three to five IG story beats
   -> one memory bank note for the next idea cycle
```

Examples:

- Build quote follow-up tracker.
- Map current customer reactivation process.
- Research 100 possible corporate buyers.
- Create first ops dashboard.
- Write first SOP from a messy workflow.
- Compare Codex vs Claude Code for one business automation.

## Trust Ladder

```
DISCOVER - Shorts/Reels/TikTok hook
   -> TRUST - YouTube/LinkedIn proof and depth
   -> FOLLOW - audience tracks the transformation
   -> CAPTURE - template/prompt/checklist when useful
   -> CONVERT - future template library, systems sprint, cohort, or consulting
```

Do not rush conversion before proof. The first conversion is attention and trust.

## Rules

1. Every content piece must connect to a real business problem.
2. Every tool mention must answer: what workflow did this improve?
3. Show artifacts, not private data.
4. Admit uncertainty and avoid unearned results claims.
5. Protect the family business. Blur or anonymize customers, vendors, finances, and employee details.
6. The goal is to become the AI operator people trust for real SMB implementation.
