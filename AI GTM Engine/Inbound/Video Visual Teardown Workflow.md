# Video Visual Teardown Workflow

## Purpose
When Ryan sends creators/videos to study content style, do not rely only on transcript if visuals matter.

## What Hermes Can Analyze
- Transcript and spoken structure
- Sampled video frames / contact sheets
- Screen-share layouts
- captions/text overlays
- pacing cues from scene changes
- thumbnail/title if fetched separately
- visible production patterns

## Visual Analysis Method
1. Fetch transcript with timestamps.
2. Use `yt-dlp` to download a short relevant clip or sampled sections.
3. Use `ffmpeg` to sample frames every 3–10 seconds.
4. Create a contact sheet.
5. Analyze with vision.
6. Combine transcript + visuals into content lessons.

## What To Look For
- First 5-second visual hook
- Text/headline on screen
- Face cam placement
- screen-share credibility/proof
- switching between blog/docs/terminal/social proof
- pacing and pattern interrupts
- visible receipts/benchmarks
- simple visual hierarchy
- whether the visuals make abstract ideas concrete

## Ryan Takeaway
For Ryan's content, use visual proof from real workflows:
- AIOS dashboard
- HubSpot tasks
- lead CSV before/after
- Loom/demo snippets
- blurred buyer/workflow examples
- Obsidian notes
- screen shares of systems being built

Transcript teaches what they say. Frames teach why it feels credible and watchable.
