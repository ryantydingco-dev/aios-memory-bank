# Content Grader

Use this before publishing Ryan content.

## Scorecard

Score each 1–10.

- Hook: does the first line make someone stop?
- Clarity: is there one clear idea?
- Specificity: is there a real workflow, story, screenshot, call, or example?
- Ryan Voice: does it sound casual, useful, lightly funny, and non-guru?
- Usefulness: does the reader learn something practical?
- Trust: does it avoid overclaiming, fake results, and weird side-business energy?
- CTA: is there only one soft CTA or none?

## Minimum Bar
Do not publish below 8/10 unless it is intentionally casual/low-stakes.

## Rewrite Checklist
- Cut throat-clearing.
- Make vague AI claims concrete.
- Add one real example.
- Shorten paragraphs.
- Remove corporate/guru language.
- Make the first 2 lines stronger.
