## Honest Take
The video is partly hype/affiliate-style AI cash-flow content, but the core mental model is right: Hermes gets valuable when you stop treating it like a chatbot and start treating it like an employee you onboard into workflows.

However, do not assume Hermes automatically creates perfect reusable skills after every task. In practice, the winning pattern is:
1. Run a workflow manually.
2. Notice repeated steps.
3. Turn the process into a skill/SOP/script.
4. Reuse it on future batches.
5. Patch the skill when it breaks.

That is the real compounding loop.
