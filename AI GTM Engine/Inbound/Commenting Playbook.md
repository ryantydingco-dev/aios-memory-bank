# Commenting Playbook

## Purpose
Use thoughtful comments to create visibility with buyers, learn the market, and warm outbound touches.

## Daily Target
10 thoughtful comments/day.

## Where To Comment
- commercial finance broker posts
- specialist lender posts
- bridging/development finance posts
- NACFB posts
- broker award/event posts
- deal closing posts
- hiring posts from brokerages/lenders
- posts about market speed, lender appetite, documentation, underwriting, completions

## Comment Style
Sound curious and useful. Do not pitch.

## Comment Templates

### On Deal Closing Posts
```text
Congrats on getting this over the line. Curious — on deals like this, is the bigger timeline risk usually lender appetite/decisioning, or getting the borrower file clean enough before submission?
```

### On Speed / Fast Funding Posts
```text
The speed piece is interesting. I keep coming back to how much of fast funding depends on clean intake before the lender even starts reviewing.
```

### On Lender Panel Posts
```text
The lender panel piece is underrated. More options are great, but I imagine the checklist variation creates a lot of behind-the-scenes process drag too.
```

### On Hiring Posts
```text
Interesting role. I’ve been studying where commercial finance teams add support as volume grows — seems like borrower docs, checklist follow-up, and file prep are often the hidden capacity sinks.
```

### On Compliance / Process Posts
```text
The process side of this is fascinating. The more complex the product/lender mix, the more important the file quality seems before anything gets submitted.
```

## Comment-to-Outbound Bridge
If Ryan/Sway comments on a prospect's post, outbound should reference the interaction lightly:

```text
Saw/commented on your post about [topic]. The part that stood out was [specific point]. It got me thinking about the doc intake/checklist side before lender submission...
```

## Rules
- No links in comments unless asked.
- No direct pitch in first comment.
- No generic "great post."
- Ask smart workflow questions.
- Save useful buyer language into GTM Brain.
