# Channel Strategy

## Recommendation
Start with LinkedIn as the primary channel. Use YouTube for deeper authority. Use TikTok/Instagram/Shorts as repurposed reach, not the core GTM motion.

## Why LinkedIn Wins First
- Existing lead signals are already from LinkedIn.
- Commercial finance brokers and lenders post closings, funding updates, hiring, awards, market commentary, and lender-panel content there.
- Ryan/Sway can interact with the same people they are prospecting.
- Content can make outbound feel warmer: prospects see Ryan's POV before or after the touch.
- It is safer and more aligned with Ryan's professional context than hypey short-form.

## LinkedIn Roles
### Ryan Personal Profile
Best for:
- build-in-public lessons
- AI GTM system lessons
- commercial finance workflow observations
- founder/operator reflections
- soft Oloxa education

Tone:
- practical
- curious
- learning in public
- no fake guru energy

### Oloxa Company Page
Best for:
- product education
- use-case posts
- document-intake content
- proof/case studies when ready
- credibility assets

Tone:
- more focused and professional
- less personal

## YouTube Role
Use YouTube as a credibility library.

Recommended series:
1. Building Oloxa's AI GTM Engine
2. How AI can automate document intake workflows
3. Signal-based lead generation breakdowns
4. Commercial finance workflow teardown series

Cadence:
- one 6–12 minute video every 2 weeks at first
- cut each into 3–5 short clips

## TikTok / Reels / Shorts Role
Use as reach and repurposing, not primary buyer acquisition.

Best topics:
- AI systems lessons
- cold outbound lessons
- "job title is not a lead" takes
- boring AI workflows
- founder building-in-public snippets

Avoid:
- pretending to be a commercial finance guru
- over-explaining Oloxa to a cold consumer audience
- chasing viral jokes that do not build buyer trust

## Comment Strategy
Comments may be more important than posting at first.

Daily target:
- 10 thoughtful comments/day on posts from brokers, lenders, industry bodies, NACFB-connected people, specialist finance operators, and commercial finance influencers.

Comment types:
1. Add a useful workflow observation.
2. Ask a smart question about process.
3. Reference lender-ready package / intake bottleneck.
4. Congratulate a closing while asking about speed/process in a non-salesy way.

Bad comments:
- pitch Oloxa directly
- generic "great post"
- AI hype
- obvious sales bait

## Buyer/Community Targets To Monitor
- NACFB and UK commercial finance ecosystem

- development finance brokers
- specialist lending operators
- private lenders / commercial mortgage brokers
- SBA/commercial lending brokers in US
- equipment finance/factoring later

## Channel Scorecard
Track weekly:
- posts published
- comments left
- profile views
- connection requests/inbounds
- DMs started
- buyer comments
- content-generated meetings
