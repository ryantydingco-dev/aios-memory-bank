# Lead Scoring Rubric

## Purpose
Prioritize the leads most likely to book qualified Oloxa meetings. This rubric should prevent us from confusing a big list with a good list.

## Score Components
Use 1–10 for each score.

### Fit Score
Measures how closely the company/person matches Oloxa ICP.

#### 9–10
- commercial finance / CRE debt / commercial mortgage / development / bridge / SBA / equipment / factoring workflow
- broker/advisor/intermediary model
- founder/owner/partner/commercial broker/ops leader reachable
- clear multi-lender or document-heavy workflow
- company size 1–500

#### 6–8
- lending/mortgage/finance fit but commercial workflow needs verification
- senior practitioner reachable
- some product complexity

#### 3–5
- residential-heavy, generic loan officer, or real estate-adjacent with unclear finance workflow

#### 1–2
- real estate-only, consumer-only, bank too large/generic, or no plausible Oloxa use case

### Behavioral Score
Measures whether there is a reason now.

#### 9–10
- explicit PAIN
- HIRING for processor/admin/ops
- recent CLOSING with commercial/document complexity
- strong VOLUME plus strong fit

#### 6–8
- CLOSING/VOLUME/MOVE combo
- good deal activity but less direct pain
- clear speed/complexity promise

#### 3–5
- MOVE only
- older/weak activity
- generic growth claims

#### 1–2
- no current signal

### Total Score
Total Score = Fit Score + Behavioral Score

## Intent Confidence
- HIGH: total 16–20 with strong evidence
- MEDIUM: total 12–15 or good fit with weaker evidence
- LOW: below 12 or weak/no current trigger

## Recommended Next Action
- READY_FOR_LINKEDIN: specific evidence, strong buyer, best for white-glove outreach
- READY_FOR_EMAIL: verified email + clean campaign angle
- READY_FOR_LOOM: exceptional lead with strong signal and clear demo path
- NEEDS_RESEARCH: fit looks good but signal/evidence missing
- WATCHLIST: good company but weak timing
- DISQUALIFIED: wrong market/persona or no plausible Oloxa pain

## Assignment Rules

### Ryan
Assign leads that are:
- top 10% quality
- founder/owner/partner/commercial broker
- strong signal/evidence
- likely need nuanced LinkedIn-first outreach

### Sway
Assign leads that are:
- qualified and ready for direct execution
- verified email or clean LinkedIn
- campaign angle is clear
- follow-up/Smartlead-ready

## Hard Rule
Do not call something outreach-ready unless it has a real reason and a real next action.
