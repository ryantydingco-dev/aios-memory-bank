# 30-Day Local Business Revenue Sprint — Chapin / Lake Murray

## Objective
Turn Ryan's OpenClaw/local-business postcard idea into a legitimate revenue generator in 30 days.

Ryan context:
- Lives in Chapin, South Carolina.
- Already tried LOB postcards for free websites/local business marketing.
- LOB got expensive and did not create leads.
- Hypothesis: model is directionally good, but message/CTA/channel/follow-up economics need tightening.

## Key Decision
Do not continue bulk LOB postcards yet.

Use LOB only after the offer and CTA convert manually.

Why:
- Current Lob 4x6 Developer postcard pricing is about $0.872/postcard.
- 100 postcards costs about $87 before any creative/work time.
- 350 postcards costs about $305, which roughly consumes the entire first month of a $297/mo client.
- Direct mail can work, but not as the first lever when Ryan needs money in 30 days.

## New 30-Day Strategy
Use a high-touch, proof-asset-driven local sales sprint.

The model:
1. Pick one high-value local niche.
2. Build a visible audit/mockup for each prospect.
3. Reach out through warm intros, phone, email, SMS where appropriate, and selective mail.
4. Sell a simple cash-now offer.
5. Onboard fast and create a case study.

## Offer

### Name
**Local Google Calls Sprint**

Alternative names:
- Lake Murray Google Growth Sprint
- Local Visibility Sprint
- Google Review + Calls Engine

### Positioning
"I help local service businesses turn their Google presence into more calls by fixing their profile, review flow, missed-call follow-up, and landing page."

### First 30-Day Offer
**$497 setup + $497/month**

If resistance is high:
**$497 setup, first 30 days included, then $497/month if they want to keep it running.**

Do not lead with free if Ryan needs money in 30 days. Free website can stay as a bonus, not the whole offer.

### Deliverables
- Google Business Profile audit and optimization checklist
- Top 3 competitor visibility/review comparison
- Review request automation
- AI-assisted review response workflow
- Missed-call text-back workflow
- Simple landing page or website refresh mockup
- 4 Google/GBP posts for the month
- Monthly performance snapshot

### Core Promise
More trust, more calls, less manual follow-up from Google.

Do not promise guaranteed rankings. Promise setup, execution, and measurable activity:
- profile completeness improved
- reviews requested
- missed calls followed up
- posts published
- competitor gap tracked

## Best Starting Niches
Prioritize high-ticket, local, phone-driven businesses where one job pays for the service.

### Tier 1
- Roofers
- HVAC
- Plumbers
- Landscapers / hardscapers
- Dock builders / lake contractors around Lake Murray
- Remodelers / contractors

### Tier 2
- Med spas
- Dentists
- Chiropractors
- Local gyms / boutique fitness

Avoid first:
- restaurants
- low-ticket retail
- businesses with no phone-based buying process
- businesses with tiny margins

## Lead Criteria
Target businesses that already have demand but weak conversion infrastructure.

Must have:
- 4.3+ rating or obvious local reputation
- 20+ reviews OR clear service value if fewer reviews
- recent reviews within 90 days
- bad/dated/no website OR weak mobile experience
- weak Google Business Profile: missing posts, poor photos, incomplete services, no review responses
- owner/manager contact path

Best triggers:
- strong reviews but bad website
- high-ticket service
- competitors have more/better reviews
- no online booking/contact clarity
- seasonal demand window
- recent negative review about communication/scheduling
- ads running to a weak site

## Outreach CTA
Do not ask for a call first.

Primary CTA:
"Want me to send the 60-second audit?"

Other CTAs:
- "Want me to send the before/after?"
- "Want the 3 fixes I found?"
- "Should I text you the preview?"

The CTA should get a yes to viewing the asset, not a yes to buying.

## Outreach Message

### SMS / Email / DM
```text
Hey {{first_name}} — quick thing. I was looking at {{business}} on Google.

You’ve got solid reviews, but your Google profile/website is leaving some easy trust points on the table compared to {{competitor}}.

I made a 60-sec audit showing the 3 fixes I’d make first to help turn more local searches into calls.

Want me to send it over?
```

### Slightly funnier Ryan version
```text
Hey {{first_name}} — I was looking at {{business}} on Google.

Your reviews are doing the heavy lifting, but the website/profile is making them carry a refrigerator upstairs.

I made a quick 60-sec audit with the 3 fixes I’d make first.

Want me to send it over?
```

Use the funny version carefully for friendlier niches/owners.

## Sales Call Structure
Keep calls to 15 minutes.

1. Rapport: "How long have you been running the business?"
2. Show audit: their profile/site vs top competitor.
3. Ask: "How do most new customers find you right now?"
4. Ask: "What happens when you miss a call?"
5. Present offer: "I fix the Google/profile/review/missed-call layer and run it monthly."
6. Close: "$497 setup + $497/mo. No long contract. We can have the core setup live in 72 hours."

## LOB Decision
Pause bulk LOB.

Use LOB only for:
- 20-30 handpicked high-intent prospects
- after Ryan has a working audit/mockup CTA
- paired with phone/email follow-up
- with QR tracking to the exact audit/mockup

Never send postcards without follow-up. Postcard-only is too passive for a 30-day revenue sprint.

## 30-Day Execution Plan

### Days 1-2: Offer + Demo Asset
- Pick one niche: recommended starting point = roofers/HVAC/plumbers around Chapin, Irmo, Lexington, Columbia, Lake Murray.
- Build a sample audit template.
- Build one demo landing page/mockup.
- Create call booking link or simple reply CTA.
- Prepare payment link/invoice process.

### Days 3-5: Build First 30-50 Prospect List
For each prospect:
- company
- owner/manager
- phone
- email/contact form
- website
- Google rating/reviews
- top competitor
- visible issue
- audit URL or notes
- outreach status

### Days 5-10: Warm Network + Manual Outbound
Daily target:
- 10 warm referral asks
- 10 cold local business touches
- 3 phone calls
- 3 audits recorded/sent

Warm network script:
```text
Quick question — do you know any local service business owners around Chapin/Lake Murray who rely on Google for calls but probably don’t have their reviews/website/follow-up dialed in?

I’m running a 30-day local visibility sprint and doing quick audits for a few businesses. Not asking you to buy anything — just wondering who you know that might want me to take a look.
```

### Days 11-17: First Close Push
Goal:
- 10 audits sent
- 5 conversations
- 1 paid client

Follow-up sequence:
1. Send audit
2. Next day: "Any of those 3 fixes worth tackling first?"
3. Day 3: "If helpful, I can just set this up this week — Google profile, review flow, missed-call text-back, and a simple page."
4. Day 5: "Want me to hold one of the 3 sprint spots for you this month?"

### Days 18-24: Deliver + Proof
For first client:
- complete setup within 72 hours
- screenshot before/after profile improvements
- launch review request automation
- publish GBP posts
- create first report/proof asset

Use the work as content/proof.

### Days 25-30: Referral + Second/Third Close
Ask first client / warm network:
```text
I just finished the Google/review/follow-up setup for {{business}}. Do you know 1-2 other local owners who should probably have this looked at?
```

Goal by Day 30:
- minimum: 1 paid client
- good: 2 paid clients
- strong: 3 paid clients

## Success Math
At $497 setup + $497/month:
- 1 client = $994 collected if setup + first month collected together
- 2 clients = $1,988
- 3 clients = $2,982

At $497 setup with first month included:
- 1 client = $497
- 3 clients = $1,491
- 5 clients = $2,485

For 30-day cash, collect setup + first month when possible.

## Biggest Risks
1. Overbuilding websites before demand.
2. Sending postcards without follow-up.
3. Vague "AI marketing" pitch.
4. Too many niches.
5. CTA jumping straight to booking calls.
6. No proof asset.

## Rule
Manual first. Automate after the message converts.

Ryan does not need 350 postcards. Ryan needs 30 high-intent local businesses, 10 sharp audits, 5 real conversations, and 1-3 closes.
