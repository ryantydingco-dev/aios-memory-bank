**Builds on:** `Product/Deal Threads AI Enrichment Webinar Kit.md` · **Created:** 2026-06-01
**Goal:** turn the webinar (one-and-done) into an evergreen, branded video *system* that compounds on YouTube + communities and books free Dead-Form Teardowns on autopilot.

> **Public brand = "DealThread / dealthreads.io."** "Deal Threads" is the internal codename — never say it on camera.

---

## Why recorded > live (the reframe)
| Live webinar | Recorded video system |
|---|---|
| Do the work once, ~handful attend, it's gone | Record once → ranks, embeds, shares **forever** |
| Needs registrations + a date | Always-on; works while you sleep |
| One 45-min asset | One shoot → **1 flagship + 8–12 clips + a blog + a carousel** |
| Hard to measure compounding | YouTube SEO + community reshares compound monthly |

The webinar content is the *script*. We're re-architecting the *delivery*.

---

## The architecture — 3 layers

1. **The Flagship** (~10–11 min, evergreen pillar) — the tightened webinar, on camera + live demo + branded graphics. Lives on YouTube as the anchor; embedded on dealthreads.io; the thing you link in communities.
2. **The Clip Engine** (8–12 shorts) — cut straight from the flagship (markers in the script). Feed LinkedIn, YouTube Shorts, community posts. This is the *distribution* compounding.
3. **The Teardown-Reaction Series** (the secret weapon) — you record yourself running a **real** company's form through the demo and reacting to the gap. Each episode = a unique evergreen video **and** a warm outreach asset (you can literally send it to that company). This is the highest-compounding, most-differentiated, and it doubles as selling. Build #1 first, then run #3 weekly.

## LAYER 1 — THE FLAGSHIP · camera-ready script (~10–11 min)

> "If you run a B2B company and you're still personally Googling every lead that fills out your contact form before you call them back — your form is costing you deals. I'll show you exactly why, and a fix that runs itself. Watch."
> → hard cut to a 2-sec flash of the dossier building `[SCREEN]`. **✂️CLIP A — the hook.**

**[0:18–0:24 · INTRO STING · `[GFX]`]** — our Higgsfield brand-motion → DealThread wordmark (already built: `outputs/deal-threads-demo/clip/`).

**[0:24–2:00 · THE GAP · CAM + `[GFX]`]**
> "Your form captures the easiest part of the lead — name, email, maybe a sentence — and leaves your rep the hardest part: everything that decides whether this is a deal." `[GFX: form = name/email/message]`
> "Because what closes the deal lives *outside* the form — company size, funding, what they use, how urgent they are, whether there's budget, who actually signs." `[GFX: the 8 fields sales needs]`
> "So the rep Googles, checks LinkedIn, digs the CRM — 10, 20 minutes a lead. And while they research, the lead cools and the faster vendor calls first. That gap — between submit and a prepared callback — is where good deals quietly die." **✂️CLIP B — the gap.**

**[2:00–3:10 · THE FIX · CAM + `[GFX]`]**
> "So what if the form did that research itself? Not a chatbot deflecting them. Not an 'AI SDR' pretending to be you. A short conversation that asks what a form can't — need, urgency, budget, who they are — then enriches the company, scores fit, and writes a finished buyer profile into your CRM, before you call back." `[GFX: comparison table — form / visitor-ID / data tool / AI SDR / DealThread]`
> "The human still sells. The system just makes sure the human walks in prepared." **✂️CLIP C — what it's NOT.**

**[3:10–7:00 · LIVE DEMO · `[SCREEN]`, you narrate]** — *the money segment.*
> "Let me show you. Normal company site, but with our chat instead of a dead form." [submit a realistic lead] "Watch what happens after they finish…" [the build] "…here's what the rep opens: what they *told* us — intent, timeline, budget, who's deciding — and separately, what we *enriched and verified*: company, size, funding, tech stack, decision-makers, an ICP score. And notice this — *'what we still don't know.'* It flags what it couldn't verify instead of inventing it. That's the line between a useful CRM note and a liability." [show CRM note] "≈26 seconds — and in production it runs after submit, so the buyer never waits." **✂️CLIP D — the reveal (your best short).**

**[7:00–8:00 · BEFORE / AFTER · `[GFX]`]**
> "Before: 'Jordan, jordan@company.com, wants a demo.' After: VP of Ops, 40-person firm, evaluating this quarter, budget approved, strong ICP fit, here's your opener. Same lead, same minute — one your rep can actually act on." `[GFX: before/after dossier]` **✂️CLIP E — before/after.**

**[8:00–9:20 · WHO + ROI + HONESTY · CAM]**
> "Who's this for? You sell real deals — $8k and up — you get inbound to a form, and nobody's full-time job is to enrich and route it; it's you or a rep or two. If that's you, one rescued deal pays for a year of this. And if you *don't* have inbound yet, or your deals are low-ticket self-serve — it's too early, and I'll tell you that. I'd rather you not buy it than buy it wrong." **✂️CLIP F — honesty/who-it's-for.**

**[9:20–10:40 · THE OFFER · CAM + `[GFX]`]**
> "Here's what I'll do, free: send me your form URL and I'll record you a short teardown — what your form captures, what it's throwing away, what a full profile would look like for *your* leads. The risk is mine." `[GFX: free-teardown CTA + the ladder]` **✂️CLIP G — the offer.**

**[10:40–11:00 · CLOSE · CAM → OUTRO `[GFX]`]**

## LAYER 3 — THE TEARDOWN-REACTION SERIES (the compounding engine)
The single most differentiated, highest-ROI format — *show, don't tell*, and every episode is also outreach.

**Per-episode template (~5–8 min):**
1. `[CAM]` "I'm going to run a real [vertical] company's contact form through this and show you what it's throwing away. Today: [Company]." *(use public companies; or your real teardown prospects.)*
2. `[SCREEN]` Pull up their actual site + form. Narrate what it captures (thin).
3. `[SCREEN]` Run their domain through the enricher / demo. React honestly to what builds — and what it can't.
4. `[CAM]` The verdict: the gap, what a full profile would change, install / audit / wait.
5. `[GFX/CAM]` CTA: "Want yours? Drop your form URL."

**Why it compounds:** each episode is (a) an evergreen YouTube video, (b) 2–3 clips, (c) a warm DM asset — *"I made you a 6-minute teardown of your form, want it?"* lands far better than a cold pitch. Run **one a week**; the security/compliance batch you already have (ATX Defense, MAD Security, etc.) is your first season.

## DISTRIBUTION — where it compounds
**YouTube (the pillar):**
- **Title** (SEO + curiosity): *"Your Contact Form Is Killing Good Leads (here's the fix)"* — or vertical cuts: *"Why MSPs Lose Inbound Security Deals (and the 2-minute fix)."*
- **Description:** 2-line hook → the free-teardown CTA + dealthreads.io link in line 1 → chapters (use the script timestamps) → keywords.
- **Tags/keywords:** AI lead enrichment, contact form optimization, inbound lead qualification, B2B lead routing, HubSpot lead enrichment, [vertical] inbound.
- **Chapters:** paste the script timestamps — YouTube rewards them + they create clip points.

**Clips (the reach):** cut CLIP A/D/E/G first (hook, demo reveal, before/after, offer). Vertical 9:16, burned-in captions, brand pill, your face. → LinkedIn, YouTube Shorts, then community posts.

**Communities (the warm inbound):** value-first, not spam.
- Security/compliance: r/msp, r/cybersecurity, MSP/MSSP peer Slacks & Discords, CMMC groups.
- Founder/B2B: relevant founder Slacks, indie-hacker/SaaS communities, RevOps groups.
- Post the teardown episodes especially — "I made a teardown of [public company]'s form" is native, useful content, not an ad.

**Repurpose 1 shoot → many:** flagship video → 8–12 clips → a LinkedIn carousel (the before/after + the 8 fields) → a blog post (the script as text, for SEO) → the email sequence you already have.
