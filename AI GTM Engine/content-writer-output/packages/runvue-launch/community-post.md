# Post title
**I built a client portal that runs my whole lead engine in front of the client. Demo inside — and I'm installing it for 5 agencies.**

# Body

Quick backstory. My delivery "system" for outbound was a Telegram brief and shared folder links. The work happened all day. My clients saw none of it. If your retainers die in month two, I'd bet money this is why — the work is invisible between deliverables.

So I built the thing I kept wishing existed, and pointed it at my own engine first.

It's a white-label portal that plugs into the stack most of us already run (SmartLead, a dialer, HubSpot) and shows the client what their engine did, live. Mine is showing 36,337 emails sent, 147 replies, 11 interested prospects across 20 campaigns right now. Real numbers, synced twice a day, nothing staged.

The parts that surprised even me:

- Every Monday it writes a 60-second voice briefing from that week's actual numbers and reads it in my cloned voice. I don't record anything.
- Clients approve lead lists and copy from the portal, or with one tap from the digest email on their phone.
- There's a watchdog that monitors bounce rates and pauses a burning campaign on its own. I ran the fire drill this week: it paused the campaign, posted an incident card, and the client's approve button restarted actual sending in SmartLead, not just a status field.
- On the agency side there's a radar that scores every client red/amber/green for churn drift, and its twin reads the same numbers looking for money. It found $1,750/mo of upsells in my own engine and drafted the pitch emails.
