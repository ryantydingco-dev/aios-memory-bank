## The one honest thing to validate in your first 5 calls

The "leads go cold" pain is your **hypothesis, not yet proven** (the research couldn't confirm it from the outside). So in the first conversations, *ask*: How much inbound do you get? How fast do you respond? Do you lose deals to slow follow-up?

- **If they don't get enough inbound →** expand the offer to *"I'll get you more qualified inbound, too"* (outbound — the engine you've also already built). Lead with "more qualified pipeline," deliver with capture + outbound.

Let the first 5 calls tell you which. That's discovery and selling at the same time.
