# Deal Threads LinkedIn Openers

Last updated: 2026-06-02  
Purpose: LinkedIn connection notes and DMs that lead with useful form diagnosis instead of a sales pitch.

## Strategy

LinkedIn is the highest-trust outbound surface for Deal Threads.

Use it to:

1. connect around a specific website/form observation.
2. offer the teardown.
3. move interested prospects to a Loom/doc.
4. ask for install mapping only after they see the gap.

## Connection Notes

Keep under 250 characters when possible.

### Demo Form

```text
FIRST - looked at COMPANY's demo form while researching B2B form handoffs. It captures the lead, but I think sales still has to build the buyer profile manually. Thought it was worth connecting.
```

### RevOps

```text
FIRST - saw your RevOps/GTM work at COMPANY. I'm studying where demo forms fail the CRM handoff. Thought it would be worth connecting.
```

### Founder

```text
FIRST - looks like COMPANY has a high-touch B2B sales motion. I'm researching how founders turn thin form fills into better first calls. Thought it was worth connecting.
```

### Paid Traffic / Demand Gen

```text
FIRST - noticed COMPANY points demand to a demo/contact page. I'm studying what those forms miss before sales follow-up. Thought it would be worth connecting.
```

## After Acceptance

### Default

```text
Appreciate the connect, FIRST.

I looked at COMPANY's [demo/contact] form. It captures [fields], but sales probably still has to research [missing context] before the first reply.

Want me to send a quick teardown?
```

### More Specific

```text
Appreciate the connect, FIRST.

The thing that stood out on COMPANY's form: it asks for [field], but not [need/timeline/CRM/use case].

That means the rep likely gets the lead before they get the buyer profile.

Want me to send the quick teardown?
```

### If They Ask What Deal Threads Is

```text
Deal Threads is a lightweight AI intake widget for B2B demo/contact pages.

It asks the few questions sales needs, enriches company context behind the scenes, and pushes a buyer profile into CRM before callback.
```

## Comment-To-DM Bridge

Use after someone engages with a post about dead forms:

```text
Saw your comment on the dead-form post.

If you want, send me your demo/contact form URL and I can do a quick teardown: what it captures, what sales still has to research, and what the buyer profile could look like.
```

## Ryan Rules

- One observation only.
- No pitch deck in the DM.
- No calendar link until they say yes to teardown or install mapping.
- Never lead with "AI."
- Always make the next ask easy to answer.
