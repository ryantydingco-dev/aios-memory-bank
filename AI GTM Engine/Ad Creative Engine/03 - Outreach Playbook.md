# Outreach Playbook

## Core motion

The outreach is evidence-first:

1. make one factual observation;
2. connect it to a plausible creative opportunity;
3. offer a private sample or concept;
4. ask for permission to send it;
5. sell the paid pilot after interest.

Do not lead with “AI ads,” automation, or a broad agency menu. Do not claim an ad
is running merely because a tracking tag was detected.

## First-touch email

**Subject:** `[Company] ad concept`

Hi `[First name]` — `[Factual personalization line]`

I mapped a private 15-second `[sample concept]` for `[Company]` using only public
brand context—not for publishing, just to show the angle.

We build three client-approved video ad concepts in seven days, then turn the
winners into fresh variations. No filming day required.

Worth sending the private sample?

## Follow-up

Quick follow-up: the sample angle is a `[sample concept]`. If it misses the mark,
no problem—I won't keep chasing. Should I send it?

## Call opener

Hi `[First name]`, Ryan here. I was looking at `[Company]`'s public marketing and
noticed `[primary need signal]`. I mapped one private `[sample concept]` as an
example. Did I catch you with 30 seconds?

## Four-touch sequence

- **Day 1:** short evidence-led email and, where appropriate, a call.
- **Day 3:** one-sentence follow-up naming the private concept.
- **Day 7:** send a concise new observation or proof angle; do not repeat the
  original email.
- **Day 12:** respectful close-the-loop message.

## Layup operating procedure

For the top 25:

1. open the company's website and the prebuilt Meta Ad Library search;
2. verify current activity and identify one repeated or missing angle;
3. choose one proof asset the company owns publicly;
4. create a private, watermarked concept board or sample;
5. confirm the contact and address;
6. send no more than the four-touch sequence;
7. record reply, objection, meeting, pilot, and revenue outcomes.

## Guardrails

- Public assets are research inputs, not permission to publish.
- Keep samples private and watermarked until the company approves their use.
- Never invent reviews, offers, pricing, customers, outcomes, or certifications.
- Suppress opt-outs and respect channel rules.
- Human-review every message; the generated personalization is a draft.
- Stop after the close-the-loop touch unless the account produces a new signal.
