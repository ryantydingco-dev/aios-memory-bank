# ICP and Buying Signals

## Ideal customer

The primary ICP is an owner-led or professionally managed, high-ticket
home-service company in roofing, restoration, HVAC, windows, waterproofing,
remodeling, solar, pools, plumbing, garage doors, flooring, or adjacent visual
trades.

A strong account usually has:

- enough ticket value to justify paid acquisition;
- proof assets such as reviews, project galleries, testimonials, or financing;
- an identifiable decision maker or a callable business number;
- demand-generation infrastructure already installed;
- a visible creative gap that can be addressed without a filming day.

## Priority order

1. **A — Layup:** strong fit, observable paid-demand infrastructure, creative
   gap, and reachable contact.
2. **B — Strong:** strong evidence of need but one element needs verification.
3. **C — Verify:** ICP fit with limited or ambiguous buying evidence.
4. **D — Nurture:** weak need evidence, poor reachability, or low current
   readiness.

## Signal model

| Dimension | Maximum | Evidence |
|---|---:|---|
| Vertical and capacity fit | 30 | trade, employee/revenue bands, review volume |
| Paid-demand infrastructure | 30 | Meta Pixel, Google Ads tag, call tracking, TikTok Pixel, lead platform |
| Creative gap | 20 | no embedded video, limited social/video links, dated site, complaint themes |
| Proof/raw material | 15 | gallery, testimonials, financing, offer, review volume |
| Reachability | 10 | email, phone, decision-maker title |
| Penalties | -15 | supplier/manufacturer signals or failed site scan |

## Evidence hierarchy

**Hard, observable evidence**

- a tag or tracking script exists in the scanned homepage HTML;
- a gallery, testimonial section, financing language, or offer appears on the
  public site;
- a public review count appears in the existing Maps export;
- a callable number or contact record exists in the supplied source files.

**Hypotheses requiring verification**

- a tag means the company is actively spending today;
- no homepage video means the company has no video creative elsewhere;
- a detected problem automatically has budget or executive urgency;
- a named contact remains in the role.

Before contacting an A-tier account, manually check current ads, current social
creative, contact validity, and whether the observation is still accurate.

## Exclusions

Deprioritize suppliers, distributors, manufacturers, staffing firms, inactive
sites, businesses with no reachable channel, and low-ticket services without
clear paid-acquisition economics. High-risk claims, regulated categories, and
customer likenesses require explicit client review.
