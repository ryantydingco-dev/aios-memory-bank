Most B2B forms capture a lead, but they do not create a buyer profile. This audit helps founder-led and RevOps-light B2B teams score their current form, identify what sales still has to research manually, and map the buyer-profile fields their CRM should receive before follow-up.

## Page 1: Introduction

# Your Form Got The Lead. Your Rep Still Got Homework.

A demo/contact form usually returns:

- name.
- email.
- company.
- phone, if you are lucky.
- one vague message box.

Then the real work starts.

Someone opens LinkedIn. Then the company website. Then the CRM. Then maybe Google, Apollo, Crunchbase, BuiltWith, funding/news searches, job pages, and three tabs that should have come with a seatbelt.

That research determines whether the lead is high-fit, urgent, senior, funded, technical, a tire kicker, or worth calling right now.

But the form did not capture it.

So the buyer waits.

The rep guesses.

The CRM gets another half-empty record.

This worksheet helps you find the gap.

## What You Need

- your website demo/contact form.
- one recent inbound lead, if available.
- the CRM record for that lead, if available.
- 20 minutes.
- a little honesty about how much work happens after someone hits submit.

## Scoring

You will score your form across five areas:

1. buyer context.
2. company context.
3. intent and urgency.
4. CRM handoff.
5. rep follow-up readiness.

Each section is scored 0-5.

Final score:

| Score | Meaning |
| ---: | --- |
| 0-8 | Dead Form: sales is doing most of the work after submit. |
| 9-15 | Basic Intake: useful contact capture, weak buyer profile. |
| 16-21 | Useful Form: good foundation, missing automation or CRM context. |
| 22-25 | Buyer-Profile Engine: your form is preparing sales, not just collecting leads. |

Most teams are not at 22+.

That is the point.

## Why This Matters

A great form that creates a messy CRM record is still a weak handoff.

Sales should not have to leave the CRM to understand:

- who the buyer is.
- what company they represent.
- why they reached out.
- whether they fit.
- what to say first.
- what still needs verification.
