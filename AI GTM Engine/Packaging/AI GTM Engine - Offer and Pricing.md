# AI GTM Engine — Offer & Pricing

> Productized version of the exact machine running on Oloxa. Pricing grounded in cited market research (`deep-research` run 2026-05-31, 10 adversarially-verified findings, 17 sources). All competitor figures dated 2025–2026; flagged vendor vs independent in the research output.

## The one-line offer
> I build and run an AI-powered GTM engine for niche B2B companies: every week you get the highest-intent prospects, *why they're worth contacting now*, the exact multi-channel messages to send, and a market-learning review — so your team books more qualified meetings without hiring an SDR or blasting spam.

## What the buyer actually gets (weekly)
- **Signal-scored prospect list** — ranked by buying signals (closing / hiring / volume / pain), not job titles
- **Meeting-ready research cards** — company, contact, signal, evidence, pain hypothesis, personalized opener
- **Multi-channel outreach copy** — LinkedIn, cold email, cold call, SMS — written + brand/QA-checked
- **CRM/Smartlead-ready batches** — drops into their stack, no cleanup
- **Weekly GTM learning review** — what's working by signal/segment, objections heard, what to change
- **Human-in-the-loop, capped volume** — quality over spray-and-pray (this is the deliverability moat — see below)

## Why this commands a premium (the wedge)
The research surfaced a structural tailwind: since **Feb 2024, Google/Yahoo enforce a hard 0.3% spam-rate ceiling** and authentication rules above 5,000 msgs/day (official platform policy, high confidence). High-volume spray-and-pray is now actively punished. Meanwhile the **AI-SDR tool category is commoditizing and is associated with deliverability damage** (medium confidence, narrative). That creates a trust gap a **vertical-focused, signal-based, human-in-the-loop** service is built to exploit. We sell *relevance and timing*, not volume — which is exactly what survives the new deliverability regime.

**Verdict on the original hypothesis ($1–2.5k setup / $1–3k/mo):**
- **Setup ($1–2.5k): KEEP — well-calibrated.** Sits squarely inside the documented $1–3k industry norm.
- **Monthly ($1–3k): TOO LOW — raise it.** The $3k top is *below where established multi-channel agencies even start*. A multi-channel, signal-based offer priced at $1–3k signals "boutique LinkedIn tool," not "GTM engine." Leaving margin on the table and anchoring perceived value low.

**UK:** quote the same numbers in **£** (£1,500 / £2,500 / £3,500–£5,000+ per month). Research shows UK runs ~10–25% lower in absolute terms but quoting in £ at parity lands in-band and protects margin (UK confidence LOW — sparse sourcing; treat as starting point, adjust on first UK deals).

### Pricing rules
- **Flat monthly retainer is the model** (dominant in the category, HIGH confidence). Retainer = predictable, and it's what established agencies use; per-meeting concentrates risk on us and signals "new/unproven."
- **Setup is separate and non-negotiable** — it's the ICP map, signal taxonomy, lead-source setup, and CRM wiring. Charging it filters tire-kickers and funds the build week.
- **Anchor on Growth ($3.5k).** Starter exists to make Growth look safe; Scale exists to make Growth look reasonable.
- **Don't price below $2k/mo.** Below that you're competing with $397 LinkedIn boutiques on price and can't deliver multi-channel profitably as a solo operator.

## De-risking the first sales (no case study yet — the honest constraint)
We have **zero external client results** to cite (Oloxa is client #0, in progress). So the first 2–3 deals sell on **system + de-risk**, not proof:
1. **90-day pilot** at Starter price → converts to Growth once meetings show.
2. **Founder-led**: "I run this on my own company (Oloxa) live — you're getting the same machine, not a junior SDR's first month."
3. **Meeting floor, not a hard guarantee**: commit to a transparent activity + qualified-meeting target; if missed, a free month. (Research: pure pay-per-meeting concentrates risk on the vendor and is a boutique trust-builder — use a *soft* version, don't bet the business on it.)
4. **Show the artifacts**: the battlecards, research cards, and weekly review *are* the sales collateral. They're real and already built.

## Positioning vs the alternatives (how to frame on a call)
- **vs hiring an SDR** → see the ROI model. Short version: a fully-loaded US SDR is ~$10–12k/mo and takes 3–4 months to ramp; the engine is live in week one at a fraction of that.
- **vs a commodity lead-gen agency** → "they sell you 1,000 contacts and blast them — which now wrecks your domain. We send fewer, better, timed touches that survive the spam filter and sound human."
- **vs an AI-SDR tool (11x/Artisan)** → "those hand *you* the software and the deliverability risk. This is done-for-you, with a human reviewing every batch."
