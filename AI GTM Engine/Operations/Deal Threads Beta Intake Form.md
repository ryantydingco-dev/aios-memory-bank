# Deal Threads Beta Intake Form

Last updated: 2026-06-02  
Purpose: collect everything needed to configure and launch a beta client without scattered follow-up.

## How To Use

Turn this into a Tally, Typeform, Google Form, HubSpot form, or send as a doc before kickoff.

Recommended form title:

**Deal Threads Beta Intake**

Recommended form description:

```text
This helps us configure your Deal Threads beta around your sales motion, CRM, target buyer, and current form handoff. Most teams finish it in 10-15 minutes.
```

## Section 1: Company Basics

1. Company name.
2. Website.
3. Primary contact name.
4. Primary contact email.
5. Primary contact role.
6. Who owns the sales follow-up process?
7. Who owns the website or tag manager?
8. Who owns the CRM?

## Section 2: Current Form And Inbound Flow

1. Which form should Deal Threads replace or test alongside?
2. Form URL.
3. Which pages should the widget appear on?
   - pricing.
   - demo.
   - contact sales.
   - product/use case.
   - other.
4. Which pages should the widget not appear on?
5. What happens today after someone submits the form?
6. Who gets notified?
7. How fast does someone usually follow up?
8. What does sales usually research manually before replying?
9. What makes a form fill high quality?
10. What makes a form fill low quality?

## Section 3: CRM And Tools

1. CRM used today:
   - HubSpot.
   - Salesforce.
   - Pipedrive.
   - Other.
   - None.
2. CRM admin contact.
3. Do you use a meeting scheduler?
4. Do you use routing today?
5. Do you use enrichment today?
6. Do you use Slack or email for lead alerts?
7. Preferred notification channel for high-priority leads.

## Section 4: ICP And Qualification

1. Who is your ideal customer?
2. Ideal company size.
3. Ideal industries.
4. Best-fit regions.
5. Common buyer roles.
6. Common pain points buyers mention.
7. Buying triggers.
8. Disqualifiers.
9. Existing customer or support request indicators.
10. What should be routed to sales immediately?

## Section 5: Conversation Configuration

1. What should the widget say first?
2. Which fields are required?
   - name.
   - work email.
   - company.
   - business need.
   - role.
   - timeline.
   - budget/buying stage.
   - CRM/tools.
   - phone.
3. Which questions feel too sensitive for a first interaction?
4. What tone should the widget use?
   - direct.
   - consultative.
   - friendly.
   - premium.
   - technical.
5. Any words or claims to avoid?
6. What should the confirmation message say?

## Section 6: Routing Rules

1. Who should receive high-priority leads?
2. Who should receive medium-priority leads?
3. What should happen to low-priority leads?
4. What should happen to support/customer requests?
5. What should happen to partner/consultant inquiries?
6. What should happen if owner is unknown?

## Section 7: Privacy And Approval

1. Privacy policy URL.
2. Consent language preference.
3. Are there regulated data concerns?
4. Any industries or data types Deal Threads should avoid collecting?
5. Who approves the consent language?
6. Who approves the script installation?

## Section 8: Baseline Metrics

1. Monthly demo/contact form submissions.
2. Current average response time.
3. Current median response time.
4. Current demo booking rate.
5. Current MQL-to-SQL rate.
6. Current average manual research time per lead.
7. Current biggest handoff problem.
8. One recent good inbound lead example.
9. One recent bad or low-fit form fill example.

## Section 9: Beta Success

1. What would make this beta clearly useful?
2. Which metric matters most?
   - time saved.
   - faster response.
   - better lead quality.
   - cleaner CRM records.
   - more meetings.
   - better routing.
3. How many weeks should we review before deciding?
4. Who needs to see the weekly beta report?
5. What would block a paid rollout?

## Confirmation Copy

```text
Thanks. We will use this to configure your Deal Threads beta, map the CRM fields, and define what a successful first profile should look like.

Next step: we will send the install packet and schedule the kickoff/configuration call.
```
