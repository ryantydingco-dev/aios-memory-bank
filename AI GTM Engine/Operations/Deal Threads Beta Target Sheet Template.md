# Deal Threads Beta Target Sheet Template

Last updated: 2026-06-02  
Purpose: build the first 50-account beta universe for Deal Threads.

## Ideal Beta Target

A high-fit target is a B2B company where:

- the website has a demo/contact-sales form.
- the company sells high-value deals, ideally USD 8K+.
- founder, sales, marketing, or RevOps still handles inbound manually.
- the form captures contact details but not buyer context.
- CRM follow-up matters.
- HubSpot is likely or visible, though Salesforce can be a later fit.
- the company is reachable through founder/content/LinkedIn.

## Scoring Rubric

Score each company 1-5.

### 5 - Teardown First

- Clear B2B offer.
- Visible weak demo/contact form.
- High-value deal likely.
- Founder or GTM buyer reachable.
- Obvious missing sales context.
- Strong reason now: hiring, growth, paid traffic, new product, active content, or sales expansion.

### 4 - Strong Fit

- Good B2B fit and weak form.
- Deal value likely supports the offer.
- Buyer is identifiable.
- Reason now is present but weaker.

### 3 - Maybe

- B2B fit is plausible.
- Form exists, but deal value or buyer access is unclear.
- Keep for later.

### 2 - Low Fit

- Weak sales motion, unclear deal value, or poor buyer access.

### 1 - Avoid

- B2C, low-ticket, no real sales motion, no form, or no CRM-like process.

## Target Sheet Columns

| Column | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| Company | Company name. |
| Website | Main website. |
| Form URL | Demo/contact-sales/pricing contact page. |
| Buyer Name | Founder, sales, marketing, RevOps, or consultant contact. |
| Buyer Role | Founder, Head of Growth, VP Sales, RevOps, Demand Gen, etc. |
| LinkedIn URL | Buyer or company profile. |
| Segment | Founder-led B2B, RevOps-light, vertical SaaS, agency, services, etc. |
| Company Size | Estimate. |
| Deal Value Guess | Low / medium / high, or estimated ACV. |
| CRM Guess | HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive, unknown. |
| Weak Form Observation | What the form fails to capture. |
| Sales Research Hypothesis | What sales probably Googles after submit. |
| Reason Now | Hiring, growth, content, paid traffic, new launch, form issue, etc. |
| Fit Score | 1-5. |
| Teardown Priority | Teardown first / later / avoid. |
| Outreach Status | Not started / asked / replied / teardown sent / call booked / closed. |
| Next Action | Specific next step. |
| Notes | Buyer language, objections, context. |

## Daily Target Research Prompt

```text
Find 5 founder-led or RevOps-light B2B companies with weak demo/contact forms.

For each, capture:
- company
- website
- form URL
- likely buyer
- weak-form observation
- what sales still has to research
- reason now
- fit score
- next action

Prioritize companies where one good lead is worth at least $8K and the form captures contact info but not buyer context.
```

## Outreach Trigger

When fit score is 4 or 5, send:

```text
I looked at your demo/contact form and noticed it captures the lead, but probably still leaves sales doing the buyer-profile research manually.

Want me to send over the quick teardown?
```
