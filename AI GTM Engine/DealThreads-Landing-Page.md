## The gap between a name and a deal

A contact form returns two fields: a name and an email.

The four facts that actually decide whether the deal closes are nowhere on it:

- **Budget** — can they afford what you sell?
- **Timeline** — are they buying this quarter or "just looking"?
- **Authority** — is this the person who signs, or someone forwarding it up?
- **Fit** — company size, tech stack, recent funding — does this even match your ICP?

That data exists. It just sits behind 20 minutes of research the form was never built to do. So here's the sequence that plays out on every inbound lead:

1. A form fills. Your rep gets a name and an email.
2. Ten minutes later, the rep starts Googling — company, headcount, LinkedIn, funding news.
3. By the time they have enough to make a real call, the lead has gone quiet. The first vendor to follow up with a real conversation already booked the meeting.

**The deal didn't die because the lead was bad. It died in the gap.**

The rep who calls back at 5 PM with budget, timeline, and the decision-maker already in front of them takes the meeting the slower competitor lost. DealThreads closes that gap before the rep ever picks up the phone.

## How it works

Three steps. The first one takes a minute. The other two happen on their own.

### 1. Install one script tag

Paste a single line into your site's `<head>`. The DealThreads widget goes live on your pages in place of (or alongside) your existing form. No rebuild, no new platform, no engineering sprint.

### 2. The AI runs the conversation

Instead of a static form, a visitor lands in a natural chat. DealThreads asks the questions a good SDR would — what they're trying to solve, rough budget, timeline, who else is involved — and captures **intent, budget, and timeline** in the visitor's own words. No 11-field form to abandon.

### 3. Enrichment + routing hand the rep a full profile

While that conversation runs, the enrichment layer works behind the scenes: **company size, decision-makers, recent funding, and tech-stack signals.** Intelligent routing then drops the lead — fully enriched — to the right rep, in your CRM, with intent and budget already attached. The rep opens it ready to call, not ready to research.

## FAQ

**How much setup work is this on my side?**
Close to none. You drop one script tag in your site's `<head>`; the founder handles the rest — tuning the conversation, configuring enrichment, and wiring routing into your CRM. Most of the work happens on our side during the pilot, not yours.

**Does it fit my CRM and current stack?**
Yes — fitting your existing stack is the point. Routing delivers enriched leads into the CRM your team already uses, so reps work where they already work. Killing integration headaches is one of the four problems DealThreads is built to solve; if there's a wrinkle specific to your setup, the founder sorts it during the founder-led install.

**How accurate is the data, and is the enrichment compliant?**
Enrichment pulls from business-data signals — company size, decision-makers, recent funding, tech-stack — sourced from legitimate, business-grade providers, not scraped personal data. Accuracy and freshness are a core promise, not an afterthought: the premium tier exists specifically to keep you current on tech-stack changes and funding rounds. If a field can't be verified, we'd rather flag it than hand your rep a confident guess.

**What about security?**
The widget installs as a single script with a defined scope, and lead data flows into your CRM — your system of record — rather than living in a separate silo. Founding clients get to review exactly how data is handled during setup, and the founder will walk your team or your security reviewer through it directly.

**What are the contract terms?**
The pilot is a one-time $500 with no contract attached. The monthly plans ($2,500 Core, $5,000 Premium) are month-to-month — you're not locked into an annual commitment to get started. Founding-seat pricing is locked for the cohort.

**What happens after the pilot?**
You decide with real data in hand. If the pilot put better leads in front of your reps, you move to Core or Premium and it keeps running. If it didn't, you walk — no monthly plan, no obligation. Founding clients who continue keep their locked early terms.

**How is this different from Drift, Qualified, Intercom, Chili Piper, or Clearbit?**
Short version: nobody else does all three jobs — qualify, enrich, route — as one managed product for the independent mid-market.

- **Drift** was sunset in 2026 after the Salesloft acquisition, with the floor raised to ~$30K/yr and smaller customers deprioritized.

- **Intercom** and **Chili Piper** route chat well but don't carry an integrated AI enrichment + management layer — you'd bolt that on yourself.
- **Clearbit** enriches data but doesn't run the conversation or route the lead.

DealThreads combines the conversation, the enrichment, and the routing into one managed setup — built for the mid-market the others left behind.

**Who is DealThreads actually for?**
Mid-market B2B companies running $10K+ deals — GTM and RevOps leaders, sales-ops managers, and sales teams without a dedicated coordinator or analyst to do the catching, qualifying, and research by hand. If that work is currently falling on reps between calls, DealThreads is built for you.
