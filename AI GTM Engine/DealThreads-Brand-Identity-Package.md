# DealThreads — Brand Identity Package

> **The inbound-intelligence layer.** A standard contact form returns a name and an email — then drops the conversation. The data that actually closes the deal sits behind 20 minutes of research the form refused to do. The deal stalls in that gap. **DealThreads keeps the thread unbroken.** One script tag replaces the form with an AI conversation that captures intent, budget, and timeline while an enrichment layer weaves in company size, decision-makers, recent funding, and tech-stack signals. The rep calls back already knowing the deal.

**Master invariant (the spine of this entire system):** `WHOLE THREAD OVER LOOSE ENDS`. The name *DealThreads* carries two readings that reinforce each other — the **conversation thread** (the widget literally is one) and the **connective thread** woven through scattered signals into one continuous record. Every expression dramatizes *loose, broken ends becoming one unbroken thread the rep can pick up.* If a choice doesn't make *loose ends → one thread* felt, it's off-brand.

**Naming hygiene:** The customer-facing brand is **DealThreads** and only DealThreads (logo, site, widget, invoices, reports). Legacy working names from source research are retired and must never appear on a rendered surface. Supporting category line for SEO/first-touch: **"DealThreads — conversational enrichment for inbound."** *(Bonus vs. a coined name: "DealThreads" is self-evident and trivially pronounceable, so there's no pronunciation/meaning to teach — a real GTM advantage with a skeptical buyer.)*

## 1. Logo Concepts

**Locked system:** **The Thread** (primary, large surfaces) · **DealThreads wordmark** (primary signature) · **Thread Tile** (the *only* favicon + widget launcher). *The Snapped Thread* and *Woven Signals* are alternates / campaign + motion assets. Rendered previews were generated with `nano_banana_pro` — treat them as concept directions; final marks get drawn in vector with real Fraunces.

### 1.1 The Thread — ⭐ Recommended Primary
- **Rationale:** The master invariant as the mark itself. A single continuous, unbroken connective line travels through scattered nodes (data signals + conversation turns), gathers them, and resolves into a tidy threaded record. It performs *loose ends → one thread* in one frame, reads as a *record/connective line* (analyst-in-a-box), never a chat bubble, and fuses conversation + enrichment + completeness into one form.
- **Visual description:** Strict 64×64 grid. A continuous uniform-weight stroke enters left, passes through 4 small solid circular nodes at even intervals, and resolves on the right into a stack of 3 short horizontal lines (a complete woven record). The line + nodes are **Dossier Ink**; exactly **one** segment near the resolving record is **Thread Amber** (the live thread — the single accent). Unbroken stroke (never dashed), precise spacing. Reads as a connective *digital* thread, not sewing thread.
- **Style:** Combination (icon + text); symbol independently usable.
- **Mood:** Composed, precise, premium, knowing.

### 1.2 DealThreads — Primary Wordmark
- **Rationale:** Own the name as the signature. Optional thread motif integrated into the type ties the verbal and visual spine together.
- **Visual description:** Set **DealThreads** (one word, initial-cap with a camel-cap **T** — *locked*) in a high-contrast geometric-humanist editorial serif (Fraunces-style, ~560 weight, tight even tracking, generous x-height; never rounded/bubbly/script), in Dossier Ink. **Optional accent treatment:** a single fine continuous **thread underline** that runs beneath the word and ties off in a small node at the end — rendered in Thread Amber, used only in the master lockup, dropped at small sizes. Monochrome/knockout version for one-color print/emboss.
- **Style:** Wordmark (logotype).
- **Mood:** Premium, editorial, plainspoken-confident.

### 1.3 Thread Tile — Favicon + Widget Launcher (the *only* one — locked)
- **Rationale:** The product ships as a script tag and lives as a small embedded element, so it needs a compact glyph that survives at 16px and as the on-page launcher — and the deliberate anti-chat-bubble launcher.
- **Visual description:** 64×64. Solid rounded-square tile (10-unit radius) in Dossier Ink. Inside, in knockout white, a minimal thread glyph: a continuous unbroken line weaving through 3 white nodes and converging to one neat connection point; exactly one short segment in Thread Amber. Reads as a connective thread tying scattered points into one — not a chat bubble, not sewing thread. Variants: full tile (app icon / launcher), reversed (ink line on light tile) for light UIs, line-only for large-format/monochrome.
- **Style:** Lettermark / monogram tile.
- **Mood:** Solid, dependable, infrastructural.

### 1.4 The Snapped Thread — Alternate (before/after, campaign)
- **Rationale:** Dramatizes the core insight directly: *the form snaps the thread.* A thread that is visibly cut/frayed (the form: name + email, then nothing) beside one continuous unbroken thread weaving every field (DealThreads). The strongest explainer/campaign device.
- **Visual description:** Two small record cards, same scale. Left: two filled rows then a thread line cut and stopped, in Graphite Slate (the broken state). Right: all rows filled, threaded by one continuous Thread-Amber line through every node. Remove the unbroken thread → an obvious loose end (before); add it → whole (after).
- **Style:** Symbol pair / narrative device.
- **Mood:** Recognition, then relief — not playful.

### 1.5 Woven Signals — Alternate (motion / loading states)
- **Rationale:** Multiple scattered threads converging and weaving into one strong line — *scattered signals → one record.* Best fit for animation, loading, and the "building your profile" moment.
- **Visual description:** 3–4 thin threads enter from the left at different heights, converge, and weave into a single bold resolved line on the right that terminates in the threaded record. Threads in Dossier Ink; the resolved line's live segment in Thread Amber. Animation: threads draw in and weave together, then settle (no looping shimmer).
- **Style:** Combination (dynamic symbol + text); symbol works alone.
- **Mood:** Momentum resolving into calm order.

## 2. Color Palette

> **Deliberate break from the category.** The Drift/Intercom default (and the source demo) is indigo/violet on white. DealThreads anchors on an ink-**GREEN**-black (not slate-blue-black), acts with deep **teal** (not blue), and runs entirely cool **except one disciplined warm accent** — amber, the color of the live, unbroken thread. **Brand-defining non-negotiables: the green anchor + the scarce amber spark.** Dilute either and it collapses into the purple/blue chatbot category.

### Primary Colors

**Dossier Ink** — the master anchor
- **Hex:** `#0E1C19` · **RGB:** `14, 28, 25`
- **Usage:** Primary dark backgrounds (hero, footer, full-bleed dark), the wordmark, all body/heading text on light surfaces, the dossier-card frame and thread lines. The dominant color — the brand reads as ink-on-paper with disciplined accents.
- **Psychology:** A near-black with a deliberate **green** undertone (not the category's slate-blue-black). Reads considered and intelligent; the depth signals *Composed* and premium infrastructure that justifies $2,500–$5,000/mo. Black-green is the color of a serious reference document — the *Knowing* Sage made visual.

**Signal Teal** — primary action + live intelligence
- **Hex:** `#0F8C7E` · **RGB:** `15, 140, 126`
- **Usage:** Primary CTAs (white label), active/selected states in the widget, links, the live cursor, the send affordance. *Guard (locked):* always on the ink-green ground or beside green-grey neutrals — **never a standalone accent on white** (reads as generic SaaS blue). White-on-teal = 4.14:1 (large/UI only). For small teal text on light, use **Signal Teal Bright `#0B6B60`** (6.39:1 on white — full AA).
- **Psychology:** Deep desaturated teal sits cool-and-credible with the anchor but lands decisively outside the banned blue-purple zone. Reads as clarity, signal, instrumentation — intelligence arriving — without the candy cheer of a toy bot.

### Accent Colors

**Thread Amber** — the single hero accent (the *live, unbroken thread*)
- **Hex:** `#E8A33D` · **RGB:** `232, 163, 61`
- **Usage:** *Sparingly, with intent.* Reserved for the connective/complete moment: the live segment of the thread, a node connecting, the key ROI figure ($30K vs $280K; "20 minutes, gone"), the before/after highlight. **Priority when candidates collide (locked):** thread-connect moment > ROI figure > secondary CTA. **One amber moment per view.** Sings on Dossier Ink (8.12:1, AAA). On light, use **Amber Deep `#B9791C`**, large/UI only. Never a large background fill.
- **Psychology:** One warm spark against an all-cool ground is the entire thesis in a single color — the **live, unbroken thread** that ties scattered signals into one. Carries ROI/value warmth (gold, return) without dollar-sign gaudiness. Its scarcity is the point: the eye goes exactly where the thread connects.

**Verified Green** — trust + evidence (strictly functional)
- **Hex:** `#4E9E7A` · **RGB:** `78, 158, 122`
- **Usage:** Source-cited signal tags, "field confirmed / verified" states, accuracy/compliance indicators, small check affordances on enriched fields. Deeper variant for green text on Paper (5.20:1, AA); this value for labels/UI on dark Ink (5.42:1). Never rely on color alone — pair with an icon/label.
- **Psychology:** Desaturated sage signals *verified, sourced, reliable* — the *Substantiated* pillar and the exact reassurance a compliance-conscious RevOps buyer needs. Muted away from bright success-green: this brand shows its work calmly.

## 3. Typography System

> **The "intelligent research desk" triad.** An authored serif on top is the fastest signal that DealThreads is a different species from the geometric-sans chatbots. A neutral, CRM-native sans substantiates it. A true monospace makes numbers and field labels feel reliable.

**Primary Font (Headers) — Fraunces**
- **Backups:** Fraunces 72, Charter, Lora, Georgia, "Times New Roman", serif
- **Character:** Contemporary old-style "soft-serif" display with genuine stroke contrast, a variable optical-size (`opsz`) axis and a `SOFT` axis. Reads authored, intelligent, considered. Set `SOFT`≈0, weight 500–600.
- **Usage:** Headlines, hero statements, section titles, pull-quotes, before/after beats. Use `opsz` (high contrast/tight for hero/H1; smaller for H3/eyebrows). **Cap weight ~600.** *Scope (locked):* **marketing layer only — never inside the product widget/dashboard** (those use Inter — a literary serif on a RevOps dashboard reads "brand-y," not "infrastructure").

**Secondary Font (Body / UI) — Inter**
- **Backups:** "Inter Variable", "Helvetica Neue", Arial, "Segoe UI", Roboto, system-ui, sans-serif
- **Character:** Neutral, screen-native humanist sans engineered for UI legibility — open apertures, tall x-height, variable weight, deep OpenType set (`tnum`, `zero`, `ss01`). The font the buyer's HubSpot/Salesforce dashboards render in, so it reads native and trustworthy.
- **Usage:** All body copy, UI/widget chrome, nav, buttons, captions, table body, form labels, long-form. Body 16–18px / 400–450; UI labels 500. `font-feature-settings: 'tnum','zero'` wherever a number appears.

**Accent Font (Data / Mono) — IBM Plex Mono**
- **Backups:** "Martian Mono", "JetBrains Mono", "Roboto Mono", "SF Mono", "Cascadia Code", ui-monospace, "Courier New", monospace
- **Character:** Engineered, slightly mechanical monospace with a true slashed zero and IBM-grade technical credibility. Fixed-width → figures and labels lock into a perfect grid. "Plex" carries an infrastructure connotation — *real tool, replaces a hire.*
- **Usage:** *A precision accent, never body.* Enrichment field labels/values in the dossier card (`COMPANY SIZE`, `FUNDING`, `TECH STACK`, `DECISION-MAKERS`); all hard numbers + ROI math; the `<script>` one-line-install motif; data-source citations + timestamps.
- **⚠️ Delivery caveat:** IBM Plex Mono's slashed-zero (`zero` feature) **does not activate over the Google Fonts CDN** (documented issue). **Self-host** via Fontsource or the IBM/plex GitHub static webfonts and enable `font-feature-settings: 'zero','tnum'`. If self-hosting isn't feasible, fall back to **Martian Mono** / **JetBrains Mono**. *(Infra decision — §8.)*

**Scale (1.250 Major Third, strict baseline grid):** Hero/Display Fraunces 56–72px (high opsz, 560, line-height 1.05, tracking −0.01em) · H1 40/1.1/560 · H2 30 · H3 22 (lower opsz). Body Inter 17–18/1.6/420 · small 14/1.5/440 · UI labels & buttons 14–15/540/+0.01em. **Data:** field labels Plex Mono 12–13px/500/ALL CAPS/+0.06em; figures at context size, 450–500, `tnum`+`zero` always on; install snippet Plex Mono 14px in a bordered code block. **Weight discipline (this reads premium):** only three working weights — Inter 420/540, Fraunces 560, Plex Mono 450–500. **Never Inter 700 for headlines** (the generic-SaaS tell). Color hierarchy with size/weight/whitespace, *not* the accent. No italics in product UI; Fraunces italic for editorial pull-quotes only.

## 4. Brand Voice & Personality

**Archetype:** **The Sage** (primary), with a disciplined edge of **the Magician.** The pragmatic, hype-skeptical RevOps/GTM buyer distrusts the Hero ("we'll transform your pipeline!") and the Jester ("cute support bot!"). They trust the analyst who already did the homework. The Sage earns the right to be plainspoken because it's the most informed thing in the room. The Magician edge is the *felt* payoff — one tag, and the whole thread appears — kept **grounded** (this buyer wants accuracy and receipts, not a black box). Reads *analyst-in-a-box*, not *toy chatbot.*

### Brand Personality
- **Knowing** — Speaks from already having the answer. States findings, not features: *"Your rep opens the call knowing budget, timeline, headcount, and who signs."* No question marks in the logo or hero.
- **Precise** — Every claim numerically anchored ($30K replaces ~$280K; "20 minutes, gone"). Tight grids, exact alignment, monospace figures. Precision doubles as a data-accuracy trust signal.
- **Composed** — Calm, unhurried, premium. No exclamation marks, no "revolutionary," no neon urgency. Reassures a buyer making a $2,500–$5,000/mo decision that this is infrastructure. (Composed ≠ cold — self-assured.)
- **Plainspoken** — *"the form gives sales homework," "calling leads blind."* **Banned words:** seamless, unlock, leverage, revolutionize, synergy, supercharge, transform-your-pipeline, AI-powered-revenue-acceleration. Keeps the Sage from sounding pompous.
- **Substantiated** — Shows its work: enriched fields cite their source signal; ROI claims show the math; positioning leads with the bottleneck removed, never with "AI." Evidence-forward (before/after, threaded profile, real numbers) over abstract AI imagery.

### Tone of Voice
- **Formal ↔ Casual:** ~60/40 professional — **plainspoken-professional**, not corporate-formal. Contractions, short declaratives, direct address. *How a sharp senior analyst briefs a VP.* (Layering: the founder-led demand-gen voice can run more casual/cheeky; the **product brand stays one notch more composed.** Coherent, not identical — and the founder voice does not use Fraunces/the product type system.)
- **Technical ↔ Simple:** **Simple on the surface**, technical depth one click down (named enrichment signals, routing logic, HubSpot/Salesforce sync, data-accuracy/compliance posture). **Never lead with "AI"** — lead with the bottleneck removed.
- **Playful ↔ Serious:** Decisively **serious**, dry wit as seasoning — aimed at the **shared enemy, the dumb form** (*"Some forms are spiritually a shoebox with a submit button"*). Never whimsy, mascots, or exclamatory hype.

**Emotional target:** quiet, grounded **relief** — *"finally, someone gets the actual problem and already solved it."* (1) **Recognition** ("they named the exact thing costing us deals"). (2) **Confidence/control** (every inbound call already knowing the deal). (3) **Credible seriousness** ("real infrastructure that replaces a hire"). North star, one line: **the relief of being prepared.**

## 6. Visual Style Guidelines

### Photography
- **Subject matter:** Real people in real revenue work, **mid-competence** — not stock-smiling teams. The recurring subject is *the rep prepared:* on a call with a complete threaded dossier open, relaxed and in-command. Favor over-the-shoulder / side-angle shots of an actual screen showing the threaded profile — **the populated record is itself a hero subject.** Senior focused operators (mid-thirties+). **Hard ban:** cartoon robots, glowing brains, swirling-particle "AI," humanoid androids, floating holographic UIs, grinning headset call-center stock, and literal sewing thread/fabric.
- **Mood:** Composed, prepared, quietly in control — the relief of being ready. Calm, never frantic. Serious and premium with human warmth. Every frame = *the moment after the homework is already done.*
- **Color treatment:** Restrained, editorial, locked to the anchor — desaturate toward deep ink/charcoal-green; clean slightly-cool neutrals; never candy-bright, never the purple/blue wash. The single signal accent appears sparingly as the only real color note (a lit thread node, one highlighted field). Natural directional light, soft-but-defined shadow; avoid flat ring-light glow and teal-orange grade. Consistent grade across the system.

### Graphic Elements
- **Shape language:** Precise, architectural, document-grade. Governing forms: the rectangular **dossier card**, the **label-value field row**, and **the continuous connective thread/line** that weaves nodes together. Crisp corners, slight softening only (4–10px); never pill-soft. Strict baseline grid, hairline dividers, exact alignment — *structure is the aesthetic.* The recurring visual atom is **the THREAD: a continuous line connecting scattered nodes into one unbroken path** — the seed for the logo/launcher.
- **Pattern usage:** Sparse and meaningful. Permitted: (1) the thread-through-nodes motif (scattered points connected into one line); (2) a fine baseline/ledger rule system as quiet texture; (3) a restrained monospace "code" treatment for the script-tag motif. Low-contrast, ink-on-ink or hairline. **Hard ban:** candy gradients, swirling particles, dot-grid AI clichés, circuit-board motifs, sewing/textile motifs, playful repeating shapes.
- **Icon style:** Thin, precise line icons on a consistent grid — drafting-table, not app-store. Uniform ~1.5px stroke, square/minimally-rounded terminals, geometric and literal: a form field, a threaded record, a routing arrow, a provenance tag, a verified check, a headcount glyph, a funding signal, a `<script>` tag, a conversation turn (two clean aligned lines, not a fat bubble). The accent marks only live data, verification, or a CTA. No filled cartoon glyphs, duotone blobs, "friendly" rounded sets, or emoji.

### Widget UI Motif — *the widget IS the brand*
- **Shape & geometry:** Architectural, not bubbly. Launcher = the **Thread Tile** (calm near-square rounded rectangle, 8–10px radius), **not** a circular chat-bubble blob; no mascot/smiley/`?`/`...`. Open panel = a clean, generously-padded card with crisp corners and a single hairline border — a document, not a toy. Messages on a strict left/right rhythm differentiated by alignment and weight, **not candy-colored bubbles.**
- **Palette in-widget:** Deep ink/charcoal-green surface, near-white message field, **one** signal accent (active prompt, send affordance, live-capture confirmations). Hard ban on the purple/indigo/blue gradient default. The accent reads like a precise instrument light, not a glow.
- **Density & pacing:** Spacious and unhurried — *one question on screen at a time.* Feels like a senior analyst asking the two questions that matter (intent, then budget/timeline). Short, plainspoken turns — no exclamation marks, no "Hi there 👋", no emoji. Opening states purpose: *"I'll get you to the right person fast. What are you trying to solve?"*
- **Motion:** Confident and minimal. Smooth, slightly slow ease (250–350ms); typing indicator = a restrained even pulse, not a jiggling ellipsis. Nothing bounces/wobbles/pops. **Reduced-motion support mandatory.**
- **How enriched data surfaces (the signature moment):** As the buyer talks, a thin **thread rail** down the panel edge connects captured-signal nodes (`COMPANY` → `HEADCOUNT` → `FUNDING` → `STACK`) as they resolve — the **grounded Magician payoff:** the buyer watches scattered signals get woven into one thread. Ambient and tasteful — never data-rain, particles, or fake "scanning…" theater. On the rep side, the conversation resolves into the full threaded dossier the instant it ends.

### Data Visualization
- **Enriched Profile Card (the hero data object — this IS the product):** A structured **dossier** threaded into three tiers separated by hairline rules + quiet monospace eyebrows: **`STATED`** (intent, budget, timeline — from the conversation) · **`ENRICHED`** (company size, decision-makers, funding, tech stack — background) · **`ROUTED`** (fit score, recommended routing), with a continuous thread line connecting the tiers into one unbroken record. Label-value pairs; figures/dollars/domains/tech in monospace.
- **Source-cited signals (the trust invariant made visual):** every enriched field carries a provenance tag — *`funding: press, Mar 2026`*, *`stack: detected`*, *`headcount: firmographic`*. **Never a fact without a footnote.** Tappable to expand the receipt — the single most differentiating data-viz behavior.
- **Thread rail** (not a generic progress bar): a thin line along the card edge that connects nodes as fields resolve — loose ends at the start, one unbroken thread at the end. Signal accent for connected, ink-15 for pending.
- **Confidence with restraint:** a small monospace % or a 3-state dot (verified / inferred / unconfirmed) — never a colored gauge or emoji. Inferred is visibly distinct from verified.
- **Intelligence Reports ($5K tier):** Editorial, like an equity-analyst brief — headline finding in the serif, supporting figure, then chart. *The number leads, the chart substantiates.* Horizontal bar, thin line with one emphasized point, dot/range plots, clean stat callouts. **Avoid pie/donut/3D/area fills.** One-accent rule; hairline axes; direct-labeled points; units + `n=` source line under every chart.
- **Global:** dense where it earns trust (data), airy everywhere else. Tabular numerals everywhere. No drop shadows on data, no gradients/animation in report charts. Print-and-hand-to-a-VP grade.

### Do / Don't
- **DO** build every layout, image, and animation around *loose ends → one unbroken thread.* The threaded, source-cited dossier is the hero motif; before/after (snapped vs. unbroken thread) is the recurring device.
- **DON'T** touch the generic purple/blue/indigo SaaS-chatbot palette or aesthetic — anti-Drift/Intercom territory. No chat-bubble mascots, winking robots, smiley launchers, candy gradients, glowing brains, swirling particles, "scanning…" theater, or literal sewing-thread/fabric clichés.
- **DO** show the work and cite the source on every data point — provenance tags, ROI math, chart units + `n=`, inferred-vs-verified distinction.
- **DON'T** use any banned word in rendered copy. No exclamation marks in product copy, no emoji in the widget, never lead with "AI."
- **DO** signal premium through discipline and air — tight grids, exact alignment, generous whitespace, deliberate pacing. Pair the tiny input (one `<script>` tag) with the large output (a complete profile, a replaced hire).
- **DON'T** let motion or imagery get playful, bouncy, or mysterious. Keep the Magician payoff grounded — the thread "appears," but always tied to concrete, cited data.
- **DON'T** let legacy working names leak onto any rendered surface. The brand is **DealThreads**, only DealThreads. If a category line is needed, use "conversational enrichment for inbound."
