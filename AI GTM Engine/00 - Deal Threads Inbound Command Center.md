## Daily Execution Blocks

### Block 1: Publish

Time: 10-15 minutes.

Action:

- Pick one post from the weekly pack.
- Lightly edit in your voice.
- Publish.
- Add soft CTA only if the post naturally supports it.

Best CTA:

"I am doing a few free Dead Form Teardowns this week. Send me your form URL if you want one."

### Block 2: Comment

Time: 15-20 minutes.

Action:

- Search LinkedIn for founder-led B2B, RevOps, HubSpot, demand gen, sales leadership, website conversion, demo request, inbound sales.
- Leave 10 comments.
- Do not pitch.
- Add smart observations about form quality, lead context, sales handoff, CRM quality, or speed-to-lead.

Comment goal:

Be visible as the person thinking clearly about the inbound handoff.

### Block 3: Converse

Time: 10-20 minutes.

Action:

- Reply to every comment.
- DM anyone who asks, likes repeatedly, or clearly fits ICP.
- Use the lightest possible CTA.

Default DM:

```text
Appreciate you engaging with that. I made a short Dead Form Audit for B2B teams that want to see whether their demo/contact form gives sales a buyer profile or just homework. Want me to send it?
```

If they say yes:

```text
Here you go: [link]

If you want, send me your form URL after you skim it and I will give you the quick version of what I would change.
```

### Block 4: Ask For The Form URL

Time: 5 minutes.

Action:

Ask one qualified person per day:

```text
Want me to take a look at your demo/contact form and show what sales still has to research after someone hits submit?
```

If yes:

```text
Send the form URL and I will record a quick teardown: what it captures now, what is missing, and what a rep-ready buyer profile could include.
```

### Block 5: Log

Time: 5 minutes.

Update:

- posts published.
- comments made.
- DMs started.
- audit downloads.
- form URLs submitted.
- teardowns delivered.
- calls booked.
- best buyer language.
- strongest objection.

```text
I made a 20-minute Dead Form Audit for this. Happy to send it if useful.
```

### Teardown CTA

```text

```

### Install CTA

```text
Want me to map what the lightweight install would look like for your site and CRM?
```

## Disqualification Rules

Do not spend teardown time if:

- no B2B sales motion.
- no real demo/contact form.
- no CRM or willingness to use one.
- average customer value is too low to justify the workflow.
- they want generic AI lead generation, not inbound handoff improvement.

## Minimum Viable Inbound Day

On low-energy days, do only this:

1. One useful LinkedIn comment.
2. One post or repost with a sharp observation.
3. One ask for a form URL.
4. One scoreboard update.

Never let the machine go completely cold.
