# First-Customer Sprint — Offer Brief

## Positioning spine

**You built the thing. I build the machine that gets it in front of buyers.**

The First-Customer Sprint helps founder-led B2B teams turn a vague product/service offer into a focused buyer-exposure system: clear ICP, proof asset, target accounts, outreach, follow-up, and weekly learning loop.

## Primary buyer

Founder-led B2B service/product companies with a real offer but weak distribution.

Best first ICP for Ryan's launch:
- AI automation agencies
- AI consultants
- RevOps / HubSpot / Clay / Make / n8n consultants
- B2B agencies or consultants selling $5k+ services
- Niche dev shops/productized service firms

Avoid for first sprint:
- broke indie hackers with no paid offer
- huge SaaS with existing GTM teams
- generic local SMBs unless using a specific workflow wedge
- people wanting broad “AI transformation” without sales urgency

## Pain

They have capability, demos, tools, or an offer — but not enough qualified buyer conversations.

Symptoms:
- website says “AI automation” or “streamline operations” but not a specific painful workflow
- no sharp first ICP
- no proof asset before asking for a call
- random posting/content instead of targeted exposure
- unclear outreach/follow-up loop
- founder keeps building instead of selling

## Entry offer: $500 GTM Leak Audit

Promise:
> I’ll audit your offer, website, proof assets, and current buyer-exposure path, then give you a 14-day plan to get the right prospects seeing the right proof.

Deliverables:
1. Current offer diagnosis
2. Best first ICP / anti-ICP
3. Pain and offer rewrite
4. Proof asset recommendation
5. First 10 target-account criteria or sample targets
6. Outreach copy
7. Follow-up path
8. 14-day buyer exposure plan
9. Loom walkthrough

CTA:
> Want me to send 2–3 quick observations from your current positioning?

## Core offer: $2,500 First-Customer Sprint

Promise:
> In 14 days, we build and run the first buyer-exposure loop: sharpen offer, define ICP, create proof assets, build targets, write outreach, track follow-up, and learn from replies.

Deliverables:
- sharpened offer and ICP scoring criteria
- 30–50 researched targets
- top 10 outreach-ready prospects
- 3–5 proof assets / mini teardowns / Loom scripts
- LinkedIn/email/call copy
- follow-up tracker
- call prep + objection bank
- daily/weekly money brief
- end-of-sprint learning report

Important boundary:
- We do **not** promise closed customers.
- We promise qualified exposure and evidence: replies, objections, calls, market feedback, or a clear diagnosis of what broke.

## Recurring offer: $1,500–$3,000/mo AI GTM Operator

Promise:
> I keep the GTM machine running: weekly target research, proof assets, outbound copy, follow-up tracking, reply learning, and market intelligence.

Ongoing deliverables:
- weekly top-target batch
- personalized openers / Loom scripts
- daily or weekly follow-up brief
- objection/reply handling
- pipeline learning log
- content/field-note ideas from market conversations

## Offer ladder

1. Free/public content or mini-observation
2. $500 GTM Leak Audit
3. $2,500 First-Customer Sprint
4. $1,500–$3,000/mo AI GTM Operator
5. $5,000+ custom GTM Command Center buildout

## Sales rule

Do not sell “Hermes,” “Ideabrowser,” or “AI workflows.” Sell the painful outcome:

> You need more of the right buyers seeing the right proof.
