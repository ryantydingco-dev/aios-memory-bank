# Daily Execution Checklist — First-Customer Sprint Sales

## Two-hour block

### 1. Pick today’s money metric

Choose one:
- new prospects researched
- messages sent
- mini-audits created
- follow-ups sent
- calls booked
- paid audits pitched

## 2. Prospecting — 30 min

Find 10 possible targets.

Must capture:
- founder/contact
- website
- current offer summary
- observed GTM leak
- LinkedIn/contact path

## 3. Mini-audits — 30 min

Create 2 quick reads:
- buyer clarity leak
- outcome clarity leak
- proof/CTA leak
- one sharper offer angle

## 4. Send — 30 min

Send 3–5 permission-based messages:

> Want me to send 2–3 quick observations?

## 5. Follow-up — 20 min

Follow up with anyone in:
- MESSAGE_SENT
- REPLIED_INTERESTED
- AUDIT_PITCHED
- NURTURE

## 6. Tracker update — 10 min

Update:
- status
- last touch
- next action
- notes/objection

## 7. Content/comment — 20 min

Post or comment one useful teardown insight.

Example themes:
- broad AI agency positioning
- offer rewrite before/after
- why “AI automation” is not a buyable offer
- proof assets before asking for calls
- founder-built product with no buyer exposure

## Weekly targets

- 50 prospects researched
- 15–25 contacted
- 5+ replies/engagements
- 2+ calls
- 1 paid audit target

## Rule

No new building until at least 10 high-fit prospects have seen a specific mini-audit or teardown.
