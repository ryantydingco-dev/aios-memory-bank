# 14-Day Action Plan

## Daily block structure

1. **Review — 10 min**: open tracker, pick highest-leverage actions.
2. **Research — 30 min**: verify 5 prospects or find 10 new ones.
3. **Mini-audit — 40 min**: create 1–2 quick observations for top prospects.
4. **Send/follow-up — 30 min**: send 3–5 messages or follow-ups manually.
5. **Log — 10 min**: update tracker and learning log.
6. **Public proof — 20 min**: post/comment one teardown insight.

## Day 1 — Set the machine live

- Verify 10 prospects from seed list.
- Create 3 mini-audits.
- Send 3–5 permission-based LinkedIn/email messages.
- Post one public “AI automation agency GTM leak” observation.

## Day 2 — First follow-up + more audits

- Follow up with Day 1 sends if appropriate.
- Verify 10 more prospects.
- Create 3 more mini-audits.
- Send 5 messages.

## Day 3 — Increase exposure

- Build/send 5 more messages.
- Create one stronger sample audit from a high-fit prospect.
- Post before/after offer rewrite.

## Day 4 — Reply handling

- Classify replies.
- Send quick observations to anyone who opted in.
- Pitch $500 audit only to engaged prospects.
- Add 10 more prospects.

## Day 5 — First audit pitch day

- Aim to pitch the full $500 audit to 1–2 prospects.
- Create follow-up copy for non-responders.
- Post a “what AI agencies get wrong in GTM” thread.

## Day 6 — Batch expansion

- Research 15 prospects.
- Create 3 mini-audits.
- Send 5–7 messages.

## Day 7 — Weekly learning review

- Count researched/contacted/replies/calls/audits pitched.
- Identify strongest opener and strongest ICP subtype.
- Rewrite positioning if needed.

## Day 8 — Double down on strongest subtype

- Build 20 prospects in the best subtype.
- Send 7 messages.
- Follow up with all open threads.

## Day 9 — Proof asset day

- Turn best mini-audit into sample asset.
- Send sample to warm prospects.
- Pitch audit again where appropriate.

## Day 10 — Call conversion day

- Push interested threads toward a short call or paid audit.
- Prepare call script and close language.

## Day 11 — Second wave

- Send 7–10 messages to refined list.
- Create 3 mini-audits.

## Day 12 — Objection cleanup

- Analyze objections.
- Patch offer language.
- Create objection-response snippets.

## Day 13 — Last push

- Follow up with every open thread.
- Send “still useful?” nudge.
- Pitch audit to any warm prospect.

## Day 14 — Sprint retro

- Score the offer.
- Decide: double down, change ICP, change proof asset, or pause.
- Package results into content/case study.
