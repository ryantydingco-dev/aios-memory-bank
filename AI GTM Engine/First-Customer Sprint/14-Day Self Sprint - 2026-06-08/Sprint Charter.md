## Thesis

If this system works, we should be able to use it on ourselves to create qualified buyer exposure for the new offer.

We are not trying to prove the full agency yet. We are trying to prove:

1. Can we find high-fit founder-led AI/B2B service prospects?
2. Can we create useful mini-audits fast?
3. Do prospects engage with “Want me to send 2–3 quick observations?”
4. Can we convert at least one interested reply into a paid $500 GTM Leak Audit?

Founder-led AI automation agencies / AI consultants / workflow automation consultants.

Why:
- They already believe in AI.
- Many have broad/vague offers.
- They need pipeline.
- They understand the value of GTM systems.
- They can pay for a $500 audit if the diagnosis is good.

## Success metrics

Minimum viable proof:
- 50 prospects researched
- 25 contacted
- 10 mini-audits created
- 5 replies/engagements
- 2 calls or detailed async conversations
- 1 paid audit pitched

Strong proof:
- 75+ prospects researched
- 40+ contacted
- 15 mini-audits created
- 10 replies/engagements
- 3+ calls
- 1 paid audit won or sprint-qualified opportunity

## Daily non-negotiable

No new product/build work until at least one of these happens each day:
- 3 new prospects contacted, or
- 2 mini-audits created, or
- all due follow-ups sent.
