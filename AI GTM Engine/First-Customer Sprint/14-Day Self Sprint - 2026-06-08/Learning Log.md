# Learning Log

## 2026-06-08 kickoff

Hypothesis: founder-led AI automation agencies with broad offers will be receptive to a GTM Leak Audit because many have technical capability but weak buyer-facing packaging.

Initial opener to test:
> Looked at your positioning. The AI capability is clear, but I couldn’t immediately tell which painful workflow a buyer should hire you for first. Want me to send 2–3 quick observations?

Things to watch:
- Do people object to “GTM Leak Audit” language?
- Do they prefer “offer audit,” “buyer exposure audit,” or “positioning teardown”?
- Does AI agency ICP have money or are they too early/broke?
- Are RevOps/HubSpot/Clay consultants better buyers than generic AI agencies?
