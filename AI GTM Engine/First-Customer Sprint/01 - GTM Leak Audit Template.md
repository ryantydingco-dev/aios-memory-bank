## 9. 14-day buyer exposure plan

Days 1–2: sharpen offer + ICP  
Days 3–4: build target list  
Days 5–7: create proof assets / mini teardowns  
Days 8–12: send outreach + follow-up  
Days 13–14: analyze replies, objections, calls, and next sprint decision

Success metric:
- 30–50 targets researched
- 15–25 contacted
- 5+ engaged/replied or clear market feedback
- 2+ calls attempted/booked target
- 1 paid next step target if signal is strong
