# Sample GTM Leak Audit — AI Automation Agency

> This is a public/anonymized sample to show the format of the $500 GTM Leak Audit.

## Client snapshot

- Company: Example AI Automation Agency
- Current positioning: “We help businesses streamline operations with custom AI agents and automations.”
- CTA: “Book a free consultation”
- Apparent buyer: broad SMB/business owner

## Executive diagnosis

The main GTM leak is not the AI capability. The leak is that the buyer cannot tell which expensive problem to buy first. “AI automation for businesses” sounds capable, but it creates homework for the prospect: they have to imagine the use case, quantify the pain, and decide whether the agency is relevant. The first fix is to pick one painful workflow and turn it into a buyer-facing offer.

## Biggest leaks

### Leak 1 — Buyer is too broad

“Businesses” is not a market. It makes outreach, proof, and referrals weaker.

Better first ICP examples:
- MSPs with renewal/invoice follow-up pain
- agencies with proposal/client onboarding bottlenecks
- mortgage/commercial finance brokers chasing borrower documents
- home-service companies missing inbound estimate requests

### Leak 2 — Outcome is too vague

“Streamline operations” is true but not buyable enough.

Sharper:
> We help MSPs automate invoice and renewal follow-up so account managers stop chasing clients manually.

Or:
> We help agencies turn messy inbound requests into qualified project briefs before the first call.

### Leak 3 — No proof asset before the call

The current CTA asks for a consultation before showing the buyer what is leaking.

Better proof asset:
- Workflow Leak Audit
- 2-minute teardown of their current intake/follow-up process
- before/after workflow map
- sample automated follow-up/report

### Leak 4 — No first-customer sprint loop

The current motion appears to rely on networking/content. That can work, but it is not a controlled test.

Better:
- 50 targeted accounts
- 10 mini-audits
- 20 personalized messages
- 5 follow-ups
- calls/offers tracked by objection

## Offer rewrite

Current:
> We help businesses streamline operations with custom AI agents.

Sharper version:
> We help founder-led agencies turn inbound requests into complete client briefs before the first sales call — without adding another form or CRM migration.

Alternate versions:
1. We help MSPs automate invoice and renewal follow-up so overdue accounts stop living in someone’s inbox.
2. We help B2B agencies turn scattered lead inquiries into qualified project briefs before callback.
3. We help commercial finance brokers chase missing borrower documents automatically so deals reach lenders faster.

## Recommended proof asset

**Workflow Leak Audit**

Show:
1. Current workflow: where manual follow-up/research happens
2. Cost of leak: delayed replies, manual admin, lost opportunities
3. New workflow: approval-gated AI assist that drafts, routes, or summarizes without going rogue
4. Example output: email draft, lead brief, invoice chase queue, or weekly owner report

## First 10 target-account criteria

Target:
- founder-led agencies/consultants with $5k+ services
- website has broad AI/process language
- founder active on LinkedIn
- no strong niche offer or proof asset

Search:
- “AI automation agency founder”
- “Make.com automation consultant”
- “AI workflow consultant for businesses”
- “custom AI agents agency”

## Outreach example

```text
Hey {{first_name}} — quick specific note.

Looked at {{company}}. The AI capability is clear, but from a buyer’s POV I couldn’t immediately tell which painful workflow someone should hire you for first.

I’m doing GTM Leak Audits for AI agencies/consultants — offer clarity, first ICP, proof asset, and a 14-day buyer exposure plan.

Want me to send 2–3 quick observations from your current positioning?
```

## 14-day buyer exposure plan

Days 1–2: pick first workflow wedge and rewrite offer  
Days 3–4: build 50-account target list  
Days 5–7: create 5 workflow leak mini-audits  
Days 8–12: send 20 targeted messages + follow-ups  
Days 13–14: analyze replies and decide whether to double down, pivot ICP, or change proof asset

## Recommendation

Do the $500 GTM Leak Audit first. If the founder agrees with the diagnosis and wants execution help, convert into the $2,500 First-Customer Sprint.
