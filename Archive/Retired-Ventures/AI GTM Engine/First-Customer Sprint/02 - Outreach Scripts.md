# First-Customer Sprint Outreach Scripts

## Core positioning line

You built the thing. I build the machine that gets it in front of buyers.

## Primary CTA

> Want me to send 2–3 quick observations?

This is better than asking for a call first.

---

# LinkedIn sequence — AI agencies / consultants

## Connection note

```text
Hey {{first_name}} — saw you’re building around AI automation/workflows. I’m mapping how AI agencies are packaging + selling right now. Would be good to connect.
```

## Message after acceptance

```text
Appreciate the add.

Quick specific note — looked at {{company/site}}. The AI capability is clear, but the offer feels a little broad from a buyer’s POV. I couldn’t immediately tell which painful workflow you’d want a prospect to buy first.

I’m doing GTM Leak Audits for AI agencies/consultants: offer clarity, first ICP, proof asset, and a 14-day target/outreach plan.

Want me to send over 2–3 quick observations from your current positioning?
```

## Follow-up 1

```text
Tiny example of what I mean:

If the offer is “AI automation for businesses,” most buyers don’t know where to start.

But if it’s “we help {{niche}} automate {{painful_workflow}} so {{business_outcome}},” it becomes much easier to sell.

Happy to send a quick read on where I’d narrow {{company}}.
```

## Follow-up 2

```text
No worries if not useful.

I’m mostly looking for founder-led AI/service teams that have built real capability but haven’t turned it into a clean buyer-facing sales motion yet.

That seemed potentially true from your site, so figured I’d ask.
```

## If they say yes

```text
Awesome. I’ll send the quick version.

If it’s useful, the full GTM Leak Audit is $500 — I map the offer leak, best first ICP, proof asset, first 10 target-account criteria, outreach copy, and 14-day buyer exposure plan.

But I’ll start with the quick read first.
```

---

# Cold email version

Subject options:
- quick note on {{company}}’s AI offer
- small GTM leak I noticed
- buyer-facing offer thought for {{company}}

```text
Hey {{first_name}},

Looked at {{company}} — quick specific thought.

The AI/workflow capability is clear, but from the outside I couldn’t immediately tell the first painful workflow a buyer should hire you for.

That’s usually where AI agencies leak pipeline: the build capability is real, but the buyer-facing offer is too broad to create urgency.

I’m testing a GTM Leak Audit for founder-led AI agencies/consultants. It maps:
- sharper first ICP
- clearer paid offer
- proof asset before the sales call
- first 10 target-account criteria
- 14-day outreach/follow-up plan

Want me to send 2–3 quick observations from your current positioning?

Ryan
```

---

# Warm network ask

```text
I’m testing a new offer for founder-led B2B teams.

It’s a GTM Leak Audit: I look at their offer/site/outreach and show why it’s not turning into enough buyer conversations, then map a 14-day buyer exposure plan.

Best fit is AI agencies, consultants, or B2B service founders with a real offer but weak pipeline.

Know anyone who’s built something decent but isn’t getting it in front of enough buyers?
```

---

# Call close for $500 audit

```text
My recommendation would be to start with the GTM Leak Audit.

I’ll map the clearest buyer wedge, what your current GTM is missing, the proof asset we should use, and the first 14-day outreach plan.

It’s $500. If we decide to run the sprint after, I’ll credit it toward the buildout.
```

---

# Upsell to $2,500 sprint

```text
The audit gives you the map.

If you want me to actually build and run the first buyer-exposure loop, that’s the First-Customer Sprint.

Over 14 days I’ll help sharpen the offer, build the target list, create proof assets, write outreach/follow-ups, prep calls, and keep the daily sales loop moving.

That sprint is $2,500. The goal is not magic “guaranteed customers”; it’s getting the right buyers to see the right proof and producing real market evidence: replies, objections, calls, or a clear diagnosis of what needs to change.
```
