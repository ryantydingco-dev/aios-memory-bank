# Prospect Scoring and Research SOP

## First campaign ICP

AI automation agencies / AI consultants / B2B workflow consultants with vague positioning and visible founder/operator.

## Search phrases

LinkedIn / Google:
- AI automation agency founder
- AI automation consultant
- custom AI agents for businesses
- AI workflow consultant
- Make.com consultant AI automation
- n8n consultant AI automation
- Zapier automation agency
- AI systems consultant
- AI transformation consultant
- RevOps automation consultant
- HubSpot consultant AI workflows
- Clay consultant outbound automation

## Must-have filters

- founder/operator visible
- active or semi-active LinkedIn profile
- has website or landing page
- sells B2B service/productized service
- current positioning is broad enough to audit
- likely deal size $2k+
- can plausibly pay $500 audit

## Disqualifiers

- no website or no visible offer
- obvious large mature agency with strong GTM already
- only sells courses/templates to consumers
- pure local SMB with no B2B offer
- crypto/gambling/adult/sketchy verticals
- no identifiable person to contact

## Scoring — 100 points

- Founder-led/reachable decision maker: 20
- Clear paid service/product exists: 20
- Vague or leaky GTM positioning: 20
- Active enough to likely care now: 15
- Strong fit for AI/GTM audit language: 15
- Evidence quality / personalization hook: 10

## Mini-audit formula

For each strong prospect, write 3 observations:

1. **Buyer clarity leak** — who is this obviously for?
2. **Outcome clarity leak** — what specific expensive workflow is removed?
3. **Proof/CTA leak** — what proof should appear before asking for a call?

Then add one recommended rewrite:

> Instead of “{{current_broad_offer}},” test “We help {{specific_buyer}} {{specific_workflow_outcome}} without {{friction}}.”

## Daily execution block

2-hour version:
1. 30 min — find/score 10 prospects
2. 30 min — create 2 mini-audits
3. 30 min — send 3–5 messages
4. 20 min — follow up with previous prospects
5. 10 min — update tracker

Weekly target:
- 50 researched
- 15–25 contacted
- 5+ replies/engagements
- 2+ calls
- 1 paid audit target
