## Problem

Most early B2B offers don’t fail because the founder can’t build.

They fail because the right buyers never see a clear enough reason to care.

Common leaks:
- your offer is too broad
- your ICP is fuzzy
- your website asks for a call before showing proof
- your outreach sounds like everyone else
- follow-up lives in your head
- every week creates more ideas, not more buyer conversations

## What I do

I help you turn the messy “we need customers” problem into a focused buyer-exposure loop:

3. build a target list
4. create proof assets/teardowns
5. write outreach and follow-up
6. track replies, calls, objections, and next actions
7. learn what the market actually says
