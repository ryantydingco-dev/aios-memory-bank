## Why this beachhead

- **Your unfair advantage applies.** You reach agency owners as a *peer who built a tool*, not a vendor cold-pitching a stranger — the one edge "no warm doors" can't erase.
- **Instant pain recognition.** Agencies live on lead quality + CPL (for clients and themselves). No education needed.
- **Product unchanged.** Firmographic enrichment still applies — no repositioning pivot.
- **Less saturated / less skeptical** than SaaS RevOps; owner-operators buy fast.

## The channel multiplier (the strategic reason)

Agencies are not 1 customer = 1 install. One agency can deploy DealThreads across **every client whose ads they run** — a managed add-on they bill for.

- **Direct (start here):** DealThreads on the agency's *own* site → qualify their inbound → $500 pilot. Simple, fast, low-risk.
- **Channel (the prize):** "Add an AI lead-qualification layer to every client you run ads for — a recurring line item; we white-glove it." **1 agency → 5–20 downstream installs.** It's a *make-you-money* pitch, not a cost — which is also your strongest cold hook.

Sequence: land the direct pilot, prove it on their own funnel, then open the channel conversation.

## The ICP

### Best-fit (Tier 1)
- **Type:** B2B marketing / digital / performance-media / growth / web / lead-gen agencies
- **Size:** 5–50 employees · **Revenue:** ~$500K–$10M
- **Deal size:** sells **$10K+ retainers / projects**
- **Runs paid ads** (for clients and themselves) → obsessed with CPL & lead quality
- **Simple stack:** HubSpot / Pipedrive / GoHighLevel / spreadsheets
- **Geo:** US / Canada (English-speaking) to start

### Champion / buyer
- **Agency Owner / Founder / CEO** (small agency = owner decides fast)
- Secondary: Head of Growth / New Business / Client Services

### Trigger signals (rank higher)
- Positions around **lead gen / demand gen / performance marketing** (lead quality *is* their product)
- **Active Meta / Google ads** (check the ad libraries)
- **Owner active on LinkedIn** (reachable + community access)
- Uses **GoHighLevel / HubSpot** (lead-tooling-aware → gets the value fast)
- Hiring / visibly growing (budget + pain)

### Disqualify
- Solo freelancers (too small to pay or deploy) · pure brand/creative shops (no lead-gen motion) · 100+ employees (slower) · agencies that already built their own lead tooling
