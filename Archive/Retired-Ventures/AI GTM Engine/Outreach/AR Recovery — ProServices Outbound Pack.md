## The Offer (Grand Slam)

**External name:** The 30-Day Cash Recovery System
**Internal angle:** you stop chasing people for money; the chasing runs itself.

### One-sentence promise
```
I put the money you're already owed back in your account — automatically — in 30 days.
You don't chase anyone, you don't touch anything, and if it doesn't pay for itself, you don't pay.
```

### The ladder (sequenced — soft for cold, paid for warm, guaranteed for ready)
1. **Free AR Leak Check** *(cold opener / breaks the ice)* — a 15-min look at their aging report; you eyeball roughly how much is stuck and how old. No deliverable, just the conversation. This is the value-first front door (same role your form-teardown plays in the other motion).
2. **AR Recovery Audit — $500** *(for the interested; 100% credited to the build)* — a mapped one-pager: total outstanding, aging buckets, oldest debts, how much is "just not chased," projected 30-day recovery. Gets you paid to scope, and the number sells the build.
3. **Done-For-You AR Recovery — $2,000 one-time** *(the close; 50% up front)* — the system wired to their billing tool: tone-matched escalating reminders in their voice, weekly "who owes you" digest, escalation-to-call flags. Live in 7 days.
4. **Managed AR — +$500/mo** *(optional MRR upsell)* — adds automated renewal invoicing, monthly tuning, and a monthly collections report. (Mirror of your proven $1.5–2.5K + monthly pattern.)

### The guarantee (this is the "stupid to say no" part)
```
Recover at least 3x your fee in collected invoices within 30 days,
or full refund — and you keep the system.
```
Why it's safe for you: you only offer the guaranteed build to firms whose **audit showed real recoverable AR**. The money is genuinely there, so the guarantee almost never triggers. The audit de-risks the guarantee for you as much as it sells them.

### Value stack (anchor high, price low)
- AR Recovery Audit — every stuck invoice mapped (──$500 value)
- Done-for-you chase system wired to QuickBooks/Xero/Stripe
- Tone-matched reminder sequences — gentle for good clients, firm for repeat-late
- Automated renewal invoicing — never forget to bill again
- Weekly "who still owes you" report
- Escalation flags — which ones need a call
- 30 days managed and tuned
- Guarantee: 3x or refund + keep the system

### Risk reversal (audit)
```
If your audit doesn't show real recoverable money sitting in overdue invoices,
I'll tell you straight and not pitch the build. You'll just have the map.
```

### Operational scarcity (real, not fake)
```
I only take a few of these a month because each build is wired by hand to your billing tool
and tuned weekly. So it's first-come.
```

### Qualification questions (before the build)
1. What are you invoicing out of today — QuickBooks, Xero, Stripe, something else?
2. Roughly how much is sitting past 30 days right now?
3. Who chases late payers today — you, an admin, or nobody?
4. Is it mostly recurring/renewal clients or one-off projects?
5. What would make the first 30 days a clear win?

### The DIY-disarm (accountants will say "I can do this myself")
```
100% you can — for your clients. But your OWN invoices? Cobbler's kids.
You're too busy doing their books to chase your own money, and chasing your own clients
is the most awkward version of it. That's the whole point: I do it so you never think about it.
```

## Audit → Build delivery flow

1. They say yes to the audit → invoice $500 (credited to the build).
2. Get read access to their billing tool (QuickBooks/Xero/Stripe) or a CSV export of open invoices.
3. Produce the **AR Recovery Map** (one-pager): total outstanding · aging buckets (0–30 / 30–60 / 60–90 / 90+) · oldest debts · how much is "just not chased" · projected 30-day recovery.
4. Present on a call: "Here's $X stuck. Here's the $Y we pull in 30 days. Build is $2K, guaranteed 3x or refund, and your $500 comes off it."
5. Close → wire the chase → live in 7 days.

**Delivery engine:** Claude for Small Business drafts the tone-matched reminders; the invoice-chase capability pulls overdue data from QuickBooks/PayPal/Stripe. Per client = connect data → set tone + intervals → go live. A day or two of configuring. **Build once, reuse the sequence forever. Do not build until the audit is paid or the 50% deposit is in.**
