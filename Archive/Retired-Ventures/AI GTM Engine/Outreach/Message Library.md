# Deal Threads Message Library

Last updated: 2026-06-02  
Purpose: copy-paste outbound language for turning weak demo/contact forms into Dead Form Teardowns, install calls, and beta clients.

## Positioning Principle

Do not sell "AI chat" or "lead enrichment."

Sell the visible gap:

> Your form captured the lead. Sales still has to build the buyer profile manually.

## Message Formula

Specific form observation -> sales homework gap -> buyer-profile preview -> low-friction teardown CTA.

## Approved Tone

- useful.
- specific.
- calm.
- short.
- operator-to-operator.
- never over-explains AI.

## Avoid

- "just checking in."
- "quick question" with no proof.
- "revolutionize."
- "AI SDR."
- "we help companies like yours."
- asking for 30 minutes before giving value.

## Core Deal Threads Explanation

```text
Deal Threads replaces thin demo/contact forms with an AI intake widget that asks the few questions sales actually needs, enriches company context behind the scenes, and pushes a rep-ready buyer profile into the CRM before callback.
```

## Default Teardown Ask

```text
I looked at your demo/contact form and noticed it captures the lead, but probably still leaves sales doing the buyer-profile research manually.

Want me to send over the quick teardown?
```
