# Ryan Sway Daily Operating System

## Purpose
Give Ryan and Sway a repeatable daily system that turns AI Arc/research data into meetings and revenue.

## Daily Target
20 high-confidence actions per day:
- Ryan: 10
- Sway: 10

A high-confidence action is not just a lead. It is a specific person + signal + message + channel + next step.

## Daily Workflow

### Step 1 — Select the Market Focus
Choose one focus for the day:
- UK commercial finance brokers
- US commercial/private lending/mortgage brokers
- Canada commercial mortgage/finance only
- SBA/equipment/factoring expansion segment later

### Step 2 — Pull Candidates
Start from existing files before sourcing net-new:
- HOT UK enriched
- HOT US enriched after commercial filter
- HOT CA enriched after finance filter
- WARM if HOT is exhausted

### Step 3 — Quality Gate
Every daily lead must pass:
- correct market/persona
- evidence-backed signal
- plausible document/follow-up/lender-package pain
- reachable contact
- opener that references real evidence

If any of those are missing, mark WATCHLIST or NEEDS_RESEARCH.

### Step 4 — Assign Ryan vs Sway
Suggested split:
- Ryan: highest-personalization leads, founder/owner/partner, strongest signal, LinkedIn-first
- Sway: strong verified-email leads, campaign-ready segments, follow-up and Smartlead motions

Use company balance: avoid giving both Ryan and Sway multiple people at the same company on the same day unless it is intentional.

### Step 5 — Execute Outreach
Preferred sequence:
1. LinkedIn connection or message for Tier 1.
2. Email for verified, campaign-ready leads.
3. Loom only for strongest prospects or after interest/acceptance unless Sway prefers otherwise.

### Step 6 — Log Outcome
For each contact, record:
- sent date
- channel
- message angle
- reply status
- objection
- booked meeting yes/no
- next follow-up date

## What Counts As Winning
- booked meeting
- positive reply asking for more info
- objection that teaches positioning
- lead disqualified for a clear reason
- new pattern discovered in market

## What Does Not Count
- raw lead count
- generic emails sent
- CSV files no one acts on
- leads with no evidence
- fake personalization
