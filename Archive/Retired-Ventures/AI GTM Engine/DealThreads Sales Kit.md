## 0. Positioning spine (use this language everywhere)

**One-liner:**
> DealThreads replaces your contact form with an AI chat that captures intent, budget, and timeline — and hands your rep a fully-researched buyer profile before they call back.

**The problem narrative (your cold-open story):**
> A contact form returns a name and an email. The data that decides whether the deal closes — company size, decision-makers, recent funding, tech stack, ICP fit — sits behind 20 minutes of research the form refused to do. The buyer already typed it. Your rep Googles it ten minutes later. The deal stalls in the gap.

**The shift you're selling:** static form → a 60-second conversation that captures *why they're here, what they can spend, and when* — while enrichment runs behind the scenes. The rep opens the lead at first contact with the full picture.

**Why now (say this out loud):** Drift got acquired and gutted; Qualified moved upmarket to enterprise-only. Mid-market teams running $10K+ deals lost their conversational-capture tooling and have no managed replacement. That window is open *now*.

**The offer ladder:** $500 pilot → $2,500/mo core (widget + enrichment + routing) → $5,000/mo premium (signal monitoring + monthly intelligence reports + ICP gap campaigns). Standalone reports SKU at ~$1,000/mo if they only want intelligence.

## 1. The One-Page Offer (paste into a deck / PDF / email)

### Turn your contact form into your best SDR

**What it is:** an AI chat widget (one line of code) that replaces your contact form. It has a short, natural conversation with every inbound visitor, captures **intent, budget, and timeline**, enriches the company in the background (size, funding, decision-makers, tech stack), scores ICP fit, and drops a complete buyer profile into your CRM — routed to the right rep within minutes.

**What you get in the 30-day pilot:**
- Widget installed on one high-traffic page (pricing, demo, or contact)
- Every lead captured with intent + budget + timeline, not just name/email
- An enriched, scored profile pushed to your HubSpot/Salesforce
- A weekly read on what's converting and which questions predict good deals

**How it works:** Install (1 line) → Converse (60 sec) → Enrich (behind the scenes) → Route + sync to CRM (minutes).

**Investment:** **$500** one-time pilot setup. **30 days.** If it's working, it converts to **$2,500/mo** — no long-term contract, cancel anytime. (Replaces ~$280K/yr of analyst + coordinator headcount at the full tier.)

**What we ask of you:** you're an early design partner. White-glove setup from the founder, a discounted rate locked for 12 months, and a direct line into the roadmap.

**The bar we hold ourselves to:** a measurable lift in qualified-meeting conversion vs. your current form, and faster time-to-first-meeting on DealThreads leads — inside the pilot window.

## 2. Ideal Customer Profile (who to target)

**Fit (all/most true):**
- Mid-market B2B, ~50–500 employees, selling **$10K+ ACV** deals
- Has a sales team (AEs/SDRs) but **no dedicated RevOps analyst or sales coordinator**
- Runs **inbound through a static contact form** on a site with real traffic
- Uses **HubSpot or Salesforce**
- Verticals to start: **B2B SaaS, fintech, professional/managed services**

**Best-fit trigger signals (prioritize these accounts):**
- 🎯 **Former Drift or Qualified customers** — abandoned by their tool, actively shopping. This is your hottest list. Find them via BuiltWith/Wappalyzer (sites still running Drift/Qualified scripts) and ex-Drift communities.
- Recently raised funding (budget + growth pressure)
- Hiring SDRs/RevOps (feeling the lead-handling pain)
- Complaining publicly about lead response time or CRM data quality

**Disqualifiers:** pure B2C, sub-$2K ACV/transactional, no website traffic, enterprise procurement (12-month cycles — not for beta), already have a mature RevOps team + tooling.

**Champion vs. economic buyer:** champion = RevOps / Sales Ops manager or a Head of Demand Gen. Economic buyer = VP Sales / VP Marketing / founder. Sell the champion, arm them to sell up.

## 5. Discovery call framework (20 min)

**The 4 qualifying questions:**
1. "Walk me through what happens when someone fills out your contact form today." (find the gap)
2. "How fast does a rep actually follow up — and what do they research first?" (quantify the pain)
3. "What's a deal worth to you, roughly?" (confirm $10K+ ACV)
4. "HubSpot or Salesforce?" (confirm you can deliver)

**Qualify for the pilot if:** $10K+ ACV, real form traffic, HubSpot/SF, a named champion who feels the pain.

**Objection handling:**
- *"Is it built / does it work?"* → "You're an early design partner — that's why it's $500 and not $2,500, and why you get me directly. I'll show you a live demo on your own site so there's no hand-waving."
- *"How do I know it'll convert better?"* → "That's exactly what the 30-day pilot measures, against your current form as the baseline. If it doesn't move qualified meetings, you don't continue."
- *"$2,500/mo is a lot."* → "It replaces the analyst-plus-coordinator work you'd otherwise hire for — about $280K/yr at the full tier. The pilot is $500 to prove it first."
- *"We're busy / bad timing."* → "Install is one line of code and I do the setup. Your team does nothing until qualified leads start showing up in your CRM."

## 6. The demo (3 options, ranked by impact-per-effort)

- **A) Loom + clickable mockup (fastest, day 1):** record a 3-min walkthrough of the conversation flow on a mockup. Good for email/DM. Lowest effort, lowest impact.
- **B) Live demo site (recommended, build once):** a real DealThreads widget on a fake company's site (e.g. `demo.dealthreads.com`) running the actual conversation. Reusable in every call. This is the sweet spot.
- **C) "Your-own-site" demo (the closer):** before/during the call, stand up the widget configured for *their* homepage and let them talk to it as their own visitor. Highest effort, highest close rate — use it on your most-qualified prospects. (This doubles as the start of their pilot setup.)

Lead with B in calls; deploy C to close the accounts you most want.
