# ICP Map

## Primary ICP
Commercial finance/mortgage brokers and debt advisors with document-heavy deal workflows.

## Core Buyer Problem
Deals stall before lender review because borrower documents arrive messy, incomplete, inconsistently named, or require repeated follow-ups. The broker/team wastes capacity turning borrower chaos into lender-ready packages.

## Firmographic Filters
- Geography: US first, then UK/Canada based on available lead data; UK commercial brokers currently look cleanest
  - ⚠️ **UNVALIDATED (2026-05-31):** the first 20-lead triage contradicted "UK cleanest" — US was more send-ready (US 44% vs UK 18%), likely a UK-side verifiability artifact (gated LinkedIn), not proof US buyers convert better. Provisional, n=20, zero outcomes. Let the first real send batch arbitrate before re-weighting. See `Experiments/Weekly GTM Review - 2026-05-31.md`.
- Size: ~1–500 employees
- Business type: broker/advisor/intermediary, not generic bank unless document workflow is explicit
- Ability to pay: ACV around $10k

## Strong Fit Segments — Priority Order

### 1. UK Commercial Finance Brokers
Why strong:
- closest match to Eugene/Owusu proof
- titles and workflows are explicitly commercial finance
- existing HOT list has 26 rows and many clean fits

Buyer titles:
- Commercial Finance Broker
- Senior Commercial Finance Broker
- Commercial Credit Broker
- Founder / Managing Director / Partner

### 2. US Commercial / Private / Construction / Portfolio Lending Operators
Why strong:
- deal closings and private/construction lending imply docs, checklists, borrower follow-up, and speed pressure
- current US file is large but noisy, so filtering matters

Include titles/companies with:
- commercial loan officer
- private money lender
- construction lender
- portfolio lender
- CRE/multifamily/DSCR/business lending clues

### 3. Canada Commercial Mortgage / Finance Brokers
Why cautiously interesting:
- 145 enriched rows exist
- but many are commercial real estate brokers, which may not equal lending/document-intake pain

Include only if:
- mortgage/finance/debt/advisory is explicit
- not purely property brokerage

### 4. Adjacent Finance Verticals Later
- SBA lending brokers/advisors
- equipment finance brokers
- factoring / AR finance firms
- CRE debt advisors
- bridging/development finance brokers

## Disqualifiers
- residential-only mortgage loan officers unless strong doc/ops pain is explicit
- real estate agents/brokers without finance/debt workflow
- consumer lending with low document complexity
- generic banks with no reachable buyer or broker-like process
- leads with no signal and no current reason

## Persona Split

### Founder/Owner/Managing Partner
Best angle:
- capacity and revenue throughput
- more lender-ready submissions without adding admin headcount
- fewer borrower follow-ups

### Senior Broker / Commercial Finance Broker
Best angle:
- less document chase on active deals
- cleaner packages and fewer annoying follow-ups
- time back for borrowers/lenders/referral partners

### Ops/Admin/Processor Leader
Best angle:
- exceptions-only workflow
- missing-doc flags
- borrower nudges
- cleaner folders/file names

## ICP Rule
A lead only becomes outreach-ready when it has:
1. commercial/debt/document-heavy workflow fit
2. a recent or strong behavioral signal
3. a plausible Oloxa pain hypothesis
4. a reachable decision maker/champion
