# Oloxa GTM Brain

## Purpose
Central source of truth for Oloxa GTM strategy, market learning, lead quality rules, and messaging lessons.

## Core Positioning
Oloxa helps commercial finance brokers/debt advisors automate deal paperwork by sorting, renaming, and filing borrower docs, flagging missing docs, and automating borrower follow-ups so lender-ready packages move faster.

## Buyer
- Commercial finance brokers
- Mortgage brokers with commercial/debt desks
- Debt advisors
- Managing partners, founders, owners, principals, senior brokers, ops leaders

## Primary Pain Hypotheses
- Borrowers send messy documents.
- Teams spend too much time chasing missing docs.
- Submission packages get delayed before lenders can review.
- Admin/ops work eats broker capacity.
- Multi-lender/package complexity creates avoidable back-and-forth.

## Proof Points Allowed
- 7+ hours saved per deal
- 97% doc classification accuracy
- 0 follow-up emails after automation
- Eugene/Owusu proof acceptable when relevant

## Message Style Rules
- Casual and indirect.
- Outcome-driven around time saved and cleaner lender packages.
- Avoid aggressive wording: wasting time, broken, drowning, replace admin.
- AI is the engine, not the headline.

## Current Open Questions
- Which segment replies best: commercial mortgage, SBA, equipment finance, factoring/AR, CRE debt?
- Which signal is strongest: closings, hiring, volume, speed promise, document complexity?
- Which message angle lands: fewer follow-ups, lender-ready packages, faster submissions, saved admin time?

## Learnings Log

### 2026-05-31 — Pre-launch triage (PROVISIONAL: n=20 battlecards, ZERO outreach sent)
> Source: `Experiments/Weekly GTM Review - 2026-05-31.md`. These measure the lead pipeline (verifiability + fit), NOT buyer behavior. No market claims yet — nothing has been sent. Confirm or kill each once real replies exist.

- **[PROVISIONAL] Lead quality is gated by signal RE-VERIFIABILITY, not signal relevance.** 35% of cards were recency-compromised before any fit judgment; most REVIEW/HOLD were gated LinkedIn (HTTP 999) or model-mismatch. → Add "re-verifiable from a public, non-gated source" as a **Lead Scoring Rubric** input, weighted *ahead of* signal type.
- **[PROVISIONAL] PAIN is highest-relevance but lowest-currently-sourceable.** PAIN (taxonomy #1) produced 0 of 6 SEND, but 3/3 went to REVIEW (0 HOLD) — a sourcing gap, not a weak signal. → Don't deprioritize PAIN; give it a dedicated non-gated evidencing method (Trustpilot/Google reviews, public job-post language, blogs/forums).
- **[PROVISIONAL] CLOSING is the most abundant + re-verifiable signal** (55% of batch, public deal posts), so it should fuel the first send batch — but its conversion edge is unproven (36% within-type vs 30% avg). Treat as "easiest to evidence," not "most likely to book."
- **[PROVISIONAL] The ICP "UK cleaner than US" assumption is UNVALIDATED and was contradicted this batch** (US 44% vs UK 18% send-ready). Likely a UK-side verifiability artifact. → Don't re-weight toward UK on the ICP doc's strength alone; let batch-one outcomes arbitrate. (Mirror note added to `ICP Map.md`.)
- **[PROVISIONAL] HIRING needs entity-resolution + a buyer-side (broker-vs-lender) check before send.** 50% of HIRING leads hard-failed (HOLD): one misattributed to a *different firm's* recruiter ad, one lender-side. → Confirm the hiring firm IS the target firm, and that it's a broker, not a lender.
- **[PROCESS] QA rewrote 60% of cards** (brand/voice/claim). → Fold the 3 most common fixes into the *generation* prompt, not just the QA pass.
