# Oloxa 90-Day Meeting Game Plan

## Goal
Book as many qualified Oloxa meetings as possible over the next 90 days while building a repeatable AI-powered GTM system Ryan can later package as a service/product.

## Revenue Math
Oloxa ACV target is roughly ~$10k. If Ryan/Sway need ~4 closes/month combined, the near-term engine should optimize for:

- more qualified meetings with commercial finance operators
- better reply quality
- faster learning loops
- clearer proof/positioning
- fewer wasted touches on weak-fit residential/generic leads

## Strategic Diagnosis
The current lead universe already has fuel. The bottleneck is not raw leads; it is **routing the right leads into the right outreach motion with strong evidence and fast feedback**.

Existing source data includes:
- 5,061 verified-email leads that passed a fit screen
- 936 behavioral leads
- 138 original HOT leads from US/UK
- 329 WARM leads
- 469 MILD leads
- 4,125 NONE leads
- separate enriched CA file with 145 rows, but many are real estate brokers and need stricter filtering

## 90-Day Strategy

### Phase 1 — Foundation + Fast Activation (Days 1–14)
Purpose: turn existing AI Arc data into a disciplined daily meeting machine.

#### Priorities
1. Stop treating all leads equally.
2. Separate commercial finance from residential/general lending.
3. Prioritize HOT/WARM leads with CLOSING, HIRING, VOLUME, or PAIN.
4. Create Ryan/Sway daily action batches.
5. Track every sent message, reply, objection, and meeting.
6. Validate top 2–3 positioning angles.

#### Daily Output
- 20 outreach-ready lead actions/day total
  - 10 Ryan
  - 10 Sway
- each row must include signal, evidence, opener, recommended channel, and next action

#### Success Metrics
- 100–200 prioritized actions taken
- first 5–15 replies
- first qualified meetings booked
- clear signal/message learnings

#### Primary Experiments
- UK commercial finance brokers vs US loan officers/commercial lenders
- CLOSING signal vs HIRING signal
- lender-ready package angle vs follow-up automation angle
- LinkedIn-first vs email-first for verified emails

### Phase 2 — Message + Segment Optimization (Days 15–30)
Purpose: identify the highest-response segment + signal + opener combo.

#### Priorities
1. Review reply data weekly.
2. Split campaigns by segment/persona.
3. Build separate scripts for founders/owners vs senior practitioners.
4. Retire weak messaging quickly.
5. Tighten proof around Eugene/Owusu and 7+ hours saved.

#### Success Metrics
- 300–400 total high-quality touches by day 30
- 10–30 replies
- 5–10 qualified meetings booked if market/message fit is real
- one clear leading segment

#### Likely Campaign Splits
1. UK commercial finance brokers — closest to Eugene/Owusu proof.
2. US commercial loan officers / private lenders / commercial mortgage — only if commercial/document-heavy.
3. Canada commercial mortgage/finance — stricter filter; watch out for real estate-only brokers.
4. Warm/Mild nurture — signal-aware but lower personalization depth.

### Phase 3 — Scale the Proven Motions (Days 31–60)
Purpose: scale what works without burning domains, relationships, or attention.

#### Priorities
1. Load only qualified segments into Smartlead.
2. Keep LinkedIn white-glove for Tier 1.
3. Add fresh signals from news/job/company pages before outreach.
4. Build weekly market intelligence summaries.
5. Start producing content/case-study notes from lessons.

#### Success Metrics
- 600–800 total qualified touches by day 60
- repeatable meeting source identified
- 10–25 qualified meetings cumulative depending on reply rate
- clear objection library
- clear ICP changes based on market feedback

### Phase 4 — Case Study + Packaging (Days 61–90)
Purpose: turn Oloxa progress into a durable GTM asset and external offer.

#### Priorities
1. Document before/after system impact.
2. Capture winning signals and conversion data.
3. Package the workflow as an AI GTM Engine service.
4. Identify 20 external companies that could buy a similar system later.
5. Do not sell external offer aggressively until Oloxa has credible results.

#### Success Metrics
- Oloxa meeting engine is running weekly
- Ryan/Sway have a clear daily workflow
- first case study draft exists
- external offer has proof-backed messaging

## Daily Operating Cadence

### Morning — Lead Selection
- Pull 20 highest-priority leads from current batch.
- Confirm each has evidence and a specific opener.
- Split 10 Ryan / 10 Sway.

### Midday — Outreach Execution
- Send LinkedIn connection/message or email depending on channel.
- Log touch, channel, and message angle.

### Afternoon — Replies / Follow-Up
- Process replies.
- Book meetings or send Loom/demo where relevant.
- Mark objections.

### End of Day — Learning Capture
- Which lead felt strongest?
- Which signal felt strongest?
- Which opener was used?
- Any reply/objection?
- What should change tomorrow?

## Weekly Operating Cadence

### Monday
Choose the week’s focus segment and signal.

### Tuesday–Thursday
Execute daily Ryan/Sway batches and capture replies.

### Friday
Run weekly GTM review:
- top leads
- signals that worked
- replies/objections
- message edits
- next week’s focus

## Best Current Focus
Start with:
1. UK commercial finance brokers from HOT/WARM lists.
2. US leads only where the title/company/product suggests commercial, private money, construction, CRE, portfolio, or business lending.
3. Canada only after stricter filtering to remove real estate-only brokerage noise.

## Pushback / Guardrails
- Do not send the 4,125 NONE leads yet.
- Do not dump broad lists into Smartlead.
- Do not spend weeks perfecting the system before daily outreach starts.
- Do not use one generic sequence across UK commercial brokers and US residential-ish loan officers.
- Do not lead with "AI." Lead with lender-ready packages and fewer borrower follow-ups.
- Do not confuse activity with revenue progress. Meetings and learning are the scoreboard.
