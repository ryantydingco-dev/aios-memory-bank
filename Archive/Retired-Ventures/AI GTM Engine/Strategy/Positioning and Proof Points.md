# Positioning and Proof Points

## Positioning Principle
Do not sell "AI." Sell the expensive bottleneck removed.

## Current Best Positioning
Oloxa helps commercial finance brokers turn messy borrower docs into lender-ready packages faster, with fewer follow-ups and less admin drag.

## Outcome Language
- Cleaner lender packages
- Fewer borrower follow-ups
- Faster submission readiness
- Less broker/admin time lost to document chasing
- More deals moved without adding headcount

## Proof Points
- 7+ hours saved per deal
- 97% document classification accuracy
- 0 follow-up emails

## Phrases to Avoid
- "You're wasting time"
- "Your process is broken"
- "You're drowning in admin"
- "Replace your admin"
- Overhyping AI as the headline

## Safer Message Frame
"Usually the annoying part isn't finding borrowers — it's getting docs clean enough for lender submission without a dozen back-and-forths."
