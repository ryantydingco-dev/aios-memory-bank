# AR Market Validation — Deep Research Brief

Date: 2026-06-09
Method: 111-agent deep-research harness — 28 sources fetched, 120 claims extracted → 25 verified, **15 confirmed / 10 killed** by 2-of-3 adversarial vote.

## VERDICT: QUALIFIED GO
The **pain is real, big, urgent, near-universal** — go test cold now. But the *"market is actively BUYING / deals close FAST"* half is **unproven by data** — only live outreach proves it. Lead with the **agency wedge + a cash-flow message**, and **guard the guarantee**.

---

## ✅ Confirmed (lead with these — all fact-checked)
- **56% of SMBs are currently owed money on unpaid invoices, ~$17,500 avg each**; ~half of all B2B invoices run overdue (only ~42% paid on time, ~8% bad debt); overdue invoices that get paid arrive ~**20 days late**. *(QuickBooks Intuit 2025, Atradius US 2024)*
- **Most-affected SMBs are 1.4× more likely to hit cash-flow problems (50% vs 34%).** *(QuickBooks 2025)* → **Lead with CASH FLOW, not admin convenience.** This is the bridge from "annoying" to "urgent."
- **Agencies = the single sharpest, best-evidenced wedge:** 97% hit late payments in 2025; 63% say cash flow is "unpredictable"; **84% spend 3–10+ hrs/month chasing**; 82% delayed/cancelled hiring; 71% have ≥1 in 4 invoices late. *(PYMNTS / Ignition 2025)*
- **Voice of customer language to steal:** chasing is "deeply frustrating and demoralizing," "draining"; 75% of freelancers report negative mental-health impact. → use "stop nagging clients," "get paid faster."
- **Your $2K price is credible:** sits inside what SMBs already pay outsourced bookkeepers ($200–$1,000+/mo). → **price is a weak objection.** *(NerdWallet)*
- **AR automation's proven value = cut DSO 20–35% without adding headcount** — but measured only at mid-market/enterprise *software* users.

## ⚠️ What CHANGED vs our assumptions
- We (and the Hermes scoring) leaned **trades/restoration/construction** as the sharpest wedge. The research says **only AGENCIES have strong verified pain evidence** — and the **"construction has the most acute AR pain / highest DSO 60–90 days" claim was REFUTED** (didn't survive verification).
- This does **not** mean trades don't hurt — structurally they bill commercial/insurance on terms and absolutely chase. It means trades are an **unproven** wedge, not a proven one. **Test trades AND agencies live; let booked audits decide — don't assume.**

## 🚩 Risks to guard
- **THE GUARANTEE.** "Recover 3× the fee in 30 days or refund" rests on the 20–35% DSO-reduction figure — proven only at enterprise *software*, never for a 5-person firm via a *service*. **Refund risk.** HEDGE: **audit-gate it** — only extend the 3× guarantee to firms whose audit proves the recoverable AR is actually there. Test it on the first few clients before making it the headline.
- **BUYING-INTENT IS UNPROVEN.** No search-volume or buying-behavior claim survived verification. The evidence proves **suffering, not buying.** Only live reps answer "do they buy fast?"
- **"It's free in QuickBooks" objection** is unresolved — have the done-for-you rebuttal ready (they won't set it up themselves; it runs in their voice; it's managed).

## ❌ Stats to KILL — refuted, do NOT use in copy (they'll get you caught)
- "25% of bankruptcies caused by late payment"
- "$39K average annual late-payment cost"
- "65% spend ~14 hrs/week on collections"
- "Construction has the highest DSO at 60–90 days"
- "Payment recovery decays 65% → 15% over 14 days"
- "~$43/hr freelance bookkeeper"
- "73% say delinquency increased this year" / "55% of invoices overdue / 64% carry 90+ day invoices" / "70% have DSO above 46 days"

2. **Wednesday:** run the SC-trades calls **and** start agency outreach (you have agencies in the 1,000 pro-services list — LinkedIn now, email when warm). **Track audits booked BY SEGMENT** — that's your real wedge answer.
