# Deal Threads Dead Form Teardown Template

Last updated: 2026-06-02  
Purpose: create repeatable teardowns that convert weak forms into install conversations.

## Teardown Goal

The goal is not to criticize the website.

The goal is to show:

> Your form captures the lead, but sales still has to build the buyer profile manually.

Every teardown should make the next step obvious:

> Want me to map the lightweight install that would push this buyer profile into your CRM before callback?

## 15-Minute Teardown Workflow

### Step 1: Identify Current Form

- Company:
- Website:
- Form URL:
- Page type: demo / contact sales / pricing / product / other.

- Current fields:

- Does it ask for business need?
- Does it ask for timeline?
- Does it ask for budget/buying stage?
- Does it ask for CRM/tech stack?
- Does it ask for role/authority?

### Step 3: What The Form Captures Now

Write this plainly:

```text
Today, the form mostly captures:
- [field]
- [field]
- [field]

That is enough to notify sales that someone raised their hand.
It is not enough to tell sales who this buyer is, why they care, and what to say first.
```

### Step 4: What Sales Still Has To Research

Use this checklist:

```text
Sales probably still has to research:
- company size
- industry
- role/seniority
- decision authority
- recent funding/news
- tech stack
- current CRM
- likely pain
- urgency
- budget or buying stage
- ICP fit
- suggested opener
```

### Step 6: Three Better Questions

Recommend only questions that improve sales action:

1. `What are you trying to improve or solve right now?`
2. `When are you hoping to solve this?`
3. `What CRM or sales workflow are you using today?`

Adapt by context:

- For sales-led companies: ask timeline and team size.
- For RevOps: ask CRM and handoff problem.
- For demand gen: ask campaign/source and lead-quality issue.
- For founder-led teams: ask what happens after submit.

### Step 7: Behind-The-Scenes Enrichment

Suggested fields:

- employee range.
- industry.
- company description.
- tech stack.
- recent funding/news.
- hiring/growth signals.
- likely decision-makers.
- ICP segment.
- source page and campaign.

Rule:

Unknown stays unknown. Do not make up data.

### Step 8: CRM Handoff

Write what the rep should see:

```text
Deal Threads note:

[Name] from [Company] reached out from [page]. They appear to be [ICP segment] and are likely interested in [business need]. Timeline is [timeline/unknown]. Budget signal is [budget/unknown]. Company context: [size, industry, tech]. Recommended next action: [specific opener or follow-up].

Unknowns to confirm:
- [unknown 1]
- [unknown 2]
```

### Step 9: Install CTA

Use:

```text
The form is technically working, but it is making sales build the buyer profile manually.

Want me to map what the lightweight Deal Threads install would look like for this page and your CRM?
```
