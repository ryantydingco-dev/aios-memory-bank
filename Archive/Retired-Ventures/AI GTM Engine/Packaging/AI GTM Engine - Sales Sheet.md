## Stop guessing who to contact. Start every week knowing.

**[Prospect company]** doesn't have a lead problem. It has a *relevance* problem — too many contacts, not enough reasons to reach out *now*.

The AI GTM Engine fixes that. Every week, you get:

- **The right people** — prospects ranked by real buying signals (just closed a deal, hiring, scaling, showing pain), not job titles.
- **Why now** — a research card per lead: the signal, the evidence, the likely pain, and a personalized opener.
- **What to say** — ready-to-send messages for LinkedIn, email, calls, and SMS. Written, brand-checked, human.
- **Drop-in ready** — formatted for your CRM / Smartlead. No cleanup.
- **What's working** — a weekly review: best signals, best segments, objections heard, what to change next week.

All human-reviewed and capped-volume — so your domain stays clean and your messages don't read like a robot.

## Why not just…

**…hire an SDR?** A fully-loaded SDR runs ~$10–12k/month, takes 3–4 months to ramp, and leaves in ~18 months. This is live next week at a fraction of the cost — and the market learning stays with *you*.

**…use a lead-gen agency?** Most sell volume: 1,000 contacts, blasted. Since 2024, that volume wrecks your email deliverability (Google/Yahoo now hard-cap spam at 0.3%). We send fewer, better, *timed* touches that actually land.

**…use an AI SDR tool?** Those hand you software and the deliverability risk. This is done-for-you, with a human on every batch.
