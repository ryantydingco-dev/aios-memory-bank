## The core anchor (HIGH/MEDIUM confidence)
> A fully-loaded in-house US SDR costs **~$10,000–$12,000/month (~$118k–$170k/yr)** and takes **3–4 months to ramp** before producing — and the average SDR stays only **~1.5 years**, so you get ~12 months of full productivity per hire.

The AI GTM Engine is **live in week one** at **$2,000–$5,000/mo** with no ramp, no payroll tax, no PTO, no churn-and-rehire.

## Honest framing rules (do NOT oversell)
1. **Lead with the cost-replacement math (strong), not meeting-count promises (weak).** Cold outbound reply rates are 1–5% and it takes ~300 emails per meeting — real numbers. Promising a meeting flood gets you fired in month two. Promising *better economics than an SDR + better targeting than spray-and-pray* is defensible.
2. **Present ranges, not single hero numbers.** "Fully-loaded SDR is $10–12k/mo depending on your market" beats "$170k!" — the buyer trusts the one who shows the range.
3. **The engine ≠ a replacement for sales judgment / closing.** It books and researches; the buyer's team still closes. Say this out loud — it's the honest scope and it pre-empts the "so it'll just do everything?" objection.
4. **No invented results.** Until Oloxa produces a real meeting/close, the ROI story is "cost vs an SDR + deliverability safety," not "we booked X meetings for client Y."
