# Case Studies — DealThreads

## Creative Alternatives (creative agency) — THE headline proof. Public attribution: APPROVED by Ryan.
- **Challenge:** $650,000 in unpaid invoices sitting on the books. Not deadbeat clients — chasing was nobody's actual job, so it sat. The owner wrote "circling back" emails himself, between pitches; most never got written.
- **Solution:** Automated follow-up in their brand voice — reminders from the day each invoice was due, escalating until paid.
- **Results:** Half ($325K) collected inside 30 days; the rest within 90. No collections agency.
- **To get:** one-line written testimonial + logo permission. Placeholder on site until then — never fabricate a quote.
