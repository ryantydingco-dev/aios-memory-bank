## Brand identity
- **Name:** Ryan Tydingco · **Company:** DealThreads (one word, camel-cap T — never "Dealthreads"/"Deal Threads") · **Domain:** dealthreads.io
- **What it does (current offer):** Done-for-you accounts-receivable recovery. Free AR audit → ~$2K automated invoice-chasing setup (reminders in the client's brand voice, start the day an invoice is due, escalate until paid) → optional managed monthly.
- **Tagline:** "Never lose the thread." Brand spine: loose ends → one unbroken thread (now: invoice sent → invoice paid).

## Audience
Skeptical agency and service-business owners (1–50 staff) who personally chase six-figure receivables, hate being the bad guy with clients they want to keep, and delete 99 of 100 cold pitches. They know their numbers — never sell them relief from ignorance.

## Voice (Sage archetype, "analyst briefing a VP")
- **Knowing · Precise · Composed · Plainspoken · Substantiated.** Every claim numerically anchored. No exclamation marks. Dry wit aimed at the shared enemy (the awkward "just following up" email).
- **Banned words:** seamless, unlock, leverage, revolutionize, synergy, supercharge, robust, delve, elevate, transformative, "AI-powered." Never lead with "AI."

## Judge-panel copy laws (validated 2026-06-09 — apply to ALL sales copy)
1. Name the mechanics of any free offer ("send your AR aging export — one click in QuickBooks — we do the rest"). Hidden asks read as bait.
2. Sell relief from CHASING and from being the bad guy — owners already know what they're owed.
3. Spine line: "chasing it was nobody's actual job, so it sat." Blameless diagnosis.
4. Transparency converts: "if the number's big, we'll offer to fix it — that's the business model."
5. Never the $17.5K/56% small-business stat with agencies (shrinks stakes).
6. Banned patterns: "closing the file," "last one from me," "Yes or no?", keyword-reply CTAs, manufactured urgency, "isn't X. It's Y." inversions, em-dash chains, rule-of-three stacks.
7. CTAs first-person, near-zero friction ("Get My Free AR Audit," "Want the number?").

## Content strategy
Pillars: getting paid / the cost of chasing / the $650K story / build-in-public founder notes. Primary goal: booked free AR audits (the reply IS the conversion). Publishing: dealthreads.io, LinkedIn, Smartlead sequences.
