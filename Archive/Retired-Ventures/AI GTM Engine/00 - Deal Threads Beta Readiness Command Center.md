> Get 5 high-fit beta clients from form teardown to working buyer profiles in CRM.

## The Beta Readiness Loop

```text
Find weak form
  -> score fit
  -> create teardown
  -> ask for form URL / install call
  -> collect beta intake
  -> configure widget + CRM
  -> launch
  -> capture proof weekly
  -> convert to paid
```

## This Week's Minimum Build

By end of week:

- 50 companies added to the target sheet.
- 10 marked as `teardown first`.
- 5 Dead Form Teardowns drafted.
- 3 buyer-profile examples polished enough to show.
- HubSpot sandbox checklist completed.
- Beta intake form ready to send.
- Install packet ready to send.
- Proof log ready for first beta.
- 10 form URL asks sent.

## Daily 60-Minute Beta Readiness Block

### 20 Minutes: Find

Add 5 companies to the target sheet.

Required:

- company URL.
- form URL.
- buyer name or role.
- weak-form observation.
- fit score.

### 20 Minutes: Teardown

Create or improve 1 teardown.

Use:

[Dead Form Teardown Template](</Users/ryantydingco/Documents/AIOS-Memory-Bank/AI GTM Engine/Conversion/Deal Threads Dead Form Teardown Template.md:1>)

### 10 Minutes: Ask

Send 2 form URL / teardown asks.

Default message:

```text
I looked at your demo/contact form and noticed it captures the lead, but probably still leaves sales doing the buyer-profile research manually.

Want me to send over the quick teardown?
```

### 10 Minutes: Log

Update:

- target sheet.
- teardown status.
- next action.
- proof/learning.

## Weekly Review

Ask:

1. How many companies are in the target sheet?
2. How many are high fit?
3. How many teardowns are done?
4. How many form URL asks were sent?
5. How many people replied?
6. Which teardown was strongest?
7. Which objection appeared?
8. What needs to be added to the product/spec from market feedback?
