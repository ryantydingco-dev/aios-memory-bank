# Deal Threads Widget Copy Bank

Last updated: 2026-06-02  
Purpose: ready-to-use widget copy variants for beta configuration.

## Copy Principles

- Start with the buyer's goal, not contact fields.
- Ask one question at a time.
- Keep the flow short.
- Do not pretend to be human.
- Do not overuse AI language.
- Be clear about follow-up.
- Make the visitor feel routed, not trapped.

## Opening Messages

### Founder-Led B2B

```text
What are you trying to solve right now? I will ask a few quick questions so the team can follow up with useful context.
```

### RevOps

```text
What workflow or CRM handoff are you trying to improve? I can capture the context the team needs before follow-up.
```

### Demand Gen

```text
What are you hoping to improve from this request: lead quality, routing, conversion, or follow-up?
```

### Sales Leader

```text
What should sales understand before they follow up with you?
```

### Consultant / Partner

```text
Are you asking for your own team or exploring this for a client?
```

## Business Need Questions

1. `What are you trying to improve or solve right now?`
2. `What brought you here today?`
3. `What problem would make this worth exploring?`
4. `What is not working in the current process?`
5. `What would you want the team to understand before they follow up?`

## Business Need Follow-Ups

If vague:

```text
Can you give me one example of what is not working today?
```

If pricing-focused:

```text
Happy to help. What kind of team or use case would this be for?
```

If demo-focused:

```text
What would make the demo useful for you?
```

If support-like:

```text
Is this a new sales request or do you need help with an existing account?
```

## Company Context Questions

1. `What company is this for?`
2. `What is your company website?`
3. `Which team or business would use this?`
4. `Can you share your company name so we route this correctly?`
5. `Is this for your company or a client?`

## Role / Authority Questions

```text
What is your role in evaluating this?
```

Quick replies:

- `I own the decision`
- `I influence the decision`
- `I am researching for the team`
- `I am a consultant or partner`

## Timeline Questions

```text
When are you hoping to solve this?
```

Quick replies:

- `This week`
- `This month`
- `This quarter`
- `Later this year`
- `Just researching`

## Budget / Buying Stage Questions

```text
Is there already budget or an active project for this?
```

Quick replies:

- `Budget approved`
- `Budget likely`
- `Need to build the case`
- `No budget yet`
- `Not sure`

Optional range question:

```text
Roughly what annual range are you planning around?
```

Quick replies:

- `< $10K`
- `$10K-$30K`
- `$30K-$60K`
- `$60K+`
- `Not sure yet`

## CRM / Workflow Questions

```text
What CRM or sales tools are you using today?
```

Quick replies:

- `HubSpot`
- `Salesforce`
- `Pipedrive`
- `Other`
- `Not sure`

Follow-up:

```text
Where should the sales team see this request after it comes in?
```

## Contact Capture Copy

```text
Got it. What is the best work email for follow-up?
```

If phone is needed:

```text
If you prefer a call, you can also add a phone number.
```

## Consent Copy

Short:

```text
By continuing, you agree that your responses may be used to qualify and route this request. We may enrich business contact and company details from trusted sources.
```

Friendly:

```text
We will use your answers to help the team follow up with the right context. We may enrich business/company details so you do not have to fill out a long form.
```

Stricter:

```text
Your responses, source page, and business contact details may be processed to qualify, route, and respond to this request. Business/company data may be enriched from approved sources.
```

## Confirmation Messages

### Standard

```text
Thanks. I have enough context to route this to the team. They will follow up with your request and the details you shared.
```

### High Intent

```text
Thanks. This looks like a good fit for a fast follow-up. I will pass the context to the team so they can respond with the right next step.
```

### Researching

```text
Thanks. I will send this over with the context that you are researching and may need help building the case.
```

### Partner

```text
Thanks. I will route this as a partner/client-fit conversation so the team can follow up appropriately.
```

## Fallback Form Copy

```text
Something is not loading correctly. You can still send your request here and the team will follow up.
```

Fields:

- name.
- work email.
- company.
- what are you trying to solve?

## Error Copy

AI unavailable:

```text
I am having trouble loading the next question. You can still submit the request and the team will follow up.
```

Invalid email:

```text
That email does not look right. Can you enter your work email?
```

Missing company:

```text
Can you share the company this is for so we route it correctly?
```

Timeout:

```text
No worries. You can come back anytime or submit what you have so far.
```
