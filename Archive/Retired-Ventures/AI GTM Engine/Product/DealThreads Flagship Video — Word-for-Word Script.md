# DealThread — Flagship Video / Webinar · Word-for-Word Recording Script
**Runs ~12–14 min spoken (~150 wpm). Maps to the 14-card Gamma deck.**

### Delivery notes
- Talk to **one founder**, not an audience. Look at the lens.
- Conversational pace. Pause at the **[ / ]** beats — they're where it lands.
- `[ON CAM]` = you on camera · `[SLIDE n]` = the Gamma card on screen · `[SCREEN: DEMO]` = screen-record of the live demo · `✂️` = a standalone clip lives here.
- It's fine to paraphrase — keep your energy over hitting every word exactly. Honest and plain beats polished.

### [0:50 · THE PROBLEM · SLIDE 2 — "Your form captures the lead. It doesn't prepare the sale."]
Here's the problem, and it's the same on almost every B2B site. /

Someone fills out your contact form. And what does sales actually get? A name. An email. A company, maybe. And a one-line message. /

That's it. It *feels* like a lead. But it tells your rep nothing about whether this person is urgent, whether they're senior, whether the company's funded, whether they fit — or whether this is the one they should call in the next ten minutes instead of tomorrow. **✂️ (clip: the gap)**

### [1:45 · THE 8 FIELDS · SLIDE 3 — "The context that closes the deal lives outside the form."]
Because everything that actually decides the deal… lives *outside* the form. /

What's their real need. How urgent is it. Is there budget. Do they even sign, or are they just gathering quotes. How big is the company. What do they run. Did they just raise, or just hire. Are they actually a fit. /

A form can't ask any of that. So your rep has to go dig it all up by hand.

### [2:30 · THE COST · SLIDE 4 — "The lead cools while the rep researches."]
And that's the real cost. /

It's ten, twenty minutes of research on every serious lead. It's slow follow-up on your *best* buyers. It's good leads and bad leads landing in your inbox looking exactly the same. /

And while your rep is heads-down Googling… the faster competitor already called back, walked in knowing who they were, and booked the meeting. /

Here's the one line I want you to take from this whole thing: **a form can only collect. It can't qualify.** **✂️ (clip: a form can't qualify)**

---

### [3:20 · THE FIX · SLIDE 5 — "Replace the form with a conversation."]
So here's the idea. What if the form did that work itself? /

Not a chatbot trying to deflect people. Not an "AI SDR" pretending to be you. / Just a short, helpful conversation — the kind of opening questions your *best* rep would ask. What are you trying to solve. How soon. Is there budget. Who's involved. /

And then, in the background, it enriches the company, scores how well they fit, and writes a finished buyer profile straight into your CRM — before your rep ever calls back. /

The human still sells. The system just makes sure that human walks in prepared. **✂️ (clip: the fix)**

### [4:15 · WHAT IT'S NOT · SLIDE 6 — "Not visitor-ID. Not a chatbot. Not an AI SDR."]
Let me be clear about what this *isn't*, because the space is noisy. /

It's not a visitor-ID tool guessing at anonymous companies. It's not a data tool like Apollo or Clearbit that hands you raw data and leaves the work to you. And it's not an AI SDR auto-blasting your buyers and putting your reputation at risk. /

Most tools solve one slice. The whole point here is the *handoff* — a qualified profile, in your CRM, ready for a human. **✂️ (clip: what it's not)**

---

### [5:05 · BEFORE / AFTER · SLIDE 7 — "Same lead. Same minute."]
So here's the before and after — same lead, same minute. /

Before, your rep gets: "Jordan, jordan@company dot com, wants a demo." /

After, your rep opens: VP of Ops, forty-person B2B company, budget approved, evaluating this quarter, strong fit — and here's your opener. /

Same person. One of those, your rep can actually do something with. **✂️ (clip: before/after)**

### [5:45 · THE PROFILE · SLIDE 8 — "The profile your rep opens."]
And I want you to notice how that profile is built, because this part matters. /

There's what they *told* you in the conversation — intent, timeline, budget, who's deciding. / Separately, there's what we *enriched and verified* — the company, the size, the funding, the tech stack, the decision-makers. / There's a fit score and a recommended next step. /

And then there's this: **what we still don't know.** It flags what it couldn't verify — instead of inventing it. Because an honest "unknown" is worth more in your CRM than a confident wrong answer.

### One-take tips
- Record the **demo segment separately** as a clean screen capture, then drop it in — don't try to do it live in the same take.
- If you flub a line, **pause, clap once, and restart the sentence** — easy to find and cut later.
- Shoot the **7 ✂️ clip moments** with a tiny beat of silence before and after so they cut cleanly into shorts.
- A 6-minute version = keep cold open, the gap, the fix, the demo, the offer, the close. Drop slides 8, 10, 12.
