## Webinar Strategy

### Webinar Title

### Short Title

The AI Lead Enrichment Webinar

### Subtitle

See how a normal demo/contact form can become a rep-ready buyer profile with company context, intent, budget, timeline, ICP fit, and CRM-ready notes.

### Target Audience

Primary:

- Founder-led B2B companies with 5-40 employees.
- B2B companies selling USD 8K+ deals or high-LTV services.
- Founders, CEOs, operators, first GTM hires, and small sales teams.
- Companies with a real `Request Demo`, `Contact Sales`, or `Talk to Us` form.
- Teams using HubSpot or a lightweight CRM.

Secondary:

- 40-150 employee B2B teams with thin RevOps.
- VP Sales, Head of Growth, RevOps, Demand Gen, and Marketing Ops.
- Teams evaluating or replacing Drift, Chili Piper, Warmly, Qualified, visitor ID tools, or enrichment tools.

### Webinar Promise

In 45 minutes, attendees will see exactly how to turn a low-context form submission into a sales-ready buyer profile before the first callback, without hiring SDRs, buying enterprise software, or letting AI invent facts in the CRM.

### Core Mechanism

The Contact Form Gap:

1. A buyer submits a form.
2. Sales receives a name, email, and maybe a message.
3. The real deal context sits outside the form: company size, buying role, urgency, budget, tech stack, funding/news, CRM fit, likely decision-makers.
4. The rep researches later, after the lead cools.
5. Deal Threads moves that research step into the form-to-CRM handoff.

"Send me your form URL. I will show you what buyer context your form is throwing away."

## Q&A Bank

### Is this just Clearbit/Apollo/RB2B?

No. Those tools can provide data. Deal Threads turns the form submission into a sales workflow: qualification, enrichment, fit, rep summary, CRM note, and follow-up context.

### Is this an AI SDR?

No. Deal Threads does not pretend to be your salesperson. It prepares the human seller with better context.

### Will this hallucinate?

The system should separate visitor-provided facts, enriched facts, AI inferences, and unknowns. Unknown fields should remain unknown.

### What if we do not get many leads?

Then this may be too early. Deal Threads makes sense when a serious inbound lead is valuable enough to justify better follow-up.

### Will buyers hate the form?

The first version can be a smart form or lightweight guided flow. It should ask only questions that improve routing, qualification, or follow-up.

### Does it work with HubSpot?

HubSpot is the preferred first CRM. The safest beta sync is contacts, companies, and activity notes before complex deal/routing automation.

### What if the enrichment is wrong?

Low-confidence facts should be labeled or held back. Deal Threads should never overwrite protected CRM fields without approval.

### How long does installation take?

The first version should be installed in days, not months, if the client provides form/site access, CRM access, and field mapping approval.

### How much does it cost?

The founder-led install is USD 2,000 setup plus USD 1,000/month. For the first proof clients, billing can start only after enriched profiles are landing in the CRM. Larger teams with multiple forms, routing, reports, or campaigns move to USD 2,500/month or USD 5,000/month packages.
