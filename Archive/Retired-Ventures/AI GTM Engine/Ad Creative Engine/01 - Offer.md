# Offer: Home-Service Creative Testing Engine

## Positioning

We help high-ticket home-service companies turn their existing proof—projects,
reviews, offers, and brand assets—into a repeatable stream of ready-to-test
short-form video ads without scheduling another filming day.

The product is a creative testing service. It is not a promise of leads,
appointments, or revenue, and media buying is not included unless separately
scoped.

## Entry offer: Seven-Day Creative Pilot

**Price:** $750

**Deliverable:** Three client-approved, ready-to-test vertical video ad concepts
in seven business days.

Each concept includes:

- one distinct hook and angle;
- a 15–30 second edited video;
- captions and platform-safe formatting;
- one primary call to action;
- one revision round after the first review.

The client supplies brand guidelines and approves any factual claims. We can use
client-owned assets, licensed assets, and AI-generated supporting visuals. We do
not fabricate testimonials, project results, team members, certifications, or
customer likenesses.

**Risk reversal:** If we cannot produce three usable, client-approved concepts
within the agreed scope, we redo the concepts or refund the pilot. This is a
deliverable guarantee, not a performance guarantee.

**Upgrade credit:** Apply the $750 pilot fee to the first month when the client
upgrades within 14 days.

## Core offer: Monthly Creative Testing System

**Starting price:** $2,000 per month

**Deliverable:** Twelve ready-to-test short-form variants per month, organized
into four creative themes.

The monthly system includes:

- monthly research and creative brief;
- four net-new concepts;
- hook, proof, offer, and CTA variations;
- human quality and compliance review;
- organized delivery with naming and test hypotheses;
- one monthly creative scorecard using performance data the client supplies.

Media spend, campaign management, landing pages, on-site filming, influencer
fees, and claim substantiation are separate.

## Why this offer is sellable

Local businesses do not need “more content” in the abstract. They need more
credible creative angles to test against expensive demand. The pilot makes the
buying decision small, concrete, fast, and reversible. The retainer becomes
valuable when the client or its agency already has distribution but lacks a
reliable creative production cadence.
