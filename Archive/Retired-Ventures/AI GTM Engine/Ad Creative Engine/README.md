# Home-Service Creative Testing Engine

This system turns existing prospect records and public website evidence into a
need-ranked sales queue for an AI-assisted ad creative offer.

It is designed for high-ticket local home-service companies that already show
evidence of demand-generation infrastructure or have enough proof assets to
support a credible creative test.

## Outputs

- `outputs/home_service_creative_engine_1000.csv`: ranked 1,000-account universe
- `outputs/home_service_creative_engine_top_50.csv`: first manual-review batch
- `outputs/home_service_creative_engine_layups.csv`: all A-tier accounts
- `scripts/build_prospect_universe.py`: reproducible enrichment and scoring script

## Recommended workflow

1. Manually verify current ads for the top 25 accounts using each row's Meta Ad
   Library link and the company's current social feeds.
2. Confirm the contact and email before sending.
3. Make a private, watermarked sample only for accounts with a verified creative
   gap. Do not publish it or imply client approval.
4. Use the factual personalization line, then ask permission to send the sample.
5. Sell the paid pilot; graduate qualified wins to the monthly testing retainer.

## Important limitation

Website tags indicate marketing infrastructure, not proof that a company is
currently spending on ads. “No video detected” means no embedded video was found
in the scanned homepage HTML. Both signals require human verification before
outreach.
