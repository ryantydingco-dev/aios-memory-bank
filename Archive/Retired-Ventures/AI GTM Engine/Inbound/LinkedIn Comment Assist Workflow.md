# LinkedIn Comment Assist Workflow

## Purpose
Make LinkedIn commenting easier without risky auto-commenting.

## Rule
Do not automate posting comments directly on LinkedIn. Use a human-in-the-loop workflow:

1. Find relevant posts.
2. Generate suggested comments.
3. Ryan reviews/edits.
4. Ryan manually posts.
5. Log promising interactions.

This protects account credibility and avoids spammy behavior.

## Daily Workflow

### Step 1 — Find Posts
Use saved LinkedIn searches, followed creators, industry pages, and lead posts.

Search terms:
- commercial finance broker
- bridging finance
- development finance
- specialist lending
- commercial mortgage broker
- lender panel
- business finance broker
- NACFB
- commercial finance awards
- fast funding
- lender-ready
- document checklist

Target accounts/pages:
- NACFB
- commercial finance brokers from lead universe
- specialist lenders
- bridging/development finance firms
- commercial mortgage brokers
- broker award/event posts

### Step 2 — Paste Post Text Into Hermes
Prompt:

```text
Draft 3 LinkedIn comments for this post. Tone: curious, useful, non-salesy. Goal: create credibility around commercial finance workflow/document intake without pitching Oloxa. Keep each under 50 words.

POST:
[paste post]
```

### Step 3 — Pick/Edit One Comment
Use comments that:
- add a useful thought
- ask a smart workflow question
- do not pitch
- sound human
- connect to lender-ready packages, intake quality, or broker capacity when relevant

### Step 4 — Log If Useful
If the poster is a prospect or replies, capture:
- LinkedIn post URL
- prospect name/company
- topic
- comment used
- reply outcome
- next action

## Comment Types

### Deal Close
Ask about timeline/process without pitching.

### Speed/Funding
Discuss how clean intake supports speed.

### Lender Panel
Discuss checklist variation and process drag.

### Hiring
Discuss capacity strain and borrower docs/checklist support.

### Compliance/Process
Discuss file quality, process robustness, and lender confidence.

## Safety Guardrails
- No mass commenting.
- No browser extensions that auto-like/comment/connect.
- No copy-pasting the exact same comment repeatedly.
- No links unless asked.
- No Oloxa pitch in comments.
- Keep daily target reasonable: 5–10 thoughtful comments to start.

## Best Version
Hermes can help you create a daily "comment queue" from posts you paste or export, but Ryan should manually publish the comments.
