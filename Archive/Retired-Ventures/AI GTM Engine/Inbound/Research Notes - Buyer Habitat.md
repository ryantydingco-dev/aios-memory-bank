# Research Notes - Buyer Habitat

## Sources Checked
- LinkedIn search results around commercial finance broker content, lender networks, document preparation, and bridging finance.
- NACFB membership/compliance pages.
- Search results for NACFB Commercial Broker Magazine, commercial broker awards, bridging finance, and specialist lending posts.

## Observations

### LinkedIn Is The Main Public Signal Layer
Commercial finance brokers/lenders publicly post about:
- closings
- bridging finance
- development finance
- commercial finance awards
- lender panels
- market commentary
- slow/clunky back office experiences
- completion timelines
- hiring
- specialist lending

This supports LinkedIn as the primary organic/inbound channel.

### NACFB Ecosystem Is A Buyer Map
NACFB membership is positioned around:
- lender confidence
- professional standards
- lender access
- compliance support
- lead generation
- professional development
- events/forums/expos
- template documents
- assurance consultations

Implication:
Oloxa content should connect to process quality, lender confidence, professional workflow, and document readiness — not just "AI automation."

### Compliance/Process Angle Matters
NACFB compliance resources emphasize template documents, assurance consultation, compliance support, consumer duty, and regulatory analysis.

Implication:
Do not overclaim compliance automation. But do talk about robust process, cleaner documentation, and better file readiness.

### Buyer Language To Watch
- lender-ready
- completion timeline
- bridging finance
- development finance
- specialist lending
- lender panel
- document preparation
- file quality
- assurance/process
- borrower confidence
- fast funding
- adviser focus
- chasing paperwork

## Content Implication
The strongest content will not be generic AI content. It will be commercially literate workflow content:

- what slows a deal before lender submission
- what makes files lender-ready
- how multi-lender workflows create hidden admin drag
- why borrower follow-up matters
- why speed promises depend on intake quality
- how AI can run the boring intake layer in the background
