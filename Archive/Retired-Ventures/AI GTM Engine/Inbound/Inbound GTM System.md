# Inbound GTM System

## Purpose
Create a credibility and demand layer around Oloxa so outbound is not the only growth motion.

## Core Idea
Outbound creates conversations now. Inbound creates trust, familiarity, proof, and warmer replies over time.

The goal is not to become a generic AI influencer. The goal is to become known for a specific market POV:

> Commercial finance teams lose capacity before lender submission because borrower docs arrive messy, incomplete, and hard to chase. The future is cleaner intake, faster lender-ready packages, and brokers spending more time on deals instead of document coordination.

## Inbound North Star
Generate warm conversations with commercial finance brokers, debt advisors, and finance operators by publishing useful, specific content about:
- borrower document chaos
- lender-ready packages
- commercial finance workflows
- broker capacity
- buying signals and AI GTM systems
- lessons from building Oloxa and the GTM engine

## Best Channel Prioritization

### 1. LinkedIn — Primary
Why:
- commercial finance brokers, lenders, industry bodies, and operators already publish there
- our existing signals come from LinkedIn posts
- outbound and inbound can reinforce each other
- Ryan needs professional credibility, not viral consumer attention

Role:
- trust engine
- founder-led learning journal
- broker workflow education
- market POV
- light Oloxa proof over time

### 2. YouTube — Secondary / Authority
Why:
- good for deeper explainers, workflow breakdowns, case studies, and "building an AI GTM engine" credibility
- slower to compound but creates authority assets

Role:
- long-form credibility library
- product/workflow walkthroughs
- market education
- repurposing source for clips

### 3. TikTok / Instagram Reels / YouTube Shorts — Optional Reach
Why:
- useful for clipping broad AI workflow/GTM lessons
- less likely to directly reach commercial finance buyers
- can help Ryan build broader personal brand

Role:
- reach engine
- top-of-funnel for AI systems/GTM/building-in-public
- not primary buyer acquisition yet

## Buyer Habitat Research
Commercial finance buyers appear active around:
- LinkedIn posts about closings, bridging finance, commercial finance, lender panels, specialist lending, and funding speed
- NACFB ecosystem: member directory, compliance resources, events, magazine, awards, and training
- industry magazines and associations
- lender/broker announcement posts
- specialist lending events and award posts

Research notes:
- NACFB positions membership around lender confidence, professional standards, lender access, compliance support, lead generation, and professional development.
- NACFB compliance resources emphasize template documents, assurance consultations, compliance support, consumer duty, and regulatory analysis.
- LinkedIn search results show brokers/lenders posting about bridging finance, lender networks, slow/clunky back offices, completion timelines, commercial broker awards, and NACFB magazine content.

Implication:
Oloxa content should not sound like SaaS bro marketing. It should sound like someone studying the commercial finance workflow and making useful observations about speed, lender-readiness, compliance-adjacent process quality, and broker capacity.

## Content Pillars

### Pillar 1 — Broker Workflow Education
Audience: commercial finance brokers and finance operators.

Topics:
- why lender-ready packages get delayed
- common borrower doc bottlenecks
- missing-doc follow-up patterns
- file naming/folder chaos
- lender checklist variation
- why speed promises break at intake

Example hooks:
- "The hidden bottleneck in commercial finance isn't always finding the lender. Sometimes it's getting the file clean enough to send."
- "A borrower sending 14 PDFs named Scan.pdf is not a small problem at scale."
- "Fast funding promises usually die in the document intake layer."

### Pillar 2 — Signal-Based GTM / Lead Research
Audience: founders, operators, sales teams, and future AI GTM Engine buyers.

Topics:
- job title vs buying signal
- CLOSING/HIRING/VOLUME/PAIN signals
- why generic lead lists fail
- how we split Ryan/Sway daily actions
- how AI Arc + Hermes creates meeting-ready research

Example hooks:
- "A job title is not a lead. A buying signal is."
- "Most outbound fails before the message is written. The lead list is already too generic."

### Pillar 3 — Building Oloxa in Public
Audience: buyers, founders, operators, future partners.

Topics:
- what we are learning from commercial finance brokers
- how positioning is changing
- how we are building the GTM system
- what objections teach us
- why we sell outcomes, not AI

Example hooks:
- "We're learning that the word AI matters less than the phrase lender-ready package."
- "This week we stopped thinking in leads and started thinking in reasons now."

### Pillar 4 — Practical AI Systems
Audience: business owners and operators.

Topics:
- AI as a workflow layer, not a chatbot
- boring workflows that actually save time
- document intake, follow-up, lead research, reporting
- systems vs tools

Example hooks:
- "The best AI use cases are usually boring. That's why they work."
- "Most businesses don't need more AI tools. They need one repeated workflow mapped clearly."

### Pillar 5 — Proof / Case Studies / Lessons
Audience: future buyers and partners.

Topics:
- anonymized workflow audits
- before/after examples
- meeting learnings
- objection patterns
- buyer language we hear repeatedly

Rule:
Never invent proof. Mark hypotheses as hypotheses until validated.

## Content-to-Revenue Loop
Every piece of content should support one of these:
1. Make outbound warmer.
2. Build Ryan/Oloxa credibility.
3. Teach the market how to think about the problem.
4. Surface inbound comments/DMs.
5. Create proof for future calls.
6. Package the AI GTM Engine later.

## Organic CTA System
Use soft CTAs only.

For broker-focused posts:
```text
Curious if other commercial finance teams see the same doc-intake bottleneck before lender submission.
```

```text
If you're a broker, where does the file usually slow down: borrower follow-up, missing docs, lender checklists, or internal handoff?
```

For GTM/AI posts:
```text
I'm documenting how we're building this GTM system in real time.
```

```text
If you're building outbound, start with reasons now, not just job titles.
```

## Publishing Cadence
Minimum viable cadence:
- LinkedIn: 4 posts/week
- Short-form video: 2 clips/week
- YouTube: 1 deeper video every 2 weeks
- Comments: 10 thoughtful comments/day on broker/lender/finance posts

Aggressive cadence:
- LinkedIn: 5–7 posts/week
- Short-form: 4–5 clips/week
- YouTube: weekly
- Comments: 15–20/day

## Daily Inbound Workflow
1. Capture one lesson from outbound or market research.
2. Turn it into a LinkedIn post or comment angle.
3. Comment on 10 buyer/industry posts.
4. Reply to all comments/DMs.
5. Save any buyer language into the GTM Brain.
6. Add any inbound leads to the outcome tracker.

## What Not To Do Yet
- Do not run paid ads.
- Do not make generic AI hype content.
- Do not over-promote Oloxa before proof is strong.
- Do not build content around broad virality if buyers are on LinkedIn.
- Do not pretend Ryan is a commercial finance veteran. Be honest: Ryan is building/studying the workflow and documenting what he learns.
