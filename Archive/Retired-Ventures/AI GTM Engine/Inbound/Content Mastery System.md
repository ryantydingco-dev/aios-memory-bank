# Content Mastery System

## Purpose
Help Ryan master content by turning daily work, screenshots, Looms, calls, notes, and market observations into repeatable content assets.

## Core Lesson
The bottleneck is not only creating new ideas. The bottleneck is capturing raw material, grading it, repurposing it, and publishing consistently without burning out.

## Content Operating System

### 1. Capture Raw Material Daily
Save anything that can become content:
- screenshots of workflows
- HubSpot/task screenshots
- lead research before/after examples
- call notes
- objections
- buyer language
- messy docs/workflow examples
- Loom recordings
- whiteboard notes
- daily lessons with Sway
- AIOS/Hermes outputs

Use folders:
- `Raw Assets/Screenshots`
- `Raw Assets/Videos`
- `Raw Assets/Voice Notes`
- `Raw Assets/Call Notes`
- `Raw Assets/Workflow Proof`

### 2. Turn Raw Material Into Angles
Every asset should become one of:
- story post
- practical lesson
- short-form video
- carousel
- comment angle
- YouTube topic
- proof point

### 3. Grade Before Publishing
Every post gets checked for:
- strong first line
- one clear idea
- concrete example
- useful takeaway
- Ryan voice
- no fake guru energy
- no overclaiming
- no Drata-conflict framing
- soft CTA only

### 4. Repurpose, Don't Recreate
One real idea can become:
- LinkedIn post
- 30–60 sec video
- Instagram story
- carousel
- YouTube segment
- comment prompts
- newsletter/thread later

### 5. Weekly Batch Rhythm
Once/week:
- review raw assets
- pick 5–7 best ideas
- draft posts/scripts
- grade/edit them
- schedule or queue them

Daily:
- publish/comment/respond
- capture new raw material

## Ryan's Content Pillars

1. AI systems for business workflows
2. Oloxa/building in public
3. Signal-based outbound and GTM
4. Founder/operator lessons
5. Helping business owners find boring AI wins

## Beginner Weekly Cadence

- LinkedIn: 4 posts/week
- Short-form video: 2 clips/week
- Comments: 5–10/day
- Instagram story: 2–3/week from raw life/workflow assets
- YouTube: 1 deeper breakdown every 2–4 weeks

## Workflow Prompt
Use this whenever Ryan has raw material:

```text
Turn this raw material into a content pack in my voice.

Goal: build trust around AI systems, Oloxa, GTM, and practical business workflows.

Output:
1. Core insight
2. LinkedIn post under 220 words
3. Short-form video script under 60 seconds
4. 5 alternate hooks
5. One carousel angle
6. Quality grade out of 10 and what to improve

Raw material:
[paste screenshot/notes/transcript]
```

## Rule
Content is not separate from GTM. Content is the public learning layer of the GTM engine.

Every post should do at least one of:
- teach a useful workflow lesson
- build trust
- document real progress
- attract business owners/founders/operators
- create warmer outbound
- clarify Ryan's thinking
