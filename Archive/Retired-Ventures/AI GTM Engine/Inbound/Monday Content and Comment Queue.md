### Post: A job title is not a lead

A job title is not a lead.

A buying signal is.

We're building Oloxa's GTM system around this idea right now.

Instead of saying:
"Find me commercial finance brokers."

The better question is:
"Which brokers have a reason to care this week?"

Signals we're watching:
- recent deal closings
- hiring / capacity strain
- high deal volume
- lender panel complexity
- speed-to-funding promises
- signs that borrower docs/checklists create drag

The goal is not more contacts.

The goal is better reasons to start conversations.

AI is useful here, but not because it can blast 1,000 emails.

It's useful because it can help turn messy market signals into focused daily actions.

That's the difference between a lead list and a GTM engine.
