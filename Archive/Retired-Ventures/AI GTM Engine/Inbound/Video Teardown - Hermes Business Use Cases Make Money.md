# Video Teardown - Hermes Business Use Cases Make Money

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2TKrNCANcSk&t=10s  
Created: 2026-06-01

## Big idea

Hermes is not valuable as a chatbot. Hermes is valuable as a work operating layer: memory, artifacts, Slack/team collaboration, project management, APIs, content ingestion, email/revenue agents, and remote control of your AI workstations.

## Useful takeaways for Ryan

1. **Artifacts are communication leverage**  
   Use Hermes to create branded PDFs, carousels, one-page teardowns, case studies, SOPs, and internal decision docs. For Dealthreads, the highest-value artifact is the personalized Dead Form Teardown.

2. **Slack/team channels are where AI becomes multiplayer**  
   Telegram is fine for Ryan personally, but Slack makes sense when workflows involve clients, Sway, contractors, or future team members. Threads, channels, and shared artifacts turn AI from assistant into team operator.

3. **Specialist agents beat generic chat**  
   The creator references revenue systems, closed-loop systems, media-kit agents, outbound infrastructure agents, ad creative agents, etc. This maps directly to Ryan's AIOS direction: narrow agents with a defined job, memory, input/output format, and review loop.

4. **Linear/project management closes the loop**  
   AI work needs tickets, review states, and definitions of done. For Dealthreads, every prospect teardown, beta install, bug, and client workflow should become trackable work, not scattered chat spaghetti.

5. **Use /go/definition-of-done workflows**  
   The important idea is not the literal command. It is giving agents a clear definition of done and letting them continue until the artifact is complete and verified.

6. **Obsidian/durable memory compounds learning**  
   Daily logs, decisions, experiments, objections, replies, and GTM lessons should flow into the memory bank. That is how the system gets smarter instead of resetting every Monday like a goldfish in a Patagonia vest.

7. **APIs make Hermes commercially useful**  
   HubSpot, call recording/transcripts, X, YouTube, email, content systems, CRM, and analytics APIs turn Hermes into a business nervous system.

8. **Content ingestion is a revenue asset**  
   Pull in YouTube, X, meetings, internal ideas, replies, and calls. Then decide what content to double down on based on real signal.

9. **Email/revenue agents are the money layer**  
   Draft emails, approve sends, revive stalled deals, trigger outreach from hiring/funding/activity signals, and learn from replies.

10. **Remote access matters**  
    Tailscale + SSH/mobile terminal lets Ryan control the AIOS when away from the main computer.

## What this means for Dealthreads

The immediate monetizable workflow is not “build a giant AIOS.” It is:

Lead/source signal → teardown artifact → Loom/message → reply tracking → beta install task → CRM/profile workflow → weekly learning loop.

Dealthreads can be the customer-facing proof of the AIOS: a compact revenue workflow that turns AI memory + tools + artifacts into booked conversations.

## What to build now

2. Teardown Target Tracker
3. Reply/Objection Logger
4. Beta Install Checklist

## Caution

The video is broad and hypey. Do not copy all 12 things at once. For revenue today, focus on the artifact + outreach + follow-up loop. Slack, Linear, and more APIs become important once there are clients or collaborators.

## One-line lesson

Hermes makes money when it stops being a chatbot and becomes a closed-loop revenue workflow that creates artifacts, follows up, remembers lessons, and moves work forward.
