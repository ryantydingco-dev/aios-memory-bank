# Monday Go-Hard Launch Plan

## Mission
Monday is not a planning day. Monday is a revenue execution day.

Goal: Ryan and Sway wake up with clear HubSpot tasks, know exactly what to send/post/comment, and end the day with outcomes logged so Tuesday gets smarter.

## Monday Success Criteria

By end of day Monday:
- Ryan completes 10 high-quality Oloxa actions.
- Sway completes 10 high-quality Oloxa actions.
- At least 5–10 thoughtful LinkedIn comments are posted on buyer/industry posts.
- 1 LinkedIn post goes live from Ryan or Oloxa angle.
- Every action is logged in HubSpot.
- Every reply is categorized.
- Every positive reply is routed to a quick example or 15-minute paperwork audit.
- Tuesday's next batch is either ready or clearly scoped.

## Daily Rule
Replies and booked meetings come before new outbound.

Execution order:
1. Hot replies
2. Meeting follow-ups
3. Ryan/Sway daily tasks
4. Content/comment tasks
5. New lead generation
6. End-of-day logging

## Monday Execution Blocks

### Block 1 — Hot Replies / Follow-Ups
Before any new outbound:
- respond to positive replies
- send quick examples if promised
- offer 15-min paperwork audit
- create/update HubSpot deal if meeting booked

CTA ladder:
1. Worth comparing notes?
2. Worth me sending a quick example?
3. Want to do a 15-min deal paperwork audit?
4. Worth putting 15 mins on the calendar?

### Block 2 — Ryan 10 Actions
Ryan should prioritize:
- founder/owner/partner/MD profiles
- UK commercial finance brokers
- PAIN/HIRING/CLOSING signals
- leads where a Loom or voice note would feel natural

Minimum action quality:
- specific observation
- workflow/pain hypothesis
- low-friction CTA

### Block 3 — Sway 10 Actions
Sway should prioritize:
- clean verified-email leads
- clear campaign-ready segment
- HIRING/CLOSING/VOLUME signals
- follow-up tasks

Minimum action quality:
- use assigned opener or adapt slightly
- log sent status
- set follow-up date/status

### Block 4 — Inbound Credibility
Daily minimum:
- 1 LinkedIn post OR one drafted post ready to publish
- 5–10 thoughtful comments on commercial finance / lender / broker posts

Do not pitch Oloxa in comments. Ask smart workflow questions and create credibility breadcrumbs.

### Block 5 — End-of-Day Scoreboard
Update:
- Ryan actions completed / 10
- Sway actions completed / 10
- comments completed / 5–10
- content posted yes/no
- positive replies
- meetings booked
- objections captured
- best learning
- what changes Tuesday

## Monday Non-Negotiables

### No list goblin behavior
Do not spend Monday hunting for 500 more leads if the Top 20 is already waiting in HubSpot.

### No dashboard cosplay
If a workflow does not create action, replies, meetings, or learning, it waits.

### No over-explaining in first touch
First touch earns curiosity. It does not need to explain the entire Oloxa product.

### No vague logging
Every meaningful action needs a HubSpot status update or note.

## Monday Mantra
Do the work, log the market, improve tomorrow.
