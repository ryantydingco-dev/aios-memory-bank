## What this is

A 90-day working partnership to install and run an outbound system that puts your firm in front of companies that need your placements, using your recruiters' phone time and my infrastructure, data, and process. You keep everything we build whether or not we continue past day 90.

## What Dealthreads delivers

1. **Target list, built and maintained.** A researched, verified list of companies and decision makers that fit your placement specialty, refreshed as the campaign runs. You own the list.
2. **Sending infrastructure in your name.** Domains and inboxes purchased on your card at cost (typically $250 to $1,000 per month depending on data and volume, sized to your budget). Warmed and configured by me. You own all of it.
3. **Copy and sequences.** Email copy written for your firm, approved by you before anything sends. Follow-up sequences included.
4. **Weekly operating cycle.** Every week: send results reviewed, replies triaged, list refreshed, one improvement shipped. You get a short written report each week.
5. **Reply-to-conversation coaching.** When replies come in, I work them with you: what to say, when to call, how to move it to a live conversation.

## What Client delivers

1. Tool and data costs on your card (you own the accounts).
2. Copy approval within 2 business days of each draft.
3. Your recruiters make the calls. I supply the call list and talk track; the dials come from your team.
4. Replies worked within 1 business day of my handoff.
5. A 30-minute working session each week.

## What success looks like

By day 90 you have: a warmed sending system in your name, a living target list, copy that has been tested against real prospects, and a repeatable weekly rhythm your team can run. The goal of the pilot is qualified sales conversations with decision makers at companies that fit your list, and the data to decide whether to scale.

## Risk reversal

If we run the full 90-day cycle together, meaning copy was approved, campaigns ran continuously, your team made the agreed dials, and replies were worked within a business day, and the system has not produced a single qualified sales conversation with a fitting prospect, I refund the full $1,500. You still keep the list, the infrastructure, and the copy.

A qualified sales conversation means a live exchange (phone or email, back and forth) with a decision maker at a company fitting the target profile about using your services. No promises are made about meeting counts or placements; what is promised is the system, the work, and the refund terms above.

## Reasons we would not continue past day 90

Honest list, stated up front: reply volume that stays below what the phone time justifies, a target market that turns out too small for repeatable outbound, or a working rhythm that does not fit how your team operates. If any of those show up, we will both see it in the weekly numbers, and walking away clean is a fine outcome. You keep everything either way.

## After the pilot

If the partnership works, we discuss a monthly retainer shaped by what the 90 days proved. Nothing is locked in advance and no retainer is required to keep what was built.
