# Kickoff Questionnaire

Send this the moment payment lands. Delivery starts when it comes back. Ten questions, fifteen minutes.

1. **Who is your perfect client?** Industry, company size, region, and the one sentence you'd use to describe them to a friend. If you have 3 to 5 dream accounts, name them.
2. **Who signs?** Title of the decision maker you want conversations with (and any titles to avoid).
3. **What do you place?** Roles, seniority, typical fee or placement value, and your close rate from a real conversation to a signed deal (rough is fine).
4. **Why do clients pick you?** Your honest edge in one paragraph. War stories welcome; this becomes copy.
5. **Do-not-contact list.** Current clients, active prospects, competitors, burned bridges. A CSV export from your CRM or ATS is perfect. Anyone not on this list is fair game.
6. **Card for tools.** Domains, inboxes, and data run on your card so you own everything ($250 to $1,000/month depending on volume). Who sets that up on your side, and what is the monthly ceiling?
7. **Sending identity.** Whose name do emails come from (usually the owner), and do you have 2 to 3 domain name ideas close to your main one? (Example: shinehire.com → shinehiretalent.com.)
8. **Calendar link.** Your booking link for prospect calls. If you don't have one, say so and I'll set one up.
9. **Phone time.** Who on your team makes calls, how many hours a week, and when? I feed the list and the talk track; the dials are yours.
10. **A voice sample.** Forward 2 to 3 emails you've written to prospects or clients that sound like you. Copy gets written in your voice, not mine.
