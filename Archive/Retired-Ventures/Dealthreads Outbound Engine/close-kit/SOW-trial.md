## Purpose

Dealthreads will install the Staffing Client Pipeline OS within five business days and operate it for a 14-day proof period. The engagement tests one defined client market using real activity, conversations, and pipeline evidence before Client commits to a 90-day managed program.

This engagement does not guarantee meetings, placements, opportunities, or revenue.

## Dealthreads deliverables

- One written specialist ICP including geography, company criteria, decision-maker roles, exclusions, and suppression rules.
- 500 scored and verified target client accounts.
- Current reasons to contact the highest-priority accounts and an opening angle for each.
- Domains and inboxes registered in Client's name and warming.
- Approved email, call, and LinkedIn playbooks.
- Live email from the Dealthreads warmed fleet during the Proof while Client's infrastructure warms.
- Staffing Client Pipeline OS configuration: morning brief, reply classification, hot-reply alerts, SLA timers, Call-Ready Queue, suppression, calendar logging, HubSpot attribution, and scorecards.
- One 60-to-90-minute live dial block with Ryan using the signal-ranked queue.
- A day-14 Pipeline Verdict covering agreed activity, conversations, objections, held meetings, qualified opportunities, sourced pipeline, and the recommended next investment.

## Delivery schedule

- **Days 1–2:** kickoff, ICP and economics locked, suppression imported, domains and inboxes ordered.
- **Days 2–4:** list built and verified, signals attached, playbooks drafted and approved.
- **By business day 5:** campaigns, routing, alerts, CRM, calendar attribution, and reporting live.
- **Days 6–13:** coordinated execution, reply handling, queue refinement, and live dial block.
- **Day 14:** written Pipeline Verdict and go, refine, or stop call.

## Continuing after the Proof

The Pipeline Verdict makes one recommendation:

- **Staffing Client Pipeline OS — $4,500/month:** managed email, signals, coordinated LinkedIn/video actions, reply routing, daily Call-Ready Queue, CRM/calendar attribution, weekly learning, and call coaching. Client's team works the call queue.

The $2,000 Proof fee is part of the first $4,500 month, not an added setup charge. If Client continues with the core service, $2,500 is due for month one. The initial managed term is 90 days, followed by month-to-month service.
