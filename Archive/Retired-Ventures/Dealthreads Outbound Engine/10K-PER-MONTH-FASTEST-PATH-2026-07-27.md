# Dealthreads: Fastest Credible Path to $10K/Month

**Date:** 2026-07-27  
**Goal:** First dollars inside 10 days; $10K+ recurring monthly revenue inside 45–60 days.  
**Decision:** Sell Dealthreads as a managed service now. Treat the software as the delivery advantage, not as a standalone SaaS product.

## Executive diagnosis

Dealthreads is substantial. The local product has a real operating layer for signals, replies, suppression, CRM handoff, attribution, buyer confirmation, notifications, persistence, and a revenue watchdog. All 34 automated tests pass.

The business is not blocked by product depth. It is blocked by commercial proof and offer consistency:

- zero paid Pipeline Proofs;
- zero Proof-to-Engine conversions;
- zero retained Dealthreads clients;
- one detailed staffing sales call, but no close;
- multiple historical offers and ICPs still visible in the workspace;
- the live site still sells the archived 5-Day Trial while the current offer specifies a 14-Day Pipeline Proof;
- the live site still shows a completion install fee and Email Engine option that the current offer removed.

The commercial system is sophisticated enough to create activity. The missing behavior is asking for money, holding enough qualified sales calls, and running paid Proofs.

## What is actually valuable

The strongest product is not the dashboard, automation, AI, enrichment, or inbox fleet individually.

It is this:

> Dealthreads gives a specialist staffing firm a managed new-client acquisition motion: the right accounts, a reason to contact them now, coordinated outreach, fast reply handling, and a visible path from first touch to sourced pipeline.

The buyer owns the infrastructure. Dealthreads runs the execution and operating discipline. That is meaningfully different from rented agency infrastructure and self-serve AI SDR software.

### Not ready to sell as standalone SaaS

A live readiness run passed 27 of 38 checks in the local production process. The remaining checks failed because the local instance was not hardened and protected surfaces returned 503s. The local run also reported stubbed HubSpot, heuristic extraction, dry-run email, and disabled SDR automation.

That is not a reason to delay selling the managed service. It is a reason not to sell “software access” as the product. Production wiring should be completed as paid onboarding work after a Proof is sold, not as another speculative build cycle.

### Core positioning problem

The current buyer language says staffing owners lack time for business development. The $4,500 offer then asks their team to work a daily call queue. That can work, but it must be qualified explicitly before the Proof is sold.

The sales call must establish one of two paths:

- **Core:** Dealthreads runs email, signals, reply operations, follow-up, attribution, and coaching; the client supplies a named caller for 30 minutes per day.
- **Managed:** if the client lacks calling capacity, Dealthreads measures the workload during the Proof and scopes calling separately.

Do not discover the absence of call capacity after the Proof.

## Decision rules

- **Under four held calls per week:** acquisition execution problem. Do not change the offer.
- **Six or more qualified calls and no Proof sale:** sales-call/close problem. Review calls and tighten the ask.
- **Three paid Proofs and no continuation:** fulfillment/value problem. Inspect what buyers used and what they ignored.
- **Prospects repeatedly lack calling capacity:** use Proof activity to price the required coverage, then quote managed calling separately with sufficient margin.
- **Prospects repeatedly cannot afford $4,500 despite strong economics:** the list is too small or too low-revenue; move upmarket within staffing, not into another vertical.

## Bottom line

The fastest path is not to launch software. It is to sell six paid Proofs and convert three of them.

Dealthreads already contains enough product, fulfillment infrastructure, lists, scripts, and warm opportunities to do that. The next bottleneck is a human one: make the ask, collect payment, run the Proof, ask for continuation, and repeat without changing the machine.
