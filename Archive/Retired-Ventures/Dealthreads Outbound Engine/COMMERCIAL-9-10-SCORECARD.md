## The proof ladder

Do not replace these gates with vanity metrics.

1. **Self-proof:** Dealthreads books and holds five qualified Pipeline Math calls through its own outbound engine.
2. **Paid proof:** Two staffing firms buy the $2,000 14-Day Pipeline Proof.
3. **Conversion proof:** At least one Proof converts to the $4,500 90-day engagement.
4. **Outcome proof:** The client receives attributable positive replies, qualified conversations, and meetings, with every stage visible.
5. **Retention proof:** At least one client stays beyond 90 days because the system keeps learning and producing.
6. **Transfer proof:** A second seller can explain, qualify, and close the offer using the playbook.
