# Dealthreads Sales Playbook v3

## The 30-second explanation

Dealthreads installs and runs the client-acquisition operating system for specialized staffing firms. Every morning, the team gets the exact companies to call, why they may need talent now, and the opener to use. Email, calling, LinkedIn, replies, follow-ups, calendar activity, and CRM attribution run as one account thread. The client owns the machine; Dealthreads operates the brain and execution.

## What we sell

### Entry

**14-Day Pipeline Proof — $2,000**

Installed in 5 business days and run for 14 calendar days. The client receives 500 verified target accounts, signals, playbooks, client-owned infrastructure, live email, a signal-ranked call queue, reply routing, CRM/calendar attribution, one live dial block, and a written Pipeline Verdict.

### Continue

**Staffing Client Pipeline OS — $4,500/month**

The client's team works the prepared daily call queue. Dealthreads manages email, signals, reply routing, attribution, weekly learning, and call coaching. Ninety-day initial term. The $2,000 Proof fee credits completely against month one. No extra setup fee.

### Managed calling

Managed calling may be scoped after the Proof when the client does not have daily calling capacity. Quote it only after the required volume, coverage, and contractor cost are understood.

### Downsell

**List Sprint — $497**

Five hundred verified buyers with signals and opening angles, delivered within 48 hours. Offered only after a Proof no or timing stall. Credits to a Proof within 60 days.

## Who qualifies

A prospect is qualified only when all five are true:

1. They specialize enough to define the client-side buyer.
2. One new client relationship has meaningful gross-profit value.
3. They can handle more client conversations.
4. Someone will work the call queue or they will buy managed calling.
5. If the Proof works, they can fund $4,500 monthly for 90 days.

Do not sell around a missing qualification condition.

## The acquisition CTA

Do not ask a cold prospect to "jump on a quick call."

Ask:

> Give me the niche you recruit in. I will show you 10 companies with a current reason to need talent and the exact buyer I would contact.

The sample earns the Pipeline Math call.

## The five-stage sales process

### 1. Permission

Earn permission to build the 10-account niche proof.

Required CRM fields:

- staffing specialty;
- geography;
- typical client profile;
- highest-value placement type;
- likely owner of client acquisition.

### 2. Proof

Deliver one complete package within the positive-reply SLA:

- 10–20 precise target accounts;
- the reason each account is relevant now;
- the buyer role;
- the opening angle;
- one paragraph explaining the selection logic;
- one call to action: a 30-minute Pipeline Math session.

Do not send a placeholder acknowledgment if the package can be completed inside the SLA.

### 3. Pipeline Math

The call has four jobs:

1. validate the target;
2. calculate the economics;
3. expose the current workflow leak;
4. recommend one fixed next step.

Questions:

- "Which placement or client relationship is most valuable for you to win this quarter?"
- "What is the gross profit of one placement in that desk?"
- "How many placements does a good new client produce in the first year?"
- "When a recruiter spots a possible client today, what happens next?"
- "Who owns the follow-up when somebody replies?"
- "How many new client conversations could your team absorb next month?"
- "Who would work a prepared call queue for 30 minutes a day?"
- "If the 14-day Proof shows a workable motion, can you fund $4,500 a month for the next 90 days?"

### 4. Recommendation

Do not present a menu.

Say:

> Based on the value of one client relationship and the fact that your team can work the queue, I recommend the $2,000 Pipeline Proof. We install it in five business days, run it for 14 calendar days, and make the continuation decision from your data. If you continue, the full $2,000 becomes part of month one—there is no additional setup fee.

If they lack calling capacity:

> The standard plan will fail if nobody works the queue. We can use the Proof to measure the required coverage and then scope managed calling separately. I will not invent that price before we know the workload.

### 5. Close

Ask directly:

> Do you want the first 14 days to start on [specific date]?

If yes:

1. confirm the start date;
2. send the fixed SOW and payment link within one hour;
3. book kickoff while still on the call;
4. assign the human next action and due date in the deal registry.

No custom proposal unless scope is genuinely outside the standard offer.

## Objection responses

### "Can you guarantee meetings?"

> No. I can guarantee that the system goes live, the agreed activity is completed, every response is routed, and you can audit every step. A meeting guarantee before testing your market rewards vendors for booking low-quality calls. We measure held meetings and sourced pipeline, but we do not fabricate the result in advance.

### "Why not buy an AI SDR for $500?"

> You should if you want software and already have someone to design the market, protect deliverability, coordinate calling, handle replies, enforce follow-up, and learn from pipeline. Dealthreads is the managed operating system and accountability layer, not another sending tool.

### "Why are we making the calls at $4,500?"

> Because your recruiters already understand talent and live conversations. We remove the research, prioritization, scripting, follow-up, and system work so their 30-minute block is spent talking to the right accounts. If you do not have that capacity, we scope managed calling after the Proof from the measured workload.

### "We have been burned by agencies."

> That is why the Proof is paid, short, auditable, and client-owned. You keep the list, infrastructure, playbooks, CRM data, recordings, and verdict. The decision to continue comes from your evidence, not our deck.

### "We grow through referrals."

> Keep the referral channel. This is for the quarters when referrals do not arrive on schedule. The Proof tells us whether a controlled outbound channel can complement what already works.

### "The price is too high."

> It may be. Let us use your placement economics rather than argue about price. If one realistic client relationship cannot support the 90-day investment, I will not recommend the program.

### "Send me information."

> I will send the one-page scope. Before I do, what specialty should the example use, and who is the ideal client-side buyer?

## Scoreboard

The sales team is measured on:

- sample permission rate;
- sample delivery time;
- sample-to-Pipeline-Math rate;
- booked-to-held rate;
- held-to-Proof rate;
- Proof-to-Engine rate;
- sourced qualified pipeline;
- median positive-reply response time;
- fulfillment hours and gross margin.

Raw sends and raw booked meetings are diagnostic metrics, not the definition of success.

## Claims discipline

Allowed:

- the system is live;
- integrations and safeguards that can be demonstrated;
- client-owned assets;
- verified activity and response-time receipts;
- specific case evidence with attribution and permission.

Not allowed:

- guaranteed meetings, placements, or revenue;
- "fully autonomous SDR";
- invented ROI;
- implying multiple retained staffing clients before they exist;
- claiming the product is 9/10 proven before the proof gate in `OFFER.md` is met.
