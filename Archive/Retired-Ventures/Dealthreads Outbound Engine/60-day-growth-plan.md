# The 60-Day Growth Plan — 2026-08-04 → 2026-10-03

Cold email is now a background process. It sends 2,450/day whether or not you
touch it. The question is what fills the other five hours, and the answer is the
four channels that compound, ranked by how fast they turn into money.

---

## 1. WARM NETWORK — fastest money, never worked, zero cost

The single most underexploited asset you own: **1,510 LinkedIn connections and
20 years of relationships**, and the block has never once been run.

Kirby, roofing, the SDR floor, the AM job, Drata colleagues, every recruiter you
ever competed with. These people already know you can sell. Some of them work at
or know staffing firms. Nobody in that group knows Dealthreads exists.

**The play:** 20 personal touches a day. Not a pitch, a founder note.
> "Hey [name], long time. I got laid off Tuesday and I'm going all in on the
> thing I've been building nights and weekends: an AI system that runs client
> acquisition for staffing firms. If you know anyone running a staffing or
> recruiting shop, I'd love an intro. If not, no worries at all, hope you're well."

Honesty is the hook. People help someone who just got laid off and is building
something real. **20/day × 40 weekdays = 800 touches.** At a 10% reply rate that
is 80 conversations with people predisposed to help you.

**Time: 1 hour/day. Expected: the highest hit-rate channel available to you.**

## 2. PARTNERSHIPS / REFERRAL CHANNELS — built, written, never sent

39 partner rows and 8 personalized first-touches are sitting in the execution
pack unsent. Staffing lenders (Advance Partners, eCapital, Payro, Encore,
Madison), coaches (Mike Gionta / RecruiterU), and staffing-marketing firms
(David Searns / Haley Marketing).

These are **leveraged**: one funding rep knows 200 staffing firms, and every one
of their borrowers is asking "how do I get more clients?" — a question the
partner cannot answer and you can.

**The play:** send the 8 written touches this week. Then 5 new partner
conversations a week. Ask for nothing but a 20-minute call to compare client
bases. Referral relationships take 2-4 weeks to produce their first intro, which
lands inside this window.

**Time: 45 min/day. Expected: 1-3 referral relationships live by day 30.**

## 3. PODCASTS / BORROWED AUDIENCE — 22 shows mapped, 8 pitches written

Staffing has a dense podcast ecosystem and every host needs guests weekly. You
have the one thing none of their usual guests have: real numbers from running the
system on your own company, including the failures.

**The play:** submit the two guest forms (The Staffing Show, Elite Recruiter) and
send the 6 written pitches. Booking lag is 4-8 weeks, so the ones you send this
week air inside the window. Each episode is a permanent credibility asset that
prospects find when they Google you.

**Time: 30 min/day for a week, then done until bookings land.**

## 4. CONTENT — slowest to pay, but it multiplies all three above

Be honest about the timeline: **content does not produce revenue in 60 days.**
It produces credibility that raises close rates on everything else immediately,
and inbound at month 4-6. Both are worth having. Neither is a rescue plan.

**The play, minimum viable:**
- **The tour video, recorded once.** "I built an AI sales team. Here's the honest
  tour." Every prospect Googles you before a call. This is the single highest-ROI
  recording you can make and it has been pending for three weeks.
- **3 LinkedIn posts a week** as Ryan, from the changelog: what the machine did,
  what broke, what a real number was. No hooks, no hustle-porn. Your buyers are
  ON LinkedIn, and posts get seen by the exact staffing owners you're inviting.
- **One YouTube episode a week**, sprint-log format, one take.

**Time: 1 hour/day. Expected: zero direct revenue in 60 days, materially better
close rates starting week 2.**

## The 5th thing: GET EXCELLENT AT DELIVERY

The fastest path from one client to three is **one client who is thrilled**.
When the first deal closes, over-deliver visibly, then ask for the referral and
the case study in the same week. A named case study on the site converts every
future cold reply at a higher rate than any copy change ever will.

## The daily shape (8 hours, weekdays)

| Time | Block | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 7:00-7:30 | Brief, triage, plan the day's asks | The machine reports overnight |
| 7:30-9:00 | **ASKS** — replies, deals, packages, proposals | Money lives here, do it first |
| 9:00-11:00 | **DIAL BLOCK** — warm sheet first, then cold bench | East Coast morning |
| 11:00-12:00 | **WARM NETWORK** — 20 personal touches | Highest hit rate, untouched |
| 1:00-1:45 | **PARTNERSHIPS** — 5 new conversations/week | Leveraged channel |
| 1:45-2:45 | **CONTENT** — record or write, one take | Compounds, never urgent |
| 2:45-3:45 | **DIAL BLOCK 2** — Central/West | Second time zone |
| 3:45-4:30 | LinkedIn manual: comment, engage accepts, Looms | Feeds the invite engine |
| 4:30-5:00 | Fulfillment, admin, tomorrow's setup | |

**Fridays:** swap Dial Block 2 for recording.

## The 60-day arc
- **Weeks 1-2:** close the 7 warm. Payment links live. Partner + podcast pitches
  sent. Warm network block starts. Tour video recorded.
- **Weeks 3-4:** first referral conversations. First List Sprints delivered. First
  case study from whoever closes. Content cadence established.
- **Weeks 5-8:** referrals start producing. Podcasts air. The machine's own
  at-bats compound. Second and third close.

## What does NOT grow this business (do not do these)
New tools. New verticals. A website redesign. Rebuilding anything that works.
More lists — you have 7,600 unworked rows. More automation — the machine is
built. A job search that quietly eats the selling hours.

## The scoreboard for 60 days
Four numbers, weekly: **asks made · warm touches · partner conversations ·
things recorded.** Everything else is the machine's job.
