## Why no Salesfinity import
Six contacts is a phone and a piece of paper, not a dialer queue. Salesfinity earns its keep
on hundreds of cold rows; importing six warm ones adds a step and removes the thing that makes
warm calls work — you looking at their reply while the phone rings. Boss Mode and number
scoring stay on the roadmap for the cold bench, where they belong.
