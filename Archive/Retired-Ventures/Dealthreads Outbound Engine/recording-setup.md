# Recording Setup — Operator Log flow

Gear: Sony ZV-E10 II · Sigma 16mm f/1.4 · Rode Wireless mic · Mac.
Principle: ONE record button, ONE file, transcript comes out the other end. No file juggling, no audio syncing, no timeline editing.

## One-time setup (do once, ~45 minutes)

**Camera as webcam:**
1. Plug the ZV-E10 II into the Mac with USB-C. On the camera screen pick **USB Streaming (UVC/UAC)** when prompted (Menu → Setup → USB → USB Streaming if it doesn't prompt). The camera now shows up on the Mac as a webcam AND a microphone. USB-C also powers the camera, so battery never matters at the desk.
2. Camera settings for the desk: Movie mode, 4K 24p internally doesn't matter for webcam use (USB streams 1080p, plenty for a corner bubble). Aperture f/2.2, shutter 1/50, ISO Auto, Face/Eye AF ON, Product Showcase OFF, Soft Skin OFF, Picture Profile OFF (default color looks right without grading).
3. Position: camera at eye level or a touch above, arm's length plus a hand. The 16mm at that distance frames head and shoulders with a little room. Sit FACING your brightest window. That is the whole lighting plan.

**Mic:**
4. Rode transmitter clipped to your collar, 6 inches below your chin. Receiver plugs into the camera's 3.5mm mic jack. Audio rides the same USB cable as video — one source, always in sync.
5. Set camera audio level so your normal voice peaks around the -12 mark on the meter (Menu → Audio → Audio Rec Level). If the Rode has a pad/gain setting, leave it at 0 and adjust on camera.

**Software (one app: Descript, ~$24/mo):**
6. Install Descript. It records screen + webcam + mic together, transcribes automatically, and editing = deleting words from the transcript. You never touch a video timeline. The transcript is also what feeds the LinkedIn posts and warm-list email, so this one app IS the content pipeline.
7. In Descript: New project → Record → Screen + Camera. Pick "ZV-E10M2" (or similar) as BOTH camera and microphone. Choose the screen. Camera bubble bottom-right, medium.
8. Do a 60-second test: talk, play it back, check the audio meter moved and your face is sharp. Done forever.

## Every Friday (the actual flow, ~50 minutes)

1. **Coffee + beat sheet.** Telegram has it at 7:00. Read once. (5 min)
2. **Set the screen.** Open the tabs the beats mention: SmartLead analytics, Salesfinity, the Origami table, whatever broke this week. Close everything else. Hide bookmarks bar (Cmd+Shift+B), turn on Do Not Disturb.
3. **Record.** Descript → Record. Say beat 1 and go. ONE TAKE. Flub a sentence? Pause two seconds, say it again, keep rolling — you'll delete the flub in text later. Stop at 15 minutes even if you're on a roll. (20 min)
4. **Edit by reading.** Descript shows the transcript. Delete the flubbed sentences like editing an email. Click Remove Filler Words once. That is the entire edit. (10 min)
5. **Export + hand off.** Export 1080p for YouTube. Highlight the 2 best 60-90s moments (the faceplant and the lesson) → export vertical clips with captions. Copy the transcript, paste it to me. I cut the LinkedIn text post and the warm-list email from your words, and package title + thumbnail. (15 min)

## Face-only videos (war stories, the trust video)

Same chain, no screen: Descript → Record → Camera only. OR record internally on the camera (4K 24p, same settings) and drag the card file into Descript for the same transcript-edit flow. Internal recording looks noticeably better for full-screen face — use it for evergreen videos like "My first client fired me."

## Rules that keep it easy

- Never script. The beat sheet is the only paper on the desk.
- Never re-record a whole take because of one flub. Fix it in text.
- Never edit for polish. Cut mistakes, keep the human. The pauses and "look, here's the thing" moments are the anti-slop.
- Publish Monday. Friday's recording does not need to be perfect by Friday.
- If Descript ever feels heavy, fallback is OBS (free) for recording + upload raw. Rough beats unpublished.
