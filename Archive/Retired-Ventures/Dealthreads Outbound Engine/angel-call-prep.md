## Call structure (30 min)

1. **2 min — rapport + agenda.** "I put a short working deck together. Plan: what we found in your market, exactly how your list was built, your math of sales, and what a test looks like. Sound right?"
2. **5 min — discovery (the slide says "tell us where we're wrong").** Questions, in order of importance:
   - "Where does new business come from today, honestly?" (expect: referrals, repeat, job boards)
   - "What's your average fee per placement, and how many placements does a new client turn into in year one?" (feeds the math slide; typical direct-hire fee $15-25K, so ONE client pays for everything, let her do that math out loud)
   - "How many more open job orders could you take on right now before delivery breaks?" (sizes the engine, also surfaces urgency)
   - "You came from Randstad. What did having a real BD engine behind you feel like versus now?" (the emotional question, ask it plainly)
   - "What did you try for outbound before, and what happened?"
3. **8 min — the methodology walkthrough** (slides 5-7). Screen-share her actual sample CSV at some point; the product demo IS the list. Hit CallRail/Avanos/Curant signals and the "req does the opening" line.
4. **5 min — math of sales live** (slide 8). Fill the blanks with HER numbers. Land: "so one client covers the system for months."
5. **5 min — the trial** (slides 9-10). Day-by-day, what she keeps, the anti-guarantee verbatim.
6. **5 min — the ask.** "Pipeline Proof capacity is real, and the next opening starts Monday. It's $2,000, credits fully into month one if we keep going. Want the slot?" Then STOP TALKING. Send the payment link while on the call.

## Objection prep

- **"We do outreach ourselves, that's literally our job."** "Then you know the grind better than anyone, and you know what happens to it the week you're closing placements. This runs whether your desks are full or not. And you keep the machine, so worst case you've bought your team a system."
- **"How is this different from lead gen agencies?"** "They rent you results from infrastructure they own. Everything here gets registered in Shine Hire's name on day one: domains, list, recordings, data. Fire me Friday and keep it all."
- **"$2,000 to test?"** "Your average placement fee is $___ (use her number). If the machine produces one client a quarter it's the cheapest BD hire you'll ever make. And if the math doesn't work, the verdict says so in writing and you keep everything anyway."
- **"I need to think about it."** "Totally fair. The only pressure that's real: I run two of these a month because I'm the one dialing, and Monday's slot goes to whoever takes it first. Want me to pencil you in and you confirm by Friday?"
- **"Does this work for contingency/no-win-no-fee shops?"** "It's better for you: your pitch costs the client nothing to say yes to, which makes the cold conversation easier, not harder. That's why we open with the req they can't fill."
