# Dealthreads Loom script — v1 (ported from Oloxa v3.1 structure)

90 seconds, three sections, three screens. Record in one take; done beats
perfect. Sections 2's spine is LOCKED (same proof every time — consistency
compounds); Sections 1 and 3 personalize from the queue row.

## Recording setup (once)
Loom desktop, webcam bubble bottom-left, browser with two tabs ready:
their LinkedIn profile + your live scoreboard/signal table. That's it.

---

## Section 1 — OFFER (~30s, their LinkedIn profile on screen)

"Hey [FIRST NAME] — thanks for connecting. I recorded this instead of sending
a wall of text. I'm looking at [COMPANY] and [PERSONALIZATION — the queue
row's line, spoken naturally: 'you place office and admin staff in
Charlotte']. Here's why I reached out specifically: firms your size usually
have every desk full and nobody owning the client side — new business shows
up when someone finds time to prospect, which is never. I build the system
that fixes exactly that."

## Section 2 — PROOF (~30s, LOCKED — switch tabs to the live signal table)

"This is my own dashboard, live, not a mockup. Every morning this system
reads hiring activity across the market and finds companies that need
staffing help RIGHT NOW — this column is companies hiring [THEIR NICHE]
roles this week. It runs email in my name, feeds my call list, and pings my
phone the minute anyone replies. I run my whole company on it — every number
I could show you is from my own pipeline, including the ugly ones."

## Section 3 — CTA (~20s, flip back to their profile)

"If I pointed this at [COMPANY]'s market, the first thing you'd see is a
list like that one with your niche in it. I'll build you a sample this week,
free, so you can judge the rows instead of my pitch. Worth 15 minutes?
There's a link under this video — or just reply here. Either way, thanks for
watching, [FIRST NAME]."

---

## DM that carries the Loom (Ryan sends, one line, no essay)

> [First name] — 90 seconds on why I reached out, recorded just for you
> (your market's actually in it): [LOOM URL]

## F1 — 24h later, no reply
> Worth the 90 seconds? Honest answers welcome — including "not for us."

## F2 — +3 days, no reply (the value drop, then quiet)
> Either way, here are three companies in your niche hiring right now:
> [3 real rows — company, role, signal]. That's the machine's output for
> [their metro/niche] this week. If you want the full list, it's a
> 15-minute call. If not, keep these on the house.

## Rules
- Never promise meetings. The guarantee lives in the trial, not the DM.
- Every number spoken must be real and current (anti-hype constitution).
- [PERSONALIZATION] comes from the queue row; if it's blank or wrong on
  their profile, fix it live or skip the prospect — never guess on camera.
- One take. A stumble makes it human; a third take makes it Tuesday.
