## What they actually teach (condensed)

**Eric Bledsoe's $154k/60-day model:**
- Acquisition: 100% in-person trust — masterminds, networking events, business lunches. Becomes "the AI guy." Zero cold outreach, zero ads. ~5 referrals per client.
- Mechanism: PAID discovery audit → interview owner + key employees → map workflows → plain-English recommendations.
- Pricing: $10k first "AI employee" install + $2,500 per additional seat.
- Delivery: audit → analysis → build → install → train → ongoing modular expansion.
- Positioning: AIOS as architectural foundation; toggle-menu of mini-solutions clients self-select from. No hard sell — clients buy modules over time.
- Core message: you don't need to be technical; you need to understand business and genuinely use the system yourself.

**Tyler's retainer model:**
- Tiers: core / pro / complete. Priced PER BUSINESS, not per seat, sold to whoever signs the check.
- Positioning: fractional C-suite (CSO/CFO/COO) at ~1/3 the market rate of the human role. URL dashboard with permission levels.
- The 85% conversion mechanism: the PAID DISCOVERY PHASE IS THE SALES CALL. Clients pay to have their problem investigated and solved live. Filters non-serious prospects instantly.
- Target: SMBs doing $5-20M/year. Avoid enterprise (sales cycles too long).
- Objection play: "no time" → Otto's five questions, let the pain sit overnight. Proposals branded in the CLIENT's logo/colors.

**Nick + Otto's audit sales process ($10-30k closes):**
- Three sales blockers every prospect has: FIT, CLARITY, TRUST. The audit resolves all three. Doctor/diagnostician frame, never vendor.
- Five-question discovery: (1) biggest problem right now? (2) what's it costing you? (3) how long has it been this way? (4) what does the business look like in 6-12 months if nothing changes? (5) magic-wand future state.
- Two-realities proposal: side-by-side of Reality 1 (cost of staying the same) vs Reality 2 (future state) — built entirely from the PROSPECT'S OWN NUMBERS.
- Close: show, don't tell — a working proof-of-concept built from audit findings.

**The audit sprint structure:**
- Pre-work: scope tied to a business goal, kickoff scheduled, shared docs folder, named stakeholders, aligned timeline.
- Phase 1 (problem): interviews, surveys, observation — NO AI TALK.
- Phase 2 (solution): synthesize use cases, prototype, validation interviews with frontline users (pre-primes the org and builds bottom-up demand).
- Comms: weekly check-ins, three-point updates (what we found / what we need / what's next).
- Proposal meeting: five moments culminating in live prototype demos — moves prospects "from evaluating to wanting."

**Their outbound module (validation for Ryan):** their own AIOS outreach system is a 7-stage funnel production line with campaign analysis and a self-improving loop — Instantly/SmartLead, Apollo for 2k+ volume pulls, Clay only for behavioral signals. Ryan's engine already does all of this plus signal-driven dialing and QC. He is not behind this community; he is ahead of it on the outbound axis.
