# Dealthreads Outbound Engine — Offer

## OFFER v3 (2026-07-26) — current commercial offer

### Category

**The Staffing Client Pipeline OS**

Dealthreads is not sold as an AI SDR, a list-building service, or a fractional rep. It is a managed client-acquisition operating system for specialized staffing and recruiting firms.

### Exact buyer

The primary buyer is a US staffing or recruiting firm that:

- has 5 to 50 recruiters or revenue-producing team members;
- works a defined specialty such as legal, executive, healthcare, IT/cyber, industrial, or professional search;
- earns enough gross profit from one new client relationship to justify a $4,500 monthly acquisition investment;
- can handle additional client conversations;
- has an owner, BD leader, or recruiter who will work a prepared daily call queue, or will purchase done-for-you calling;
- will approve messaging and provide suppression data within two business days.

Disqualify solo operators with no execution capacity, undefined generalists, firms seeking guaranteed meetings, firms unable to fund a 90-day continuation, and firms without a clear client-side buyer.

### One-line promise

**Every morning, your team gets the exact companies to call, why they may need talent now, and the opener to use. Every reply is routed, every follow-up is timed, and no interested buyer dies in an inbox.**

### The commercial mechanism

1. **Find the reason now.** Dealthreads ranks companies using hiring, expansion, leadership, regulatory, funding, facility, and niche-specific demand signals.
2. **Run one account thread.** Email, calling, LinkedIn, and personalized video use the same account context instead of operating as disconnected campaigns.
3. **Collapse response time.** Positive replies and engagement create a dossier, suggested response, SLA timer, and immediate next action.
4. **Book synchronously.** Calls place the meeting while the prospect is live. Email offers two times before falling back to a calendar link.
5. **Learn from revenue.** The weekly review follows the chain from positive reply to held meeting, qualified opportunity, trial, and sourced pipeline.

### The 14-Day Pipeline Proof — $2,000

The machine is installed within 5 business days and run for 14 calendar days so the buyer sees a real operating cycle rather than a short burst of activity.

The Proof includes:

- one written specialist ICP with exclusions;
- 500 scored, verified client accounts;
- live reasons to contact the highest-priority accounts;
- domains and inboxes registered in the client's name and warming;
- approved email, call, and LinkedIn playbooks;
- live email from the Dealthreads warmed fleet while client-owned infrastructure warms;
- the Staffing Client Pipeline OS: morning brief, hot-reply alerts, reply routing, SLA timers, Call-Ready Queue, calendar logging, CRM attribution, and scorecards;
- one 60-to-90-minute live dial block with Ryan;
- a day-14 Pipeline Verdict covering activity, conversations, objections, held calls, qualified opportunities, and recommended next investment.

The $2,000 credits completely against the first $4,500 month when the client continues within seven days of the verdict. There is no additional completion or setup fee.

### The continuation — $4,500/month

One public core offer:

- managed email execution and deliverability;
- weekly signal-ranked account refills;
- coordinated LinkedIn and personalized-video actions;
- daily Call-Ready Queue with evidence and openers;
- reply classification, suppression, SLA monitoring, and follow-up queues;
- HubSpot attribution and calendar logging;
- weekly funnel review, copy decisions, and call coaching;
- 90-day initial term, then month to month.

The client's team works the call queue. This responsibility is stated before the Proof is sold, not discovered during onboarding.

### Managed calling — scoped after the Proof

For firms without daily calling capacity, Dealthreads may scope a managed contract caller after the Proof. Pricing is based on the measured calling volume, coverage, and contractor cost. Ryan owns strategy, review, and escalation rather than acting as the permanent caller. Do not publish or quote a fixed managed-calling price before the Proof establishes the workload.

### Tools

Client-owned tools and data are billed directly to the client at cost, normally $250 to $1,000 per month depending on volume. Dealthreads never marks up software.

### Guarantee

Dealthreads does not guarantee meetings, placements, or revenue.

It guarantees:

1. the promised machine is installed within 5 business days;
2. the agreed activity is run and documented for the 14-day Proof;
3. every valid positive reply is surfaced with a next action and SLA timer;
4. suppression and negative responses are enforced across channels;
5. the client receives the list, domains, inboxes, playbooks, CRM data, recordings, dispositions, and Proof report.

If the promised system and activity are not delivered, the Proof fee is refunded. The client keeps everything already built.

### Sales math

The 90-day core service investment is $13,500 before client-owned tools. The Proof fee is part of that amount, not an added charge.

The discovery call must calculate:

`gross profit per placement × expected placements per new client relationship × close rate`

No generic ROI claim is allowed. The rep uses the buyer's own placement economics to decide whether the service can pay back.

### One CTA

Cold outreach does not ask for a generic discovery call.

**"Give me the niche you recruit in. I will show you 10 companies with a current reason to need talent and the exact buyer I would contact."**

The sample is the proof of targeting. The next call is a 30-minute Pipeline Math session, not a product demo.

### Sales paths

- **Qualified:** $2,000 Pipeline Proof.
- **Qualified but no calling capacity:** $2,000 Pipeline Proof with managed calling identified as a possible separately scoped continuation. Do not imply a fixed price before the Proof measures the workload.
- **Interested but not ready:** $497 List Sprint, offered only after a Proof no or clear timing stall; credits to a Proof within 60 days.
- **Not qualified:** decline cleanly and preserve the relationship.

### Proof standard before claiming 9/10

Dealthreads may call the offer 9/10-ready now. It may not claim the product is commercially proven until it has:

- booked five held calls for Dealthreads through the same operating loop;
- sold at least two paid Pipeline Proofs;
- converted at least one Proof to a 90-day continuation;
- measured positive reply → held meeting → qualified opportunity → sourced pipeline;
- documented fulfillment hours and gross margin;
- retained at least one client beyond the initial 90 days.

### The three-layer story (new positioning spine)

**Layer 1 — The Machine (you own it, forever):** domains, inboxes, verified buyer list, sequences, scripts, recordings, CRM data. Registered in the client's name day one. Fire us and keep it all. (Unchanged from v1; this promise is sacred.)

**Layer 2 — The Execution (we run it):** email campaigns live from day one via the warmed fleet, personalized video, list refills re-verified weekly, copy and signal tuning. Done for you.

**Layer 3 — The OS (the brain; licensed while you're a client):** the same AI operating system that runs Dealthreads itself, instanced for the client:
- Morning brief in their Slack/Telegram/email: replies waiting, who's hot, today's Call-Ready Queue with the opener written per target ("they've had the forward deployed role open 3 weeks")
- Instant hot-reply alerts with a reply-SLA clock
- The reply router: every reply classified and staged (interested → next step queued, OOO → auto-resumed + phones mined, handoffs → auto-harvested into a warm referral sequence, negatives → suppressed everywhere)
- Auto-refill: the list never runs dry, every tranche re-verified at load
- Evening scorecard with a letter grade; weekly funnel review with their real math of sales
- Signal layer daily pass: who in their market is hiring TODAY

Positioning line: "Anyone can send campaigns. The OS is why nothing gets dropped: no reply unanswered, no follow-up missed, no cold list rotting. Your team just picks up the phone when it says to."

**Retention architecture (internal, never say this out loud):** the client owns Layer 1 forever, which makes the yes easy. Layer 3 is licensed while subscribed, which makes leaving expensive in a way that's honest: they keep the machine, they lose the brain. The OS is the churn moat.
