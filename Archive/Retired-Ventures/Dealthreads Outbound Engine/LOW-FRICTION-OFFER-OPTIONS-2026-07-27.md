# Dealthreads Low-Friction Offer Options

**Date:** July 27, 2026  
**Objective:** Create a one-time front door that is easy to buy, produces commercial proof, and leads naturally to managed recurring revenue.

## First principle

The current $2,000 Pipeline Proof is already a one-time purchase. Its hidden friction is the visible $4,500 monthly decision behind it.

Reducing the entry price without changing that perception may not materially improve conversion. The cleaner move is to sell a self-contained one-time outcome and make continuation a decision reached after evidence exists.

## Option 1 — Founding Pipeline Proof

**Price:** $1,500 once  
**Term:** 14 days  
**Capacity:** First three clients  
**Recommended use:** Primary paid offer now

### Buyer receives

- specialist ICP and exclusions;
- 500 scored, verified client accounts;
- current reasons to contact the priority accounts;
- approved email and call playbooks;
- live email from the warmed fleet;
- reply routing, suppression, daily queue, and attribution;
- one live dial block;
- a written Pipeline Verdict;
- ownership of the client-specific assets.

### Commercial terms

- No retainer obligation.
- If the client wants Dealthreads to continue operating the system, the full $1,500 credits against month one.
- Do not lead with the $4,500 continuation on the public page. Discuss it during qualification and recommend it only at the Verdict.
- Price returns to $2,000 after three paid clients or one approved staffing case study, whichever comes first.

### Why it works

- It changes one number rather than inventing another product.
- It still tests the full mechanism.
- It produces the evidence needed to sell recurring service.
- Three clients create $4,500 in early cash and three chances at continuation.
- The capacity limit is real because Ryan is personally delivering the first Proofs.

### Risk

A $500 reduction will not save weak sales calls. The direct ask and follow-up still matter more than the discount.

## Option 2 — Client Pipeline Map

**Price:** $750 once  
**Term:** Five business days  
**Recommended use:** Lower-friction downsell or cautious-buyer entry

### Buyer receives

- one written niche and buyer definition;
- 50 verified target accounts;
- the 10 highest-priority reasons to contact;
- buyer roles and opening angles;
- one email sequence;
- one call opener and objection sheet;
- 45-minute Pipeline Math readout;
- all files delivered for the buyer to keep.

### Commercial terms

- Delivered without domains, inboxes, live campaigns, or ongoing management.
- Credits completely to a Pipeline Proof started within 30 days.
- Fixed scope. No custom research expansion.

### Why it works

- Easy to understand.
- Low trust requirement.
- Fast to fulfill from the existing system.
- Converts free sample recipients into wallet-out buyers.

### Risk

It can position Dealthreads as a list/research vendor. It should not replace the Pipeline Proof as the main recommendation on qualified calls.

## Option 3 — Pipeline System Install

**Price:** $2,500 once  
**Term:** Ten business days  
**Recommended use:** Buyers who want ownership but not a managed service

### Buyer receives

- 250–500 verified accounts;
- domains and inboxes in the buyer's name;
- approved email and call playbooks;
- CRM stages, suppression, and routing configuration;
- daily queue and reporting setup;
- recorded team handoff;
- 30 days of technical defect support.

### Commercial terms

- No campaigns operated after handoff.
- Client pays tools directly.
- Ongoing operation is optional and quoted separately.

### Why it works

- Clean one-time transformation.
- Strong ownership story.
- Higher immediate cash.
- No recurring-service pressure.

### Risk

This sells implementation rather than outcomes. The client can blame the system when their team does not execute. It also requires constant new sales to maintain $10K per month.

## Option 4 — List Sprint

**Price:** $497 once  
**Term:** 48 hours  
**Recommended use:** Final downsell only

### Buyer receives

- 50 verified, signal-scored decision-makers;
- one contact per company;
- reason each row made the cut;
- verified emails and available direct lines;
- opening angle per priority row.

### Commercial terms

- Credits to a Pipeline Proof within 60 days.
- Offered only after a qualified buyer declines the Proof or Pipeline Map.

### Why it works

- Lowest friction.
- High gross margin.
- Fastest plausible first dollar.

### Risk

- Weakest differentiation.
- Attracts price-sensitive buyers.
- Requires roughly 21 sales per month to produce $10K.
- Does not prove Dealthreads can operate the client-acquisition system.

## Recommendation

Use a two-rung ladder:

1. **Primary:** Founding Pipeline Proof — $1,500 once for the first three clients.
2. **Downsell:** Client Pipeline Map — $750 once, credited to a Proof for 30 days.

Keep the $497 List Sprint as the final salvage offer. Do not make it the homepage product.

This gives a cautious buyer a real choice without presenting a pricing menu:

- Qualified and ready to test live: recommend the $1,500 Proof.
- Interested but not ready for live execution: recommend the $750 Map.
- Price-sensitive after declining both: offer the $497 List Sprint once.

## Validation rule

Do not redesign this again from intuition.

Run ten qualified sales conversations:

- If fewer than 30% accept the free 10-account sample, the targeting/message is the problem.
- If samples are accepted but fewer than 20% buy either paid rung, the offer or close is the problem.
- If Maps sell but do not upgrade, the Map is becoming a comfortable dead end.
- If Proofs sell but do not continue, fulfillment or recurring value is the problem.

The first review happens after ten qualified conversations, not after the first rejection.
