# Mid-market playbook — $5-20M staffing firms (51-200 employees)

Why upmarket: the solo operator (Angel) buys with personal money and haggles. The $5-20M firm buys with a P&L line item that already exists. Market size: 1,300 sales/BD leaders at US staffing firms 51-200 emp (AI Ark, measured 2026-07-21). Wave 3 pull in progress.

## Their pains, mapped to OS modules (lead with pain, prove with receipt)

1. **The BD seat bleeds money.** ~$150k loaded cost, six months of ramp, ~18-month tenure, and half the hires are recruiters pushed into sales who hate it. Meanwhile they're HIRING MORE (60+ firms 25-190 emp hiring BD reps in the signal table right now — they have budget by definition).
   → OS: the seat walks in day one with a full pipeline: signal-driven call queue, email air cover, reply routing. "I make your $150k seat productive in week one instead of month six."
2. **Client concentration + MSP/VMS margin squeeze.** Two anchor accounts = most of revenue; one lost contract is a layoff event. Direct clients pay better margins than VMS work but nobody has time to hunt them.
   → OS: always-on direct-client acquisition that runs whether or not anyone remembers to prospect.
3. **The ATS graveyard.** Hundreds of past clients in Bullhorn nobody has called since 2023. Warm converts ~10x cold (our own CA data on a 2,700-account base).
   → OS module: reactivation engine — mine their ATS export, warm sequences in the owner's voice. ALREADY BUILT and proven; demo-able this week.
4. **Redeployment leakage.** Contract end dates sit in the ATS; contractors roll off unredeployed and the client backfills with a competitor.
   → OS module: assignment-end signal feed → redeploy + backfill outreach. (New build, ~days, huge story.)
5. **Speed-to-lead losses.** Job orders are multi-agency races decided in hours; inbound leads sit overnight.
   → OS: instant classification + phone ping + 1-hour SLA enforcement. Receipt: our replies answered <1h since day one.
6. **All their AI money is candidate-side.** Bullhorn GRID: only ~10% run full-workflow AI, and spend concentrates on sourcing/matching. NOBODY is automating the client side. (Foundation report, citable.)
   → The whole OS. Positioning line: "Your ATS has AI for candidates. Who's automating your clients?"
7. **Owner can't see BD truth.** CRM theater, sandbagged pipelines, activity reports that measure typing.
   → OS: the one-screen brief + replies-per-100-touches channel math + call dispositions. Owners LOVE this artifact — show ours live.

Supporting stat: BD/new-client growth is the #1 stated priority for 44% of agency leaders (industry survey, foundation report). Pain and budget are both pre-confirmed; the sale is trust + specificity.

## Mid-market pricing (charge for the P&L line, not the favor)

- **Outbound Audit: $2,500** — paid sales call (Tyler mechanism). Two weeks: interviews (owner, VP sales, 2 reps), their real funnel numbers, ATS graveyard count, redeployment leak estimate, two-realities proposal in THEIR numbers, live POC = sample campaign + reactivation demo on their data.
- **OS Install: $15,000** — full deployment in their name (their infra, their card for tools).
- **Operate: core $5k / pro $7.5k / complete $10k per month** — per business, not per seat. Anchor everything to the $150k BD seat: complete tier = 80% less than one rep, never quits, gets smarter weekly.
- One perm placement ($20-25k fee) pays for a quarter. Say that sentence on every call.
