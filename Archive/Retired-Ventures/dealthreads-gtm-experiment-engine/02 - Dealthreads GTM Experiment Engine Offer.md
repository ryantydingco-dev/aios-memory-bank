## The weekly learning loop (the core IP)

This is what you're actually buying — productized, repeating every week:

1. **Context** — deepen company/offer understanding; refresh what we know about fit and objections.
2. **Universe** — build/expand and enrich the account universe (your TAM), scored by fit signals.
3. **Experiment design** — generate 3–5 angle/segment/message experiments, each with a clear hypothesis.
4. **Human-approved execution** — you approve; we send LinkedIn + Loom + follow-up on cadence. Nothing un-approved ships.
5. **Measure** — reply rate **and conversation quality** (the part most "outbound" skips). Which segment, angle, and opener earned *real* conversations?
6. **Improve** — kill what flopped, double down on what worked, write next week's plan. Week N+1 is sharper than week N — on purpose.

> The durable edge is the **loop + conversation-quality measurement**, not how many messages we can fire. Volume is a commodity (and now a liability — see deliverability moat below). Compounding learning is the moat.

## Why this beats the alternatives

**vs. hiring an SDR**
A fully-loaded US SDR runs **~$10–12k/mo**, takes **3–4 months to ramp**, and averages **~1.5-year tenure** before you re-hire and re-train. The engine is **live in week one**, and the *system* (ICP, scored universe, tested messaging, weekly learning) stays even if a person leaves. At the **Growth tier (~$3.5k/mo)** you're saving on the order of **~$90k/yr vs. one SDR** — and you get a documented, compounding experiment loop instead of one person's ramp curve and inbox.

**vs. buying lead lists**
A list is a static CSV that's stale on arrival and identical to the one your competitor bought. We **build and enrich a scored account universe** with a *reason* each account is in it, then **test angles against it weekly**. You're buying a sharpening targeting system, not a decaying spreadsheet.

**vs. generic "AI automation" / blast tools**
Volume tooling is now a **liability**. Since **Feb 2024, Google and Yahoo hard-cap spam complaints at 0.3%** and **flag bulk senders above ~5,000/day** — so spray-and-pray actively damages your domain and sender reputation. Our wedge is the opposite: **low-volume, high-relevance, human-QA'd** outreach (LinkedIn + Loom-first), where **conversation quality is the metric.** Generic automations optimize for *sends*; we optimize for *qualified conversations* and weekly learning — the thing that actually compounds into pipeline.

> **Net:** an SDR is a slow, expensive, single point of failure. A list is dead on arrival. Blast tools now hurt you. The Experiment Engine is a live, reputation-safe system that gets smarter every week — for less than a third of an SDR.
