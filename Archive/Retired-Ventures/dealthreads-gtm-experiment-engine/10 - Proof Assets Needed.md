# Proof Assets Needed

> The honest starting point: **no external client case study exists. Oloxa is client #0, in progress.** So the proof can't be "look at my results." It has to be **"look at my system, watch me run it live on my own company, and read the real artifacts it produces."** That's a different — and, for a first ICP of skeptical operators, often *stronger* — kind of proof than a screenshot of someone else's pipeline.
>
> This doc ranks the proof assets by **impact × speed-to-produce**, tells you how to make each one fast (mostly by *capturing what you already do*, not building new stuff), separates **what you can claim TODAY** from **what unlocks after the first win**, and ends with a blunt **do-NOT-fake list** and the **minimum bar to start outreach this week** so you don't over-prepare.

---

## The core reframe (read this before the list)

You're selling to **agency/consultant founders** — people who themselves sell intangible expertise without a finished portfolio for every prospect. They *know* the "I've productized my own process" pitch because they live it. That works in your favor.

So your proof isn't a results claim. It's three things stacked:

1. **The system is real and specific** (the offer doc already does this — a documented operating loop, not a promise).
2. **You eat your own dog food** ("I run this exact engine on my own company, live — here's this week's review").
3. **The artifacts are real and inspectable** (research cards, weekly reviews, conversation-quality scoring they can actually look at).

Every asset below feeds one of those three. Proof of *process + transparency + skin in the game* — not proof of someone else's revenue.

## Ranked by impact × speed-to-produce

Ranking logic: top items are **high-conversion AND fast because you already generate the raw material** running Oloxa. Lower items are higher-effort or only unlock later. Build top-down; stop when you have enough to start outreach (see the minimum bar at the bottom — it's only the first ~3).

### Tier 1 — Build this week (high impact, you already have the raw material)

#### 1. The live Oloxa research card (sanitized sample)
- **What it is:** One real research card from your Oloxa motion — company, contact, the signal that flagged them (CLOSING/HIRING/VOLUME/PAIN), the evidence, the pain hypothesis, the personalized opener. Sanitized: blur/rename the actual prospect, keep the *structure and quality* visible.
- **Why it converts:** It's the single most concrete "this is what you're buying" artifact. Agency founders instantly see the depth gap between this and the scraped CSV they've been sold before. It proves the targeting is *reasoned*, not bulk. It's tangible in a way no claim is.
- **How to produce fast:** You already make these for Oloxa. Pull one good one, redact identifying details, drop it into a clean one-pager. **~30 min.** Make a blank template version too (same layout, fields empty) so you can show "here's the artifact, here's what yours looks like."

#### 2. A real Oloxa weekly GTM review (sanitized)
- **What it is:** One actual weekly review from running Oloxa — what shipped, what replied, what the replies *meant* (conversation-quality tags), what you learned, next week's experiment plan. The core recurring artifact of the whole offer.
- **Why it converts:** This is the proof that the *loop* exists and compounds — the actual IP. It shows you measure conversation quality, not vanity opens, which is the differentiator buyers can't get from a blast tool or a list. It makes "weekly learning" believable because they're reading one.
- **How to produce fast:** You're (or should be) already producing this for Oloxa. Sanitize numbers/names you don't want public, keep the *thinking* visible. **~30–45 min.** If you haven't been writing these formally, write *this* week's properly — that single act creates the asset AND tightens your own motion.

#### 3. The founder-run live demo / Loom walkthrough ("I run this on myself")
- **What it is:** A 3–6 min Loom where you screen-share the actual Oloxa engine — the account universe, a research card, this week's review, a real LinkedIn+Loom thread (sanitized) — narrated in your own operator voice.
- **Why it converts:** Highest-trust asset you can make right now. It collapses "is this real?" instantly — they're watching the machine run on a real company. It also *demonstrates the Loom skill itself* (you're proving the differentiator by using it). Peer-to-peer founder tone = exactly your ICP's wavelength.
- **How to produce fast:** You already record Looms daily. One take, screen-share what exists, talk through it. Don't over-produce — rough and real beats polished and fake. **~30–60 min including one re-record.** This becomes your warm follow-up asset on every LinkedIn thread.

#### 4. The offer doc itself, as a shareable artifact
- **What it is:** `02 - Dealthreads GTM Experiment Engine Offer.md`, lightly reformatted as a clean shareable (PDF or web page) — the documented operating loop, deliverables, honest pilot terms, honest expectations.
- **Why it converts:** Most "AI outbound" pitches are vibes. A specific, honest, well-structured operating doc — including what you *won't* promise — signals operator credibility and reads as proof of competence by itself. The honesty (1–5% reply rates, no guaranteed meetings) is disarming and rare.
- **How to produce fast:** It already exists. Export to PDF / clean doc, make sure the deliverables and pilot sections are skimmable. **~20–30 min.**

### Tier 2 — Build within 1–2 weeks (strong, slightly more lift)

#### 5. A "messaging system" sample (real DM + Loom script + follow-up sequence)
- **What it is:** A sanitized example of the messaging system v1 deliverable — an actual connection-request → DM → Loom outline → 2–3-touch follow-up sequence you've used on Oloxa, annotated with the *why* behind each touch.
- **Why it converts:** Shows the messaging is engineered and segment-mapped, not improvised. The annotations prove there's thinking behind it (which is what they're paying for vs. a template pack). Lets a prospect picture week one concretely.
- **How to produce fast:** Lift your real Oloxa sequences, redact, add 1-line rationale per step. **~45 min.** Slightly more lift than Tier 1 only because annotation takes thought.

#### 6. A short "how the engine works" one-pager / loop diagram
- **What it is:** A single visual of the 6-step weekly loop (context → universe → experiments → human-approved execution → measure reply + conversation quality → improve), with the human-approval gates marked.
- **Why it converts:** Gives a fast mental model in a sales convo and reinforces the "human-approved, not spam" positioning visually. Good leave-behind after a call. Makes the abstract loop graspable in 10 seconds.
- **How to produce fast:** The loop is already written in the offer doc (section "The weekly learning loop"). Turn it into one clean graphic. **~30–45 min** in any design tool.

#### 7. Personal credibility assets (LinkedIn profile + a couple of build-in-public posts)
- **What it is:** Your own LinkedIn profile tuned to the repositioning, plus 2–3 posts narrating the Oloxa motion in public ("week 3 of running my own GTM engine — here's what the conversation-quality data showed").
- **Why it converts:** When you DM a founder, the *first* thing they do is open your profile. It has to confirm the pitch. Build-in-public posts double as proof artifacts ("I literally publish my weekly learnings") and as inbound warmers — they make the cold DM feel warm.
- **How to produce fast:** Profile edit is ~1 hr. Posts are repurposed weekly-review content — you're already generating the substance. **~2–3 hrs total**, but front-load the profile (do that this week) and let posts accrue.

### Tier 3 — Unlocks AFTER the first pilot or a clear Oloxa win (do not wait on these)

#### 8. The Oloxa result milestone (first real outcome, honestly stated)
- **What it is:** The first *honest* outcome from Oloxa — e.g. "ran the engine 8 weeks, X qualified conversations, here's the conversation-quality trend and what targeting we killed/doubled-down on." Not "made $X." A *learning + qualified-conversation* milestone.
- **Why it converts:** First piece of real evidence the loop produces outcomes on a live niche. Still honest (no revenue guarantees), but now it's "here's what it did," not just "here's how it works."
- **When/how:** Captured naturally from the weekly reviews you're already keeping. The moment you have a defensible milestone, write it up. **Don't manufacture a deadline for this — it lands when it lands.**

#### 9. First pilot client case study (process-first)
- **What it is:** Your first paying pilot, documented — their starting state, what the engine built, the experiments run, conversation-quality movement, and (carefully, honestly) any pipeline outcome they'll let you share.
- **Why it converts:** The first *external* proof point. Even framed as process ("here's the system we ran for them"), an outside name breaks the client-#0 ceiling.
- **When/how:** Built from that pilot's own weekly reviews — so it's near-zero extra work *if* you've been keeping them. Get permission to share at kickoff (bake it into the pilot agreement). This is *why* you keep clean reviews from day one.

#### 10. A short client testimonial / quote (process & experience, not inflated numbers)
- **What it is:** 2–3 sentences from your first pilot on *what it was like to work with you* — transparency, the weekly reviews, no spam, founder-run. Even a mid-pilot "this is the most organized outbound I've ever had pointed at my business" is gold.
- **Why it converts:** Social proof from a peer. Experience-based testimonials are credible even before big results exist, and they're honest.
- **When/how:** Ask ~30–45 days into the first pilot, anchored on experience not revenue. **5 min to collect once earned.**

## What you can credibly claim TODAY

State these plainly — every one is true right now:

- ✅ **"I run this exact engine live on my own company (Oloxa) — here's this week's review and a research card."** (Your single strongest line.)
- ✅ **"Here's the actual operating system, documented end to end"** — and what it will and won't do.
- ✅ **"Here's the real artifact you'd get every week"** (research card, weekly review, conversation-quality scoring) — shown, not described.
- ✅ **"Every message is human-approved before it sends — I'll show you the approval lane."**
- ✅ **Honest market mechanics:** cold reply rates run ~1–5%; ~hundreds of touches per meeting; Feb-2024 deliverability caps make volume self-harming — so we go low-volume + high-relevance. (Honesty *is* a credibility asset with this ICP.)
- ✅ **The economics vs. an SDR** (~$90k/yr saved vs. Growth, live week one vs. 3–4-month ramp) — that's math, not a results claim.
- ✅ **"You're client-early, and I price/risk-reverse the pilot accordingly"** — founder-run, transparent weekly reviews, soft activity floor credited if missed, month-to-month after. Turning the no-case-study fact into an honest advantage.

## What unlocks AFTER the first pilot / Oloxa win

Don't claim these until they're real:

- 🔓 "Here's what the engine produced on a live niche over N weeks" (Oloxa milestone, #8).
- 🔓 "Here's a client we did this for" (first pilot case study, #9).
- 🔓 "Here's what a client said about working with us" (testimonial, #10).
- 🔓 Any conversation-quality or qualified-conversation *benchmarks* across more than one company.
- 🔓 "Repeatable across verticals" — you can *hypothesize* it today, but you can only *claim* it once a second motion proves it.

## Do NOT fake / do NOT claim yet (blunt list)

The whole moat is honesty + transparency. One fabricated number and you're just another AI-outbound guru. Hard lines:

- ❌ **No fake case studies, fake logos, or "trusted by" walls.** You have client #0. Implying otherwise is the fastest way to get caught by an ICP that does this for a living.
- ❌ **No invented metrics** — no "we get 30% reply rates," no "booked 40 meetings," no made-up conversation-quality numbers. Cite the honest ranges (1–5%) instead.
- ❌ **No guaranteed meetings or revenue.** Already banned in the offer; it's also a *proof* lie. Sell the machine, the activity, the learning.
- ❌ **No borrowed/stock screenshots** presented as your dashboard or results.
- ❌ **No fake testimonials or "results may vary" composite quotes.** Wait for the real one.
- ❌ **No "as seen in" / authority-borrowing** you haven't earned.
- ❌ **Don't dress Oloxa up as a finished win.** It's client #0, in progress — say exactly that. "In progress, here's the live artifact" is *more* credible than a vague success claim.
- ❌ **Don't over-claim repeatability across verticals** before a second motion exists. Frame it as the thesis you're proving, not a proven fact.
- ⚠️ **Always sanitize real prospect/client data** before showing any card or review. Showing a real un-redacted prospect's info to *another* prospect signals you'll leak theirs too. Redaction is itself a trust signal — narrate that you do it.

## Minimum proof bar to start outreach THIS week

Do **not** wait for the full set. You can run the wedge motion on your first ICP with just these three (all Tier 1, all built from what you already have):

1. **One sanitized Oloxa research card** (~30 min) — the "this is what you're buying" artifact.
2. **One sanitized Oloxa weekly review** (~30–45 min) — proof the loop is real and measures conversation quality.
3. **One founder-run Loom walkthrough** (~30–60 min) — "here's the engine running on my own company," your warm follow-up asset.

Plus the two near-zero-effort enablers you already have:
- Your **LinkedIn profile** aligned to the repositioning (do the profile edit before you DM anyone — it's the first thing they check).
- The **offer doc** exported as a clean shareable to send when a conversation gets warm.

**That's the bar. ~2–3 focused hours.** With those, you can start sending personalized LinkedIn connection + DM + Loom touches to agency/consultant founders this week. Everything else in Tier 2/3 is sharpening — produce it *while* outreach is already live, not before.

> **The trap to avoid:** treating proof-asset prep as a reason to delay outreach. Your ICP is skeptical operators — and the most convincing thing you can show them is that the engine is *already running, this week, on your own company.* That asset regenerates every week whether you've started selling or not. So start selling. The proof compounds in public.
