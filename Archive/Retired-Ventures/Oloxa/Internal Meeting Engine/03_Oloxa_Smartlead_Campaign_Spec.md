# Oloxa Smartlead Campaign Spec

## Goal

Use Smartlead for controlled, segmented cold email campaigns that start conversations with commercial finance brokers and debt advisors.

## Sending rules

- Plain text first
- No links in Email 1
- No images/HTML-heavy design
- Low daily volume per inbox
- Validate emails before import
- Keep bounce rate under 3%
- Suppress unsubscribes immediately

## Initial segments

1. Commercial finance brokers
2. Debt advisors
3. Commercial mortgage brokers
4. Equipment finance brokers
5. SBA lending brokers/consultants
6. Factoring / AR finance brokers

## Core message angle

Your team probably does not need another generic AI pitch.

The specific bottleneck Oloxa looks at is borrower document chaos: docs arriving messy, staff renaming/filing/chasing, lender packages delayed, and borrowers needing repeated follow-up.

## Email 1 — casual opener

Subject: quick borrower doc thought

Hey {{first_name}},

Quick one — I was looking at {{company_name}} and had a specific thought around broker deal paperwork.

A lot of commercial finance teams lose time between “borrower sent docs” and “clean lender-ready package.” The annoying part is usually sorting, renaming, filing, and chasing what’s missing.

Oloxa is built around that workflow — helping brokers clean up borrower docs faster and reduce the follow-up loop.

Worth sending over the simple version of how I’d map this for {{company_name}}?

{{sender_name}}

## Email 2 — routine menu

Hey {{first_name}},

For {{company_name}}, I’d probably look at three things first:

1. Where borrower docs land
2. How docs get renamed/filed into a deal folder
3. How missing docs get chased before lender submission

The goal is not “AI transformation.” It’s fewer admin loops before a deal is lender-ready.

Worth a quick 15 to compare notes?

## Email 3 — close loop

{{first_name}}, should I close the loop here?

Usually this only matters if borrower docs/missing-doc follow-up are slowing down lender-ready submissions.

If that’s not a headache at {{company_name}}, no worries.

## Reply CTA

Positive/send-info replies should be pushed toward:

- quick 15-min workflow call
- optional Sendr proof page
- same-day Salesfinity call if phone exists
