# 01 — Positioning Decision

> Resolves the open forks from `00 - Current Page Teardown.md`. Ryan decided 2026-05-31.

## The decision
1. **Reposition dealthreads.io wholesale to the GTM agent.** The landing page talks about the go-to-market agent **only**. Zero real estate — no FUB/Dotloop/MLS/brokerage/producer language, no RE origin story, no RE vertical mention on the page.
2. **Offer is built problem-first** — around the pipeline problems founders and GTM teams actually have, not around features or hours-saved.

## What this settles (from the teardown's open questions)
- **Reposition-in-place vs. new page** → reposition in place. dealthreads.io becomes the GTM page.
- **Two conflicting positionings on record** (live managed-agent-team vs. old Founding-50 self-serve SaaS doc) → **canonical = GTM managed-agent-team.** The Founding-50 SaaS doc is retired for this brand direction.
- **Cost-saving vs. revenue framing** → **revenue-first.** Lead with pipeline / qualified conversations / sharper targeting. "Hours saved" is demoted to a secondary efficiency point, not the headline.

## What carries over from the old page (structure only, not content)
Keep these proven patterns, re-skinned for GTM (per teardown):
- "Deploy a team, not software"
- Agent cards: name → function → "what it replaces"
- Pilot-first, "first thing live in 72h"
- "A real human monitors every deployment" (the anti-AI-slop credibility line)
- Named-founder trust + "no fake proof" DNA
- "Take the audit first" secondary CTA → becomes the pipeline/Loom audit

## What gets cut entirely
Every real-estate noun, the 7 RE agents, the 5 RE workflows, the hours-saved headline frame, the RE founder origin story, eXp/Compass/KW competitive framing.
