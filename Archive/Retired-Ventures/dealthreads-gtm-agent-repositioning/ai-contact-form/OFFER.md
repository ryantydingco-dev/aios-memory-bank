## 1. The offer in one line
**"I turn your dead contact form into an AI that pre-builds a full buyer profile on every
inbound lead — in your CRM before your rep calls back. I'll install it free; you only pay
once enriched profiles are landing."**

## 2. Who it's for (ICP — the corrected, cold-start version)
- **Small, founder-led B2B**, ~5–40 employees
- Sells **high-ACV** ($8k+ deals or $3k+/mo) — so one saved/won deal dwarfs the fee
- Has a **real inbound "Request a Demo / Contact Sales" form** that gets traffic
- **No RevOps/SDR team** — the founder or a small team handles inbound themselves (this is the wedge: they personally feel the pain and personally decide)
- Verticals to start: freight/logistics software, vertical SaaS, B2B services with demo forms
- **Beachhead targets:** Revenova (~74), Tai Software (~60), Ketch — founder-led, feel the pain
- **NOT** (yet): CaptivateIQ/Sendoso-size orgs with in-house RevOps — those come after case studies

**Why a nobody wins here:** the founder IS the buyer, so there's no committee and no "who are you, where are your logos." You give them a finished teardown of their own site first — proof beats logos.

## 3. The free wedge (the thing that replaces case studies)
A **finished, accurate teardown** of their *current* contact form — built before they pay or
even reply. Shows: what their form captures (thin) vs. the full buyer profile my system
builds from one submission (rich), plus an honest "what it still can't see" list.
Delivered as a page + a 2-min Loom. **Free, no strings.** (Already built — the engine + teardown generator + live demo.)

## 4. The paid offer — done-for-you install
What the client gets (we wire it; they run it):
1. **Smart contact form installed/upgraded** on their site (their existing form, enhanced)
2. **Every submission auto-builds a full buyer profile:** company (what they do, size, funding,
   tech stack, ICP-fit) + **the person** (title, seniority, LinkedIn, buying role) + a rep
   call-prep brief
3. **Pushed straight into their CRM** (HubSpot first; others on request) — the dossier is
   waiting *before* the rep makes the callback
4. **Honest gaps shown, never faked** — unknown fields say "unknown" (this is a feature)

Outcome they buy: **no more ~20 min of research per lead; reps stop treating a $50k deal and
a tire-kicker the same; faster, sharper callbacks; hot leads flagged on arrival.**

## 5. Pricing (quoted on calls only — NEVER on the page; page is book-only)
- **$2,000 one-time setup** (cash collected now) — install + wiring + first profiles
- **+ $1,000/month** — enrichment runs on every lead + maintenance + the data
- Path to goal: ~**10 clients ≈ $10k MRR + setup cash** → the $20k/mo blended target
- Setup fee directly feeds the "cash collected" half of the goal; monthly is the MRR half

## 6. The guarantee (the close lever for a cold start)
**Pay-when-it-works:** *"I'll install it free. You don't pay a cent — not the setup, not the
monthly — until enriched buyer profiles are actually landing in your CRM on your real leads."*
- Removes all risk for a founder who's never heard of you
- You only eat install *time* if they walk — and the install is mostly automated, so that risk is small
- Converts "why should I trust you" into "what do I have to lose"

## 7. Value equation (why $3k feels cheap)
- **Dream outcome:** every inbound lead is pre-qualified and pre-researched — close more, faster
- **Perceived likelihood:** they already saw the free teardown work on their own site + pay-when-it-works
- **Time delay:** profiles land in minutes, install in days
- **Effort/sacrifice:** zero — done-for-you, runs on their existing form + CRM
- **ROI math to say out loud:** "One extra deal you'd have fumbled pays for a year of this. It runs on every lead, forever."

## 8. Honest claims (from the coverage validation — say these, not more)
- ✅ "Company-level profile on **every** inbound lead." (always true)
- ✅ "For any buyer with a professional footprint — essentially every director-and-up B2B
  buyer — we identify the exact person + seniority **~95%** of the time, LinkedIn ~80%." (measured)
- ❌ Do NOT claim person-match on *every* lead. True population coverage is measured on the
  client's real leads (and becomes their custom proof point).

## 9. Positioning / messaging spine
- **Enemy:** the dead contact form — "name + email + a one-line message, then 20 minutes of Googling."
- **Timing/why now:** Drift shut down; Qualified went enterprise-only — the mid-market
  "conversational front door" gap is open, and you're the affordable, done-for-you fix.
- **One-liner:** "Your contact form throws away the buyer profile every time someone fills it out. I make it build the whole thing automatically."
- **Wedge line (outreach):** "I made you something — a teardown of your contact form. No ask. Want the 2-min walkthrough?"

## 10. Scope guardrails (don't slip into building a SaaS)
- This is a **done-for-you install** (wire existing tools), NOT a multi-tenant product
- v1 = smart form (enrich on submit). Chat widget = v2. In-chat real-time = v2 (current ~30s enrich is fine for async "dossier before callback")
- Prove on ONE paying client before productizing anything

## 11. The upsell ladder (the real long game)
FormIQ is the **wedge automation**. Once installed + trusted:
- → lead routing / instant-alert on hot leads
- → outbound built from the same enrichment engine
- → other AI automations (the "what else can you automate for me?" conversation)
The contact form is how you land + bank trust; the automation upsells are the LTV.
