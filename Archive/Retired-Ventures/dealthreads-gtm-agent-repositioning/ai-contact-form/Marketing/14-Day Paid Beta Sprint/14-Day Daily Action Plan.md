# Dealthreads 14-Day Daily Action Plan

## Daily 2-hour block

1. 20 min — review tracker and pick top next action.
2. 40 min — verify 2–3 target forms and sharpen teardown angles.
3. 40 min — record/send 1–2 Looms or messages.
4. 20 min — update tracker with status, reply, next action.

## Day-by-day

### Day 1
- Review top 30 target CSV.
- Pick top 10.
- Verify form URLs manually.
- Record Looms for Projul and PortPro.

### Day 2
- Send first 5 personalized messages.
- Create teardown notes for Revenova, Tai, Alvys.
- Log all sent messages.

### Day 3
- Send next 5 messages.
- Record 2 more Looms.
- Start reply classification if any replies arrive.

### Day 4
- Follow up with Day 1 targets.
- Build 3 new target profiles in freight/logistics.
- Tighten opener based on early friction.

### Day 5
- Send 5 additional targets.
- Create Field Notes/social post from the teardown pattern.
- Identify which vertical feels least saturated.

### Day 6 heavy sprint option
- Record 5 Looms in one sitting.
- Send 10 highly personalized messages.
- Update tracker.

### Day 7
- Weekly review: replies, no-replies, best angles, bad targets.
- Kill weak segment(s).
- Double down on best vertical.

### Day 8
- Source 10 new targets from winning vertical.
- Send follow-up 2 to unanswered high-fit leads.

### Day 9
- Build teardown assets for top 5 new targets.
- Send 5 messages.

### Day 10
- Push interested replies toward teardown call.
- Use CTA: “Want me to mock this against your actual form?”

### Day 11
- Run agency partner mini-batch: 5 HubSpot/RevOps agencies.
- Pitch productized form-to-HubSpot buyer profile upsell.

### Day 12
- Follow up all warm leads.
- Prepare beta install checklist.

### Day 13 heavy sprint option
- Record any remaining Looms.
- Send final 10-target push.
- Ask warmest lead for beta call.

### Day 14
- Sprint review.
- Report: targets researched, messages sent, replies, calls, objections, beta status.
- Decide next 14-day segment focus.

## Human action rule

Ryan does not need to do everything. Ryan must do the actions AI cannot do well enough yet:

- judge target quality
- record Looms
- send messages manually
- take calls
- close beta
