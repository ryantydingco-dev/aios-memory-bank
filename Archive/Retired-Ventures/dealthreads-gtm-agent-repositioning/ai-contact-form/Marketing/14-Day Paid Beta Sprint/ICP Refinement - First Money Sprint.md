# Dealthreads ICP Refinement — 14-Day Paid Beta Sprint

## The beachhead to sell first

**Low-key vertical B2B software companies with a real demo/contact form, 10–75 employees, $8k–$75k ACV, and no obvious RevOps machine.**

The best early wedge is *not* generic SaaS. Generic SaaS is overrun with GTM tools, RevOps consultants, AI SDRs, enrichment vendors, and “pipeline acceleration” copy. We want boring operational categories where the buyer context is naturally messy and valuable.

## Best first segments

1. **Freight/logistics/TMS software** — drayage, freight broker TMS, shipping operations, supply-chain execution.
   - Why likely to buy: every demo lead has hidden context: broker/carrier type, shipment volume, lanes, current TMS, integrations, urgency.
   - Why less saturated: not every AI GTM vendor is pitching niche freight SaaS.
   - First targets: PortPro, Revenova, Tai Software, Vektor TMS, MVMNT, Alvys.

2. **Construction/field operations software** — construction management, field reporting, asset/tool management, heavy civil operations.
   - Why likely to buy: buyer context depends on contractor type, crew size, project type, current workflow, and urgency.
   - Why less saturated: more operational than “SaaS selling to SaaS.”
   - First targets: Projul, Assignar, Raken, ToolWatch, Premier Construction Software.

3. **Manufacturing/job-shop/maintenance software** — shop-floor ERP, CMMS, manufacturing cloud, maintenance operations.
   - Why likely to buy: demos need current ERP/spreadsheet workflow, asset count, downtime pain, facility type, migration timeline.
   - First targets: Fulcrum, MIE Solutions, Opsima, Limble/MaintainX as benchmark not first buyer.

4. **Compliance/security workflow software** — GRC, compliance automation, cybersecurity reporting.
   - Why likely to buy: high ACV and urgent deadlines, but can get saturated fast.
   - Use cautiously: good context pain, but some are too big/overtooled.

## Scoring criteria

Score 100 max:

- Demo/contact form exists: 20
- 10–75 employees / founder-led or low-key: 20
- Operational vertical with context-heavy buyer qualification: 20
- ACV likely $8k+: 15
- Buying signal: hiring, funding, ads/content, launch, growth: 15
- No obvious enterprise RevOps stack / low saturation: 10

## Anti-segments

- Huge SaaS companies with RevOps teams and procurement
- PLG/self-serve companies where no human calls back
- Low-ticket SMB tools where a won deal cannot pay for Dealthreads
- Generic martech/GTM-tool companies unless there is a very specific angle
- Referral-only businesses with no inbound form
- AI novelty buyers who like the demo but do not have lead-flow pain

## Hard-hitting offer language

Primary CTA:

> Want me to send a 2-minute teardown of what your form is not telling sales before callback?

Sharper framing:

> If your rep still has to Google the company, guess the buyer role, and figure out urgency after the form submit, your form did not qualify the lead — it created homework.

Beta framing:

> I’ll install a managed buyer-profile workflow that turns each demo/contact submit into a rep-ready profile before callback. We start with one form and prove whether it saves rep research time and improves callback quality.
