# Dealthreads first-money sprint

Created: 2026-06-01

## Goal

Book the first 3-5 teardown conversations and convert 1 beta/pilot. Do not overbuild. Do not wait for SEO. The money motion is direct, specific, and manually delivered.

## Offer to sell today

**Free Dead Form Teardown** → **Beta install** → **$2,500/mo managed AI contact form / buyer-profile builder**.

Beta close language:

> I’ll install this manually for 5 beta companies. You get the widget/profile workflow built around your actual form and CRM. If reps don’t receive useful buyer profiles, you don’t keep paying.

## ICP for first cash

Prioritize two groups:

1. **Founder-led B2B SaaS / service companies** with demo/contact forms and $10k+ deals.
2. **HubSpot/RevOps agencies** that can buy it as an internal edge or resell it as a productized client upsell.

The agency route may monetize faster because one buyer can bring multiple client forms.

2. Say: “Your page gets the lead, but the handoff still needs a buyer profile.”
3. Show the exact fields/research their rep/operator would still need.
4. Show what the Dealthreads buyer profile would add.
5. CTA: “Want me to mock this up for your actual form?”

### Block 3 — Send LinkedIn/email

Send 10 messages today. Goal is not polish. Goal is conversations.
