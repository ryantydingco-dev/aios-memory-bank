## PROBLEM SECTION

### You don't have a "send more emails" problem. You have a targeting and learning problem.

Pipeline comes from referrals, your own network, and whatever hours are left after the actual work. When you're busy delivering, outreach stops. Two months later you're staring at an empty pipeline, scrambling.

You know outbound should work. But every run is from scratch — pull a list, write some messages, send a burst, hear crickets, lose steam, stop. Nothing compounds.

So you consider an SDR. ~$10–12k a month fully loaded, three to four months to ramp, gone in about eighteen — taking everything they learned with them. That's a big bet to place before you even know your message works.

And "just send more" is a trap now. Since 2024, blasting a list torches your own domain. Volume isn't the cheap option anymore — it's the self-inflicted wound.

Meanwhile you can't see what's actually working. Not opens. Not sends. *Which segment, which angle, which opener started a real conversation with a fit buyer.* Without that, you can't get better. You can only get louder.

**That's the gap Dealthreads fills.**

## MECHANISM SECTION

### The engine is a weekly experiment loop. That's the whole product.

Most outreach is a one-time blast. This is a loop that gets smarter every week:

**1. Context** — we learn your offer, your ICP, your proof, your objections, and the signals that mark a good-fit account.
**2. Universe** — we build and enrich your account universe (your TAM) and score it, so every account has a reason it's there.
**3. Experiments** — we design 3–5 angle/segment/message bets a week, each with a hypothesis.
**4. Human-approved outreach** — you approve; we send LinkedIn + Loom + follow-up. Nothing un-approved ever ships.
**5. Measure** — reply rate *and conversation quality* — the part most outbound skips.
**6. Improve** — kill what flopped, double down on what worked, write next week's plan.

> Volume is a commodity, and now a liability. Compounding learning is the moat. You're buying a targeting system that sharpens weekly — not a pile of sent messages.

## WORKFLOW SECTION

### How the outreach actually runs: LinkedIn + Loom + follow-up.

Not a cold-email machine. A peer-to-peer motion built for reply quality, not send count.

**1. Connect (no blast).** A signal-scored account → a research card (company, contact, the signal, the evidence, a pain hypothesis, a personalized opener). You see it before anything goes out.

**2. A real Loom.** A specific 60–90s video referencing *their* business — recorded by you (or by Ryan during the pilot). This is the part that earns the reply and the call. If a prospect doesn't warrant a real Loom, they don't belong in the universe.

**3. Human-approved touches.** Connection request → DM → Loom → 2–3 value-add follow-ups, on cadence, every message green-lit by you first. Low volume on purpose — reputation-safe by design.

**4. Measured by conversation quality.** Every reply is tagged: real interest / soft no / objection / not-fit / ghost. We optimize for *qualified conversations with fit buyers* — not a wall of "haha nice" replies that go nowhere.

## FAQ

**Is this just AI spam?**
The opposite. Low-volume, human-approved, LinkedIn + Loom-first. Every message is green-lit by a human before it sends, and we measure conversation quality, not send count. Spray-and-pray is the exact thing this is built to replace.

**How is this different from a lead-gen agency?**
Most sell volume — a big list, blasted. We build a scored account universe with a reason per account and test angles against it weekly. You're buying a sharpening targeting system, not a decaying spreadsheet. And we optimize for qualified conversations, not raw reply counts.

**Why not just hire an SDR?**
You can — for ~$10–12k/mo, a 3–4 month ramp, and the risk they leave in ~18 months with everything they learned. This is live in week one at a fraction of the cost, and the system stays regardless of any one person.

**Do you guarantee meetings?**
No, and be skeptical of anyone who does. Cold outreach runs ~1–5% reply rates; guaranteed numbers come from volume that burns your domain. We guarantee the experiments, the approved outreach, the weekly review, and a loop that gets sharper — the things that actually earn meetings.

**Do I have to be on camera?**

**What do you need from me?**
Your offer + proof, a starting ICP (we sharpen it), LinkedIn access to send from, ~30 minutes to record Looms, and a fast approval lane (~30–60 min/week). One 60-minute kickoff and we're live that week.
