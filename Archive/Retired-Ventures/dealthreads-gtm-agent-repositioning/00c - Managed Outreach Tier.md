# 00c — Managed Outreach Tier (the "I run it, you close it" offer)

> Ryan's call (2026-05-31): he'll set up + run cold-email outreach for clients, but will NOT handle replies or own meeting delivery. Domain strategy = separate lookalike domains → forward replies to the client's inbox, via **Smartlead** — the exact setup he already runs for Oloxa. So this is **low-build: existing infra pointed at a new client**, not new machinery.

---

## What this tier is
**You run the outreach machine; the firm owns every conversation.**

You: find the stuck-hiring companies (detector) → load them into Smartlead → send signal-targeted cold email from separate warmed domains → positive replies auto-forward to the client's inbox.
They: reply, talk, close. You never touch a conversation.

## The reframe that makes "I don't handle replies" a SELLING POINT
A recruiting firm does **not** want a stranger emailing their prospective clients back in their name — those relationships are their whole business. So:
> "I fill your inbox with companies that want to talk. You stay in control of every conversation and do what you're great at — closing them."

"I won't handle replies" = "you keep control." That's a feature.

## The risk boundary (crisp — protects you)
**You are responsible for:** domains warmed + healthy, a defined volume of signal-targeted companies emailed per week, sequences running, deliverability maintained, and **positive replies surfaced to them** (a weekly "you got 5 interested companies — they're in your inbox").
**You are NOT responsible for:** booked meetings or closed clients. Those depend on *their* reply speed and *their* close skill — things you don't control. Never guarantee meetings. You guarantee the machine runs and interested replies land.

This is the honest version of risk reversal: you stand behind your work (sends, deliverability, qualified interest surfaced), not their outcomes.

## How you report without handling replies
Smartlead shows sent / opens / reply rate / positive-reply detection. Your weekly report = "we emailed 180 stuck-hiring companies in your verticals, 9 replied, 6 were interested — all 6 are in your inbox now." You see the metric; they have the conversation. Clean seam.

## Domain strategy (DECIDED: separate domains, forward to them)
- Buy + warm lookalike domains (`mcubed-talent.com`, `try-mcubed.com`, etc.). Their **primary domain is never at risk** — say this explicitly, it's a major objection-killer.
- Replies forward to their real inbox. They respond from wherever they want.
- ⚠️ Warming takes ~2–3 weeks → set the expectation that campaigns go live in week 3, not day 1. (Or keep a pre-warmed domain pool ready.)
- Tooling = Smartlead (already in use for Oloxa). No new stack.

## Keep it on-brand (the discipline that matters)
Dealthreads' wedge is "relevance, not spray-and-pray." So **only email the stuck-hiring companies the detector surfaces** — targeted volume, not a 50k blast. Signal-targeted cold email is still relevance; a generic blast would contradict the whole brand AND wreck deliverability. Hold the line.

## Pricing
- **Setup: $1,000–$2,000** (domains, warming, list build, sequence copy, Smartlead config).
- **Monthly: $1,500–$3,000** (running + monitoring + optimizing + weekly positive-reply report).
- Rationale (validated research): full DFY outbound clusters $3–8k/mo; cold-email-only ~$2k/mo; you're under full-service because you don't do appointment-setting/reply-handling. Margin is strong — your real cost is Smartlead seats + cheap domains + your monitoring time.

## Effort reality (honest)
More than the $500 dashboard, less than full DFY. Setup is front-loaded (domains/warming/copy). Ongoing is **campaign management** (deliverability, copy swaps, list refills from the detector) — NOT per-reply labor. Scales to ~10 clients solo before you'd want a VA on domain/list ops. Still leverage; not infinite like Tier 1.

- **Premium add-on (any tier):** monthly strategy call, +$500–1k/mo.
