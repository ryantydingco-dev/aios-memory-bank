# 00b — Low-Effort Delivery Models (sell the machine's output, not your hours)

> Ryan: full done-for-you BD is too much delivery work for a solo operator. Correct. The fix: **productize the engine's OUTPUT so the software delivers, not you.** Ranked by least-effort-per-client. The whole goal = revenue that doesn't scale with your hours.

## The principle
The detector produces value automatically. Done-for-you outreach makes YOU the bottleneck (hours per client). Every model below keeps the *machine* as the deliverable and you as the operator of the machine, not the labor.

---

## Model A — The Productized Newsletter / "Client Radar Subscription" ⭐ lowest effort
**What they get:** the weekly dashboard, auto-emailed every Monday, for their verticals. That's it. They do all outreach.
**Your delivery time:** ~near zero once automated. The detector runs on a cron → emails the dashboard. You spec it once per client (their verticals/geo), then it's hands-off. 50 clients = same effort as 5.
**Price:** $300–800/mo (think "premium data subscription," not "service"). Low price, but **infinitely scalable** — this is a product, not a service.
**Why it's great:** zero per-client labor, scales like SaaS, and it's *exactly* the free Mandate Radar just turned on every week. The free sample → paid subscription is the cleanest possible upgrade.
**The catch:** lower price point, so you need volume. But volume costs you nothing here.

## Model B — Setup fee + self-serve dashboard (productized, "teach to fish")
**What they get:** you set up their personalized radar once (the verticals, the company list, the filters), hand them a live dashboard/login, show them how to read it. They run it themselves forever.
**Your delivery time:** a few hours of setup, ONE time. Then ~zero.
**Price:** $2–4k setup + $200–500/mo to keep it running/updated. Or even one-time $3–5k "done and yours."
**Why it's great:** front-loaded effort, near-zero ongoing. Feels high-value (a custom tool) without recurring labor.
**The catch:** needs a real self-serve dashboard product (more build than the email version). The detector is the engine; you'd wrap a simple UI.

## Model C — "Done-with-you" coaching + the tool (sell expertise, not labor)
**What they get:** the weekly radar + a 30-min weekly/biweekly call where you help them *act on it* (who to prioritize, how to open, what's working). You coach; they execute.
**Your delivery time:** ~30–60 min/client/week of high-value talking — not grinding outreach.
**Price:** $1.5–3k/mo (coaching commands more than data alone).
**Why it's great:** your time is spent on leverage (strategy/judgment) not labor (sending DMs). Caps at maybe 10–15 clients but each is high-margin and you're using your brain, not your hands.
**The catch:** still per-client time, just better-spent time. Doesn't scale infinitely like A.

## Model D — Pay-per-qualified-company / per-meeting (performance, software-delivered)
**What they get:** you send vetted target companies (or, if you add light outreach, booked intro calls). They pay per unit.
**Your delivery time:** low if "per company" (detector does it); higher if "per meeting" (you run outreach).
**Price:** $50–200 per qualified company; $300–1,500 per booked meeting.
**Why it's great:** aligns price to value, easy yes (they pay for results). The "per qualified company" version is detector-delivered = low effort.
**The catch:** per-meeting drags you back toward doing outreach. Stick to **per-company** to stay low-effort.

## Model E — License the tool to a recruiting network / niche (one sale, many users)
**What they get:** a staffing network, franchise, or industry group licenses your radar for all their member firms.
**Your delivery time:** one setup, one relationship, many end-users.
**Price:** $2–10k/mo per network.
**Why it's great:** the leverage play — one client = dozens of firms. Highest revenue-per-hour of all.
**The catch:** longer sale, needs a tiny bit of proof first. This is the *phase 3* prize, not the cold-start move.

---

## The recommendation: a 2-tier productized ladder (A as the engine, C as the premium)

For a solo operator who wants revenue without a labor trap:

1. **Core product = Model A (the weekly Client Radar subscription).** $500/mo, auto-delivered, scales infinitely, zero per-client labor. This is the business. The free Mandate Radar → "$500/mo to get it every Monday" is the easiest yes you'll ever offer.
2. **Premium tier = Model C (radar + a monthly strategy call).** $1.5k/mo for firms that want help acting on it. A few of these = nice margin, capped time.
3. **Never sell Model = full DFY outreach** unless one specific whale begs and pays $8k+/mo — and even then, that's the one you eventually hire a VA to run, not you.

**Why this fixes your confusion AND your time problem:**
- **What you deliver:** a dashboard, every Monday, automatically. (Software delivers it. Not you.)
- **What you sell:** "we find your next clients, every week, for less than a data tool costs."
- **Your actual job:** run + improve the detector, sell subscriptions, occasionally coach. Not grind outreach.

## The honest build gap
- **Model A needs:** the detector wired to a cron + an email/dashboard output per client. You have the detector; the "auto-email the dashboard weekly" wiring is a real but small build (you literally built the identical thing for Oloxa — `com.aios.oloxa-daily` → Telegram brief. Same pattern: cron → generate → deliver).
- **Model B needs:** a self-serve UI (bigger). Skip until demand proven.
- **Start:** sell Model A *manually* first (you email the dashboard each Monday by hand to your first 1–3 clients), prove they value it, THEN automate the delivery. Don't build the automation before someone's paying.

What you don't have is a *list of the right companies at the right time.* So every Monday I'll send you a dashboard of companies in your verticals that are stuck hiring right now — who to call, why, what to say. Easy to say. Easy to deliver (software does it). Easy to say yes to.
