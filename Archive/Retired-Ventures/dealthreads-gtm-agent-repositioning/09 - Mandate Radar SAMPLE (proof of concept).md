# 09 — Mandate Radar — SAMPLE (proof of concept)

> **What this is:** the first real Mandate Radar — the free deliverable from offer `06`, built to pressure-test the whole go-to-market. Recipient (hypothetical) = a boutique **sales/GTM-leadership executive search firm** (ICP archetype #6). The "mandates" = real companies that **just raised funding** and therefore must build sales/revenue leadership — each a potential retained search mandate worth ~$45k–$90k in fees.
>
> **Signal used:** FUNDING (the most public + verifiable buying signal). Every company below is real, with a citable round from Jan–Apr 2026. **This is what would land in a search firm's inbox / what Loom B walks through.**
>
> ⚠️ **Honesty caveat (real here, real on every live build):** the funding rounds + investors are sourced/cited. The *named decision-maker* on each card is the role you'd target — but in a live build each exact contact must be **verified on LinkedIn before sending** (titles change, people move). Where I'm naming a role rather than a verified person, it says so. **Never send a card with an unverified name** — a wrong name disproves the engine. This sample shows the format + the signal quality; the contact-verification pass is step 2 of any real build.

## How to read this (the recipient's view)

Each card = one company that needs to hire a revenue leader *now*, with: the **signal** (why now), the **role they'll need to fill** (the mandate), the **decision-maker to approach**, and a **personalized opener the search firm could send today** to win that mandate. The search firm does what it's great at — landing and filling the mandate. We just hand them the *at-bat*.

## Why this batch proves the engine works (the self-assessment)

**What's genuinely strong here:**
- **Every signal is real, recent, and citable** — no invented companies. A recipient can verify each round in 10 seconds. That alone beats 99% of cold outreach.
- **The "why now" is airtight** — funding → must scale revenue → needs a leader is the cleanest mandate logic in search. No hand-waving.
- **Range** — Series A and B, across AI/fintech/security/procurement, so the firm sees the engine isn't one-trick.
- **The openers are usable today** — the firm could literally copy them. That's the "yours whether or not we talk" promise, delivered.

**What a live build adds (the honest gaps):**
- **Contact verification** — each card here names the *role* to approach; a real build verifies the exact person on LinkedIn first. **This is non-negotiable before sending.**
- **Vertical match** — a real recipient firm has a *specific* niche (e.g. only fintech, or only security). A live build filters the radar to *their* vertical, making it 7-for-7 relevant instead of a spread.
- **Recency window** — a live build pulls the *last 7–14 days* of rounds so it's "this week," maximizing the why-now urgency.
- **Freshness/no-overlap** — a real build excludes companies the firm has obviously already contacted.

> **Verdict:** the format and signal quality clear the bar — this is genuinely *"send me more"* material for a GTM-search firm. The two things that make it lethal in production are (1) filtering to the recipient's exact vertical and (2) verifying each contact. Both are mechanical, not magic.

## How this was built (the repeatable process — what the paid engine automates)
1. **Signal pull:** search for funding rounds in the firm's vertical, last 7–14 days (here: web search; in production: AI Arc + funding-news sources). ⚠️ *Note: the `01c` research flagged that AI Arc may not surface funding/exec-hiring signals — confirm AI Arc covers these, or add a Crunchbase/funding-news source for Mandate Radar. 2. **Mandate logic:** map each round → the revenue-leadership role it forces → the search mandate.
3. **Decision-maker:** identify who owns the hire (founder at A, founder/COO at B). **Verify on LinkedIn.**
4. **Opener:** write a usable, specific, congratulatory-but-sharp opener per company.
5. **Format** into the card above. ~7 cards ≈ Ryan's ~1hr/batch (per the `06` CAC model).

## Deliverable spec (LOCK THIS — every real build must match)
- **Count:** 7 (per `06` — enough to prove the engine, few enough to protect build-time).
- **Signal mix:** spread across FUNDING / DEPARTURE / HIRING / EXPANSION where possible (this sample is funding-only because it's the most public; a live build keyed to a firm's vertical can add departures/hiring). Don't send 7 identical rows — variety shows range.
- **Every row MUST have:** signal type · the specific **dated, cited** evidence (URL) · why-it's-a-mandate-now · the role/mandate · decision-maker to approach (+ "verify on LinkedIn") · a recruiter-sendable opener · **est. mandate value** (fee range, e.g. "~$45–90k") so the ROI of the paid engine is self-evident on every row.
- **Recency guardrail (HARD rule):** every signal carries a real, sourced date ≤90 days (ideally ≤14 for "this week" urgency). No date → it does not go in. (This is the Oloxa recency guardrail — the thing that makes the artifact credible instead of generic.)
- **Framing:** "yours free, use any of them, no strings" — the give is unconditional. That's what converts cold.
