# Content-to-Revenue Recording Pack

## Record This First

**Working title:** I Made the Merch Before the Buyer Replied. Then They Asked About 120 Pieces.

**Audience:** law-firm operations, retreat planners, executive assistants, and other B2B buyers who need branded merchandise for a real date.

**Primary CTA:** Send Creative Alternatives a logo, retreat date, and approximate headcount for a free three-item visual concept.

Use "inquiry" rather than "sale." Do not show the customer name, logo, reply, or exact order details without written permission.

## 30-Second Hook

Most promotional-product pitches start with a catalog and a request for a meeting. We tried the opposite. We made three visual concepts before the buyer had selected a product. A law-firm prospect replied with a real retreat need and asked about two 60-unit items. That is 120 pieces from one inquiry. Here is the workflow, where AI helped, where it failed, and why a human still checks every logo, product, and price.

## 6-8 Minute Structure

1. Show three anonymized concepts immediately.
2. Explain the old catalog-first problem.
3. Show the artifact-first hypothesis: make the first useful deliverable before asking for time.
4. Walk through logo intake, three concepts, human logo QC, real-SKU mapping, and lookbook.
5. Show one rejected AI logo/product example and explain why AI concepts never go to press.
6. Explain the inquiry accurately: two 60-unit products requested, price not yet approved, no sale claimed.
7. Explain what the experiment proves and does not prove.
8. Close with the free three-item concept CTA.

## Claims To Avoid

- "AI caused the reply."
- "The order was worth" anything before a quote/order exists.
- "The prospect bought 120 pieces."
- "Three July reorders" until invoice IDs and amounts are logged.
- "$3.2M business" as a current verified financial claim.
- "75,000 orders" alongside 25,662 clean ledger rows without reconciling what each metric counts.
