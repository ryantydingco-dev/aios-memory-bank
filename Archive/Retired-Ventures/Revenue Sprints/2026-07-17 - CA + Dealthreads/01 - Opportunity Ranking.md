# Opportunity Ranking

## Scoring Model

Each opportunity receives up to 100 points:

| Factor | Weight | Question |
|---|---:|---|
| 30-day revenue proximity | 30 | Is a qualified buyer or prior customer already present? |
| Evidence strength | 20 | Is the demand supported by replies, purchase history, meetings, or invoices? |
| Ease of launch | 15 | Can the motion begin after a bounded approval step? |
| Speed to first action | 15 | Can a human act within 24-48 hours? |
| Strategic value | 10 | Does the work create a reusable system or proof asset? |
| Operator control | 10 | Can Ryan and the team materially influence the outcome? |
