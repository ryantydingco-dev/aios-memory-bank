## Decision Rule

- Continue a motion when it creates a paid result or a verified stage advance with a dated buyer commitment.
- Repair a motion when demand exists but fulfillment, pricing, or follow-up misses the service level.
- Stop a motion when contact quality is poor, positive replies remain unfulfilled, suppressions fail, or the work cannot be attributed to money.
- Do not scale cold volume until the current warm queue is fully dispositioned.
