## Fulfillment Workflow

1. Classify the reply and suppress non-human or negative events.
2. Verify recipient, domain, occasion, relationship owner, logo source, and sender.
3. Create three visual concepts using an exact logo reference.
4. Perform full-size, letter-by-letter logo and product QC.
5. Map each shown item to a real SKU, MOQ, inventory source, decoration method, and delivery window.
6. Build fully loaded pricing. Kenny approves price, margin, timing, and exceptions.
7. Ryan/Maclaine approve recipient, wording, attachments, claims, and sender.
8. A human sends in-thread.
9. Log the send, create the next-date task, pause automation, and record source attribution.
10. Customer selection triggers formal production art and proof approval. AI concepts never go directly to press.
