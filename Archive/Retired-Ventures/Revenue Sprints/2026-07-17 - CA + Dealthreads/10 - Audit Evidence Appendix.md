# Audit Evidence Appendix

Five independent read-only workstreams reviewed the AIOS Memory Bank and Creative Alternatives workspace on 2026-07-17. This appendix preserves the evidence that shaped the sprint.

## 1. Dormant-Customer Reactivation

- The existing reactivation engine produced 2,131 contacts: 981 labeled reactivation, 338 active, 502 phone-only, and 310 without contact data.
- Only 10 of the 981 reactivation records had known spend in the limited QuickBooks export used by that workflow. The list is not safe for a bulk send.
- The August 2025 cohort contained 57 customers and $140,723.53 of August revenue, but 38 already showed 2026 purchases totaling $543,721.25 in the latest local customer-year data.
- Nineteen accounts showed no 2026 purchase; Burrito Bar was excluded for an open balance. The corrected review cohort is 18 accounts representing $22,755.21 of prior August revenue.
- The broader reorder queue contains 117 accounts representing $302,077.08 of 2025 revenue. The top 25 represented $198,404.16 when built.
- Every reorder candidate requires a fresh QBO, A/R, open-quote, contact, and relationship-owner check before contact.

## 4. Content-To-Revenue

- The strongest recordable proof asset is the privacy-safe mockup-machine case study.
- One law-firm inquiry led to three concepts, a lookbook, and a question about two 60-unit items. This is an inquiry about 120 pieces, not a sale.
- The CTA is one concrete intake: logo, retreat date, and approximate headcount for a free three-item concept.
- Content is excluded from the revenue scenario until source-tagged leads, meetings, booked value, invoices, and cash establish an attribution baseline.

## 5. Adversarial Review

- The 2026-07-13 QBO snapshot showed $636,307 total A/R, $458,940 overdue, and $237,869.33 aged 31+ days. Collections may be the fastest cash action, but they are not new revenue and require a same-day refresh.
- Pause CA Construction and new cold verticals during the sprint; current construction data has zero interested leads.
- Do not add Dealthreads volume until positive replies are delivered, followed up, and dispositioned.
- Do not use `25-year-old, $3.2M` as current proof. Use `27-year, multi-million-dollar family business` until public financial language is approved.
- Do not treat rough HubSpot values, out-of-office replies, 2027 nurture records, or unlinked contacts as forecast revenue.
