# Creative Alternatives + Dealthreads: 7-Day Revenue Sprint

**Sprint window:** 2026-07-18 through 2026-07-24  
**Owner:** Ryan  
**Operating rule:** Nothing is sent, published, quoted, or changed in a customer system without the named human approval.

## Objective

Convert demand that already exists before creating more demand. This sprint prioritizes:

1. High-intent Creative Alternatives prospects waiting on a quote, mockup, meeting, or follow-up.
2. Verified reorder-due customers, after a fresh 2026 order and A/R suppression check.
3. Dealthreads prospects who have already replied, received work, or held a meeting.

Content is a supporting trust asset. It is not counted as forecast revenue.

## Cash Lane, Not Revenue

The 2026-07-13 live QBO snapshot showed $237,869.33 aged 31+ days. That could produce more near-term cash than every acquisition action, but collections are not new revenue and the balance may contain payments, credits, disputes, or bad debt. Run the existing invoice-chase workflow only after a same-day QBO refresh and Kenny/Maclaine approval. Do not include collections in sprint revenue totals.

## Hard Stops

- Do not contact all 57 August customers. Thirty-eight already show 2026 purchases in the latest local QuickBooks export.
- Do not send the raw 981-record reactivation list. Many records are unverified aliases or conflict with newer ledger activity.
- Do not launch another CA cold vertical this week. Construction has produced zero interested leads in current SmartLead data.
- Do not increase Dealthreads lead volume until the current positive replies are dispositioned.
- Do not use customer names, logos, replies, order values, or screenshots in public content without written permission.
- Do not use "25-year-old, $3.2M" as current proof. Use "27-year, multi-million-dollar family business" until public financial language is approved.
- Do not describe the 120-piece law-firm inquiry as a sale.
