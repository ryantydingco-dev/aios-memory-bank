## Decisions / changes

- Treat Runvue as a client-retention product for AI service agencies, not a generic reporting dashboard.
- The most compelling Runvue demo path is **approval-card → SmartLead write action → visible delivery result**, because it proves the portal can trigger work, not just display metrics.
- Keep Runvue’s MVP narrowly tied to agency delivery proof: campaign metrics, approvals, client-facing recommendations, and white-label reporting.
- Be cautious with “call your dashboard” voice-agent positioning: it may be screenshot-able, but AI agencies may see it as a familiar webhook/voice-agent pattern unless it clearly drives a portal-state update.
