# Exact 60-Second Video Template

Use this to create videos 31-100. Do not begin with an introduction.

## Word-for-Word Structure

### 1. Hook: 0-2 seconds

“[Specific business situation] is causing [specific consequence], and the normal AI advice would make it worse.”

### 2. Artifact: 2-6 seconds

“What I am showing you is [name the map, tracker, dashboard, sample, report, or workflow].”

Put the artifact on screen while saying this sentence.

### 3. Situation: 6-15 seconds

“Here is the actual business context: [one sentence containing the relevant constraint].”

### 4. Three Beats: 15-42 seconds

“First, [decision one]. Second, [decision two]. Third, [decision three].”

### 5. Honest Verdict: 42-52 seconds

“This does not prove [unsupported conclusion]. It does show [specific useful conclusion].”

### 6. CTA: 52-60 seconds

Choose exactly one:

- “Follow the build from day one.”
- “Join Revenue System Field Notes for the complete workflow.”
- “Read the full report before quoting the numbers.”
- “Build your first free Mandate Radar.”
- “Request a free campaign mockup.”

## Definition of Done

- 75-130 spoken words.
- One idea.
- One artifact.
- One evidence boundary.
- One CTA.
- No private data.
- No generic “AI is changing everything” language.
