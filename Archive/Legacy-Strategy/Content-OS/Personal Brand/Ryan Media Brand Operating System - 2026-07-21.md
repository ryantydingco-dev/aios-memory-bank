# Ryan Media Brand Operating System

## North Star

Ryan is the operator showing what AI implementation actually looks like inside a real traditional business.

## Positioning

I work a 9-to-5 in tech. After work, I am using AI to turn a $3.2M family-run promotional-products business from QuickBooks-only operations into a modern revenue and operations machine. I document the real builds, artifacts, numbers, mistakes, and lessons as I transition into a full-time AI operator.

## Brand Architecture

- **Ryan Tydingco:** the discovery and trust layer.
- **Creative Alternatives:** the operating proof lab.
- **Dealthreads:** the commercial revenue-system product.
- **Revenue System Field Notes:** the owned media and newsletter layer.

The loop is: business problem -> AI system -> visible artifact -> media -> audience feedback -> qualified demand -> better system.

## Audience

- Traditional-business owners who know AI matters but do not know where to start.
- Operators inside owner-led and family-run companies.
- Tech, sales, and account-management professionals building an AI operator career after work.
- Practical AI builders who want workflows and evidence, not model hype.

## Editorial Pillars

1. **The $3.2M Business AI Transformation:** real modernization work inside Creative Alternatives.
2. **AI Revenue Systems:** research, targeting, offers, outbound, follow-up, and measurement.
3. **AI Operations Systems:** quoting, handoffs, customer intelligence, repeat work, and workflow design.
4. **9-to-5 to AI Operator:** the transition, constraints, decisions, and skill development.
5. **AI Tools Inside Real Workflows:** tools judged by the job they perform in the system.

## Recurring Shows

- **Built After Work:** a daily 30-60 second artifact-first short.
- **The $3.2M Rebuild:** a weekly 8-12 minute YouTube operating episode.
- **Revenue System Field Notes:** a weekly written operator brief.
- **The Honest Scorecard:** a monthly report with claims, limitations, wins, losses, and next tests.

## Publishing Cadence

- Daily: one short on LinkedIn, YouTube Shorts, and one secondary short-form channel after review.
- Weekly: one long-form YouTube episode, one newsletter, and one site artifact.
- Monthly: one flagship research report or evidence update.

Record in two weekly batches. The queue should always hold at least seven approved scripts. Nothing is published or messaged without Ryan's explicit approval.

## Episode Formula

1. State the business problem in the first sentence.
2. Show the artifact within three seconds.
3. Explain the business reason for the build.
4. Share only evidence that can be inspected.
5. State what remains unproven.
6. Give one practical takeaway.
7. Use one CTA that matches the topic.

## CTA Ladder

- Awareness: follow Ryan or join Revenue System Field Notes.
- Intent: use the outbound break-even calculator or read a field report.
- Problem-aware: request a free Mandate Radar.
- Commercial: book a mapping call after seeing a relevant artifact.

## Voice

Practical, direct, honest, energetic, and specific. Use real business language. Explain the workflow, show the artifact, and admit what is still unproven. Avoid generic AI news, unsupported revenue claims, fake certainty, and tool reviews without a working use case.

## Measurement

Track the full path by first-touch source and journey ID:

1. Media hub or Field Notes view.
2. Subscriber captured.
3. Report or tool engagement.
4. Mandate Radar request.
5. Radar delivered and viewed.
6. Meeting booked.
7. Revenue won.

Weekly media scorecard: videos recorded, posts published, newsletter subscribers, qualified comments or DMs, Radar requests, meetings, and attributed revenue. Reach is diagnostic; owned audience, qualified conversations, and revenue are the business outcomes.
