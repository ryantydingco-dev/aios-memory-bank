## Ryan Twist Rules

- Do not make generic "AI tools" content.
- Every video needs a real business constraint: QuickBooks, old customers, no CRM, manual quotes, weak follow-up, no GTM, messy ops, repeat orders, supplier complexity.
- Every video needs a visible artifact: workflow map, prompt, spreadsheet, dashboard, SOP, scorecard, buyer list, before/after, or screen recording.
- Keep the viral structure, but change the arena from "AI tips" to "AI operator rebuilding a messy real business."
- No fake revenue claims. The hook can say the business is real and revenue-generating, but the result must be framed as experiment/documentation unless proven.
