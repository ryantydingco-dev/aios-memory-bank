## Top 10 To Test First

### 1. This $3M Business Runs on QuickBooks and Memory

Hook: This business does $3M a year, and the main operating system is basically QuickBooks plus people's memory.

Artifact: current-state workflow map.

Why it works: strong contrast between revenue and operational chaos.

### 2. I Am Using AI to Rebuild a $3M Promotional Products Business

Hook: Most AI content is tool demos. I am going to use AI inside a real business that already does $3M.

Artifact: transformation roadmap.

Why it works: clear origin story and long-term series premise.

### 3. I Would Not Start With a Chatbot

Hook: If I were installing AI into a $3M business, the first thing I would build is not a chatbot.

Artifact: first-five-systems scorecard.

Why it works: contrarian and practical.

### 4. The First AI System I Would Install Is Follow-Up

Hook: The easiest revenue leak in a traditional business is usually follow-up, not marketing.

Artifact: quote follow-up tracker.

Why it works: clear revenue tie.

### 5. Turning Old Customers Into New Revenue With AI

Hook: The fastest new revenue might be hiding in customers who already bought once.

Artifact: customer reactivation framework.

Why it works: old customers are a simple, believable growth lever.

### 6. How I Would Find 500 New Corporate Buyers With AI

Hook: Promotional products are bought by companies, schools, HR teams, sales teams, and event teams. AI can find those buyers faster.

Artifact: buyer research workflow.

Why it works: specific number plus clear market.

### 7. The Dashboard I Would Build Before Hiring Anyone

Hook: Before this business hires another person, I would build one dashboard that shows what is actually happening.

Artifact: ops dashboard prototype.

Why it works: useful for SMB owners and operators.

### 8. I Work a 9-to-5. After Work I Build AI Systems for a Real Business

Hook: I am not quitting my job to make AI content. I am using AI to become useful inside a real business first.

Artifact: weekly operator schedule.

Why it works: personal journey plus credibility.

### 9. Why Account Managers Might Be Perfect AI Operators

Hook: I think account managers are secretly built for the AI operator era.

Artifact: skill transfer map.

Why it works: speaks to Ryan's 9-to-5 audience.

### 10. My AI Stack for Rebuilding a Traditional Business

Hook: I do not need AI to replace people. I need it to find opportunities, draft follow-up, organize work, and make the team faster.

Artifact: tool stack map.

Why it works: lets Ryan mention Codex, Claude, Hermes, Meedro, Firecrawl, Apify, VidIQ, 1of10, and local models without becoming a generic tool reviewer.

## Daily Recording Menu Format

Each day, pick one:

1. **Proof video:** a system or artifact built today.
2. **Story video:** a lesson from the $3M business transformation.
3. **Tool video:** one tool used on one real workflow.

Default first pick:

> This $3M Business Runs on QuickBooks and Memory

Reason: it creates tension, explains the case study, and makes the audience want to see what gets fixed first.
