## Pattern Read

The strongest Meedro outliers are short, direct, and comment-heavy. The winning format is not "here are 10 AI tools." It is:

1. A sharp tension in the first 2 seconds.
2. One concrete business or AI use case.
3. A visible outcome or artifact.
4. A fast payoff in under 45 seconds.
5. A comment-triggering opinion or unresolved question.

Ryan's advantage:

Most AI creators talk about tools. Ryan can show AI being installed into a real $3M business.

## Recording Rules For This Batch

- Show an artifact in the first 3 seconds: workflow map, blurred QuickBooks export, quote tracker, lead list, SOP, dashboard, or whiteboard.
- Keep videos between 28 and 45 seconds unless the story needs more context.
- Say the same identity line often: "I work a 9-to-5 in tech, and after work I am rebuilding a real $3M promotional products business with AI."
- Do not claim revenue results yet. Say "I am testing," "I am building," "the hypothesis is," and "I will report back."
- Protect business data. Blur customer names, revenue detail, vendor info, and private employee context.
