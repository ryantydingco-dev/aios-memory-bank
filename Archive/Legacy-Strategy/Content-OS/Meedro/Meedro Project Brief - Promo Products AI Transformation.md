# Meedro Project Brief - Promo Products AI Transformation

Created: 2026-06-28

## Project Name

$3M Promo Products AI Transformation

## Creator Positioning

Ryan works a 9-to-5 tech account manager job and is documenting his transition into becoming an AI operator. His proof environment is a real $3M promotional products business run by his girlfriend and her dad. The business has revenue and customers, but no real go-to-market system, no modern operating system, and QuickBooks is the main source of truth.

## Core Audience

- Traditional SMB owners and family businesses with revenue but messy systems.
- Operators who want AI to create revenue and efficiency, not just content.
- 9-to-5 tech, sales, account management, and customer success people who want to use AI to become more valuable and eventually transition out.
- AI-curious business builders who want proof from a real business, not generic tool hype.

## Audience Pains

- We have revenue, but growth is too dependent on referrals and relationships.
- We have QuickBooks, but no real go-to-market or operating system.
- We do not follow up consistently enough.
- We have old customers and past buyers, but no reactivation engine.
- We know AI could help, but we do not know where to install it first.
- We have too many manual workflows and too much business knowledge trapped in people.
- We want systems without hiring a full ops or marketing team.

## Content Promise

Watch me rebuild a real $3M promotional products business with AI: lead generation, customer reactivation, sales follow-up, quoting, dashboards, SOPs, reporting, and practical automations.

## Voice Rules

- Practical, direct, and grounded in the business.
- Show real workflows and artifacts, not generic AI hype.
- Talk like an operator: revenue, leads, quotes, follow-up, customers, QuickBooks, orders, hours saved.
- Admit what is unproven.
- Protect private business information.
- No fake guru energy.
- No vague claims like "AI will change everything."

## Content Pillars

1. The $3M business AI transformation.
2. AI lead generation and customer reactivation.
3. QuickBooks-adjacent ops systems and reporting.
4. Promotional products sales workflows and campaign ideas.
5. 9-to-5 to AI operator journey.
6. Tool stack tests for real business workflows: Codex, Claude Code, Hermes, Meedro, VidIQ, 1of10, Firecrawl, Apify, local models.

### 2. This $3M Business Runs on QuickBooks and Memory

Hook: Revenue does not mean systems. This business has customers, orders, and cash flow, but too much of the operation is trapped in QuickBooks and people's heads.

CTA: Comment "audit" for the workflow audit checklist once it is ready.

### 3. The First AI System I Would Install in a $3M Promo Products Company

Hook: I would not start with a chatbot. I would start with follow-up, because buried quotes and old customers are where revenue leaks.
