# Exact Build Stream Template

This is a conversation guide, not a script. Keep it visible on a second desktop or print the short checklist.

## Before Recording

- Choose one mission that can materially advance in 45-90 minutes.
- Close email, QuickBooks, CRM records, `.env` files, payment pages, and private spreadsheets.
- Turn on Do Not Disturb.
- Use sample or redacted data.
- Open only the applications needed for the mission.
- Start Screen Studio with screen, webcam, and microphone enabled.

## Opening: First 60 Seconds

Cover these five points naturally:

1. **The mission:** "Today I am building..."
2. **The business reason:** "This matters because..."
3. **The starting point:** "Right now we have..."
4. **The constraint:** "The difficult part is..."
5. **The finish line:** "By the end, I want..."

Example:

> Today I am replacing my scripted content factory with a build-in-public system. The scripts are finished, but the friction of performing them is stopping the actual recording. Right now I have 30 prepared videos and a local teleprompter. The difficult part is keeping the strategy without making content feel like homework. By the end, I want one repeatable stream card and a workflow that turns real work into clips.

## During the Build

Do the work normally. Narrate only when one of these happens:

### A decision

- "The choice I am making is..."
- "I am rejecting the other option because..."
- "The tradeoff here is..."

### Uncertainty

- "I do not know the answer yet."
- "This is the assumption we need to test."
- "Here is what would change my mind."

### Friction or failure

- "This is where the system broke."
- "This looked like progress, but it was actually..."
- "We need to back up because..."

### Evidence

- "This number supports..."
- "This does not prove..."
- "The useful signal is..."

### A reusable lesson

Say:

> CLIP MARKER: [state the lesson in one sentence].

Then pause for one second and explain it cleanly.

## Midpoint Reset

Around the halfway point, say:

- What has changed?
- What remains blocked?
- Is the original finish line still correct?
- What are you cutting to finish on time?

## Closing Debrief: Final Two Minutes

Answer these aloud:

1. What did we actually build?
2. What changed from the original plan?
3. What worked?
4. What failed or remains unproven?
5. What artifact can the viewer inspect?
6. What happens in the next session?

Finish with one CTA:

- "Follow the build from day one."
- "Subscribe to watch the next system get built."
- "Join Revenue System Field Notes for the artifact and operating notes."
- "Comment with the workflow you would fix next."

## Emergency Privacy Rule

If private information appears:

1. Stop or pause recording immediately.
2. Close or redact the material.
3. Restart from a clean sentence.
4. Mark the timestamp for removal.
5. Never rely on remembering to blur it later.
