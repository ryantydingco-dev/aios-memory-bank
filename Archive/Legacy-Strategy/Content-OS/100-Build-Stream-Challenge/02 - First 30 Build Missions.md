## How to Choose Today’s Mission

Use this order:

1. Work the business already requires today.
2. Work that produces a visible artifact.
3. Work with a meaningful decision or uncertainty.
4. Work that can be shown without violating privacy.
5. Work that fits inside one focused block.

Do not choose a mission merely because its number comes next. The session number tracks completed reps; the mission should follow the real work.
