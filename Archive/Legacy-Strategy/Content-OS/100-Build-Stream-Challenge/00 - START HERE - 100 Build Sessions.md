# 100 Build Sessions

> [!success] The new content challenge
> Do the real work on camera. Do not perform a finished lesson. Each session ships one artifact and creates raw material for a long-form video, Shorts, LinkedIn clips, and Field Notes.

## The Goal

Complete 100 documented build sessions.

- The first 30 missions are ready now.
- Sessions 31-100 are chosen from audience response, business priorities, and unfinished work.
- A session counts when the work is real, the recording is saved, and the artifact is captured.
- Publishing is optional. Recording and learning are the daily reps.

## Definition of Done

- One clear mission stated in the first minute.
- One real artifact changed or created.
- At least one decision narrated aloud.
- At least one honest obstacle or uncertainty preserved.
- A closing debrief recorded.
- No private data, customer records, credentials, or unapproved claims exposed.
- Recording saved locally.

## The 100-Session Rule

Do not pre-write 100 performances. Pre-commit to 100 reps.

After sessions 10, 20, and 30:

- Keep the two missions producing the strongest qualified attention.
- Identify which moments create the best clips: tension, decisions, failures, reveals, or explanations.
- Turn winning moments into recurring shows.
- Remove sessions that create generic AI attention but no operator or buyer interest.
- Select the next ten missions from real work already on the calendar.

## What One Session Can Produce

- One 8-15 minute YouTube episode.
- Three to five Shorts.
- One LinkedIn post or clip.
- One artifact screenshot.
- One Revenue System Field Note.
- One follow-up mission based on unfinished work.

## Content Boundary

The channel is not "watch Ryan use random AI tools."

The channel is:

> Building AI revenue and operating systems inside real businesses after work, with the artifacts, decisions, failures, and evidence visible.
