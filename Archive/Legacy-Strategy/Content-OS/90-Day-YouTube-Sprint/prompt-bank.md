# Prompt Bank - 90-Day YouTube Sprint

## Claude — Episode Architect

```text
I am making a YouTube video in my series about rebuilding a $3M promotional products business with AI.

Topic: [business problem/system]

My wedge:
I work a 9-to-5 in tech. After work, I am installing AI into a real $3M promotional products business to improve lead generation, customer follow-up, operations, reporting, and efficiency.

Create:
1. A strong cold open.
2. A 7-part video outline.
3. The core tension of the episode.
4. What proof I should show on screen.
5. 5 title options.
6. 3 Shorts angles.
7. A business verdict.

Make it practical, specific, and not guru-ish. Every idea must connect to the real promo products business and include a visible, privacy-safe artifact.
```

## Codex — Tiny Proof Artifact

```text
Build a small screen-recordable proof artifact for this YouTube video.

Video topic: [topic]
Problem/workflow: [workflow]
Audience: [SMB owner/operator, family business, or 9-to-5 operator]

Requirements:
- Keep it tiny and demo-friendly.
- Prefer a single HTML file, CSV template, markdown workflow map, or simple local app.
- Include sample/demo data clearly labeled as demo data.
- Make it visually understandable in under 60 seconds on camera.
- Protect private business data. Use fake customer/vendor/order names.
- Do not overbuild. No auth. No database unless absolutely necessary.
- Add a short README explaining what the artifact demonstrates.
```

## Meedro / VidIQ / 1of10 Mining Prompt

```text
Find 10 viral video ideas for my creator wedge:
I am documenting my transition from 9-to-5 tech account manager to AI operator by rebuilding a real $3M promotional products business with AI.

Prioritize:
- clear business pain
- lead generation, customer reactivation, quote follow-up, operations, reporting, SOPs, or QuickBooks-adjacent workflows
- hooks that make traditional business owners and 9-to-5 operators curious
- a privacy-safe proof artifact I can show on screen
- ideas I can record today

Avoid:
- generic AI tool reviews
- fake guru claims
- pure content growth advice
- anything disconnected from the promo products business case study
```
