# Recording Card - 2026-06-28

## Title

This $3M Business Runs on QuickBooks and Memory

## Hook

This business does $3M a year, and the main operating system is basically QuickBooks plus people's memory. That sounds crazy, but it is exactly why AI has leverage here.

## One Idea

Revenue does not mean systems. A traditional business can have customers, orders, and cash flow while still leaking opportunity because follow-up, workflows, and reporting live in people's heads.

## Artifact To Show

Privacy-safe current-state map:

```text
Customers / past buyers
        -> QuickBooks
        -> quotes / orders
        -> manual follow-up
        -> owner memory
        -> missed opportunities
```

Then show future-state:

```text
Customers / past buyers
        -> customer segments
        -> quote follow-up tracker
        -> reactivation campaigns
        -> ops dashboard
        -> SOP capture
```

## Cold Open

"This is a real $3M promotional products business. It has customers, revenue, and years of relationship equity. But when I look at the systems, the business is basically running on QuickBooks and memory. So my first job is not to build a chatbot. My first job is to map where revenue and operations are leaking."

## Beat 1 - Situation

Explain the business:

- $3M promotional products business.
- Family-run.
- Real customers and revenue.
- No strong go-to-market system.
- QuickBooks is the main source of truth.

## Beat 2 - Problem

The issue is not that the business is broken. The issue is that the business has outgrown informal systems.

Say:

"When a business is small, memory works. When a business is doing millions, memory becomes a bottleneck."

## Beat 3 - AI Bet

AI should help with:

- customer reactivation
- quote follow-up
- buyer research
- ops dashboards
- SOP capture

Say:

"AI is not valuable here because it is flashy. It is valuable because the workflows are repetitive, messy, and already tied to revenue."

## Beat 4 - Artifact

Show the current-state map and future-state map.

Do not show live QuickBooks or real customer data.

## Beat 5 - Verdict

The first system to build should be follow-up/customer reactivation, not a chatbot.

Why:

- closer to revenue
- easier to prove
- uses existing customer data
- creates a visible before/after

## Beat 6 - Next Step

Tomorrow's build:

> The first AI system I would install is a follow-up tracker for quotes and past customers.
