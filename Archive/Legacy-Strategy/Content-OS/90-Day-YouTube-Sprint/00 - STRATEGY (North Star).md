# 00 - STRATEGY (North Star)

The one page everything else serves. If a video, post, tool test, or offer does not ladder to this, do not make it.

---

## The real game

**Build a creator brand around becoming an AI operator inside a real business.**

Ryan works a 9-to-5 tech account manager job and wants to transition out. The proof environment is a real $3M revenue promotional products business run by his girlfriend and her dad. The business already has revenue, customers, and product-market reality, but almost no modern go-to-market or operating systems. QuickBooks is the main system of record.

The channel exists to document Ryan installing AI into that business: lead generation, sales follow-up, customer reactivation, quoting, ops dashboards, SOPs, reporting, and practical AI workflows.

## Positioning (one line)

> I help traditional business operators see how AI can create real revenue and operating leverage by documenting my transition from 9-to-5 tech account manager to AI operator inside a $3M promotional products business.

Shorter public version:

> I am rebuilding a $3M promotional products business with AI, in public.

## Who watches

- 9-to-5 tech, sales, and account management people who want to use AI to become operators, consultants, or business builders.
- Traditional SMB owners and family businesses with revenue but weak systems: no CRM discipline, no go-to-market engine, no documented ops, too much owner memory.
- AI-curious operators who are tired of tool hype and want to watch real implementation inside a business that already makes money.

## Who pays later

- SMB owners who want AI systems installed without hiring a full internal team.
- Operators who want templates, prompts, workflows, and implementation guidance for AI revenue and ops systems.
- Potential partners, employers, and clients who want proof Ryan can turn AI into business outcomes.

## The transformation the channel sells

> From "this business runs on QuickBooks, memory, referrals, and manual follow-up" to "this business has an AI-assisted revenue and operations system that finds opportunities, follows up, tracks work, and makes the team faster."

The real expertise being built in public:

- AI-assisted lead generation and prospect research.
- Customer reactivation and sales follow-up.
- Promo products buyer research and campaign ideation.
- CRM, quoting, order tracking, and QuickBooks-adjacent workflows.
- Codex, Claude, Hermes, Firecrawl, Apify, VidIQ, Meedro, local models, and internal automations as practical operator tools.

## The funnel (how a video compounds)

```
VIDEO - one real business problem, one AI system, one visible before/after
   -> FOLLOW / SUBSCRIBE - "I am rebuilding this business in public"
   -> FREE ASSET - checklist, prompt, SOP, buyer list framework, dashboard template
   -> EMAIL / COMMUNITY - people following the full transformation
   -> FUTURE OFFER - AI operator playbooks, implementation service, cohort, consulting, or business case study leverage
```

Early phase CTA:

> Follow along. I am rebuilding a real $3M business with AI after work.

Do not over-monetize before trust. The first job is to become the person people associate with practical AI implementation in real businesses.

## Content pillars

1. **The $3M Business AI Transformation** - the main build-in-public case study: audits, before/after systems, lessons, numbers, what worked, what failed.
2. **AI Revenue Systems** - lead gen, outbound, customer reactivation, quote follow-up, referrals, promo campaign opportunities, buyer research.
3. **AI Operations Systems** - QuickBooks-adjacent workflows, CRM setup, order tracking, SOPs, dashboards, internal reporting, admin automation.
4. **9-to-5 to AI Operator** - after-work building, account manager skill transfer, learning in public, career transition, operator mindset.
5. **Tool Stack Tests for Real Businesses** - Codex, Claude Code, Hermes, Meedro, VidIQ, 1of10, Firecrawl, Apify, local models, and when each is actually useful.

## 90-day path to audience growth

- **Weeks 1-2:** Publish the origin story, business audit, and first system build. Establish the case study and make the audience understand the stakes.
- **Weeks 3-6:** Ship one revenue system and one ops system per week. Each video needs a visible artifact: list, dashboard, SOP, automation, script, prompt, or before/after.
- **Weeks 7-10:** Start reporting results: leads found, follow-ups sent, dormant customers reactivated, hours saved, workflow errors reduced, quotes moved forward.
- **Weeks 11-13:** Package the most useful workflows into free assets and soft-test the first offer: AI operator templates, implementation playbook, or SMB AI systems sprint.

## First recording thesis

The first video should not be "AI content engine." It should be:

> I am using AI to rebuild a $3M promotional products business that runs mostly on QuickBooks.

That is specific, credible, and differentiated. The content engine is the back office. The public story is the business transformation.

## Honest constraints

- Do not claim business results before they exist. Document the build, the hypothesis, the attempt, and the result.
- Protect private business data. Blur customer names, financial details, employee info, vendor data, and anything the family business would not want public.
- Do not turn this into generic AI tool reviews. Every tool review must answer: "What business workflow did this improve?"
- 100K followers comes from repeated proof and a clear story, not just posting volume.

## What this kills

- Generic "AI tools to grow followers" content.
- Random niche hopping.
- Fake guru positioning around systems that have not been tested.
- Meedro prompts about agencies/freelancers getting clients unless they connect back to the $3M promo products case study.
