# Future Offer Parking Lot

Status: Updated 2026-06-28.

The old paid cohort offer is no longer the active strategy. The current priority is trust and proof: document Ryan rebuilding a real $3M promotional products business with AI.

Do not force a paid community, cohort, or product before the case study has proof.

---

## Active Monetization Thesis

First build audience trust around one clear proof story:

> I am rebuilding a $3M promotional products business with AI, in public.

Later, monetization can emerge from the workflows that repeatedly get interest and results.

## Likely Future Offers

### 1. AI Operator Template Library

Low-friction digital product built from the actual artifacts:

- AI business audit checklist.
- Customer reactivation prompt pack.
- Quote follow-up tracker.
- Buyer research workflow.
- QuickBooks-adjacent dashboard template.
- SOP capture system.
- Weekly AI operator scorecard.

Best when: people start asking for the exact templates used in videos.

### 2. SMB AI Systems Sprint

Productized implementation service for traditional businesses with revenue but messy systems.

Potential promise:

> In 14 days, we map your biggest revenue and ops leaks and install the first practical AI workflows for follow-up, lead research, reporting, and SOPs.

Best when: SMB owners or operators DM asking for help applying the systems to their own business.

### 3. AI Operator Cohort

Small paid group for people who want to become AI operators inside real businesses.

Potential promise:

> Learn to find, map, and install practical AI systems inside traditional businesses using Ryan's live case study as the curriculum.

Best when: the audience includes enough 9-to-5 tech/sales people who want the transition path.

### 4. Consulting / Advisory

Higher-touch offer for businesses that want direct implementation help.

Best when: the case study has measurable proof: revenue recovered, leads generated, hours saved, follow-up improved, quote velocity improved.

## Current CTA Rule

For now:

> Follow along. I am rebuilding a real $3M promotional products business with AI after work.

When a strong artifact exists:

> Comment "system" and I will share the checklist/template/prompt from this build.

## Proof Needed Before Selling

Collect evidence from the promo products business:

- Number of old customers identified for reactivation.
- Number of follow-ups sent.
- Replies or opportunities created.
- Quotes recovered or moved forward.
- Hours saved on manual admin.
- Workflows documented.
- Dashboard or CRM adoption.
- Team feedback.

## What Not To Sell Yet

- A generic AI agency course.
- A "get clients with AI" cohort detached from the promo products case study.
- A broad AI tool-review community.
- Any revenue promise before the business has evidence.
