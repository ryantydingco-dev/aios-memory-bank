Where AgentMail gets powerful is after people reply.

The system would look like:

CRS / Tracerfy seller data
→ clean + segment the list
→ Smartlead sends a compliant seller drip
→ replies route into an AI seller reply desk
→ the AI classifies replies, drafts responses, flags hot sellers, and creates callback tasks
→ your team calls the hot ones fast
→ we track which leads/campaigns turn into appointments/offers/deals

So if someone replies “what would you offer?” or “I might be interested,” they don’t just sit in an inbox. They get flagged as a hot seller with a suggested reply and callback task.
