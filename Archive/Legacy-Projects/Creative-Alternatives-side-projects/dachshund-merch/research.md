# R&D — Who's winning in dachshund merch (2026-07-13)

## The anchor case: Bean Goods

The single best comp for what we're doing.

- Started 2011 in Portland as a side project — two people with apparel/graphic-design skills, both in corporate jobs, selling knit "Bean Beanies" inspired by their dachshund Bean. First-year revenue: ~$1,000.
- Scaled with paid social: after a Facebook Ads course, did $150K+ in 2017, then grew from $134K to $1M+ in annual sales.
- Today: self-described "world's #1 dachshund-themed goods brand," 70,000+ customers, human apparel + dog wear, 1% of sales to charity (over $50K donated).
- Sources: [Fashion Brain Academy case study](https://fashionbrainacademy.com/beangoods/), [Bean Goods about page](https://beangoods.com/pages/about-us), [Side Hustle School ep. 488](https://sidehustleschool.com/episode/488/), [ep. 1594 "The 7-Figure Dachshund Apparel Business"](https://sidehustleschool.com/episode/1594/)

**What Bean Goods proves:** a dachshund-ONLY brand supports 7 figures. **What they didn't have:** their own printer, distributor pricing, or a founding content engine. They scaled on paid ads; we can scale on organic + paid with better unit economics.

## The audience ceiling: Crusoe the Celebrity Dachshund

- 7M+ followers across platforms (3.1M Facebook, ~2.2M TikTok, 869K Instagram, 2M YouTube subs). Running since 2011.
- Monetizes via merch store, books, sponsorships, and a spun-off dog-ramp company ([doggoramps.com](https://doggoramps.com)) — note the IVDD/back-health angle became a whole second business.
- Sources: [celebritydachshund.com](https://celebritydachshund.com/), [business model breakdown](https://www.businessupturn.com/usa/inside-crusoe-the-dachshunds/86177/)

**What Crusoe proves:** the audience for dachshund content is enormous, and back-health (IVDD) is a commercial category of its own, not just a design theme.

## Other TikTok signal

- **Honeydew the Dachshund** — 1M followers, 21.7M likes. **Hazel & Heidi** (@hazel.bea.weenie) — senior-dog content, single videos hitting 1.5M likes. Hashtag ecosystem: #dachshund #wienerdog #minidachshund #doxiesoftiktok.
- Winning formats: daily routines, senior-dog moments, humor captions about dachshund behavior (stubbornness, burrowing, zoomies), dog-meets-world interactions.
- Pet category on TikTok Shop is established and converting — e.g. Herbal Dog Co driving ~$10K/day via TikTok Shop lives; affiliate-driven sales ~50% of GMV for some pet brands (Groomi).
- Sources: [TikTok pet brand guide](https://madebyextreme.com/insights/how-to-succeed-on-tiktok-as-a-pet-brand), [TikTok Shop pet campaigns](https://influencermarketinghub.com/tiktok-shop-campaigns-pet-brands/), [Shopify: making merch for TikTok](https://www.shopify.com/blog/make-merch-tiktok)

## Competitor tier map

| Brand | Position | Weakness we exploit |
|---|---|---|
| Bean Goods | Category king, established audience, full product line | Priced on outsourced production; no founder-dog content engine visible on TikTok |
| Crusoe | Audience king; merch is secondary to media | Merch is fan merch (his face), not identity merch for all doxie owners |
| Woof+Ween, Long Dog Apparel, Inkopious | Small POD players | Printful-tier margins, generic designs, Long Dog runs pre-order windows (slow) |
| Zazzle/Etsy long tail | Infinite cheap designs | No brand, no quality control, no community |

## The gap we occupy

**Nobody in this niche owns their production.** Every player either outsources printing (thin margins, slow) or is a media brand first. We can be: trend-reactive (idea → printed → on video in 48h), better blanks (Comfort Colors vs. cheap POD stock), better margin (survives paid ads at CPAs that kill competitors), with a native content engine (two dogs + a print shop, both filmable).
