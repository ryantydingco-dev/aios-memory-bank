# Design backlog

Pick ~10 for launch. Score each: would a stranger screenshot this and tag their doxie friend? If not, cut it.

## Tier 1 — launch candidates

**The physics of the dog**
- "Low Rider" (retro automotive-badge lockup, dog silhouette)
- "Long Boy Energy"
- Wraparound: dachshund starts on the chest, tail ends on the back
- "Built long for a reason. The reason is unclear."
- "50% dog, 50% draft excluder"

**The attitude (owners will feel seen)**
- "My dachshund heard you. He doesn't care."
- "Trained? No. Confident? Extremely."
- "Emotional Support Wiener"
- "Tiny dog. Huge audacity."
- "Certified Land Shark"

**The lifestyle**
- "Wiener Mom" / "Dachshund Dad" (hat-first designs)
- "Easily distracted by wiener dogs"
- "The Bad Backs Club" — owner + dog solidarity; IVDD-awareness tie-in (donate a cut to IVDD rescue — Bean Goods proved charity % is brand-standard in this niche, and Crusoe built a whole company on doxie back health)
- Blanket-loaf / burrito art (they live inside blankets — deeply relatable)

**The slightly unhinged tier (viral bait)**
- "Ask me about my wiener" (classic for a reason; keep one edgy SKU)
- Fake hot-dog-brand parody lockup with the dog as the logo
- Ballpark-hot-dog nutrition label, but the ingredients are dachshund traits

## Pricing sketch (validate against one-off print cost — see 30-day plan, Week 1)

- Tee $28–32 · Crewneck $48–54 · Hat $28 · Bandana $16–18 · Matching set $42–46 (bundle discount vs. separates)
- Target: ≥65% gross margin on every SKU at one-off volume. If a SKU can't hit it, it doesn't launch.

## Design production notes

- All art as vector/300dpi print-ready from day one; one design → many SKUs (tee, crew, hat, bandana, sticker).
- Keep a "reactive" slot open every week: trending sound/meme → dachshund version → printed and filmed within 48h. This is the moat nobody can copy.
