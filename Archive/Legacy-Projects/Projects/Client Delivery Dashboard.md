# Client Delivery Dashboard

## One-line

Potential SaaS/product idea for giving automation shops and clients visibility into delivery health, failures, approvals, and business outcomes.

## 2026-06-21 to 2026-06-23 context

- Ryan explored the Ideabrowser idea **“Client delivery dashboard for no-code automation shops.”**
- The original idea appeared oriented around platforms like Make, Zapier, and n8n.
- Ryan pushed back that this may be the wrong center of gravity because more operators are using Claude Code, Codex, and agentic workflows rather than traditional no-code-only stacks.
- Current strategic question: whether this should be built at all before more time is spent.

## Durable reframing

If this idea moves forward, the stronger wedge is likely **agentic automation delivery visibility**, not “Zapier dashboard.” Useful angles:

- Client trust layer: what ran, what changed, what failed, what was approved.
- Health monitoring: automations/agents that silently break, miss triggers, hit API limits, or produce stale outputs.
- Human approval and audit trail: what the AI suggested, what the operator approved, what was sent/executed.
- Delivery dashboard for agencies: proof of work, outcomes, savings, exceptions, next actions.
- Cross-tool visibility: Claude/Codex workflows, scripts, CRMs, inboxes, Airtable, Smartlead, Make/Zapier/n8n only where relevant.

## Open loops

- Decide whether the market pain is urgent enough to build now.
- If yes, define a 3–4 day MVP around one painful workflow, not a broad dashboard.
- Validate with automation agencies or no-code/AI implementation shops before investing in a full product.
- Avoid building around legacy platform assumptions unless prospects confirm that is still where the pain is.
