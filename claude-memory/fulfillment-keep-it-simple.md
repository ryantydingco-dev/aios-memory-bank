On the AlpVision PACK EXPO fulfillment (2026-08-10), Ryan rejected two polished versions before landing on the format he wanted: an image-only Gamma deck (heading + mockup per card, one closing pricing card) and a 3-line reply. His words: "I hate all of this. Make it way more simple."

**Why:** The mockups are the pitch. Tier frameworks, clever copy paragraphs, and timeline lectures bury the thing the prospect asked for and make it feel like a production instead of a favor delivered fast.

**How to apply:** For any [[dealthreads-outbound-engine]] or CA reply-fulfillment package: keep the deck's structure lean and the reply short (deliverable + one CTA + at most one timing line), and put strategy notes in the vault doc for Ryan, never in the prospect-facing asset. Maclaine Scher (maclaine@creativealternatives.com) is the quoting contact on every package.

**Correction (same day):** Ryan clarified the real objection was the CONCEPTS, not the deck format — the v4-style deck (item cards with short copy + tiers + timeline + team) is fine. Gag concepts (REAL/FAKE mismatched socks, spot-the-fake game cloth, certified-authentic duck) were "way too playful." Default to classic treatments: the client's logo, placed well, on proven items ("I'd rather just put their logo on some socks. Call it a day."). Save concept creativity for when Ryan explicitly asks.
