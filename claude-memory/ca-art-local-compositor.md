Learned on the Harvard Club of NYC Yoga tee job (2026-08-20), extending [[creative-alternatives-aios]] production art:

- **`scripts/composite_mockup.py` (CA workspace)** — local art-locked mockup lane: presses the exact 300dpi art PNG onto a blank product photo with macro-wrinkle luminance shading (Gaussian-blur the luminance map ~7px so knit noise doesn't make ink look distressed; clamp shade 0.72–1.10, ink ~248). Args: blank, art, out, cx, cy, w_px, [rot]. 100% art fidelity — preferred over model compositing when fidelity matters or Higgsfield is capped.
- **Higgsfield hit a grace-period daily generation limit (2026-08-20)** — batch submissions bounced with "daily generation limit"; a nano_banana composite that did get through redrew the O-counter art (solid disc instead of ring+figure), confirming model composites drift on fine logo geometry. Local compositor wins for art-exact mockups.
- **Recraft V4 API constraints**: landscape size `1152x896` works (1536x1024 / 1707x1024 / 1820x1024 rejected); V4 rejects style `realistic_image` — use `model: recraftv3` for photo-real blanks (validated: blank maroon tee). Blanks library gained `mockups/blanks/tee-maroon-recraft.png`.
- **"Exact match" means TRACE the customer's actual art, never rebuild the concept from memory** — Ryan rejected a from-memory recreation on this job ("you made something totally different"). Working pipeline: threshold the white print out of the sample photo → connected-component split (custom mark vs. letters) → 10x upscale + Gaussian blur ~6 + potrace (-a 1.3 -O 0.6, invert first: potrace traces BLACK) → re-set surrounding type in the matched font. Font matched by measuring glyph advance/cap ratios from the photo (this sample: Futura Condensed ExtraBold = CSS weight 800 on macOS Futura.ttc).
- Left-chest placement that passed Ryan's QC on the Recraft blank tee (1024x1365): cx 690, cy ~485, w 106px (≈3.5"), rot -4°. First attempt (cx 645, cy 555, w 160 ≈ "4+") was "way too centered, way too big."
- **Mockup base rule (Ryan, same job): when the customer supplied a product photo, use THAT photo as the mockup base** — Ryan rejected a substitute AI model ("you're using a different model than the original picture"). Working method: inpaint the old print out with numpy diffusion fill (mask whitish pixels loosely + MaxFilter dilate 11, iterate blur-fill ~500x, re-add fabric grain noise), then composite the new art at the original print's measured position. cv2 unavailable (PEP 668 blocks pip install).
- Trace-smoothing from a low-res photo NEVER reached acceptable quality (Ryan rejected two rounds: "crooked lines, looks like shit"). **The lane that finally passed: AI clean-redraw, then extract.** Recraft `imageToImage` (multipart: image + prompt, strength 0.2, style vector_illustration, model recraftv3) returns native SVG redraws — it restyles the overall layout (unusable whole) but renders individual marks with beautiful smooth curves. Rasterize its output at 3x, connected-component the mark you need, potrace at that high res (-a 1.0 -O 0.3) → clean production vector. Keep surrounding type as live fonts.
- **Division of labor (Ryan, 2026-08-23, Cowboys Wandering): "why couldn't you just do the same in canva? we are overcomplicating every aspect of this job."** Quick visual edits (recolors, simple element swaps) → tell Ryan to do them in Canva or ask the customer's artist for source vectors FIRST — never reconstruct art from low-res PDF crops when a 2-minute Canva edit or one ask-for-the-file email gets a better result. Claude's lane on art jobs: blank sourcing, PMS sampling from customer chips, placement math, composites, proof-sheet generation. When an element must be rebuilt, go straight to vector redraw (stroked beziers at measured stroke width) — raster crops upscale badly, every time.
- Higgsfield grace-period daily cap persisted ALL DAY on 2026-08-20 (every model, image-to-image included, "daily generation limit for your grace period") even though Ryan believed credits were available — the grace-period cap gates generation regardless of credit balance; the plan needs renewing to restore it. Recraft was the working substitute both times.
