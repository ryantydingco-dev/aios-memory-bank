# CA guarantee rules (Kenny, 2026-08-15)

- **Never mention late orders, missed dates, or discounts-if-late in any guarantee, offer, post, DM, or email.** Kenny's rule, stated by Ryan 08-15: it doesn't happen at CA, and naming it plants the fear. This applies verbatim across all copy — no "or X% off," no "in hand by your date or else."
- Risk reversal lives at the **proof/approval stage** instead: "Love the proof or pay nothing — you approve everything before we produce a single piece."
- Punctuality is expressed as **track record** ("27 years of on-time delivery"), never as a contingency clause.
- Scarcity/urgency is framed as **capacity** ("holiday production slots are limited; booking closes Oct 1"), never as delivery risk.
- Related standing rule: `ca-no-discount-gimmicks.md`. Any guarantee wording still requires Kenny's sign-off before publication.
