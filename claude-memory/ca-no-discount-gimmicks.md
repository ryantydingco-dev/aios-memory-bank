Ryan, 2026-07-08: "I don't ever want to see any '10% discount' that is not an offer." (Triggered by the law-firm retreat campaign's off-brand "gear shows up before the date, or it's 10% off" line, which he hated.)

**Why:** CA competes on PREMIUM done-for-you value — one contact you can actually call, on-time-or-we-make-it-right guarantee, named proof (Thermo Fisher/Trinity Health), 27 years, designed/stored/shipped. A discount/coupon line reads as commodity and drags CA into the race-to-the-bottom distributor lane it's explicitly built to escape (see [[promo-market-landscape]] two-lane positioning). It also undercuts the mockup wedge.

**How to apply (all CA copy — cold email, LinkedIn, Sendr, content, openers, mockup pitches):**
- NEVER write "X% off", "get Y% off if…", fake-scarcity discounts, or any coupon-style line — UNLESS Ryan explicitly provides a real, approved offer in the brief.
- Replace deadline/urgency framing with CONFIDENCE, not price: "on time, or we make it right", "we work backwards from your date and guarantee the in-hand date", named proof, one contact.
- Enforced in: `swag-handled-cold-copy.md` guardrail, `scripts/ca_content_factory.py` generation rules. Applies to [[ca-outbound-pipeline]] / [[ca-openings-engine]] / [[ca-tradeshow-signal]] openers too. Related: [[creative-alternatives-aios]].
