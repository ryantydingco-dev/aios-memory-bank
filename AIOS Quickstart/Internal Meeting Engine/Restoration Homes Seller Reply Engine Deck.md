# Gamma Deck Draft: Restoration Homes Seller Reply Engine Pilot

## Card 1 — Title

# Restoration Homes Seller Reply Engine

A low-risk pilot to turn seller data into tracked conversations, hot seller call lists, and closed deals.

Prepared for Restoration Homes

---

## Card 2 — The idea

Restoration Homes already has the most important asset: a clear local offer.

- Cash offer in 24 hours
- Sell as-is
- No repairs, cleanup, showings, or commissions
- Close quickly or on the seller’s timeline
- Local Midlands SC buyer since 2009

The opportunity is to add a simple system that starts more seller conversations and makes sure no interested reply slips through the cracks.

---

## Card 3 — The core strategy

We do not need to build a giant real estate tech platform.

We need one clean operating loop:

1. Pull seller data from CRS / Tracerfy / public records
2. Clean, segment, and score the list
3. Send compliant email drips through Smartlead
4. Route replies into an AI-assisted seller reply desk
5. Flag hot sellers immediately
6. Restoration Homes calls, evaluates, and closes
7. We track what works and improve the next batch

---

## Card 4 — Why AgentMail matters

AgentMail should not be the cold email blaster.

AgentMail is the reply desk.

When a seller replies, AgentMail can:

- Classify the reply
- Summarize the seller/property context
- Draft a response for approval
- Create a callback task
- Update the tracker
- Produce a daily hot seller brief

The goal: speed-to-lead without losing control.

---

## Card 5 — The stack

- CRS / Tracerfy: seller data
- Hermes: scoring, segmentation, copy, weekly learning
- Smartlead: email drip sending, deliverability, unsubscribe handling
- AgentMail: seller reply desk and lead triage
- Tracker / CRM: source of truth
- Restoration Homes: seller calls, offers, closings

Each tool has one job. No tool soup.

---

## Card 6 — Lead segments to test

Start with 500–1,000 potential sellers in the Midlands.

Suggested segments:

1. Absentee owners
2. Long-tenure / likely equity homeowners
3. Out-of-state owners
4. Vacant or problem-property indicators if legally available
5. Rental/landlord owners
6. Expired or old listings if available

Each segment gets slightly different messaging.

---

## Card 7 — Messaging angle

The campaign should not sound desperate, creepy, or overly personal.

Use a simple local, no-pressure angle:

“If selling as-is would ever be easier than repairs, showings, or waiting on financing, Restoration Homes can give you a no-obligation cash offer.”

Key points:

- Local Midlands buyer
- No obligation
- Sell as-is
- No repairs or commissions
- Close quickly or on seller’s timeline

## Card 9 — AgentMail reply classifications

Every reply gets classified:

- HOT_SELLER
- WANTS_CASH_OFFER
- HAS_QUESTIONS
- NEEDS_CALLBACK
- NURTURE
- NOT_INTERESTED
- WRONG_PERSON
- AGENT_OR_BROKER
- UNSUBSCRIBE
- LEGAL_OR_ANGRY

Hot sellers become same-day call tasks.

Unsubscribes get suppressed immediately.

---

## Card 10 — Example reply handling

Seller reply:

“How much would you offer?”

AgentMail output:

- Classification: HOT_SELLER
- Urgency: High
- Next action: call immediately
- Draft reply: “Happy to take a quick look. What’s the best number to reach you, and is the property still in {{city}}?”
- CRM status: NEEDS_CALLBACK

Restoration Homes handles the actual offer conversation.

## Card 11 — Pilot plan

60-day buddy pilot:

Week 1:
- Pull 500–1,000 seller records
- Clean/segment/verify emails
- Build Smartlead campaign
- Set up AgentMail seller reply desk
- Build tracker

Weeks 2–3:
- Launch first batches
- Review replies daily
- Call hot sellers fast

Weeks 4–8:
- Improve segments and messaging
- Track seller conversations, appointments, offers, and deals

---

## Card 12 — Success metrics

Track the full funnel:

- Leads pulled
- Emails verified
- Emails sent
- Bounce rate
- Replies
- Positive replies
- Hot seller replies
- Calls made
- Appointments booked
- Offers made
- Contracts/listings signed
- Deals closed
- Revenue / profit from closed deals

The goal is not just “emails sent.”

The goal is seller conversations that can turn into deals.

---

## Card 13 — Compliance guardrails

We should keep this clean:

- CAN-SPAM compliant emails
- Clear sender identity
- Real business contact info
- Simple unsubscribe/opt-out process
- No deceptive subject lines
- No creepy “we know your situation” language
- Avoid fair housing issues or discriminatory targeting
- Check rules before calling/texting leads
- AgentMail drafts; humans approve

This is a marketing system, not a legal-risk machine.

---

## Card 14 — Why this is worth testing

This is a low-risk pilot because:

- Restoration Homes already has a strong local offer
- Seller data can be pulled from existing sources
- The system can start small with 500–1,000 leads
- AgentMail keeps replies organized
- Hot sellers get called faster
- Results are measurable

If it works here, this can become a repeatable service for other real estate investors and cash home buyers.
