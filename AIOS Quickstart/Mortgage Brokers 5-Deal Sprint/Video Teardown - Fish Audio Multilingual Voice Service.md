# Video Teardown - Fish Audio Multilingual Voice Service

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=trdHxY1UMF0
Date captured: 2026-06-08

## Big idea

The video shows a fast AI service offer: clone a professional's voice with Fish Audio, translate common voicemail scripts into multiple languages, and sell a packaged multilingual voicemail/audio library to realtors or other multilingual-client businesses.

## Workflow

1. Clone voice from 10-60 seconds of audio or existing public video.
2. Use Claude/ChatGPT to draft common voicemail templates.
3. Translate those templates into languages relevant to the customer base.
4. Generate audio files in the cloned voice.
5. Package in Google Drive by language/message type.
6. Sell one-time setup or monthly updates.

## Useful offer patterns

- Create a wow demo before or immediately after outreach.
- Lead with a concrete artifact: “I made 10 voicemail scripts in 4 languages in your voice.”
- Start with warm network / in-person demos for faster trust.
- Teach the tactic for free; sell implementation because most operators won’t do it themselves.
- Expand same workflow across verticals: realtors, insurance, immigration law, medical offices, YouTubers, audiobooks, hold messages, training videos.

## Caution

Voice cloning has consent, trust, and compliance risk. Do not clone/send someone’s voice in outreach without permission. The tactic is interesting but less directly urgent than Ryan’s AR cash sprint.
