## 1. $100M OFFERS — the Grand Slam Offer for CA

**Value equation:** (Dream outcome × Perceived likelihood) ÷ (Time delay × Effort & sacrifice). Promo buyers' real pains, from the buyer-pain research: blown deadlines, ugly surprises at delivery, endless proof email chains, art fees, reorder hassle.

**The CA Grand Slam Offer (per ICP, same skeleton):**
- **Dream outcome:** "Swag that makes your event/firm look great, in hand ON TIME."
- **Likelihood (proof):** 27-year, multi-million-dollar family business. Real photos. Named process.
- **Time delay ↓:** Free mockups in 24–48h (already the proven motion — Kennerly, mockup magnets). Interactive proof page instead of email chains (proof-engine already built).
- **Effort ↓:** "Send us your logo and a date. We do everything else." No art fees. One approval link.
- **Risk reversal (KENNY RULE, 2026-08-15: never mention late orders in any guarantee — it doesn't happen, and naming it plants the fear):** anchor risk reversal on the PROOF stage, not delivery: *"Love the proof or pay nothing — you approve everything before we produce a single piece."* Deadline confidence is expressed as track record, not contingency: "27 years of on-time event work." Any guarantee wording still gets Kenny's sign-off.
- **Bonuses:** free proof page, free spare-units buffer, reorder file kept on file forever ("never re-send art again").
- **Name it.** e.g. **"The On-Time Swag System"** / per-ICP variants ("Booth-Ready Package" for exhibitors, "Firm Store Program" for law firms, "Season Kit" for camps).

**Lead magnet (Hormozi: give away the thing that reveals the need):** the **free 24h mockup** — their logo on the exact product, unprompted. CA has already proven this converts (Kennerly package, mockup magnets pillar). This is the DM offer, the content CTA, and the cold-email offer. One magnet, every channel.

## 1b. The Grand Slam Offer menu (one per ICP / trigger — same skeleton, different dream outcome)

Every offer below follows: named package + deadline promise + free mockup entry + risk reversal + a bonus that costs CA little but kills a pain. Guarantees need Kenny's sign-off before any go public.

1. **The Booth-Ready Package** (trade-show exhibitors — 5,200+ already in the campaign stack). *"Everything your booth needs — shirts, swag, giveaways — in one box, at your show before setup day. You approve every proof before production."* Urgency is built in (the show date IS the deadline); confidence is stated as track record, never as a lateness contingency. Bonus: free packing manifest per box + spare-units buffer. Upsell: multi-show program (they exhibit at several — CA keeps art + kit specs on file, each next show is one email).
2. **The Season Kit** (camps/schools/teams — Camp Arcadia proved demand, summer camps hit 10.1% reply). *"Your whole season's gear — staff shirts, camper tees, store items — locked in one order, delivered before opening day, art kept on file for next year."* Bonus: free 110th-anniversary-style commemorative mockup (worked on Arcadia). Continuity hook: auto-renewal ping every spring = the anniversary-reorder engine as a promise, not a campaign.
3. **The Firm Store Program** (law/professional firms — highest-value ICP). *"A branded online store for your firm — new-hire kits, client gifts, event wear — we run it, you approve once a quarter, zero inventory risk."* This is continuity AS the offer. Entry: free store mockup with their 5 best-fit items. Harbor Haven is literally requesting this — build the first one as the case study.
4. **The New-Hire Welcome Kit** (HR/office managers at growing firms — the hiring-signal enrichment pilot targets exactly these). *"Every new hire gets a branded kit on their desk day one — we hold stock, you send a name and an address."* Recurring by nature (they keep hiring). Trigger-based outreach: hiring badge = they need this NOW.
5. **The Client-Gifting Autopilot** (Q4 lane — campaign already built). *"Your top 50 clients get a premium branded gift before Dec 15 — you approve one sample, we handle names, notes, and shipping. Booking closes Oct 1 — holiday production slots are limited."* Real scarcity framed as capacity, never as delivery risk. High margin, annual continuity.
6. **The Gala Sponsor Pack** (nonprofits/galas — lane verified). *"Step-and-repeat, table gifts, auction swag, volunteer shirts — one order, sponsor logos handled, delivered a week before the event."* Bonus: free sponsor-logo proof page (sponsor approvals are their private hell — the proof-engine solves it).
7. **The Store Rescue** (Bonfire/SquadLocker refugees — Arcadia AND Harbor Haven both arrived this way). *"Leaving [platform]? We rebuild your store, migrate your designs free, and beat your current per-unit cost — first sample run free."* A switch offer aimed at a proven migration pattern. Content angle writes itself.
8. **The Grand-Opening Box** (new gyms/openings — the signal engines are built, blocked only on free API keys). *"Opening day covered: staff gear, member giveaways, banner — delivered the week before you open, one call to set up."* Trigger-based, deadline-native, and the buyer has never had a promo vendor before = zero incumbent.

**Offer-wide rule (Kenny, 2026-08-15):** no offer, post, or DM ever mentions late orders, missed dates, or discounts-if-late — CA doesn't miss dates and the copy must not imagine it. Risk reversal lives at the proof/approval stage; punctuality is proof ("27 years of on-time delivery"), not a clause.

**Prioritization rule:** lead with 1, 2, and 5 — their deadlines are real and near (Q4 gifting + winter shows + fall seasons), which makes every Hormozi urgency/scarcity element TRUE rather than manufactured. 3 and 7 are the continuity builders. 4 and 8 wait until their signal engines are unblocked.

**The LinkedIn DM engine (Ryan + Mickey's new lane) — rules:**
1. **Same ICPs as the campaigns** (law-firm admins, event/trade-show exhibitors, camp directors, HR/office managers). No new verticals — LinkedIn is a new CHANNEL, not a new market.
2. **The Hormozi DM ladder, not pitch-slapping:** connect (no note or light note) → engage with their content 1–2 touches → DM opens with the GIVE: *"Saw [firm/event]. We mocked up what your logo looks like on [product] — want me to send it?"* The mockup IS the message. Ask for nothing until they raise a hand.
3. **Volume with a floor and a cap:** 20 quality DMs/day each (100/wk/person), personalized enough that a human would reply. LinkedIn bans automation-feeling behavior — keep it manual-ish, warm accounts up slowly (Mickey's account especially if it's new).
4. **Suppression discipline:** cross-check every DM target against SmartLead sends + existing customers (same suppression rules as email). Nobody gets cold-DMed while mid-sequence in email.
5. **Content × DMs flywheel (the actual Hormozi play):** post daily proof-of-work content (mockups made, orders shipped, behind-the-scenes at a 27-year shop), then DM warm engagers first — commenters and profile viewers outrank cold lists every day.
6. **Scoreboard (weekly, same review as Monk Mode):** DMs sent → replies → mockups sent → quotes → closes → $ originated. Mickey's numbers and Ryan's numbers tracked separately.

**Mickey — settle BEFORE the weekend session (in writing, one page):** what Mickey gets paid (share of Ryan's 15%? flat per close? per qualified convo?), who owns which ICP, who fulfills mockups, and the rule that Kenny's customer data never leaves CA systems. An unwritten split is a future fight — Hormozi would say the same.

## 3. MONEY MODELS — the cash-flow ladder

- **Attraction offer (free/cheap, gets the yes):** free 24h mockup → tiny first order or sample pack at cost. Goal: a PAYING customer fast, not margin.
- **Core offer:** the named package (Booth-Ready / Season Kit / Firm Store) at standard 30–40% margin.
- **Upsell at the moment of yes:** "Add [second item] and ship together — saves X on freight/setup." Event buyers especially (lanyards + shirts + bags travel together).
- **Continuity — THE CA GOLDMINE:** this is where the model prints. (a) **Managed stores** (Harbor Haven is asking for one RIGHT NOW) = recurring revenue, zero re-selling. (b) **Anniversary reorder program** = the reactivation engine formalized into an always-on subscription-like motion ("we ping you before your season, art on file, one-click reorder"). (c) **Quarterly gifting programs** for firms. Settle with Kenny how continuity revenue counts for the 15% BEFORE building it — this is exactly the "store revenue recurs?" attribution one-liner, now existential.
- **Cash-flow rule:** deposits up front on custom orders — funds the goods, protects against the Sanmar A/P squeeze.
