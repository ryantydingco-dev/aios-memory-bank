# CA × Hormozi Playbook — Offers, Leads, Money Models (2026-08-15)

> Ryan + Mickey's operating model for CA lead gen via LinkedIn DMs + content, built on $100M Offers, $100M Leads, and the money-models framework. Companion to `Monk Mode 2026.md` (which still governs: asks are the metric, ask block untouchable).
> The good news: CA has already accidentally proven half of this. This doc names it and systematizes it.

## 1. $100M OFFERS — the Grand Slam Offer for CA

**Value equation:** (Dream outcome × Perceived likelihood) ÷ (Time delay × Effort & sacrifice). Promo buyers' real pains, from the buyer-pain research: blown deadlines, ugly surprises at delivery, endless proof email chains, art fees, reorder hassle.

**The CA Grand Slam Offer (per ICP, same skeleton):**
- **Dream outcome:** "Swag that makes your event/firm look great, in hand ON TIME."
- **Likelihood (proof):** 27-year, multi-million-dollar family business. Real photos. Named process.
- **Time delay ↓:** Free mockups in 24–48h (already the proven motion — Kennerly, mockup magnets). Interactive proof page instead of email chains (proof-engine already built).
- **Effort ↓:** "Send us your logo and a date. We do everything else." No art fees. One approval link.
- **Risk reversal:** the guarantee — *"In hand by your date or X% off"* and/or *"Hate the proof? Pay nothing."* (Price the guarantee with Kenny before publishing — commission-only means Ryan can't underwrite it alone.)
- **Bonuses:** free proof page, free spare-units buffer, reorder file kept on file forever ("never re-send art again").
- **Name it.** e.g. **"The On-Time Swag System"** / per-ICP variants ("Booth-Ready Package" for exhibitors, "Firm Store Program" for law firms, "Season Kit" for camps).

**Lead magnet (Hormozi: give away the thing that reveals the need):** the **free 24h mockup** — their logo on the exact product, unprompted. CA has already proven this converts (Kennerly package, mockup magnets pillar). This is the DM offer, the content CTA, and the cold-email offer. One magnet, every channel.

**The LinkedIn DM engine (Ryan + Mickey's new lane) — rules:**
1. **Same ICPs as the campaigns** (law-firm admins, event/trade-show exhibitors, camp directors, HR/office managers). No new verticals — LinkedIn is a new CHANNEL, not a new market.
2. **The Hormozi DM ladder, not pitch-slapping:** connect (no note or light note) → engage with their content 1–2 touches → DM opens with the GIVE: *"Saw [firm/event]. We mocked up what your logo looks like on [product] — want me to send it?"* The mockup IS the message. Ask for nothing until they raise a hand.
3. **Volume with a floor and a cap:** 20 quality DMs/day each (100/wk/person), personalized enough that a human would reply. LinkedIn bans automation-feeling behavior — keep it manual-ish, warm accounts up slowly (Mickey's account especially if it's new).
4. **Suppression discipline:** cross-check every DM target against SmartLead sends + existing customers (same suppression rules as email). Nobody gets cold-DMed while mid-sequence in email.
5. **Content × DMs flywheel (the actual Hormozi play):** post daily proof-of-work content (mockups made, orders shipped, behind-the-scenes at a 27-year shop), then DM warm engagers first — commenters and profile viewers outrank cold lists every day.
6. **Scoreboard (weekly, same review as Monk Mode):** DMs sent → replies → mockups sent → quotes → closes → $ originated. Mickey's numbers and Ryan's numbers tracked separately.

**Mickey — settle BEFORE the weekend session (in writing, one page):** what Mickey gets paid (share of Ryan's 15%? flat per close? per qualified convo?), who owns which ICP, who fulfills mockups, and the rule that Kenny's customer data never leaves CA systems. An unwritten split is a future fight — Hormozi would say the same.

## 3. MONEY MODELS — the cash-flow ladder

- **Attraction offer (free/cheap, gets the yes):** free 24h mockup → tiny first order or sample pack at cost. Goal: a PAYING customer fast, not margin.
- **Core offer:** the named package (Booth-Ready / Season Kit / Firm Store) at standard 30–40% margin.
- **Upsell at the moment of yes:** "Add [second item] and ship together — saves X on freight/setup." Event buyers especially (lanyards + shirts + bags travel together).
- **Continuity — THE CA GOLDMINE:** this is where the model prints. (a) **Managed stores** (Harbor Haven is asking for one RIGHT NOW) = recurring revenue, zero re-selling. (b) **Anniversary reorder program** = the reactivation engine formalized into an always-on subscription-like motion ("we ping you before your season, art on file, one-click reorder"). (c) **Quarterly gifting programs** for firms. Settle with Kenny how continuity revenue counts for the 15% BEFORE building it — this is exactly the "store revenue recurs?" attribution one-liner, now existential.
- **Cash-flow rule:** deposits up front on custom orders — funds the goods, protects against the Sanmar A/P squeeze.

## The one warning label

Hormozi's own rule: **more volume on a proven offer beats a new funnel every time.** CA's proven offer is the free mockup. The weekend is for aiming MORE volume at it (LinkedIn + content), not redesigning it. If Saturday turns into building a course, a funnel, or a brand instead of a target list and 10 posts — that's the build-trap in a Hormozi costume.
