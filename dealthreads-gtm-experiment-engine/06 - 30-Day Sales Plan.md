# 06 - 30-Day Sales Plan

**Goal of this 30 days:** book and start **1-2 paid pilots** of the Dealthreads GTM Experiment Engine, sold to agencies/consultants who sell high-ticket B2B services. Not "fill a pipeline." Not "go viral." Get 1-2 founders to say yes to a 90-day founder-run pilot and pay setup.

The motion you run here IS the product. You're not "doing sales tasks" — you're running the exact LinkedIn + Loom-first experiment loop you sell, on yourself, in public. Every DM, Loom, and follow-up is a live artifact you can show the next prospect. That's the whole pitch: *"I run this exact engine on my own company. Here's this week's review."*

> Scripts live elsewhere. This file is the **plan + targeting + rhythm**. Verbatim DM/Loom/discovery/objection/pilot scripts are in **`08 - Scripts & Templates`** — referenced inline below, not duplicated here.

## The number that matters (work backwards)

Honest cold conversion is brutal, so we don't pretend. Reverse-math for **1-2 pilots in 30 days**:

| Stage | Rate (honest) | Volume needed |
|---|---|---|
| Pilots closed | — | 1-2 |
| Sales calls held | ~25-40% call → pilot | 5-8 calls |
| Positive replies (warm DM/Loom) | ~30-40% reply → call | ~15-20 positive replies |
| People in real conversation | LinkedIn warm motion runs **8-15% reply**, well above the 1-5% cold-email floor | needs ~120-180 quality touches |
| First-50 list + warm network | the engine | **~50 cold targets + ~20-30 warm contacts** |

**Translation:** ~50 well-chosen cold targets + your warm network, worked properly over 4 weeks with personalized Looms, realistically yields **5-8 calls and 1-2 pilots.** If you blast 500 generic connect requests instead, you'll get worse numbers AND no artifacts to show. Volume is the trap. Don't fall in it.

**What NOT to do this month (kill these now):**
- Don't build an email-blast infrastructure. Wrong channel for this ICP, and post-Feb-2024 deliverability rules make low-volume the *premium* play anyway. Email is optional/secondary here.
- Don't buy a 5,000-row lead list. You need 50 great-fit accounts you actually understand, not a stale CSV.
- Don't build automation to auto-send DMs. The human-approved, personalized touch is the moat. Automating it kills the only thing that's working.
- Don't write a "manifesto" launch post and wait for inbound. Content supports outbound here; it isn't the engine yet.

## Part 1 — Build the first-50 target list

### The targeting filter (this IS the ICP, made operational)

You're hunting **founders/owners of agencies and consultancies selling high-ticket B2B services** ($3k+ engagements or $2k+/mo retainers) who have **pipeline pressure and no real GTM/RevOps function.** They sell outcomes for a living, so they respect a sharp outbound motion — and they feel the "founder-led sales doesn't scale" pain personally.

**Hard filters (must match all):**
- **Company type:** B2B agency, consultancy, fractional firm, or productized-service shop. Sells *services*, not SaaS.
- **Headcount:** ~2-30 people. Big enough to afford ~$2-3.5k/mo, small enough to have no RevOps hire and a founder still doing sales.
- **Deal size:** high-ticket (project $3k+ or retainer $2k+/mo). They can math out the ROI vs an SDR fast.
- **Founder is reachable + active on LinkedIn:** posts or at least engages in the last ~90 days. If they're a LinkedIn ghost, your wedge channel is dead — deprioritize.
- **Visible pipeline pressure / growth intent:** hiring a "growth/BD/sales" role, posting about "looking for clients," recently raised prices, launched a new offer, or publicly talking about wanting more pipeline.

**Soft signals (raise priority — these mirror Oloxa's signal scoring: CLOSING / HIRING / VOLUME / PAIN):**
- **HIRING** — posted an SDR/BDR/"growth marketer" req in last 60 days (they feel the gap and are about to spend $10k+/mo on a hire — you're the cheaper, faster alternative).
- **PAIN** — posted/commented about feast-or-famine pipeline, referral dependence, "DMs aren't working," or founder-led-sales burnout.
- **VOLUME** — clearly scaling (new hires, new service line, agency-growth content) but no systematic outbound.
- **CLOSING** — case studies / "welcome new client" posts (they can sell; they just need *more at-bats*).

**Exclusion filters (skip these — wrong fit, wasted touches):**
- Pure B2C / e-comm / local-services agencies (different buyer, different econ).
- SaaS companies (different motion — save for a later ICP).
- Huge agencies (50+) — they have or will hire RevOps internally.
- Solo freelancers with no budget / no intent to scale.
- Anyone who clearly already runs a mature outbound machine (they don't need you; hard to displace).
- Direct competitors (other outbound/lead-gen agencies) — unless it's a partnership play, not a sale.

### Where to pull the actual list (be honest: this needs a data pull)

I can't hand you 50 real company names from here — those require a live data pull from your own LinkedIn + a tool. Here's exactly how to build it in ~2-3 hours:

1. **LinkedIn Sales Navigator** (best for this ICP): filter by Industry (Marketing/Advertising, Management Consulting, Business Consulting & Services, Design), Headcount (2-10, 11-50), Geography (your timezone-friendly markets), Seniority = Owner/Founder/Partner/CEO. Then layer keyword searches in bios/posts: "agency," "fractional," "we help B2B," "high-ticket," "done-for-you."
2. **Job boards as an intent layer:** search LinkedIn Jobs / Indebted boards for small agencies hiring "SDR," "BDR," "Business Development," "Growth Marketer." Every result is a HIRING-signal account — drop the company + founder into your list.
3. **Engagement mining:** find 3-5 well-known agency-growth voices (people your ICP follows). Pull who comments on their posts — those are active, reachable agency founders. (Your `linkedin-engager-analytics` skill does exactly this.)
4. **Your own network + 2nd-degree:** LinkedIn connections and "people you may know" who fit. These convert highest — flag them for the warm track.

Capture each row in a simple sheet: **Company | Founder name | LinkedIn URL | Headcount | Service/offer | Deal-size guess | Signal(s) | Signal evidence (link/quote) | Pain hypothesis | Track (Warm/Cold) | Status.** That sheet becomes your first **account universe** + the raw material for **research cards** — the same artifact you'll deliver to a paying client.

### 12-15 example archetypes to anchor the search

Real names need the data pull above. These are the **personas to pattern-match against** — when a real profile looks like one of these, it goes on the list:

1. **The B2B content/SEO agency founder** — 6-12 people, $4-8k/mo retainers, grew on referrals + inbound, now plateaued and wants outbound but "doesn't want to be spammy." *Perfect fit — the anti-spam framing is the whole sell.*
2. **The fractional CMO / fractional exec** — solo or tiny, $5-10k/mo engagements, pipeline is 100% network + word-of-mouth, terrified of the feast-or-famine cycle. *PAIN signal, high willingness to pay.*
3. **The performance/paid-media agency owner** — 5-20 people, great at client results, terrible at their *own* marketing ("cobbler's kids"). Just lost a big client and needs to refill. *CLOSING signal + acute pain.*
4. **The B2B web/dev or design studio principal** — project-based $10-50k builds, lumpy revenue, wants predictable top-of-funnel instead of waiting for referrals.
5. **The PR / brand / comms boutique founder** — high-ticket retainers, sells to marketing leaders, allergic to "growth-hacky" tactics — wants a *credible, peer-to-peer* motion. *LinkedIn+Loom warmth is the differentiator.*
6. **The management/ops consultant** — ex-corporate, sells $15k+ projects, strong expertise but zero systematic BD, just went full-time on their own thing.
7. **The recruiting / talent agency founder (B2B placements)** — commission-driven, deeply gets "more conversations = more money," fast to see ROI, very responsive to a sharp pitch.
8. **The HubSpot/Salesforce/RevOps implementation partner** — ironically sells GTM tooling but has no outbound of their own; understands the category, easy to talk shop with.
9. **The B2B video / podcast / creative agency owner** — sells Loom-adjacent work, so a personalized Loom pitch lands *especially* well (they appreciate the craft).
10. **The growth/CRO consultancy founder** — analytical buyer, will love the experiment-loop + conversation-quality framing because it's how *they* think. *Sells on the methodology.*
11. **The agency owner actively hiring an SDR (HIRING signal)** — has a live req out. Your entire pitch: "before you spend $10-12k/mo + a 3-4 month ramp, here's an engine that's live week one."
12. **The newly-independent ex-agency-employee** — just left to start their own shop, has skills + a few clients, no GTM system, hungry, lower budget (Starter tier). Good pilot energy.
13. **The productized-service founder** — sells a fixed-scope offer (e.g. "$3k/mo done-for-you X"), loves systems, ideal for showing the repeatable experiment loop.
14. **The B2B coaching / training company founder** — high-ticket cohorts/programs, sells to execs, network-dependent, wants a second reliable channel.
15. **The boutique financial/legal/compliance advisory founder** — high-trust, high-ticket, conservative — needs the *human-approved, on-brand, non-spammy* positioning to even consider outbound. Strong fit for the QA story.

Aim for **~50 across these archetypes**, weighted toward anyone showing a HIRING or PAIN signal. Tag ~15-20 of them as warm (1st/2nd degree) for the warm track.

## Part 2 — The four plays (run in parallel, weighted)

### Play A — Warm network (do this FIRST; highest conversion)

Your existing network converts 5-10x better than cold. Before any cold DM goes out:

- Pull **20-30 warm contacts**: past clients/colleagues, people who've engaged with you, anyone 1st-degree who fits the ICP — *or who knows someone who does.*
- Two asks, never a hard pitch:
  1. **Direct (they fit):** low-pressure "I'm running a new thing, you're exactly who it's for, worth 15 min?" → discovery call.
  2. **Referral (they don't fit but know people):** "Who's the most pipeline-starved agency founder you know? Happy to show them what I'm running, no pressure." Warm intros skip the entire cold gauntlet.
- These are also your best **early reference conversations** even if they don't buy — they'll tell you if the offer lands.

→ Exact warm-intro + referral-ask wording: **`08 - Scripts & Templates`**.

### Play B — LinkedIn outbound (the core engine)

This is the wedge. Peer-to-peer, founder-to-founder, from **your own profile** (so fix your profile first — see Day 0).

**The sequence per target (human-approved at every step):**
1. **Connection request** — personalized one-liner referencing their actual signal/post. *No pitch in the request.* (Generic "I'd love to connect" = deprioritized into the noise.)
2. **On accept (wait ~1 day):** a short, specific opener referencing why *them* — tied to a real signal/observation. Still no pitch. Start a conversation, not a transaction.
3. **Touch 2 — the Loom** (see Play C): once there's a flicker of engagement, send the personalized 60-90s audit Loom. This is the differentiator and the highest-leverage moment.
4. **Touch 3 — value-add follow-up:** a relevant resource, a sharper observation, or a soft call ask. Then stop. **2-3 touches total, then let it rest.** No nagging.

**Throughput target:** ~10-15 new personalized connection requests/day + ~10-15 follow-ups/day. That's deliberately low-volume and high-relevance — the same discipline you sell. Stay well under LinkedIn's ~100-150 weekly invite ceiling; getting your profile restricted in week 1 would be self-inflicted.

**Track per person:** connect sent → accepted → opener sent → replied → Loom sent → Loom watched → call booked. This tracking IS the conversation-quality measurement you sell. Log it.

→ Verbatim connection-request, opener, and follow-up scripts: **`08 - Scripts & Templates`**.

### Play C — The Loom audit (your unfair advantage)

A personalized 60-90s Loom is what separates you from every other person in their DMs. It's the single highest-converting touch in the whole motion — and the clearest proof of the "personalized, human, on-brand" promise.

**Format (keep it under 90s):**
- **0-10s:** "Hey [name], made you a quick one." Face on, their LinkedIn/website on screen. The personalization is the hook.
- **10-50s:** one **specific, useful observation** about *their* GTM — a gap in how they're getting clients, a signal you noticed, a sharper angle they could be running. Give real value; don't just flatter.
- **50-90s:** "I run a weekly experiment loop that does exactly this — for my own company and for B2B agency founders. Worth a 15-min look? Either way, hope that's useful." Soft CTA.

**Rules:** ~5-10 min to make each (don't over-produce — raw and specific beats polished and generic). Send only to people showing a flicker of engagement (don't burn Looms on cold non-responders). **Every Loom you record is a reusable artifact** — anonymize a couple of the best ones to *show prospects what they'll get.*

→ Loom script skeleton + audit checklist: **`08 - Scripts & Templates`**.

### Play D — Content (support act, not the engine)

Content warms the cold list so your DMs land softer, and it builds the founder-led credibility the pilot sells on. **2-3 posts/week**, no more — don't let content eat outbound time.

**What to post (you're documenting the live build, which is the whole brand story):**
- **Build-in-public:** "Running my own GTM experiment engine on Dealthreads. Here's this week's experiment + what the numbers said." Real artifacts > opinions.
- **Anti-spray POV:** the Feb-2024 0.3% spam cap, why volume now self-harms, why low-volume + human QA is the premium move. This is your category wedge — own it.
- **Worked proof:** anonymized Oloxa engine snippets (research cards, weekly review) — "this is what a week of the loop looks like."
- **Useful teardowns:** quick GTM observations agency founders can use. (These double as Loom material.)

Then **engage daily**: thoughtful comments on your 50 targets' posts *before* you DM them. A warm comment 2-3 days pre-connection lifts accept rates a lot. (Your `linkedin-comment-drafter` / `linkedin-engager-analytics` skills support this.)

→ Post angles + hooks: **`08 - Scripts & Templates`** (and lean on the LinkedIn content skills).

## Part 3 — Daily execution rhythm

Time-boxed to ~2-2.5 hrs/day. This is the cadence you'll later run *for* clients — practice it on yourself.

**Morning block (~60-75 min) — outbound + engagement**
- (10 min) Engage: thoughtful comments on 5-10 target prospects' recent posts.
- (20 min) Send 10-15 personalized connection requests from the day's list slice.
- (20 min) Record 2-4 personalized Looms for warm/engaged prospects.
- (15 min) Send 10-15 value-add follow-ups (touch 2/3) to people mid-sequence.

**Midday — reply triage (check 2x, ~15 min total)**
- Respond to every reply fast. Warm reply → push toward a call (calendar link / time options). Lukewarm → keep the conversation human, don't force it. Log reply quality (genuine interest vs polite vs no).

**Afternoon block (~30-45 min) — content + admin**
- (15-20 min, 2-3x/week) Write + publish one post.
- (15 min) Update the tracker: touches sent, replies, Looms watched, calls booked. Note what's landing (which angle/persona/Loom style).
- (5 min) Tee up tomorrow's list slice.

**End of day (5 min):** log the daily numbers. These roll up into the weekly review.

**Weekly (Fri, ~45 min) — the loop, run on yourself:** review the week's numbers (connects, accepts, replies, reply quality, Looms watched, calls, pilots). Identify what worked. Pick the **3-5 experiments for next week** (new angle, new persona segment, new opener, new Loom hook). *This weekly review is literally the deliverable you sell — write it like a client would read it. It becomes a sales artifact.*

## Part 4 — Week-by-week 30-day plan

### Day 0 (before Week 1 — setup, ~half a day)
Non-negotiable prep so week 1 isn't wasted:
- **Profile is the landing page.** Headline, About, and Featured must make a cold founder think "this person runs a real GTM motion." (Use `linkedin-profile-optimizer`.) A weak profile sinks every DM.
- Build the **first-50 list** (Part 1) + tag warm vs cold.
- Set up the **tracker sheet** and a booking link (Calendly/Cal.com).
- Confirm Loom works + record one practice audit.
- Have `08 - Scripts & Templates` open and ready.

### Week 1 — Warm network + ignition
**Theme:** start with the highest-conversion audience and get the engine running. Don't go wide yet — get reps and find what lands.
- Work the **entire warm list (20-30)**: direct asks + referral asks.
- Begin LinkedIn cold: **~10 connects/day** (start conservative, watch accept rate), personalized.
- Record **~3-5 Looms** for the warmest prospects.
- Publish **2 posts** (intro the build-in-public thread + the anti-spray POV).
- Engage daily on target posts.

**Week 1 goals:**
- 25-30 warm touches + ~50 cold connects sent
- 3-5 Looms sent
- **2-4 calls booked** (mostly from warm network — that's expected and good)
- **Target: 1 pilot conversation seriously in motion** (likely a warm contact)

### Week 2 — Scale outbound + first pilots in motion
**Theme:** the warm well is drying up; cold engine takes over. First discovery calls happen.
- **~12-15 connects/day** + 10-15 follow-ups/day.
- **~10-15 Looms** across the week to engaged prospects.
- **Hold 2-4 discovery calls.** Use the discovery + pilot framework in `08`. Goal of each call: qualify + pitch the 90-day pilot.
- Publish **2-3 posts**, including an anonymized Oloxa artifact.
- First weekly review: double down on whatever angle/persona is replying; cut what's dead.

**Week 2 goals:**
- ~75-90 cumulative cold touches
- 8-12 positive replies
- **2-4 discovery calls held**
- **1 pilot verbally agreed / proposal out**

### Week 3 — Close + refill
**Theme:** convert the pipeline into signed pilots; keep the top of funnel full so week 4 isn't dry.
- Maintain outbound rhythm (don't let closing stall prospecting — the classic founder mistake).
- **Hold 3-5 calls.** Push verbal yeses to **signed + setup invoice paid.** Handle objections from `08`.
- Send **proposals/pilot agreements** to anyone warm. Make it easy: month-to-month after the 90-day pilot, soft activity floor, transparent weekly reviews, no guaranteed-meeting promises.
- Refresh the list: add 15-20 new accounts from fresh HIRING/PAIN signals.
- Publish **2-3 posts.** If a pilot is signed, post the (anonymized) "kicking off a new engine" story — proof compounds.

**Week 3 goals:**
- **1-2 pilots SIGNED + setup paid** ← the core 30-day outcome
- 3-5 calls held
- ~120-150 cumulative touches
- Pipeline refilled (15-20 fresh accounts queued)

### Week 4 — Convert, systematize, and set up next month
**Theme:** lock in the pilot(s), close stragglers, and turn this month's learnings into a repeatable system (this is your own first "weekly improve" at the monthly scale).
- **Onboard signed pilot(s)** — kick off their context pack + account universe. (Delivery is its own workstream; flag the handoff.)
- Close any remaining warm calls; revive lukewarm threads with one sharp value-add.
- **Hold 2-4 calls.**
- Run a **30-day retro:** which archetype replied most? Which Loom hook/opener won? What's the real reply rate and call rate? → This sets the targeting + messaging defaults for next month *and* becomes a sales artifact ("here's 30 days of my own engine's data").
- Publish **2-3 posts**, including a "30 days running my own engine — here's what the numbers actually said" recap.

**Week 4 goals:**
- **2 pilots signed & onboarding** (cumulative) — floor is 1, target is 2
- 30-day data documented (reply rate, call rate, best persona/angle/hook)
- Next-month list (50 fresh accounts) + refined messaging queued
- A public proof recap post live

## Honest critique / what to watch

- **The bottleneck won't be list size — it's Loom volume + reply speed.** Personalized Looms and fast, human replies are what convert. If you slip, it's there. Protect the morning block.
- **Closing-vs-prospecting whiplash** (weeks 3-4) is the #1 founder failure mode. When calls heat up, prospecting dies, and week 5 is empty. Keep the daily connect/follow-up rhythm no matter what.
- **One pilot is a real win.** With zero external case study, landing even one paid founder-run pilot validates the whole repositioning. Don't discount a "1." Two is the stretch.
- **Don't over-engineer delivery before you've sold it.** Build just enough to run *your own* engine well; productize the delivery system *after* a pilot is paying. Selling proof-of-demand first is the right order.
- **Keep every artifact.** Research cards, weekly reviews, anonymized Looms, the 30-day data recap — these are what make pilot #2 and #3 easier to sell than #1. The byproduct of this month is your sales collateral for next quarter.
