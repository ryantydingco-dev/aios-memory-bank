# 07 — First 72 Hours

**Goal:** Go from "I have an offer" to "I have live conversations and at least 1-2 discovery calls booked" in three working days. No new product. No new tooling. Just the wedge motion (LinkedIn DM + connection from your own profile → personalized 60-90s Loom → 2-3 structured follow-ups) pointed at the first ICP.

**ICP for these 72 hours:** agency/consultant founders selling high-ticket B2B services (2-30 people, $3k+ projects or $2k+/mo retainers, active on LinkedIn, visible pipeline pressure, no RevOps function).

**Operating principle:** ship at 70%. Every block below has a "done" bar that is deliberately rough. The whole point of the GTM Experiment Engine is that the *market* sharpens your targeting and messaging — not a week of solo polishing. If a block tempts you to perfect it, you are robbing the loop of data.

**Time budget:** ~3-4 hrs/day for 3 days. Front-loaded on Day 1 (setup once, reuse forever), then mostly outreach + conversations on Days 2-3.

---

## The one number that matters

By end of Day 3: **first connection batch out, first Looms sent, at least 1 discovery call on the calendar (2 is the target).** Everything else is in service of that. If you're behind, cut prep — never cut sends.

---

## DAY 1 — Set up the machine once, fire the first shots (~4 hrs)

> Day 1 is the only "build" day. The rest is reps. Resist the urge to spread setup across all three days — you want to be *sending* by this afternoon.

### Block 1 — Offer one-pager, finalized (45 min) ✅
You already have File 02 (the full offer). The one-pager is the *sales-facing* compression of it, not a rewrite.

- [ ] Open File 02. Pull these into a single page (Google Doc or Notion, link-shareable):
  - **One-line promise:** "A live LinkedIn + Loom-first experiment loop that makes your targeting and messaging measurably sharper every week — more qualified conversations, not spray-and-pray volume."
  - **Who it's for:** agency/consultant founders selling high-ticket B2B, no RevOps team.
  - **The weekly loop** (5-6 bullets): context → scored account universe → 3-5 angle/message experiments → human-approved sends → measure reply rate AND conversation quality → improve.
  - **What you get** (deliverables list from File 02).
  - **What stays human:** you approve ICP/list/every message, record Looms, run + close calls.
  - **Pricing:** setup $1.5-2.5k; Starter ~$2k / **Growth ~$3.5k (anchor)** / Scale $5k+. Month-to-month after pilot.
  - **The pilot:** 90-day, founder-run, soft meeting-*activity* floor (credited if missed), transparent weekly reviews. **No guaranteed meetings or revenue.**
  - **Why now / why not the alternatives:** vs SDR (~$90k/yr saved vs Growth, live week one vs 3-4mo ramp); vs lead lists (built + scored, not stale CSV); vs blast tools (post-Feb-2024 0.3% spam cap makes volume self-harm — we're low-volume, human-QA'd).
  - **Proof, honestly:** "I run this exact engine on my own company (Oloxa) live. You're an early pilot — you buy the system and the artifacts, not a fabricated case study."
- [ ] **Done bar:** one page, skimmable in 90 seconds, link works on mobile. Plain doc is fine — no design pass.

> ⛔ **Skip:** logo, brand colors, a deck, a Notion site, a Calendly redesign. None of it books a call. The one-pager exists so a warm reply has somewhere to land — that's it.

### Block 2 — Minimum proof bar (40 min) ✅
You have no client case study. That's fine — sell the *system* by showing it running on Oloxa. Assemble three artifacts that prove the engine is real.

- [ ] **One real research card** (anonymize the company if needed): company, contact, signal (CLOSING/HIRING/VOLUME/PAIN), evidence, pain hypothesis, personalized opener. You already produce these for Oloxa — copy one, scrub the name.
- [ ] **One redacted weekly review** (even a rough one): the experiments you ran, reply rate, a note on conversation quality, what you changed for next week. Screenshot or one-pager.
- [ ] **One line of personal credibility** ready to paste: "Here's the literal weekly review from running this on my own company — this is the deliverable, not a mockup."
- [ ] **Done bar:** three artifacts saved in one folder, linkable. They don't need to be pretty — *raw and real beats polished and fake* is the entire pitch.

> ⛔ **Skip:** building a "proof library," a testimonials page, or waiting until Oloxa has results "worth showing." The in-progress artifacts ARE the proof. Don't gate outreach on a milestone.

### Block 3 — LinkedIn profile tune for the motion (30 min) ✅
Prospects will click your profile within minutes of the connect. It needs to say "this person runs GTM," not "real estate brokerage agent."

- [ ] **Headline:** outcome-first, ICP-named. e.g. *"I run a weekly GTM experiment engine for B2B agencies & consultants — more qualified conversations, less spray-and-pray. Building in public."*
- [ ] **About (first 2 lines only — that's all that shows):** who you help + the anti-spray POV. Rewrite the rest later.
- [ ] **Featured section:** pin the one-pager link + one artifact (research card or weekly review).
- [ ] **Banner:** only if you have one in 5 min. Otherwise skip — it does not book calls.
- [ ] **Done bar:** headline + first 2 About lines + Featured link are live. Profile passes the "would a skeptical agency founder take my DM seriously?" test.

> ⛔ **Skip:** a full profile rewrite, a new headshot, a custom banner design, recommendations. Headline + Featured link does 90% of the work for cold-DM credibility. Come back to the rest in week 2.

### Block 4 — First 25 of the target list (60 min) ✅
You need a list to act on *today*, not a perfect 50 by Friday. Build 25 now; you'll grow to 50 by Day 3.

- [ ] **Source order (fastest signal first):**
  1. **Warm network sweep (do this first):** scroll your own connections + past conversations for agency/consultant founders who fit. These convert highest and need no enrichment. Aim for 5-10.
  2. **Sales Nav / LinkedIn search:** founder/owner title + agency/consulting + 2-30 headcount + active recently. Grab 10-15.
  3. **Intent signal — SDR/BDR job posts:** agencies hiring an SDR = pipeline pain + no RevOps = your exact buyer. Pull a few from LinkedIn Jobs / Ind_eed. 3-5.
  4. **Engagement mining:** people commenting on relevant GTM/agency-growth posts. 3-5.
- [ ] **Capture in a simple sheet** (Google Sheet, 7 columns): Name | Company | LinkedIn URL | Warm/Cold | Signal (CLOSING/HIRING/VOLUME/PAIN) | Status | Next action.
- [ ] **Done bar:** 25 rows, each tagged warm/cold + a signal. Good-enough fit beats perfect fit — the loop will tell you who's actually right.

> ⛔ **Skip:** email enrichment tools, Clay tables, scoring formulas, a CRM. A 7-column Google Sheet runs the entire first week. Don't automate a list you can read in one screen.

### Block 5 — Record the reusable Loom audit template (45 min) ✅
This is your unfair advantage. Build **one** reusable structure you can re-record in 60-90s per prospect — not 25 unique scripts.

- [ ] **Lock the 4-beat structure** (memorize it, don't read it):
  1. **Name + why them (0-15s):** "Hey [name] — saw [specific thing: your post on X / you're hiring an SDR / your service page]. Not a pitch, quick observation."
  2. **One specific GTM observation (15-50s):** one concrete thing about *their* targeting/positioning/outbound you'd test. Specific > flattering.
  3. **The frame (50-75s):** "I run a weekly experiment loop that does exactly this — sharpen targeting/messaging weekly instead of spraying volume. I run it on my own company live."
  4. **Soft CTA (75-90s):** "Worth a 15-min look at how I'd run it for [their company]? No pressure either way."
- [ ] **Record ONE master walkthrough** of your screen showing a research card + weekly review, narrating the 4 beats generically. This is your reference + a reusable artifact you can drop into follow-ups.
- [ ] **Done bar:** one master Loom recorded, structure memorized, you can bang out a personalized 90s version in <5 min. **Raw > polished.** Webcam bubble, one take, "ums" allowed.

> ⛔ **Skip:** scripting word-for-word, multiple takes, editing, captions, intro/outro graphics, a fancy thumbnail. The personalization is the value — production is noise. If you spend >45 min here you're hiding from sending.

### Block 6 — First send batch + booking link (45 min) ✅
End Day 1 with shots fired.

- [ ] **Confirm a booking path:** Calendly/Cal.com with a "15-min GTM chat" event, OR just plan to book manually in DMs. Either is fine — don't let booking-tool setup eat the block.
- [ ] **Send 10-15 connection requests** from your list, warm-first. Personalized note (1-2 lines, the Beat-1 hook). For the warmest 2-3, skip the connect formality and just message directly.
- [ ] **Queue tomorrow's Looms:** note which 3-5 connections you'll Loom the moment they accept (or today, for already-connected warm contacts — send 1-2 Looms *today*).
- [ ] **Done bar:** 10-15 connects out, 1-2 Looms sent to warm contacts, booking path confirmed.

> ⛔ **Skip:** a multi-tool sequencer, LinkedIn automation software (risks your account + breaks the human-QA'd positioning you're selling), email setup. Manual sends ARE the product this week.

**End of Day 1 you have:** one-pager, 3 proof artifacts, profile tuned, 25-name list, 1 Loom template, ~12 connects out, 1-2 Looms sent.

## DAY 2 — Outreach engine running (~3.5 hrs)

> Today is reps, not building. The machine exists; you're feeding it. The shape of today is the daily rhythm you'll run for 30 days.

### Block 1 — AM outreach block (75 min) ✅
- [ ] **Comment on 5-10 target prospects' posts** before DMing them — warms the connect. 1-2 genuine sentences, no pitch.
- [ ] **Send 10-15 new connection requests** (next batch from the list).
- [ ] **Record + send 3-4 personalized Looms** to people who accepted from Day 1 (and any warm contacts). Use the 4-beat structure, <5 min each.
- [ ] **Send 10-15 follow-ups** to Day 1 connects who accepted but haven't replied (touch 2: a value-add line or the Loom).
- [ ] **Done bar:** ~12 connects, 3-4 Looms, ~12 follow-ups, ~7 comments. Log the numbers as you go.

### Block 2 — Midday reply triage (2x, 15 min each) ✅
- [ ] Check DMs late morning + mid-afternoon.
- [ ] **Any warm reply → push toward a call same-day if possible:** "Easiest to just show you — got 15 min [today/tomorrow]? Here's my link: [...]". Don't over-nurture a warm lead; book it.
- [ ] Update the sheet's Status column for every reply.
- [ ] **Done bar:** zero warm replies left sitting. Speed is the edge.

### Block 3 — Grow the list to 50 + light content (40 min) ✅
- [ ] Add 15-25 more names to the sheet (same sources, same tagging) to reach ~50 total.
- [ ] **Post ONE build-in-public LinkedIn post** (10-15 min, ship rough): the anti-spray POV, or "what I'm testing this week," or an anonymized Oloxa observation. Content is *support*, not the engine — keep it short and don't agonize.
- [ ] **Done bar:** ~50 names total, 1 post live.

> ⛔ **Skip:** a content calendar, batching a week of posts, designing graphics. One raw post today. The engine is the DMs + Looms.

### Block 4 — Discovery call prep, lightweight (20 min) ✅
You'll likely have a call booked or close. Be ready without over-prepping.

- [ ] **Draft a 5-question discovery skeleton** (reuse for every call): current pipeline source → what's working/not → who they sell to → what they've tried (SDR? lists? agency?) → what "more qualified conversations" is worth to them.
- [ ] Have the one-pager + 2 artifacts open and ready to screen-share.
- [ ] **Done bar:** 5 questions written, artifacts ready. No deck.

**End of Day 2 you have:** ~50-name list, two days of outreach compounding, replies in motion, 1 post live, discovery flow ready.

## DAY 3 — Convert motion into booked calls (~3.5 hrs)

> The objective today is explicit: **get calls on the calendar.** Outreach continues, but every warm thread gets pushed toward a booking.

### Block 1 — AM outreach block (75 min) ✅
- [ ] Same rhythm: 5-10 comments, 10-15 connects, 3-4 Looms, 10-15 follow-ups.
- [ ] **Follow-up touch 3** for Day 1 non-responders who accepted: short, specific, no guilt — e.g. "Last nudge — here's the 90s of how I'd approach [their company]'s outbound: [Loom]. If it's not a fit, all good." Touch 3 is where a lot of replies come from. Then let them sit.
- [ ] **Done bar:** full outreach block logged.

### Block 2 — Booking push on every warm thread (45 min) ✅
- [ ] Go through the sheet. Every "replied / warm" status gets a direct booking ask today.
- [ ] For anyone hesitating: offer the lowest-friction version — "15 min, I'll just show you the engine running on my company and how I'd point it at yours. Worth a look?"
- [ ] **Done bar:** every warm lead has an explicit call ask out. **Target: 1-2 calls on the calendar.**

### Block 3 — Run any live discovery calls (variable) ✅
If a call lands today:
- [ ] Run the 5-question skeleton. Listen more than you pitch.
- [ ] Show the real artifacts. Land the honest pilot frame: 90-day founder-run, soft activity floor, weekly reviews, no guaranteed meetings.
- [ ] **Pitch the pilot toward a close** — don't just "stay in touch." Anchor on Growth (~$3.5k) + setup. If interested, send pilot terms same-day while it's hot.
- [ ] **Done bar:** call run, clear next step set (verbal yes, terms sent, or scheduled follow-up).

### Block 4 — Day-3 review + set the week (30 min) ✅
This is your first weekly review — the loop in miniature.

- [ ] Total the scoreboard across 3 days.
- [ ] **Pick 3-5 experiments for next week** from what you saw: which signal got the best replies? Which opener angle landed? Warm vs cold conversion? Loom-first vs message-first?
- [ ] Note what to *stop* doing (any angle that flopped).
- [ ] **Done bar:** scoreboard totaled, next week's 3-5 experiments written. This artifact also becomes proof you can show prospects.

**End of Day 3 you have:** ~50 contacts worked, 3 days of compounding outreach, warm threads pushed to booking, **1-2 discovery calls booked or run**, and your first weekly-review artifact.

## Tiny Daily Scoreboard (log these 4-5 every day)

Keep this at the top of your Google Sheet. 30 seconds to update. This is the *only* tracking you need this week.

| # | Metric | Day 1 | Day 2 | Day 3 |
|---|--------|-------|-------|-------|
| 1 | **Connection requests sent** | | | |
| 2 | **Looms recorded + sent** | | | |
| 3 | **Replies received** (any) | | | |
| 4 | **Warm conversations** (engaged, on-topic) | | | |
| 5 | **Calls booked** | | | |

**Read it like an operator, not a vanity-metric chaser:**
- The number that decides the week is **#5 (calls booked)** — then **#4 (warm conversations)**, because that's *conversation quality*, the thing you actually sell.
- #1-#3 are inputs. If #5 is zero but #1 is high, the problem is targeting or message, not effort — that's a Day-3-review experiment, not a reason to send more.
- Don't optimize raw reply rate. Two real conversations beat ten "thanks, not now" replies.

## What NOT to build in the first 72 hours (the honest critique)

You will be tempted by all of these. Every one is procrastination dressed as progress:

- **A website / landing page / brand.** A one-pager link is enough to book a pilot. Build the site after you've sold one.
- **Email outbound infrastructure.** Email is your *optional, low-volume secondary*. Standing up domains/warmup/deliverability now is days of work for a channel that isn't your wedge. LinkedIn + Loom first.
- **LinkedIn automation tools.** They risk your account *and* directly contradict the "low-volume, human-QA'd" positioning you're selling. Manual sends are the product right now.
- **Clay / enrichment / scoring pipelines.** A 7-column sheet runs week one. Automate the list only once you've proven which signals convert.
- **Polished Looms / a video studio / an editing workflow.** Personalization is the value; production is noise. One raw take.
- **A pitch deck.** You're selling a founder-to-founder pilot, not pitching a VC. Artifacts + a one-pager + a real conversation close it.
- **A content calendar / batch of posts.** Content supports the engine; it isn't the engine. One rough post a day, max.
- **A full profile rewrite / new headshot / banner.** Headline + Featured link does the credibility job for a cold DM. Finish the rest in week 2.
- **Waiting for Oloxa to show results before reaching out.** The in-progress artifacts are the proof. Don't gate the first 72 hours on a milestone that may be weeks away.

**The trap to name out loud:** every hour spent perfecting an asset is an hour the market *isn't* telling you what's wrong with your targeting and messaging. The engine you're selling gets smarter from contact with real prospects — so make contact. Ship at 70%, send today, let Day 3's review sharpen the rest.
