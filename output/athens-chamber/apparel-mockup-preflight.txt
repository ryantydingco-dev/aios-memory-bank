APPAREL MOCKUP PRE-SEND GATE

1. ASSET AUTHORITY
   - List every supplied logo/art file before designing.
   - Mark each as exact-use, editable, or inspiration-only.
   - Never redraw or reinterpret exact-use artwork.

2. PLACEMENT SPEC
   - Confirm garment, print locations, imprint width, collar offset, and color count.
   - Do not show a back view when the back is blank unless specifically requested.

3. MEASURED ALIGNMENT
   - Determine the collar/body centerline from the garment render.
   - Center the artwork bounding box on that line.
   - Verify equal left/right margins and consistent top offsets across colorways.

4. PAGE COMPOSITION
   - Each garment must have an independent frame.
   - No garment, label, shadow, or crop may overlap another colorway.
   - Keep identical garment scale, baseline, margins, and label spacing.

5. ARTWORK INTEGRITY
   - Compare final text, logos, dates, and sponsor marks against the supplied source.
   - Inspect edges at 100% for background remnants, clipping, halos, or blur.

6. CUSTOMER PROOF VS. QA PROOF
   - Customer proof: clean artwork only.
   - Internal QA proof: centerlines and print bounding boxes.
   - Never send the QA overlay to the customer.

7. FINAL VISUAL REVIEW
   - Inspect the entire composed page, not only each shirt.
   - Confirm no overlap, clipping, uneven spacing, inconsistent scale, or empty views.
   - Compare mockup scale with a real garment reference.

8. APPROVAL GATE
   - Show the final proof to Ryan before any prospect reply or attachment is sent.
   - Do not send until Ryan explicitly approves the exact image.
