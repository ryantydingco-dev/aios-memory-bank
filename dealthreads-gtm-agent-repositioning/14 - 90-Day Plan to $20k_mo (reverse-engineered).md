## ⚠️ First: define "$20k/mo" — it changes everything

Two very different targets hide in that number:

- **(A) $20k recurring MRR by day 90** — you have ~$20k/mo of subscriptions still paying in month 4+. The hard, durable version.
- **(B) $20k cash collected in month 3** — includes one-time setup fees ($1–2k each on Tier 2). Easier to hit (setup fees front-load cash) but not durable.

**Honest take:** (A) in 90 days cold + no case study is a stretch — doable only if you hit the high end of every conversion. (B) is realistically achievable. **Recommend: target (A) $20k MRR as the North Star, use setup fees (B) as accelerant cash, and judge success on a floor/target/stretch band, not a single number.** (Pick your real target — see end.)

## The reverse funnel (blend path, to ~16 clients)

Working backwards from clients → activity. Rates flagged 🟦estimate.

```
16 paying clients
   ↑  ÷ close rate  (🟦 Tier1 $500 closes HIGH ~40% after a killer free radar;
   |                     Tier2 $2k closes ~20-25%. Blended ~30%.)
~55 sales meetings held over 90 days   = ~4-5 meetings/week
   ↑  ÷ meeting-booked rate  (🟦 a strong personalized radar → ~30% book)
~180 radars delivered ("yes, send it")
   ↑  ÷ positive-reply rate  (🟦 strong signal-led opener → ~25% say "send it")
~720 firms CONTACTED (connection accepted + opener, or email opened+replied)
   ↑  ÷ reach rate  (🟦 LinkedIn accept ~35% + email; blended reach ~40%)
~1,500 firms TOUCHED (connection requests + cold emails sent)
```

### What that means in weekly activity (the real scoreboard)
Over ~12 weeks:
- **~125 firms touched/week** (≈ 60 LinkedIn connection requests + 65 cold emails) → ~12 LI CRs/business day (safely under LinkedIn's ~20/day limit) + email carries the rest.
- **~15 "yes send it" replies/week** → ~15 radars delivered/week.
- **~4-5 meetings/week.**
- **~1-2 closes/week** (mostly Tier 1 early, Tier 2 building).

> Reality check: ~1,500 firms touched needs a **list of ~600-800 firms** (you touch each on 2 channels: LinkedIn + email). That's the list. US staffing/recruiting firms number in the tens of thousands, so 600-800 well-fit boutique firms is findable.

## 🔑 The tension that breaks this plan (and the fix)

**~180 hand-built radars at 1 hour each = 180 hours.** Impossible solo alongside selling. The bespoke-per-firm radar does NOT scale to $20k-level volume. **This is the #1 risk in the whole plan.**

**The fix — vertical radars, not per-firm radars:**
- Build **~5 reusable radars by vertical** (tech/eng staffing, fintech, healthcare, sales-leadership, industrial). The detector makes each ~cheap.
- When a firm says "send it," you send the radar for *their* vertical — swap the firm name + one tailored line. **~5 min, not 60.**
- Only hand-build a fully bespoke radar for **hot/Tier-2 prospects** (the few worth the hour).

This turns 180 hours into ~5 vertical builds + 5-min sends. **Without this, $20k/mo is impossible; with it, it's a volume game the machine can carry.** Build the 5 vertical radars in Week 1.

## Honest risks (name them so they don't ambush you)
1. **Radar quality must stay high at volume** — vertical radars can feel generic; keep the depth signal real per firm or close rate craters.
2. **Tier 2 has a 3-week domain lag** — start warming Week 1 or Tier 2 revenue can't land by day 90.
3. **No case study until ~Week 4-5** — early close rate is the lowest it'll ever be; the funnel improves as proof accrues. Don't panic at slow Weeks 3-4.
4. **Solo capacity** — ~125 touches/week + radars + meetings is a real grind. If it cracks, the fix is a VA on list/domain ops, not working more hours.
5. **The estimates could be 2-3× off** — recompute the whole funnel after 2 weeks of real data (this is literally what the weekly learning loop is for).
