## In one sentence
**You find boutique recruiting firms their next clients.**

(Not candidates — clients. The companies that pay them placement fees.)

## What the customer actually gets
Every week, a dashboard of companies in their verticals that are **visibly struggling to hire right now** — each with who to call, why they're stuck, and what to say. They reach out and land new client conversations they'd never have found on their own.

You sell **a timed flow of "call this company now, here's why"** — so they stop depending on referrals for new business.

## Why it's valuable (the math)
- One placement ≈ **$25–35k** in fees. One new client ≈ 3–5 placements ≈ **$75–150k/yr**.
- Land them **one** new client a quarter → ~$75–150k new revenue. Your fee ~$500/mo = $6k/yr.
- **One client pays you back ~12–25×.** That's the whole pitch. (At the old $3.5k/mo it was still 2–4× — either way a no-brainer; the $500/mo subscription just makes it an instant yes.)

## Why they say yes
1. Finding clients is the thing they're **worst at, hate most, need most** (they live on referrals = feast-or-famine).
2. **Timing** — an aging job post = a stuck, desperate company *no competitor has called yet*. You get them there first.
3. **Cheaper than a $120k BD hire**, live immediately.
4. The signal is **unfindable by hand** — they can't just DIY it.

## What you are NOT
- ❌ Not a recruiter (you don't source/place candidates).
- ❌ You don't close their deals (they run the client calls).
- ✅ You do ONE thing: put the right companies, at the right moment, in front of them.
