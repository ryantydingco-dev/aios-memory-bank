# VSL Script — Dealthreads (the AI contact form)

**Duration:** ~2:15 (≈330 words at 150 wpm)
**Format:** Hybrid — short talking-head open + **screen recording of the live demo** (plays to "build, don't be the face"). You can do the whole thing as voiceover over the screen if you'd rather not be on camera.
**Drives to:** Book a 15-min call + try the live wedge (no price — book-only)
**Audience:** Founder-led B2B (5–40 people) with a real "Request a Demo" form, $8k+ deals, no RevOps
**Mechanism:** **Submit-to-Profile** — the instant a lead hits send, the buyer profile builds itself

---

**[Pain Call-Out — primary]**

"If you run a B2B company with a 'Request a Demo' form on your site — I want to show you something that's quietly costing you deals."

[TEXT OVERLAY: Your contact form is throwing away your best leads]

"It takes thirty seconds. Watch."

## SCRIPT NOTES

**Energy arc:** Hook (HIGH) → Problem (LOW/empathy) → Mechanism (MED-HIGH) → Demo (MED, let the product talk) → Proof (HIGH) → CTA (HIGH)

**Why this works:** The demo IS the proof — you're not claiming, you're showing. The "Submit-to-Profile" mechanism gives a skeptic a *reason* it's different (it's automatic, no stack). The pay-when-it-works close removes all risk for someone who's never heard of you.

**Before filming:**
- Pre-run the demo once to warm it up; the first enrichment can be slow.
- Record the demo on a real company (a recognizable one lands harder). Speed-ramp the 30s wait in the edit.
- Read it out loud — circle anything that doesn't sound like you, tell me, I'll rewrite.
- Bold/overlay lines = lean in vocally. Don't rush the [PAUSE]s.

**Fastest path to live:** screen-record the demo with voiceover (you barely appear on camera). Loom/QuickTime is fine for v1 — ship it, watch the data, refine.

**Cut points for ads:** Hook + Problem (0:00–0:38) ≈ the Pain-First ad. Demo moment + CTA ≈ Outcome-First.
