# Dealthreads /goal Command — 14-Day Paid Beta Sprint

Created: 2026-06-02

## Paste-ready goal command

```text
/goal Outcome: In the next 14 days, build and execute a Dealthreads first-money sprint that creates a pipeline of super-targeted founder-led B2B companies with demo/contact forms, books qualified teardown calls, and closes the first paid beta for the AI Contact Form / Buyer Profile Builder.

Primary success condition: first paid beta closed.
Secondary success conditions: 5 qualified teardown calls booked, 30-50 highly targeted leads researched, 15-25 personalized teardown/outreach assets ready or sent, and a follow-up tracker with clear next actions.

Business context: Dealthreads replaces weak demo/contact forms with an AI contact form and buyer-profile workflow. The pain is that a standard form captures name/email/company, but the rep still has to research company fit, buyer role, urgency, budget, timeline, use case, and likely first question before callback. The core message is: “Your form got the lead. Your rep still got homework.” The wedge is a Free Dead Form Teardown: show the company exactly what their form is not telling sales, then offer a beta install.

Target ICP: founder-led B2B companies with real demo/contact/request-sales forms, but not huge enterprise companies. Avoid saturated/over-tooled SaaS giants. Find the space between mid-market and small business: low-key B2B companies with enough inbound workflow to feel the pain, high enough deal value to care, and not enough RevOps/sales ops capacity to have already solved it. Prioritize companies with $8k-$75k ACV, 8-75 employees, founder/operator-led sales, visible demo/contact forms, HubSpot/Pipedrive/Salesforce starter/light CRM usage if detectable, and evidence that speed-to-lead/context matters.

Target segments to explore: niche B2B SaaS, services-enabled software, vertical SaaS, compliance/software vendors, B2B data/tools, cybersecurity-lite, fintech infrastructure, logistics/supply-chain software, healthcare admin software, construction/property management software, HR/payroll/workforce tools, and HubSpot/RevOps agencies if they can introduce multiple client opportunities.

Buying signals to prioritize: recent funding or hiring, active paid ads/content, new product launch, high-value demo CTA, founder/CEO selling publicly, small sales team, visible HubSpot/forms/chat tooling, recent growth language, inbound-heavy website, “book demo/request demo/contact sales” CTA, slow/manual-looking qualification flow, weak form questions, or messaging that implies complex buyer qualification.

Use these sources/tools: web search, dealthreads.io, Dealthreads Marketing folder, AIOS Memory Bank, existing CSVs, Field Notes articles, prospect websites, public LinkedIn/company pages where available, company blogs/news/pages, job posts, pricing pages, form pages, BuiltWith/Wappalyzer-style signals if available, CRM/tooling clues, and any additional tools that would make prospecting/enrichment easier. If useful prospecting tools or data sources would materially improve the workflow, recommend them with cost/benefit and exact use case before assuming access.

Constraints: no generic AI-bro positioning. No vague “transform your GTM with AI.” No spammy volume-first outreach. No aggressive insults like “your form is broken” or “you’re wasting time.” No automated sending without Ryan’s approval. All outreach must be personalized, specific, casual, and hard-hitting. The offer should feel too useful to ignore: a free teardown showing the exact buyer-profile gap on their current form. Do not overbuild dashboards before we have replies. Do not expose or store API keys/secrets. Prefer practical Telegram-ready summaries and files saved into the Dealthreads Marketing folder.

Ryan availability: Ryan can spend about 2 hours/day plus some heavy sprint days. Design the plan around actions Ryan can actually do: review the target list, record Looms, send messages, reply manually, take calls, and approve/adjust positioning.

Deliverables required:
1. ICP refinement: define the best early segment(s), anti-segments, scoring criteria, and why they are likely to buy now.
2. Target CSV: create a CSV of highly targeted prospects with company, website, form URL, segment, employee estimate, fit score, buying signals, evidence URL, suspected CRM/form/tooling if visible, contact/persona, LinkedIn/contact URL if available, teardown angle, opener, and next action.
3. Teardown assets: create reusable Dead Form Teardown format and prospect-specific teardown notes for the highest-fit targets.
4. Outreach scripts: write personalized LinkedIn/email/message templates for founders/operators and optional agency partners.
5. Loom scripts: create 2-minute Loom talk tracks for the top prospects, including before/after buyer-profile framing.
6. Daily action plan: create a 14-day schedule with Ryan’s daily 2-hour execution blocks and heavy sprint-day options.
7. Follow-up tracker: create/update a tracker with statuses: TARGET_FOUND, READY_FOR_TEARDOWN, TEARDOWN_CREATED, LOOM_NEEDED, LOOM_SENT, MESSAGE_SENT, REPLIED_INTERESTED, REPLIED_NOT_NOW, CALL_BOOKED, BETA_INSTALL, CLOSED_LOST, CLIENT_ACTIVE.
8. Landing/content updates: identify any landing page or Field Notes updates needed to support the sales motion and implement only high-leverage changes if the repo is available and changes are safe.
9. Cron job setup: propose and, if approved/possible, create a daily money brief cron that summarizes follow-ups due, Looms needed, warm replies, new target recommendations, and one money action.

Offer framing to test: “I’ll show you what your current demo/contact form is not telling your rep before callback.” The first CTA is not a sales demo. It is: “Want me to send a 2-minute teardown of what your form is missing?” Beta offer can be framed as a managed install that turns form submits into buyer profiles before sales follows up.

Execution method: First, inspect existing Dealthreads files and current target CSVs. Then refine ICP. Then build the first target batch. Then create teardown/outreach assets for the top prospects. Verify URLs/forms manually where possible. Keep evidence attached to every prospect. Do not stop at strategy; produce usable files and next actions.

Stop when: either (A) the first paid beta is closed, or (B) the 14-day sprint package is fully built and actively executing with a qualified pipeline, including at least 30 researched targets, 10 top-priority teardown-ready prospects, outreach/Loom scripts for those 10, a follow-up tracker, and a daily money brief workflow. If blocked by missing access or human action, stop only after creating the exact next human action list Ryan needs to move the deal forward.
```
