# Dealthreads AI Contact Form — Marketing folder

This folder contains the current marketing/GTM assets for the Dealthreads AI Contact Form / buyer-profile builder offer.

## Core positioning

Dealthreads turns a thin demo/contact form submission into a full buyer profile in the CRM before the sales callback.

Primary CTA:

> Want me to show what your form is not telling your rep?

## Files

- `Dealthreads AI Contact Form - User Personas.md` — primary, secondary, and anti-personas plus messaging matrix.
- `Dealthreads AI Contact Form - 90 Day Content Calendar.md` — full content strategy, platform plan, production workflow, and day-by-day calendar.
- `90-day-content-calendar.csv` — spreadsheet version of the 90-day calendar.

- founder-led B2B companies
- 5-40 employees
- $8k+ deal size or $3k+/mo retainers
- real demo/contact-sales form
