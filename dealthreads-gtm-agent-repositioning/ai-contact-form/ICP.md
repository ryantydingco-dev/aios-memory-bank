# ICP — Dealthreads AI Contact Form (the layup profile)

> Who says YES to "$2k setup + $1k/mo, pay-when-it-works, done-for-you" — fast, and means it.
> This feeds the prospecting engine: every signal below maps to a way to FIND them.

## ✅ LOCKED DECISIONS (2026-06-01)
- **Beachhead = COMBO:** boring vertical SaaS (freight/logistics, construction, field service,
  mfg, healthcare/legal ops) **+** B2B running paid ads to a demo form. The intersection
  (a vertical-SaaS company that ALSO runs ads) = the hottest layup.
- **Signal model = STACK ALL, score 0–4:** each target scored by how many fire
  {hiring sales/RevOps · recently funded · running paid ads · Drift/Qualified-or-chat tool}.
  Demo-form presence is a hard qualifier gate (no form → skip). Highest scores → 1:1 teardown
  tier; the rest → cold-email tier.

---

## The hard gate (disqualifies most "good fits")
**They must already get inbound form fills.** No inbound traffic = no pain = no sale.
This kills: referral-only businesses, pre-launch startups, "great-at-craft bad-at-selling"
firms with no demand. Our buyer is **already generating demand and fumbling it on the back end.**

## The layup, in one line
A **founder-led B2B company, ~10–40 people, selling $8k+ deals through a "Request a Demo" form**,
where the founder or a tiny team still handles inbound themselves — and **something just changed
that made inbound matter more** (a raise, a sales hire, or ad spend).

## The 5 qualifiers (all must be true)
1. **Founder-led, ~10–40 ppl** — the founder decides; no RevOps wall, no committee, no procurement
2. **Real inbound form** — "Request a Demo / Talk to Sales / Book a demo" with actual traffic
3. **ACV $8k+** — one won deal = years of the fee; $1k/mo is a rounding error to them
4. **Sales-led** — a human calls the lead back (that's where the pre-built profile pays off)
5. **No enrichment/RevOps stack yet** — the gap is open; they're doing it by hand or not at all

## Buying signals — ranked by INTENT × DETECTABILITY
Each is tagged with how the engine finds it.

### Tier 1 — use these to FIND them
- 🔥 **Hiring an SDR / AE / RevOps / Demand-Gen role right now.** They're scaling inbound by
  hand and about to spend $80–150k on a human for the *exact* gap we automate. The price
  contrast sells itself. **Detect:** Greenhouse / Lever / Ashby public APIs (our detector already
  does this) + LinkedIn Jobs.
- 💰 **Raised seed / Series A in the last 3–9 months.** Cash to spend, growth mandate, still
  small enough that the founder buys. **Detect:** funding news via Firecrawl (detector does this).
- 📣 **Running paid ads to a demo/contact form.** Every fumbled lead has a hard cost-per-lead —
  they feel it in dollars, not vibes. **Detect:** Meta Ad Library, Google Ads Transparency,
  LinkedIn Ad Library → landing pages with forms.

### Tier 2 — strong "why now", qualify/personalize with these
- 👻 **Drift / Qualified refugee.** Their conversational front door died or went enterprise-only.
  **Detect:** scan site HTML / Wayback for old Drift or Qualified snippets.
- 🗣️ **Founder posting about pipeline / sales / inbound on LinkedIn.** Reachable + already
  thinking about the problem. **Detect:** LinkedIn search (manual, top-25 tier).

### Tier 3 — the WEDGE (not for finding — for closing)
- 🕳️ **Their own form gives a slow / thin / generic response.** We literally fill it out and
  time it. Then the teardown is lethal: *"I filled out your form Tuesday. Here's the full profile
  your system could've built from it — and I still haven't heard back."* Pain made personal.

## What they're actually feeling (why-now, in their words)
- "We're booking demos but my reps call in blind and the good ones slip away."
- "I'm paying for these leads and half get a copy-paste follow-up."
- "I'm about to hire an SDR just to research leads — that's six figures for Googling."

## Beachhead recommendation
**Operational / "boring" vertical SaaS** — freight & logistics, construction, field service,
manufacturing, healthcare ops, legal ops. Why it's the layup:
- High ACV + founder-led + demo-led (they fit all 5 qualifiers)
- **Under-targeted by GTM/outbound vendors** — generic B2B SaaS and agencies are pitched to
  death; these niches aren't, so your cold outreach actually gets read (low saturation = the edge)
- **Ryan has adjacency** — already pattern-tested Revenova + Tai (freight TMS). Vertical focus
  compounds: similar teardowns, eventual referrals, "I work with [vertical] companies."

Run the **vertical** for the 1:1 top-25 (give-first Looms). Run **signal-led horizontal** for the
cold-email long tail.

## Disqualifiers (skip — these are NOT layups)
- ❌ Referral-only / no inbound form (nothing to tear down, no felt pain)
- ❌ Pre-revenue / pre-traffic startups (can't pay, no leads to enrich)
- ❌ Enterprise / 100+ ppl with a RevOps team (committee, already solved, slow close)
- ❌ Low-ACV / self-serve / PLG (a human rarely calls back; the profile doesn't pay off)
- ❌ Agencies & generic martech (over-pitched into the ground; race to the bottom)

## How this maps to the engine
Primary filter = a **Tier-1 signal** → qualify against the **5 qualifiers** → enrich with the
buyer_profile engine → score by **how many signals fire** → top scorers get the 1:1 teardown,
the rest get the cold-email sequence. The form-test wedge gets added per-target before outreach.
