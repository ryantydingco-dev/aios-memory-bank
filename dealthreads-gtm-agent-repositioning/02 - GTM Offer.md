## Part 1 — The problems (this is what the offer is built around)

Every one of these is a real, nameable pain for a founder or small GTM team trying to generate pipeline. The offer exists to remove them — in this order.

**1. Pipeline is feast-or-famine.** It comes from referrals, your own network, and whatever hours you can steal between delivery and everything else. When you're busy delivering, outreach stops. Two months later the pipeline is empty and you're scrambling. There's no engine — there's *you*, intermittently.

**2. You have no repeatable outbound motion.** You know outbound *should* work. But every attempt is a from-scratch scramble: pull a list, write some messages, send a burst, get crickets or a few "not right now"s, lose steam, stop. Nothing compounds. Next month you start over.

**3. An SDR is the obvious fix and the wrong first move.** A fully-loaded SDR runs ~$10–12k/mo, takes 3–4 months to ramp, and the average one leaves inside ~18 months — taking everything they learned with them. You'd be betting a quarter of runway and a year of ramp on one hire before you even know your message works.

**4. Lists go stale and "more volume" now backfires.** Buying a list and blasting it used to be the cheap option. Since early 2024, Google and Yahoo punish it — too many sends or spam complaints and you torch your own domain. The spray-and-pray playbook is now actively self-harming.

**5. You can't see what's working.** Opens and sends are vanity. The questions that matter — *which segment, which angle, which opener actually started a real conversation with a fit buyer* — go unanswered, because nobody's measuring conversation quality. So you can't get better; you can only get louder.

**6. Targeting is a guess.** "Anyone who might buy" is not an ICP. Most outreach fails before a word is written, because the list is too broad and there's no reason behind each name. Better copy can't save a bad list.

**7. GTM chaos eats the founder.** All of the above lands on the one person who can least afford it — you. GTM becomes a low-grade background stress instead of a system that runs.

> **The honest reframe:** you don't have a "send more messages" problem. You have a *targeting + learning* problem. Volume is a commodity (and now a liability). The thing that actually compounds into pipeline is sharper targeting and faster learning — and that's what this is built to sell.

## Part 2 — The offer

### Name
**Dealthreads GTM Experiment Engine** — a deployed go-to-market agent team that runs a weekly, human-approved experiment loop across LinkedIn, Loom, and follow-up, so your targeting and messaging get measurably sharper every week.

### One-line promise
> We run a live, human-approved go-to-market motion on your business every week — LinkedIn + Loom-first — so you get more *qualified* conversations with fit buyers, without hiring an SDR or burning your domain on spray-and-pray.

### Who it's for
Founders and small GTM teams ($1–10M, or pre-scale) selling a real B2B offer to other businesses, who:
- get pipeline from referrals + founder hustle and want a *system* instead,
- have no RevOps/SDR function and can't justify the hire yet,
- are credible operators with a real LinkedIn presence, willing to lend their profile and approve messages,
- sell where a personal, peer-to-peer first touch (LinkedIn + a short Loom) is natural.

**Not for:** pre-offer founders with no proof, anyone who wants volume-at-any-cost, regulated/anti-LinkedIn niches, or transactional/PLG motions where 1:1 outbound is the wrong tool.

### The 30-day outcome (what's true after month one — honestly)
Not "X meetings guaranteed." After 30 days:
- A real **targeting engine** exists: a defined ICP, a scored + enriched account universe, and a documented reason every account is on it.
- **3–5 named experiments have run** — distinct angle/segment/message bets, each with a hypothesis you approved before it shipped.
- Outreach is **live and on-brand** — LinkedIn + Loom + follow-up, every message human-approved, sent from your profile.
- You can **read what's working** — a weekly review showing reply rate *and conversation quality*, by segment and angle.
- You have a **next-week plan driven by evidence**, not vibes. Week 5 is sharper than week 1 by design.

> Said plainly: month one buys you the machine and the first real signal. The compounding into booked meetings is months 2–3. Anyone promising a meeting flood in week one off cold outreach is selling a number they can't control.

LinkedIn (primary) · Loom/video (the differentiator) · structured follow-up · email (optional, low-volume secondary) · CRM follow-up tasks. **Deliberately not a cold-email blast machine.** We run the opposite of volume.

### Engine vs. human (nothing ships without a human yes)
The engine builds the universe, drafts research cards, designs experiments, drafts every message, queues + tracks, triages replies, writes the weekly review. **You** approve the ICP/list, approve every message, record the Looms (your face/voice), take the real conversations, and close. Human approval is the feature, not the bottleneck.

### The weekly learning loop (the actual IP)
Context → enriched universe → experiment design → human-approved execution → measure (reply rate **+ conversation quality**) → improve. Week N+1 sharper than week N, on purpose. The durable edge is the *loop + conversation-quality measurement*, not message volume.
