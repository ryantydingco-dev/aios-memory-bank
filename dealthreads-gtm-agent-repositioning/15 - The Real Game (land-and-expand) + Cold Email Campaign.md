## The real game: land cheap, deliver hard, expand into automation

The recruiting client-finder is **not the business** — it's the **front door**. The business is becoming these firms' trusted AI person and selling them automation ($5–15k projects + retainers) once you've proven you make them money.

**What this changes about how you run the front offer:**
- **Don't optimize the radar for max profit — optimize it for max trust + foot-in-door.** Lead with cheap/free, over-deliver, look generous. A $500 client who trusts you is worth more than a $2k client who feels squeezed, because the $500 one buys the $10k automation later.
- **The radar IS an AI automation** → it's a live demo of "I build AI that makes you money." That's the bridge: *"I built the thing that finds your clients. Want me to look at what else in your business an AI agent could run?"*
- **Sequence the trust:** radar (proof) → they see real client conversations from it → "what else could you automate for me?" → discovery → automation project. You never cold-pitch automation; you earn the right to it.

**The goal math (cash collected, the soft version):**
- ~$10k MRR = ~8–10 retained radar/outreach clients (mix of $500–$2k). Reachable.
- ~$10k collected cash/mo = setup fees ($1–2k × a few) + **1–2 automation projects** ($3–10k each) once trust exists.
- → **$20k/mo cash is a blend, and the automation upsell is what carries the "cash" half.** The front offer alone gets you to ~$10–12k; automation pushes past $20k.

**Implication:** the first 30–45 days are about **landing radar clients and over-delivering** (building the trust bank). The automation cash shows up in days 45–90 as those relationships mature. Don't expect the $20k from radar subscriptions alone — that's not where the money is.

## THE COLD EMAIL SEQUENCE (copy-paste, no pricing, give-first)

> 4 touches over ~12 days. Plain, human, operator tone. The CTA is always "want the list?" (low-friction yes) — never "book a call" cold. Replace [brackets]. Send from a real human name, not "Dealthreads Team."

### Email 1 — the hook
```
Subject: companies stuck hiring in [their niche]

[First name] — quick one, no pitch.

You place [IT / engineering] talent. I track companies in that space that are visibly stuck hiring — technical roles sitting open 4-9 months on their own job boards. Each one is a company that needs a firm like yours, right now, and probably hasn't been called yet.

I can pull a short list of them for [Firm] — who to contact and why. Free, yours whether or not we ever talk.

Want me to send it over?

[Ryan]
```

### Email 2 — the proof teaser (+3 days)
```
Subject: re: companies stuck hiring

[First name] — quick example of what I mean.

One company in your space has had a senior engineering role open ~280 days, plus 11 other technical reqs against a lean team. That's not "might hire" — that's a firm that's tried and can't fill it alone. A mandate sitting there.

I've got ~8 like that tailored to what [Firm] places. Want the list?
```

### Email 3 — the why (+4 days, new angle)
```
Subject: the signal I actually track

[First name] — no worries if the timing's off. The reason I build these:

A job post aging past 90 days = a company that's tried and failed to hire on its own = the exact moment they'll take a recruiter's call. Most firms find these *after* the role gets reposted and three competitors are already pitching. I find them while they're still stuck.

Happy to send [Firm]'s batch — just say the word.
```

### Email 4 — the breakup (+5 days)
```
Subject: last one from me

[First name] — I'll leave it here.

If client-side pipeline ever gets tight, I'm at this address — I pull these every week. Either way, hope the back half of the year's a strong one.

[Ryan]
```

### When they reply "yes, send it" → the conversion message
```
Great — here it is. [link to their vertical radar dashboard]

These are [vertical] companies with engineering roles stuck open 90+ days right now — who to contact, why, and an opener you could use today. Yours, no strings.

I built this one by hand. I run a system that does it every week. Rather than explain it over email, easiest if I just walk you through the live version — grab 15 minutes? [calendar link]
```
