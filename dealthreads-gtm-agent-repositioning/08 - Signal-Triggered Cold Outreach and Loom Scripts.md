# 08 — Signal-Triggered Cold Outreach & Loom Scripts

> Verbatim, copy-paste cold scripts for the boutique-search-firm ICP, **keyed to the specific buying signal** from `07`. The whole point: every opener references a real signal we detected → the outreach itself proves the engine ("I find signals" demonstrated, not claimed). Give-first (free Mandate Radar, `06`), client-side BD only (never candidate sourcing), MP-to-MP peer tone, no AI-slop, no guarantees.
> Replace `[brackets]`. Personalize the bolded signal line per firm — that line is the proof; the rest is reusable.

---

## The structure (every sequence is the same 6 beats)

1. **Connection request** — name the signal, no pitch.
2. **DM #1 (on accept)** — the give: free Mandate Radar tied to their signal.
3. **Follow-up #1** (~3 days) — a concrete proof crumb (one real opportunity).
4. **Loom** (~day 5, if any engagement) — the differentiator; built around their signal.
5. **Follow-up #2** (~day 8) — restate the give, then rest.
6. **Meeting ask** — ONLY after the free batch lands well.

> Rule: the *ask* is always to **give** (send the free batch), never to take a meeting — until value has landed. The meeting becomes their idea.

## SIGNAL-KEYED OPENERS (the line that proves the engine)

Pick the connection-request + DM-opener that matches the firm's top buying signal. Everything after the opener is shared (see "Shared blocks" below).

### 🔥 BD-HIRE (they posted a Business Development / Client Partner role)
**Connection request:**
```
[Name] — saw [Firm] is hiring on the business-development side. I work with boutique search firms on exactly that (client acquisition, not candidates). Worth connecting.
```
**DM opener:**
```
Appreciate the connect, [Name]. Saw you're bringing on a BD/client-partner — so you're clearly investing in the client side, which most search firms put off forever.

Before that hire is fully ramped (or if you're still deciding), I can show you what a system does on the same problem. Quick one below — no pitch.
```
*Why it lands: they've already decided client-acquisition is worth real money. You're the cheaper/faster proof-of-concept before a $10k+/mo hire ramps.*

### 🔥 WENT-INDEPENDENT (recently launched / left a big firm)
**Connection request:**
```
[Name] — congrats on launching [Firm]. I work with newly-independent search principals on the client-acquisition side. Always good to connect with someone building from scratch.
```
**DM opener:**
```
Appreciate the connect, [Name]. Going independent is the dream and the trap — you're suddenly the recruiter AND the entire business-development engine.

The client side is the part I build a system for. Quick one, no pitch:
```
*Why it lands: zero BD infrastructure, hungry, fast solo decision, feels the pain acutely week one.*

*Why it lands: the macro is real and evidenced (`01c`); positions you as the response to a shift they're feeling.*

## THE LOOM SCRIPTS (the differentiator)

Two distinct Looms — know which job each does.

### Loom A — "The Opener Loom" (sent in/with DM #1, ~45–60s)
Replaces a wall of text; gets the "yes, send it." Face on, their LinkedIn open on screen.

```
[0–10s] "Hey [Name], made you a 60-second one — no slides, promise. Saw [their signal: you're hiring a BD lead / you just launched / your post on the market]."

[10–35s] "So here's what I do, and I'll just show you. You're the best in [vertical] at finding the candidate. I work the other side — finding the companies that NEED one before your competitors call them. [screen: pull up a real example] Like — [RealCompany] just [signal]. That's a mandate sitting there right now."

[35–55s] "I want to build you a free batch of about 7 of those, in [vertical], this week. Decision-maker, why-now, an opener you could use. Yours either way. Just reply 'send it' and it's yours."

[55–60s] "That's it. Hope the market's treating you alright, [Name]."
```
*Rules: raw > polished. Show a real screen, not a deck. The signal line in [0–10s] is the proof — it must be true and specific.*

### Loom B — "The Sample Walkthrough Loom" (delivered WITH the free Mandate Radar, ~2–3 min)
This is the actual deliverable + the real sell. Walk their 7 companies on screen.

```
[0–15s] "[Name], here's the batch I promised — 7 [vertical] companies showing exec-hiring signals this week. Let me walk you through a couple so it's useful, not just a list."

[15–90s] "[screen: the research card] First one — [Company]. They [signal: posted a VP Eng req / raised a Series B / their CTO just left]. Decision-maker's [Name], [title]. Why now: [reason]. And here's an opener you could send today: [read the opener]. … [repeat for 1–2 more, faster]."

[90–150s] "The other 4 are in the doc, same format. Use any of them, no strings — that's yours.

What I'll mention once: this was one week, built by hand as a sample. The actual engine runs this every week — fresh signals, your approval on every message, sent on LinkedIn + a short video like this one, with a weekly review of what's turning into real client conversations. If you ever want it running on your client side so you're not thinking about it, that's the 15-minute conversation. Either way — enjoy the 7."
```
*Rules: lead with THEIR value (the 7), mention the paid engine ONCE near the end, exit on generosity. The walkthrough IS the demo — if these 7 are genuinely good, the engine sells itself.*

## "Apply our GTM to our GTM" — the recursion made explicit (use it on the call)
When an MP asks "so how does this actually work?" the answer is what you just did to them:
> *"Honestly? What I just did to you is the product. I found your firm because you [their signal]. I led with that signal instead of a generic pitch. I gave value before asking for anything. That's the exact engine — I just pointed it at recruiters instead of pointing it at your clients. If it got you to reply, imagine it pointed at the companies that pay you $75k a mandate."*

That line is the close. The proof isn't a case study — **it's their own inbox.**

## What NOT to do (guardrails)
- ❌ Don't send a signal-keyed opener with a **wrong/stale signal** — a fabricated "I noticed X" is worse than generic; it disproves the engine. If you can't find a real signal, use the MACRO opener (honest) or skip the firm.
- ❌ Don't pitch candidate sourcing. Client-side only.
- ❌ Don't ask for the meeting before the free batch lands.
- ❌ Don't over-produce the Loom. Raw + specific + true beats polished + generic.
- ❌ Don't guarantee mandates/placements. Guarantee the work.
- ❌ Don't fake the ~5/week scarcity — it's real (build-time).
